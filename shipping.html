Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: Aldovra sells only online services. Nothing is shipped to your address. A paid assessment unlocks on your dashboard within minutes of a successful payment.

1. What we deliver

Aldovra provides online skill assessments for students and freshers in India. A payment buys one assessment attempt for one skill (a new skill unlock or a reattempt), at the price shown on the dashboard and on our Assessment Pricing page. Job listings on Aldovra are free; we never charge for jobs, applications or referrals.

2. No physical shipping

We do not sell or ship any physical goods. There are no shipping charges and no courier delivery.

3. How and when it is delivered

  • Assessment attempt: unlocked in your Aldovra dashboard (aldovra.com/dashboard.html) usually within 5 minutes of a successful payment. You can take it any time after that.
  • Result: shown on screen as soon as you submit the assessment, and saved in your dashboard.
  • Certificate: issued in your dashboard after you score 75% or more, submit your environment video and our team reviews it, usually within 3 working days. You can download it as PDF or PNG and share its verification link.

A confirmation of your payment is also sent by our payment partner to the email or phone you used at checkout.

4. If it is not delivered

If money left your account but the attempt did not unlock within 24 hours, email support@aldovra.com with the transaction ID, date and amount. We will unlock the attempt or refund you in full, as described in our Refund and Cancellation Policy.
