24-hour cooling period

A minimum gap of 24 hours is required between reattempts.

A student can take up to four reattempts in a calendar month, subject to the cooling-period rule.

What hiring teams actually see.

Traditional resume

What you say

  • SkillsSkills are generally listed by the candidate.
  • AssessmentNot normally part of the resume itself.
  • EvidenceSupporting evidence may be shared separately.
  • VerificationThe resume itself does not create a verification status.
  • Career directionDepends on the resume and employer review.

What you can prove

  • Skills
    Selected skills connected to assessment status.
  • Assessment
    Assessment score recorded with the skill profile.
  • Evidence
    Relevant assessment evidence connected to the skill.
  • Verification
    75%+ profiles eligible for verified HR/recruiter preview.
  • Career direction
    Skills viewed in relation to career tracks.

Aldovra does not replace a resume. It adds a skill-verification layer so hiring teams can review assessment-backed information alongside a candidate's broader profile.
