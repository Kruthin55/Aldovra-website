What happens after your assessment?
Your result is recorded, then the score determines the next step.
01 · Result Score + evidence recorded

Your assessment result and relevant evidence can be connected to the skill profile.

02 · Outcome Score determines your status

75%+ can move toward verified profile eligibility; lower scores follow the reattempt rules below.

03 · Next step HR preview or reattempt

Eligible verified profiles can be available for HR/recruiter preview. Otherwise, an eligible reattempt can be scheduled after the cooling period.

⏳
1 Week Cooling Period

A minimum gap of one week is required between reattempts.

📅
Maximum 4 Reattempts Per Month

A student can take up to four reattempts in a calendar month, subject to the cooling-period rule.

Important: Only profiles with a score of 75% or above are eligible to be shown as verified profiles for HR/recruiter preview and further hiring steps. A lower score does not make the profile verified; it follows the applicable reattempt rule.
