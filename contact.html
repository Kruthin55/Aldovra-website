Contact Aldovra

Need help with your account, resume, assessment or platform experience? Reach out using the support details below.

Email support

support@aldovra.com

Please include the email associated with your Aldovra account and a brief description of the issue. Do not email passwords, one-time passcodes or payment-card details.

General enquiries and partnerships

helloaldovra.com@gmail.com

For general questions, hiring partnerships, recruiter access, campus collaborations, media or business enquiries.

What to include

  • Account or assessment issue: the registered email and assessment name, if relevant.
  • Payment query: transaction date, amount and gateway reference if one exists. Never send full card or UPI PIN details.
  • Privacy request: clearly describe the data or action you are asking about.

Response time

We aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary; a fixed response-time guarantee is not currently offered.

Grievances

For unresolved concerns, email support@aldovra.com with “Grievance” in the subject line and explain the earlier support request, if any.
