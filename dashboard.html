How ranking works

Points. Each verified skill adds its best score × 10. A verified 86% earns 860 points. More verified skills and higher scores mean more points.

What counts. Only skills passed at 75% or above, with your exam environment video submitted, are ranked. Practice and unverified attempts don't count.

Ties. Equal points are broken by your average verified score.

Seasons. "This month" counts only skills verified since the 1st of this month (IST), so everyone gets a fresh shot every month.

🔒 Only your first name and last initial are shown on the leaderboard.

Having trouble applying? Read these quick tips

College / university not listed? Type the full official name and wait for suggestions; select an exact match only. Try a spelling variant such as “Guru Nanak” only if it matches your actual institution. Do not choose a different college just to continue.

Greenhouse form: If the structured School Name list doesn't contain your college, select “Other” if the employer provided it. Greenhouse lets the employer maintain that list, so only the employer can add a missing option. Greenhouse school-list guidance ↗

Lever University Picker: It's a standardized list and doesn't accept free text in that field. If your college isn't listed, don't select an unrelated school; look for a separate text field or contact the employer to request the correct option. Lever university-picker guidance ↗

Job says closed, filled or completed: Employer pages can change between feed updates. Close that tab, use “Report issue” on the Aldovra job card, and try another live listing.

Good to know: Applications open on the employer’s website. Aldovra can't edit their required fields or school lists, but your issue report helps us review the listing and guide future applicants.
