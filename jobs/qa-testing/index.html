Questions

Are these jobs really for freshers?

Yes. Every job here passed the same fresher filter as the Aldovra student dashboard: roles asking for years of experience or senior titles are removed, and 0–1 or 0–2 year roles are kept.

How fresh are the listings?

Only jobs posted in the last 30 days are shown, and each employer link is re-checked several times a day. Closed jobs disappear automatically.

Is Aldovra free?

Creating an account and browsing jobs is free, and your first skill assessment is free. A verified skill helps your application stand out.
