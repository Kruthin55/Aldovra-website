Important information

Please read this carefully before you begin.

Aldovra is built for students, fresh graduates and job seekers who are serious about proving their skills and pursuing genuine career opportunities.

! An important note before registration

Please take a few minutes to read the information on this page before creating your Aldovra account. It explains what Aldovra is designed for, what we do and do not promise, how assessment attempts work, and when reattempt charges may apply.

01 · Who Aldovra is for

Built for serious, job-focused candidates.

Aldovra is primarily designed for students, fresh graduates and job seekers who are actively working toward career opportunities and want to demonstrate their skills through a genuine assessment process.

We respect the time and effort of candidates who are preparing seriously for employment. Our platform and assessment process are therefore designed to encourage genuine participation and discourage repeated casual or non-serious attempts.

If your goal is to genuinely build your profile, demonstrate your abilities and explore relevant opportunities, Aldovra is designed with that purpose in mind.

02 · What Aldovra aims to provide

We help you present proof of your skills.

Aldovra is intended to create a structured way for candidates to demonstrate selected skills through assessments and supporting evidence.

  • Build a profile around the skills you want to demonstrate.
  • Request and complete relevant assessments through the Aldovra assessment process.
  • Build assessment-based evidence around your skills.
  • Become eligible for verified-profile visibility when the applicable verification criteria are met.
  • Allow eligible verified profiles to be considered for relevant HR or hiring-team discovery.
03 · Please understand this clearly

Aldovra does not guarantee a job.

Aldovra is not a job-placement guarantee platform. Creating an account, completing an assessment, receiving a verification status, or becoming visible to hiring teams does not guarantee an interview, selection, offer or employment.

Our purpose is to help serious candidates demonstrate their skills, build credible evidence and access additional possibilities for being discovered by relevant hiring teams.

  • Assessment success does not guarantee an interview.
  • A verified profile does not guarantee selection.
  • HR preview or discovery does not guarantee an offer.
  • Final hiring decisions are made by employers based on their own requirements and processes.
04 · Fees and registration

Creating your Aldovra account is free.

Aldovra does not charge a registration fee simply for creating an account or building your profile.

No registration fee Creating an Aldovra account and profile does not require a registration payment.
First assessment attempt — FREE Your first eligible assessment attempt is provided without an assessment fee.

Reattempt charges may apply only when another assessment attempt is required under the applicable Aldovra assessment policy.

06 · Why reattempt rules exist

Designed to protect the assessment process.

Aldovra's assessment process is intended to be conducted in a controlled and secure manner so that assessment results can provide meaningful evidence of a candidate's demonstrated skills.

Reattempt limits, cooling periods and applicable reattempt charges are designed to support responsible assessment administration and discourage repeated non-serious attempts.

These rules help us focus our assessment resources on candidates who are genuinely interested in demonstrating their skills and pursuing career opportunities.

07 · Your responsibility

Please participate genuinely.

By using Aldovra, candidates are expected to provide accurate information and participate in assessments honestly.

  • Provide accurate profile and educational information.
  • Attempt assessments genuinely and independently according to the applicable assessment instructions.
  • Do not attempt to manipulate, bypass or compromise assessment controls.
  • Do not provide misleading evidence or information.
  • Follow the assessment instructions and platform policies communicated by Aldovra.
