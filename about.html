About Aldovra

Aldovra is a skills-focused career platform designed to help students and early-career candidates present their abilities more clearly and help hiring teams discover talent.

What the platform is designed to do

  • Help candidates build a profile with education, interests and skills.
  • Support skill assessments and display results where assessments are available.
  • Use resume information to help candidates understand relevant skills and career directions.
  • Surface job opportunities and career-readiness resources that may be relevant to a candidate.

Assessment and verification

Assessment availability, formats and verification criteria may vary by skill and platform status. A payment, if introduced for an eligible service, does not guarantee a passing score, verified status, job offer or interview. Results depend on the assessment criteria and the candidate's performance.

Our approach

We aim to make skill evidence and early-career opportunities easier to understand. Aldovra does not guarantee employment, recruiter responses or hiring outcomes.
