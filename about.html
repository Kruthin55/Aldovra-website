About Aldovra

Aldovra is a career platform for students and freshers in India. We show live fresher jobs from companies' own career pages, and we help students prove their skills with tests that recruiters can verify.

What the platform is designed to do

  • List live fresher and entry-level jobs in India from companies' official career pages, checked every day, and hide jobs once they close.
  • Read a student's resume to find their skills and set up a skill test for each one. The first test is free.
  • Run proctored tests and issue a verified certificate and a shareable profile link to students who score 75% or more and complete the video check.
  • Help recruiters find freshers whose skills have already been verified.

Assessment and verification

Assessment availability, formats and verification criteria may vary by skill and platform status. A payment, if introduced for an eligible service, does not guarantee a passing score, verified status, job offer or interview. Results depend on the assessment criteria and the candidate's performance.

Our approach

We want freshers to spend their time on real, open jobs and to have proof of what they can do. Aldovra does not guarantee employment, recruiter responses or hiring outcomes.
