Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: if you were charged twice, or charged but the attempt never unlocked, you get a full refund. If you paid but have not started the attempt, you can cancel within 7 days for a full refund. Once an attempt has started, it cannot be refunded.

1. What you pay for

Payments on Aldovra are for a single assessment attempt (a reattempt, or unlocking a new skill), at the price shown on the dashboard and on our Assessment Pricing page. Payments are collected by our payment partner, PayU.

2. Full refunds

You get a full refund when:

  • you were charged more than once for the same attempt;
  • money left your account but the attempt did not unlock on your dashboard; or
  • a technical fault on our side stopped you from starting or submitting the paid attempt, and we cannot restore the attempt for you.

3. Cancelling before you start

If you paid for an attempt but have not started it, you can ask to cancel it within 7 days of payment and get a full refund.

4. When refunds are not given

Once a paid attempt has started, it is used and cannot be refunded, including when you leave midway, run out of time, or score lower than you hoped. An attempt that ends because of an integrity violation (for example, switching tabs or using another device) is also not refunded.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email support@aldovra.com from your registered email with the transaction ID, date, amount and reason. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, password or full card number. We reply within 2 working days.

6. Processing time

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through PayU within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit.

7. Contact

Refund and payment support: support@aldovra.com.
