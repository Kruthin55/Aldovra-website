Device Associate, Relay TAQ

Amazon · Chennai

About the role

Device Associates at Amazon work on Manual Testing for the cutting edge applications and products at the user level. They are pure Black Box Testers. They should be able to understand the functionality and execute the test cases accurately and deliver on the committed deadlines. They should be able to accurately report the results with proper reporting. Key job responsibilities Execute test cases prepared for testing Software builds on the Desktop and Mobile platforms for Amazon Assistant Products. Perform test case execution and report bugs accurately. Understand testing procedures and guidelines for new builds / releases. Perform regression and repetitive testing exercises to qualify builds without compromising on quality. Use software tools for data capture on a daily basis like adb command. Be comfortable with capturing results, communicating and escalating failures and providing individual status reports Raising all failures/doubts related to the execution of test cases in the clarification portal and closing the same as per the SLA’s Basic knowledge of reading scripts for trouble shooting. A day in the life Execute a predefined set of instructions prepared for testing Software builds on the Relay Products. Own the daily targets against internal and external dependencies ensuring to meet timelines with quality.

About the team

Middle Mile - Once the goods reaches the port terminal and after it clears custom duties, the goods will be transported to the Inbound Cross-Dock through Intermodal (combination of truck and train). In IxD, many LTL and small parcels are consolidated into as few dense inbound shipments as possible. From IxD, the goods are transported and stored in one of the Amazon FCs through line haul trucks. The Middle Mile Product and Tech QA team (TAQ) owns the experience of every customer-facing product under the Middle Mile Product and Tech organization. We own quality and automation deliverables for the following products: Relay for Carriers, Relay for Drivers and Yard and Gate Tech.

- Bachelor's degree - Knowledge of QA methodology and tools
