About the role

Job Description:- Accounting and Audit assistance Maintain accurate financial records through data entry, ledger updates, and account reconciliations. Ensure information is complete, reliable, and up to date. Support audit teams by carrying out audit procedures, testing controls, and preparing clear audit documentation. Financial Analysis Assist in preparing financial statements, schedules, reports, and analysis that support business decisions and meet reporting requirements. Compliance Ensure work follows accounting standards, statutory guidelines, and internal policies. Help maintain a strong compliance environment. Taxation Support Contribute to tax compliance work, including preparing and filing income tax returns and maintaining supporting documentation. Documentation and Workpaper Organize audit workpaper, supporting documents, and reports in a structured and easy‑to‑retrieve format. Training and Development Participate in learning sessions and skill‑building workshops to strengthen your technical and professional capabilities. Teamwork Work closely with colleagues, seniors, and mentors to deliver high‑quality work and meet engagement dead lines.
