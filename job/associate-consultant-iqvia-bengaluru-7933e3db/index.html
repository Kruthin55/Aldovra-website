Associate Consultant

IQVIA · Bengaluru

About the role

Associate Consultant | Commercial Strategy Consulting Start your consulting career where strategy, data and life sciences come together. Your Opportunity at IQVIA What if your first role could help shape how life sciences companies enter new markets, launch products, understand patients, evaluate investments and make critical growth decisions? As an Associate Consultant in Commercial Strategy Consulting at IQVIA, you will work on real-world business challenges for organizations across the healthcare and life sciences industry. You will combine structured problem solving, research, data analysis and strategic thinking to turn complex questions into clear insights and actionable recommendations. Working alongside experienced consultants and cross-functional teams, you will gain exposure to diverse projects and stakeholders. This is an opportunity to begin your career in Management Consulting while developing deep expertise in one of the world's most dynamic and purpose-driven industries: life sciences. What Could You Work On? Your projects could involve helping healthcare and life sciences organizations answer questions such as:

Go-to-Market Strategy - Analyze markets, customers and competitors to help develop effective commercialization strategies.

Market & Portfolio Assessment - Evaluate market attractiveness, growth opportunities, competitive dynamics and potential portfolio choices.

Commercial Due Diligence - Bring together market research, data and strategic analysis to support critical business decisions.

Patient Journey - Use healthcare data and research to identify important insights across the patient journey and uncover opportunities to improve outcomes.

Forecasting & Analogue Analysis - Analyze historical trends, market dynamics and comparable products to support forecasts and strategic planning.

Market Access - Support engagements that explore the commercial and market considerations influencing access to healthcare products What You Will Do As an Associate Consultant, you will learn how consultants take an ambiguous business question and transform it into a structured recommendation. You will:

Solve complex business problems using structured thinking, research and analysis.

Analyze healthcare data from IQVIA and other relevant data sources to uncover trends and insights.

Conduct secondary research on markets, companies, competitors, therapies and industry trends.

Translate data into the "So what?", identifying what an insight actually means for the client's business.

Develop compelling PowerPoint presentations, analyses and recommendations for project teams and stakeholders.

Participate in project discussions and contribute your perspectives and insights.

Collaborate with cross-functional teams with opportunities to present findings to project stakeholders.

Take ownership of defined workstreams, managing quality and timelines.

Identify potential risks, data gaps and emerging issues and communicate them proactively.

Contribute to high-quality client deliverables in a dynamic consulting environment. What We Are Looking For We are looking for students who are curious, analytical, ambitious and excited by the idea of solving difficult business problems. Education Graduate degree in:

Life Sciences

Engineering

Or another relevant discipline You Could Be a Great Fit If You...

Love solving problems - You enjoy taking unstructured questions and breaking it down logically.

Are curious about business - You want to understand why companies grow, how markets evolve and what drives competitive advantage.

Enjoy working with data - You are comfortable exploring information, finding patterns and using evidence to reach conclusions.

Ask "So what?" - You don't stop at finding the answer. You want to understand what the answer means and what someone should do next.

Think in a structured way - You can organize complex information into a clear and compelling story.

Communicate effectively - You can articulate your thinking clearly through conversations, presentations and written communication.

Enjoy learning quickly - You are comfortable getting up to speed on unfamiliar topics, markets and business problems.

Take ownership - You are proactive, dependable and committed to delivering highquality work.

Thrive in teams - You enjoy collaborating with people across different disciplines, locations and perspectives. Skills That Will Help You Succeed

Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities

Critical and structured thinking

Research and data interpretation skills

Strong written and verbal communication

Effective presentation and storytelling skills

Ability to work in a fast-paced, project-based environment

Interest in business, healthcare, life sciences and market trends

Ability to understand how broader economic and market developments can influence industries and companies

Strong working knowledge of Microsoft PowerPoint and Excel is an advantage Prior consulting experience is not essential. What matters most is your curiosity, analytical mindset, willingness to learn and ability to turn information into insight. Why IQVIA? At IQVIA, you won't simply analyze a business problem. You will have the opportunity to understand the intersection of: Strategy + Healthcare + Data + Technology + Human Science You can build capabilities highly valued across the consulting industry: Structured Problem Solving | Commercial Strategy | Data Analysis | Market Research | Storytelling | Client Communication | Healthcare Expertise You will also have the opportunity to:

Gain global exposure by collaborating with teams and stakeholders across geographies.

Work on meaningful problems facing healthcare and life sciences organizations.

Learn through data and develop an evidence-based approach to strategy.

Build consulting skills early through hands-on project experience.

Develop a differentiated career profile combining management consulting capabilities with healthcare expertise. Your Consulting Career Can Start Here. If you are someone who enjoys asking why, finding answers in data, challenging assumptions and turning complex information into clear recommendations, we would like to meet you. Bring your curiosity.
