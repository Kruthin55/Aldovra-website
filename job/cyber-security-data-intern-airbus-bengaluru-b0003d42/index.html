Cyber Security Data Intern

Airbus · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description: Job Description - Data Intern Duration: 6 Months Job Description: As a VCR Data / Cyber Risks WatchTower intern, you will support the Airbus Corporate Cybersecurity team in collecting, engineering, analyzing, and visualizing cyber risk data across our enterprise ecosystem. You will have the opportunity to work alongside experienced professionals in the field, gaining practical technical experience in data pipelines, graph technology, knowledge modeling, and platform operations while contributing to the development and maintenance of our cyber risk intelligence and early-warning systems. Key

Responsibilities

Cyber Data & Data Engineering: Support the design, execution, and monitoring of ETL pipelines using Python to ingest and integrate cybersecurity telemetry from heterogeneous data sources into our centralized repositories. Knowledge Graphs & Ontology Management: Assist in ontology definition, semantic data linking, and knowledge graph engineering using Eccenca (Corporate Memory / CMEM) , along with schema design and graph data modeling in Neo4j (Cypher) and exploration via Neo4j Bloom . Data Analytics & Reporting: Assist in the preparation of risk visibility reports, track data model health, monitor scheduled reloads in Qlik , and pull operational metrics and incident records using ServiceNow . Platform Operations & Troubleshooting: Assist in first-level diagnostics on containerized environments ( OpenShift / OCP ) across INT, VAL, and PROD, analyzing logs and troubleshooting service anomalies using standard Linux environments. Documentation & Process Support: Author and update step-by-step Knowledge Base (KB) runbooks, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and gap analysis documentation on Confluence , while maintaining codebase hygiene in Git . Stakeholder Management: Collaborate with cross-functional technical teams (Data, Infrastructure, Build, Run, and Business teams, other supporting teams) to clarify data requirements, follow up on task handovers, and track operational issues through Jira and ServiceNow . Process & Tool Improvement: Help identify missing procedural steps, automate repetitive routine health checks, and build quick scripts/enablers to increase operational efficiency across WatchTower workflows.

Qualifications

A recent graduate or postgraduate with a background in Computer Science, Data Engineering, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, or related technical fields. Basic understanding and operational awareness of core data engineering and analytics tools, including: Scripting & Pipelines: Python basics and API/ETL concepts. Graph & Knowledge Models: Foundational exposure to graph databases ( Neo4j , Cypher) and semantic concepts/ontologies ( Eccenca CMEM ). Platforms & Systems: Basic knowledge of Linux commands, container concepts ( OpenShift / OCP ), and version control ( Git ). Analytics & Workflows: Awareness of BI reporting ( Qlik ) and enterprise ticketing platforms ( Jira , ServiceNow ). Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with an ability to trace data issues, identify operational gaps, and propose structured solutions with attention to detail. High eagerness to learn, experiment, and rapidly upskill on specialized enterprise technologies. Clear written and verbal communication skills, with a collaborative team spirit to work effectively across diverse engineering groups. s such as but not limited to Open Systems Interconnection (OSI) model, TCP IP, Surface internet, deep/dark web. Duration of Internship : 6 Months Only Educational Qualification: B.Tech / Mtech in Computer Science Engineering, BCA, MCA.
