About the role

Job Summary The Sales FS is on the lookout for Senior Management Trainee with strong leadership and management skills, bringing out the best in their talented and high-performing team. Key

Responsibilities

Responsibilities: As part of the role, candidate will be aligned to the Strategic Initiatives Office for HCL Financial Services Vertical. Key

Responsibilities

include: Strategic Growth Initiatives

Research and analyze the industry trends, competitor analysis and benchmark performance to shape HCLTech Financial Services strategy

Work on Mergers and Acquisitions, involving researching the market, presenting findings and suggestions for merger and acquisition prospects for the business.

Coordinate the execution of strategic initiatives and oversee projects of high importance.

Aid in the initiation and implementation of action plans for due diligence as well as for the formulation of preferred routes of approach

Help coordinate business wide procedures and work to improve existing processes for maximum clarity, efficiency, and success. Planning and Budgeting

Drive the Annual Growth Plan and Budgeting exercise.

Act as an advisor to the entire FS senior management team during business planning

Participate in regular meetings with the Executive management team to discuss business issues, update processes, analyze issues, and challenge suggestions. Performance Management

Organize and prioritize critical issues and required information for FS president to facilitate efficient decision making

Preparing quarterly and other periodic reports for the sales leaders highlighting the performance of the unit as well as execution results/progress of business strategy

Assume and supervise business operations and all day-to-day responsibilities for projects and tasks ensuring business plan is being executed within budget and given deadlines

Understanding sales funnel and coming up with ideas/analytics to improve/create statistical models to ensure near accurate forecasts on order book creation and revenue generation for the unit Skill
