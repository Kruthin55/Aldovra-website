IT Asset Management Analyst

Johnson Controls · Pune

About the role

Software Asset Management Analyst Overview The Software Asset Management (SAM) Analyst supports the governance, compliance, optimization, and lifecycle management of the organization’s global software assets. This role follows established processes to help maintain licensing compliance, reduce financial and operational risk, improve data quality, and optimize software spend. The successful candidate will collaborate with IT, Procurement, Finance, Legal, Security, vendors, and business stakeholders to support software asset activities, audit readiness, entitlement and usage-data accuracy, and process improvements using ServiceNow SAM Pro and related IT Asset Management platforms. Key

Responsibilities

Software Asset Lifecycle Management Support software asset lifecycle activities, including request, procurement, deployment, usage tracking, optimization, renewal, and retirement. Maintain accurate software entitlement, contract, and inventory records. Assist with software renewal planning and lifecycle-governance activities. License Compliance & Risk Management Perform software license reconciliations by comparing entitlements with deployment and usage data. Analyze data to identify and escalate potential compliance risks, licensing gaps, and optimization opportunities. Support remediation activities under the direction of senior team members and appropriate stakeholders. Maintain audit-ready documentation and supporting evidence. ServiceNow SAM Pro Administration & Data Management Maintain software models, entitlements, contracts, and reconciliation data in ServiceNow SAM Pro. Support data-quality and software-normalization activities. Prepare and maintain standard dashboards, reports, and metrics. Collaborate with platform teams and contribute ideas for SAM process and automation improvements. Software Audit & Vendor Support Support internal and external software audits and publisher true-up activities. Collect, validate, and analyze licensing and deployment data. Coordinate audit requests and findings with vendors and internal stakeholders as directed. Assist with analyzing licensing terms and contractual obligations, escalating complex matters to senior team members, Legal, or Procurement. Cost Optimization & Financial Management Analyze data to identify potential license reclamation, reuse, consolidation, downgrade, and non-renewal opportunities. Support software budgeting, forecasting, and renewal-planning activities. Analyze software consumption and utilization trends. Contribute findings to cost-avoidance and spend-optimization initiatives. Reporting & Analytics Prepare and maintain standard reports and dashboards covering software compliance, optimization, renewals, and risk. Analyze software asset data, identify findings, and contribute to recommendations. Prepare findings for review and communicate approved insights to appropriate stakeholders. Process Improvement & Governance Support the development and maintenance of SAM policies, standards, and procedures. Identify process gaps and contribute improvement ideas. Follow and support established software asset management governance processes. Support enterprise software-centralization and spend-management initiatives. Cross-Functional Collaboration Collaborate with IT, Procurement, Finance, Legal, Security, and business stakeholders. Coordinate with software publishers and vendors as directed. Communicate licensing, compliance, and optimization findings to technical and nontechnical audiences, escalating complex matters when appropriate. Required Skills &

Qualifications

Software Asset Management Knowledge Understanding of software asset management and IT asset management practices. Knowledge of software licensing models including perpetual, subscription, SaaS, cloud, and enterprise agreements. Ability to review licensing terms, entitlements, contracts, and renewal conditions and escalate questions or complex matters appropriately. ServiceNow & Technical Skills Familiarity with ServiceNow SAM Pro or comparable SAM/ITAM platforms; hands-on experience preferred. Understanding of CMDB, software discovery, endpoint management, and asset management processes. Experience working with procurement, contract, and inventory data. Analytical Skills Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities. Ability to interpret complex datasets and identify trends, risks, and opportunities. Proficiency in Excel and familiarity with reporting and dashboard tools. Strong attention to detail and data accuracy. Communication & Collaboration Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Ability to communicate technical licensing concepts to non-technical stakeholders. Strong organizational, collaboration, and time-management skills. Compliance & Governance Familiarity with software compliance reviews, audits, and licensing governance practices. Ability to maintain accurate records and support audit readiness activities. Education & Experience Bachelor’s degree in a related field; equivalent experience may be considered. One to three years of relevant experience in SAM, ITAM, software licensing, procurement operations, IT operations, vendor management, compliance, audit support, or a related analytical area. Relevant internship or transferable experience may be considered. Ability to manage assigned priorities in a fast-paced environment while meeting deadlines. Preferred

Qualifications

Relevant IAITAM, ServiceNow, or ITIL certification—or interest in pursuing certification. Familiarity with licensing for one or more major software publishers. Exposure to software audits, true-up engagements, or compliance reviews. Experience working in a large or global organization. Familiarity with software financial management, optimization, and governance concepts. Role Summary This role is ideal for a detail-oriented and analytical professional who can help transform software entitlement, usage, contract, and procurement data into useful business insights. The successful candidate will support efforts to improve compliance, reduce financial and operational risk, strengthen governance, and optimize software spend across the enterprise. The Analyst will perform assigned software asset management activities using established processes and tools, escalating complex licensing, contractual, compliance, and audit matters to senior team members or appropriate stakeholders. Success requires analytical ability, attention to detail, collaboration, and the ability to manage assigned work with appropriate guidance. Software Asset Management Analyst
