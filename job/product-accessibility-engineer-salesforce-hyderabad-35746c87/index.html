About the Role We are actively seeking a highly enthusiastic and deeply empathetic Product Accessibility Engineer to become an integral part of our dynamic team. This role offers a unique opportunity to contribute significantly to our core mission: ensuring all our applications are universally accessible to every individual, irrespective of their abilities or challenges. As a Product Accessibility Engineer, you will be immersed in the complete product development lifecycle, from the initial conceptual design phases through to meticulous testing and final deployment. This hands-on experience will provide invaluable insights and practical skills within the rapidly expanding and critically important field of digital accessibility. You will collaborate closely with various teams, including product managers, designers, and engineers, to identify potential accessibility barriers, propose innovative solutions, and implement best practices that adhere to global accessibility standards (e.g., WCAG). Exceptional opportunity for students who possess a keen interest and foundational knowledge in front-end development, human factors engineering, or interaction design. You will have the chance to apply your academic learning and emerging professional skills in a truly meaningful way, directly impacting the lives of diverse users and contributing to a more inclusive digital landscape. Beyond technical skills, we are looking for individuals who demonstrate a strong sense of empathy, a proactive problem-solving approach, and a commitment to fostering an inclusive user experience. What You'll Do Design Phase: Collaborate with our design teams to review mockups and prototypes, ensuring they meet accessibility standards. You’ll check for proper color contrast, clear descriptions, and logical heading structures. Development Phase: Work with our development teams to ensure code is written with accessibility in mind, focusing on proper use of HTML, JavaScript, and CSS. Testing Phase: Assist in testing applications for accessibility. You'll use a combination of automated tools, manual checks, and testing with assistive technologies like screen readers to identify and report issues. AI Development (Bonus): If you have experience in AI, you may have the opportunity to contribute to projects involving AI-powered accessibility features. Skills We're Looking For Front-End Development: A solid understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Empathy: A genuine desire to understand and advocate for the needs of users with disabilities. Design Skills: An eye for good design and an understanding of user experience principles. Bonus Skills: Experience or interest in AI development is a plus.
