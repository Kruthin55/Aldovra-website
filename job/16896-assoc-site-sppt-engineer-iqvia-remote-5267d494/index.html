16896-Assoc Site Sppt Engineer

IQVIA · Remote, India

About the role

Job Overview Under general supervision, provide remote second-tier Information Technology (IT) support to end-users, to restore service and / or identify and correct core problem. Essential Functions

Adhere to established IT policies, procedures and standards and ensure conformance with information systems, goals and procedures.

Develop, implement, maintain, and adhere to desktop support policies, processes, and procedures to support our end users.

Review and participates in ticket quality activities and addresses areas that need improvement.

Adherence to and enforcement of all information security protocols, SOPs and Work Instructions.

Maintain a thorough working knowledge of the day-to-day operating environment, available tools, and client applications. Maintain a working knowledge of IT Operations procedures related to End User services.

Track and update the IT Assets life cycle in ITAM.

Assist on global and regional IT project teams for Real Estate and M&A.

Assist in Customer Engagement audits and ISO audits.

Participate in review and update Business Continuity Plan and IT Disaster Recovery Plan document.

Provide VIP support and Town Hall meeting support for online and offline events.

Retire and dispose unusable and non-compliant IT assets.

Escalate more complex problems to appropriate level of management and provide information on problems that are severe in nature or that are exceeding target dates.

May be required to perform duties outside working hours. This will be managed according to the applicable country legislation.

Qualifications

Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related field Req

Experience of PC software (Microsoft operating systems and applications suites, email systems, etc.) within a network environment or equivalent combination of education, training and experience Req

Working knowledge of Microsoft server, workstation and networking technologies. Intermediate

Working knowledge of industry standards with regards to system and network administration. Intermediate

Possess excellent verbal and written communication skills.
