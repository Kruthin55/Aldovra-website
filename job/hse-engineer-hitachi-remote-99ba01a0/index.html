HSE Engineer

Hitachi · Remote, India

About the role

Location: Maneja, Gujarat, India Job ID: R0149853 Date Posted: 2026-10-27 Company Name: HITACHI ENERGY INDIA LIMITED Profession (Job Category): Legal, Compliance & Audit Job Schedule: Full time Remote: No Job Description: Key Tasks Ensure compliance with health, safety, and environmental regulations and internal policies. Perform comprehensive HSE risk assessments to identify potential hazards. Define and implement effective HSE control actions to mitigate identified risks. Implement Hitachi Energy HSE standards and programs across the factory. Ensure appropriate HSE equipment and tools are available and used correctly. Monitor workplace practices and provide guidance to employees to mitigate risks. Investigate incidents and develop corrective action plans based on findings. Report all HSE incidents promptly and accurately. Monitor key HSE performance indicators (KPIs) and prepare detailed reports for management. Communicate and roll out HUB/BU/Corporate action plans, including HSE targets. Define HSE training needs and conduct or coordinate training sessions for employees. Lead actions through the HSE management system, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives. Support internal, external, and certification audits, implementing corrective action plans as necessary. Foster a culture of continuous improvement by promoting HSE initiatives and best practices. Serve as a role model to enhance awareness and compliance with HSE policies. Bring technical HSE support to the factory management team for effective operations. Conduct regular HSE audits and inspections to assess compliance with standards. Collaborate with various departments to integrate HSE considerations into daily operations. Establish and maintain emergency response plans, conducting drills for preparedness. Engage in market research to stay updated on industry trends for safety performance. Support operations with HSE supervision and technical guidance. Monitor and assess the availability and usage of HSE tools and equipment. Prepare and deliver presentations on HSE performance and improvement initiatives. Gather and analyze customer feedback to enhance HSE practices. Collaborate with external stakeholders to promote company HSE standards and practices. Define environmental improvement projects and programs to improve, among others, waste, water and energy management (depending on factory set up).

Requirements

Degree in Engineering, Environmental Science, Occupational Health and Safety, or a related field. Minimum of [3] years of experience in health, safety, and environmental roles within the industrial or energy sector. Strong understanding of health, safety, and environmental regulations, standards, and best practices. Proficiency in conducting risk assessments and developing effective control measures to mitigate hazards. Ability to analyze HSE performance data, prepare detailed reports, and communicate insights effectively to stakeholders. Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to conduct training sessions and engage employees in HSE practices effectively. Ability to drive behavioral change and promote a culture of health, safety and environmental responsibility.
