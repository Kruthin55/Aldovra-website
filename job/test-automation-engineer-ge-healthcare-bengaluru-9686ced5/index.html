Responsibilities

Responsible for planning and implementing verification and validation strategies for PET-CT systems across system and subsystem levels. Execute manual and automated testing for product verification, validation, ensuring compliance with functional and nonfunctional requirements. Design, develop, and maintain automation frameworks using Python for scalable testing. Perform automation testing using Selenium and Playwright to ensure high-quality product releases. Develop and manage Docker-based test environments for reproducible and efficient execution Perform testing and debugging in Linux-based environments to validate system reliability Analyze system failures, perform troubleshooting, and provide detailed defect and root cause analysis reports. Determine appropriate test environments, tools, and automation strategies aligned with product requirements. Collaborate closely with development, system, automation framework team, and global teams to ensure end-to-end product quality. Participate in Agile ceremonies and contribute to continuous quality improvement initiatives. Execute verification & validation activities in laboratory and system environments, contributing to product quality and integration. Support development of test infrastructure, instrumentation, and verification and validation tools. Develop strong understanding of system behavior and contribute to improving testing efficiency and product quality. Non-hybrid, Full-time onsite position with a five-day workweek.
