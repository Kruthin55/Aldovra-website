About the role

The HR Analyst role is an entry level position at Citi as the Analyst receives a foundation of knowledge and training required to contribute as a valuable resource in Citi HR. Analysts receive real-world work and add value to their teams during their time at Citi. Assignments focus on attaining core competencies in any of the following:

Responsibilities

Reward and Mobility Diversity; Employee Relations and Engagement HR Generalist and Advisor Talent and Performance Management Resourcing Staffing and Recruitment (Professional, Executive, Campus, and Temporary) Compensation Learning and Development HR Systems and Technology HR Control and Compliance Appropriately assess risk when business decisions are made, demonstrating particular consideration for the firm's reputation and safeguarding Citigroup, its clients and assets, by driving compliance with applicable laws, rules and regulations, adhering to Policy, applying sound ethical judgment regarding personal behavior, conduct and business practices, and escalating, managing and reporting control issues with transparency.
