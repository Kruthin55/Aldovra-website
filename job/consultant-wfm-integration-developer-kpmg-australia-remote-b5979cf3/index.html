Consultant– WFM Integration Developer

KPMG Australia · Remote, India

About the role

KPMG Global Services (KGS) is currently seeking an WFM Consultant for the Human Capital Advisory (HCA) practice. Job Summary: We are in search of a Consultant focused on Workforce Management to join our Human Capital Advisory team. This is a great opportunity for those looking to step up in their career within a leading global professional services organization. The individual will assist on client assignments concerning Assessments, Advisory, or Implementation of WFM solutions such as UKG Kronos in a dynamic environment with frequently changing requirements and interaction with onsite engagement teams and clients. As a partner resource implementing UKG Pro WFM Integrations, you are an extension of the UKG delivery team responsible for high-quality customer implementations of our award-winning UKG Dimensions solution. Our implementation partners are accountable for all phases of the customer’s implementation lifecycle. The partner resource must have exceptional organizational, problem-solving, and consulting skills experience with knowledge of workforce management concepts and regulations to be accepted into the program.
