Talent Acquisition Intern- Product hiring

Paytm · Delhi NCR

About the role

About Paytm Paytm is India's leading digital payments and financial services company, serving millions of consumers and merchants across the country. Our Product teams build the experiences that power payments, commerce, and financial services at scale. Hiring great product talent is central to that mission, and this internship puts you right at the center of it. About the Role We are looking for a driven and curious Talent Acquisition Intern to support end-to-end hiring for our Product function. You will work closely with the Talent Acquisition team and hiring managers to source, screen, and engage candidates. This is a hands-on role with real ownership, ideal for someone who wants to build a career in recruitment or HR. Roles and

Responsibilities

Sourcing candidates: Identify and build pipelines of product talent using LinkedIn, job portals, internal databases, referrals, and social platforms.

Job description

support: Work with recruiters and hiring managers to draft and refine job descriptions for product roles. Resume screening: Review applications, shortlist candidates against role requirements, and maintain a quality pipeline. Candidate outreach: Reach out to prospects through email, LinkedIn, and calls to pitch opportunities and gauge interest. Initial screening calls: Conduct preliminary conversations to assess experience, expectations, notice period, and cultural fit. Interview coordination: Schedule interviews across candidates, interviewers, and hiring managers, and ensure a smooth candidate experience. Candidate experience: Be a point of contact for candidates, keeping them informed at every stage of the process. ATS and data management: Keep candidate records, feedback, and hiring trackers accurate and up to date in the applicant tracking system.

Requirements

Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's or Master's degree (MBA/PGDM in HR preferred) Strong written and verbal communication skills. Comfortable with LinkedIn, Excel/Google Sheets, and basic reporting. Highly organized, with the ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines. Strong interpersonal skills and a positive, professional attitude. Able to commit to the full 3-month duration. What You Will Learn End-to-end recruitment lifecycle for product roles Advanced sourcing and talent mapping techniques Stakeholder management with senior hiring managers Data-driven hiring and recruitment analytics How a large-scale fintech organization builds its product teams.
