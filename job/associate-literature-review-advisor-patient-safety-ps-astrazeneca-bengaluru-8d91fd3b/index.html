Associate - Literature Review Advisor, Patient Safety (PS)

AstraZeneca · Bengaluru

About the role

GCL: C1/C2 Introduction to role: Are you ready to turn global scientific literature into safety insights that protect patients and guide high‑stakes decisions? As a Literature Review Advisor based in India, you will help power the decisions that keep our medicines safe and effective, working at the intersection of evidence, technology, and regulatory expectations. Reporting to the Literature Review Coordinator, you will be a core member of our Patient Safety team—partnering across functions and geographies to deliver comprehensive, high‑quality literature reviews that inform drug development, regulatory submissions, and clinical practice. You will build strong relationships with internal experts and external partners, ensuring our processes are robust, our data are accurate, and our work stands up to scrutiny. Can you see yourself elevating how literature shapes pharmacovigilance strategy and audit readiness across markets? Accountabilities: Literature Search Excellence: Ensure high‑quality, comprehensive literature searches, using AstraZeneca databases to surface the most relevant evidence that supports timely signal detection, submissions, and patient safety decisions. Data Reconciliation and Quality Assurance: Conduct reconciliations to ensure the accuracy and completeness of pharmacovigilance data transferred and received, strengthening data integrity for audits and inspections. Scientific Evidence Synthesis: Oversee the identification, collection, and analysis of scientific publications, clinical studies, and regulatory reports; deliver concise, scientifically sound summaries and reports that influence benefit‑risk assessments and labeling. Technology and Automation Awareness: Stay current on automation and technology trends; recommend and adopt improvements that keep AstraZeneca at the forefront of literature review efficiency and rigor. Cross‑Functional Partnership: Collaborate with team members in R&D, Regulatory, Medical Affairs, Clinical Operations, and commercial teams to provide literature‑based insights that shape development and regulatory strategies. Expert Collaboration and Communication: Work closely with subject matter experts to craft clear, accurate, and actionable materials that drive consistent decision‑making across countries. External Collaboration: Partner with external customers and academic institutions on literature review activities to extend reach, depth, and scientific robustness. Compliance and GVP Stewardship: Chip in to Good Pharmacovigilance Practice across safety processes and brand activities; be audit and inspection ready at all times. Vendor and Partner Oversight: Support external service providers to meet company and local regulatory pharmacovigilance requirements, ensuring aligned standards and timelines. Training and Systems Readiness: Complete required patient safety training; maintain access and proficiency with core systems including Safety Database, Regulatory systems, HR systems, Quality Management Systems, and other global or local platforms. Knowledge Management: Maintain current, in‑depth knowledge of global and local procedures and guidance, marketed product status and reference documents (e.g., Core Data Sheet), and any obligations related to product safety or safe use—enabling proactive compliance and risk management. Essential Skills/Experience: Pharmacovigilance knowledge excellence Good Pharmacovigilance Practice Knowledge of health authority’s regulations Cross functional collaborative approach Effective and lateral thinking Problem solving Vendor management Excellent attention to detail Excellent written and verbal communication skills Desirable Skills/Experience: Influencing, and Conflict Resolution skills. Medical knowledge in company Therapeutic Areas Project management Successful participation in above-market projects Audit & Inspection experience When we put unexpected teams in the same room, we fuel bold thinking with the power to inspire life-changing medicines. In-person working gives us the platform we need to connect, work at pace and challenge perceptions. That's why we work, on average, a minimum of three days per week from the office. But that doesn't mean we're not flexible. We balance the expectation of being in the office while respecting individual flexibility. Join us in our unique and ambitious world. Why AstraZeneca: Here, your work with evidence directly shapes how life‑changing medicines reach and protect patients around the world. You will join a high‑performing, digitally fluent community that brings diverse experts together to spark bold ideas, apply design thinking and data‑driven decisions, and turn them into real‑world outcomes—always with ethics, compliance, and clarity at the core. Expect intellectually stimulating challenges, modern tools, and the freedom to own ideas and move at pace, supported by colleagues who value persistence alongside ambition and who celebrate progress that advances patient safety and the business.
