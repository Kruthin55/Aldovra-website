SOC Functional Validation Engineer

Intel · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Details: Job Description: The Role and Impact: As a SoC Functional Validation Engineer, you will play an integral role in ensuring the quality and reliability of Intel's state-of-the-art System-on-Chip (SoC) solutions. In this position, you will define, develop, and execute validation plans for integrated SoCs, focusing on post-silicon validation, debugging, and optimization. Through collaboration with cross-functional teams, your contributions will directly impact the performance and functionality of Intel products, driving innovation and excellence in the industry. Business Group: The Data Center Group (DCG) is at the forefront of developing cutting-edge technologies that enable high-performance computing and data-driven solutions. DCG focuses on creating advanced products and systems that power cloud, enterprise, and AI applications. By joining our team, you will contribute to Intel's broader mission of providing world-class solutions that shape the future of technology and computing. Key

Responsibilities

- Develop and execute post-silicon validation plans to ensure compliance with design specifications and requirements. - Perform functional validation for integrated SoCs, focusing on IP integration, system-level features, and interaction between components. - Collaborate with architects, RTL designers, firmware teams, and other engineering groups to understand system-level requirements and features. - Design and implement directed and random test cases to validate hardware/software interactions and ensure functional coverage closure. - Debug SoC failures, identify root causes, and implement solutions or workarounds as required. - Utilize tools such as Python, C, or C++ for test development and automation scripting. - Analyze and modify test results, adapt test codes, and address coverage gaps to improve validation accuracy. - Prepare and track validation requirements in collaboration with firmware, hardware, and platform teams. - Develop and optimize validation methodologies, tools, and automation frameworks to streamline testing processes. - Participate in project meetings, provide regular status updates, and escalate critical issues to management and stakeholders. - Contribute to the development of new validation tools and methodologies, offering feedback and technical inputs for improvement.

Qualifications

Minimum

Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electronics, Electrical, or Computer Engineering, or related field, with 0-1 years of relevant experience in validation, test development, or debugging. Alternatively, a Master's degree in the relevant field with no prior professional experience. - Proficiency in Python, C, or C++ for test content creation and automation scripting. - Strong understanding of computer architecture, microarchitecture, and operating system concepts. - Experience with pre-silicon platforms such as emulators or Virtual Prototypes for test code development. - Demonstrated ability to debug software and hardware issues, identify root causes, and provide effective solutions. - Knowledge of system hardware/software validation techniques and methodologies. - Familiarity with data visualization tools (e.g., Power BI) for reporting metrics and tracking progress. Preferred

Qualifications

- Solid understanding of x86 architecture and its interactions with hardware and software components. - Hands-on experience with silicon debug tools, failure analysis methods, or system-level validation infrastructure. - Familiarity with AI-driven workflows or tools for test plan development and analysis. - Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with strong attention to detail. - Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a cross-functional team environment. - Enthusiasm for innovation and advancing validation processes through new methodologies and tools.
