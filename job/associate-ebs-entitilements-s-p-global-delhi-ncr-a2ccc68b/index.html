Associate - EBS Entitilements

S&P Global · Delhi NCR

About the role

About the Role: Grade Level (for internal use): 07 Department overview S&P Global EBS is specialist provider of managed and installed data services, delivering world-class data, technology and service solutions focusing on the complex and evolving Index and ETF data needs. Used in the front, middle and back office by the world’s leading Investment Banks, Asset Managers, Fund Administrators, Prime Brokers and Hedge Funds. Duties & accountabilities Candidate must be well versed with Index and ETF concepts Product Entitlement for Clients Trialing and have signed the contract for live subscription for Index & ETF data Reading through the Client Contract and making sure they have all the necessary services delivered to them as per the contract Liaising with various data provider and issuers for the license check before enabling access to clients Ensure all customers receive first class service consistent with S&P expectations including but not limited to identifying and resolving client issues, following up with clients based off SLAs, assisting with ad hoc projects, and answering customer inquiries. Collaborating with different stack holders like the sales teams, TechOps and Content Management team to raise any client specific or technical issues Ensure the accuracy and time-efficient production of financial information to respective products. Creating and maintaining the MIS for product trails Identify data quality improvement projects, and good design practices Become highly skilled in understanding and maintaining Processes, including assisting peers and leadership with creating SOPs, implementing a quality control review of operational activities. Education and experience Knowledge of capital market, corporate action, trade processing Advance Proficiency of MS Excel, working knowledge of SQL(Sequel) and AI will be added advantage Flexibility to work in different shifts, if required Have an excellent client service ethos and be able to communicate effectively with onshore team Personal competencies Personal impact Knowledge of Index and ETF and other financial instruments / Market is preferable Candidate must be a self-starter, proactive work ethic and should be able to take on multiple tasks & Attention to detail Communication Must demonstrate superior communication skills and is expected to interact professionally across business units within the company. Teamwork Being a team player is a vital aspect of the position, and it is expected that the candidate will work well individually, as well as in a global team environment.
