Tax Analyst I, Indirect Taxation

Amazon · Bengaluru

About the role

Are you passionate about tax compliance and eager to make an impact at one of the world's most innovative companies? Amazon is looking for a Tax Analyst I to join our Tax team. In this role, you will support the preparation and review of tax filings, conduct targeted tax research, and help ensure accuracy across reporting processes. You will partner with stakeholders across the business to advance tax compliance objectives while building your technical expertise in a collaborative, fast-paced environment. If you are detail-oriented, curious, and ready to grow your career in tax, we want to hear from you. Key job responsibilities - Research and analyze tax issues across multiple published sources, drawing conclusions and making recommendations that inform business decision-making and support compliance with applicable tax regulations. - Prepare and review tax returns, work papers, and supporting schedules while thoroughly checking data to ensure completeness and accuracy. - Partner with internal stakeholders to answer targeted tax questions, clearly explaining both the "what" and the "why" behind your analysis. - Identify and implement opportunities to simplify or automate tasks within your assigned work, contributing to continuous improvement projects that strengthen compliance and controllership. - Leverage available analytics tools, including generative AI where appropriate, to evaluate data, resolve questions, and deliver high-quality work product on time. A day in the life You will start your day reviewing tax data and reconciling information to ensure accuracy before deadlines. Throughout the day, you will collaborate with teammates and cross-functional partners to research open tax questions, prepare filings, and refine supporting documentation. You will also participate in team meetings where you share findings, learn from peers, and contribute to process improvement initiatives that help the team work more efficiently.

About the team

Our Tax team is a collaborative group focused on ensuring Amazon meets its tax compliance and reporting obligations accurately and on time. We are committed to building an inclusive environment where team members learn from one another and grow their careers. As the business continues to evolve, you will have the opportunity to contribute to meaningful projects, develop your technical skills, and help shape how we approach tax compliance at scale.

Basic qualifications

- Bachelor's degree - Bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, business or related field - Knowledge of Microsoft Office products and applications at an advanced level - Experience working in a large public accounting firm or multi-national corporate tax department

Preferred qualifications
