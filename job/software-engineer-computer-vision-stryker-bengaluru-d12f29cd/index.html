Software Engineer-Computer Vision

Stryker · Bengaluru

About the role

Work Flexibility: Hybrid We are seeking a motivated entry-level Computer Vision Engineer to join our AI engineering team. In this role, you will assist in developing, training, and deploying deep learning models with a specific focus on medical imaging applications. This role offers hands-on experience working alongside senior engineers and researchers to solve real-world healthcare challenges through computer vision. What You will do: Key

Responsibilities

AI Modeling & Development Assist in the training, evaluation, and optimization of deep learning models for medical imaging and computer vision tasks. Preprocess, clean, and manage 2D/3D medical imaging datasets across modalities such as MRI, CT, X-ray, and Ultrasound. Implement and experiment with computer vision architectures for tasks including segmentation, detection, classification, registration, and reconstruction. Work with architectures such as CNNs, Vision Transformers, U-Net variants, nnU-Net, and foundation/self-supervised models for imaging. Engineering & Deployment Support Write clean, modular, and well-documented Python code using deep learning frameworks (such as PyTorch or TensorFlow). Support the team in building data pipelines and assisting with model testing, validation, and deployment workflows. Assist in optimizing models for inference speed and hardware constraints across cloud and edge environments. Adhere to medical software quality standards, data privacy guidelines, and security protocols when handling clinical datasets. Gain exposure to clinical data standards and formats (such as DICOM) and how AI solutions integrate into healthcare workflows. What You need:

Qualifications

& Skills Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Biomedical Engineering, or a related quantitative field. Experience: 0–1 years (Recent Graduates / Entry-Level) Location: Bangalore Department: AIA Core Technical Skills: Strong foundation in Python, linear algebra, image processing (e.g., OpenCV, SimpleITK,MONAI), and deep learning frameworks (PyTorch/TensorFlow). Project Experience: Academic projects, internships, hackathons, or research publications demonstrating hands-on experience in computer vision or medical image analysis. Mindset: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, eagerness to learn, and the ability to collaborate effectively in a cross-functional team. Travel Percentage: None
