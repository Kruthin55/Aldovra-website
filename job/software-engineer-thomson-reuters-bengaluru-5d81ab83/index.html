Software Engineer

Thomson Reuters · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description: We are looking for a Software Development Engineer to build secure, high-performing applications. You will work with event-driven architectures, feature flags, Infrastructure as Code, and modern data storage technologies. You will also mentor junior engineers and support operations and other development teams. You will join project planning, suggest design options and improvements for review by senior developers, and help apply AI solutions responsibly to create business value. Key

Responsibilities

Develop software using disciplined processes that follow team and company standards and best practices. Implement secure coding practices and deliver solutions within security standards. Write unit tests using test-driven development (TDD) to ensure quality and reliability. Profile and tune application performance. Implement event-driven architectures and feature flags. Implement Infrastructure as Code (IaC) with moderate guidance. Work with database, big data, and data storage solutions. Perform root cause analysis on problems of moderate complexity. Support the support team in resolving customer issues, including remote access. Provide technical support to operations and other development teams as needed. Join project planning sessions to analyze requirements, offer design options, and break down work. Suggest solutions and software enhancements for review by a more senior developer. Give status updates on your own scope of work. Take an active part in agile ceremonies, meetings, and work with other technology groups. Create and maintain all required technical documentation. Mentor and guide junior engineers. Explain complex technical concepts clearly, and build agreement when needed. Commit to team decisions once they are made. Identify opportunities to improve business processes, technology processes, and AI applications. Understand product strategy and objectives, and align your work with them. Build knowledge of the business domain and its products. Show how AI solutions deliver business value. Apply ethical AI practices and responsible technology implementation. Required

Qualifications

Experience in software development with disciplined engineering practices (add years of experience here) . Working knowledge of secure coding practices. Experience with unit testing and TDD. Experience with performance profiling and tuning. Understanding of event-driven architecture and feature flag implementation. Experience with databases, big data technologies, or data storage solutions. Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code concepts. Ability to perform root cause analysis. Strong written and verbal communication, including explaining technical concepts to varied audiences. Experience working in agile teams. Ability to write clear technical documentation. Adaptability and resilience. Preferred

Qualifications

Hands-on IaC experience with tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or similar. Experience mentoring or guiding junior engineers. Experience supporting production systems or working with operations and customer support teams. Knowledge of the company's product domain. Understanding of how AI solutions create business value, and awareness of ethical AI considerations. Experience applying emerging technologies to business problems.
