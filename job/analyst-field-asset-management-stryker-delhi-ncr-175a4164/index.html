Analyst- Field Asset Management

Stryker · Delhi NCR

About the role

Work Flexibility: Hybrid A. JDE Operations · Process approved kit replenishment transactions. · Process kit deployment for new accounts. · Validate approvals, item codes, quantities, and account details. · Track transactions through closure and maintain records. B. MIS and Analytics · Prepare regular MIS reports. · Create kit recall and damage analytics. · Highlight trends, exceptions, ageing, and actions. · Reconcile JDE data with operational trackers. C. Counting Efficiency · Improve kit-counting speed and accuracy. · Track counting performance and process gaps. · Support digital or AI-based counting solutions. · Maintain testing, feedback, and improvement of records. D. Physical Verification Audits · Travel to dealer locations for PV audits. · Match physical inventory with JDE records. · Record shortages, damages, excess stock, and mismatches. · Prepare audit reports and track action closure. Key Performance Indicators · JDE transaction accuracy. · Replenishment and deployment turnaround time. · MIS and analytics accuracy and timeliness. · Kit recall and damage action closure. · Counting time and accuracy improvement. · PV audit completion and discrepancy closure. · Documentation and audit of readiness. Qualification and Experience · Bachelor's degree in engineering. · 2-4 years in inventory, supply chain, ERP and MIS operations. · JDE or SAP experience preferred. · Willingness to travel for dealer audits (Travel 20%). Travel Percentage: 20%
