Job Summary We are looking to add a skilled tax accountant within the Indirect Tax CoE (Centre of Excellence) based in Bangalore, India. This role is responsible for managing EMEA VAT compliance processes, leveraging technology platforms such as Vertex/Avalara to ensure accurate filings, data integrity, and payment workflows. The Accountant will bring deep multi-jurisdictional tax knowledge, audit compliance expertise, and a proactive approach to process improvement and automation. The indirect tax CoE will be responsible for the periodical indirect tax compliance for Global regions. This team will be responsible to extract and interpret the tax data from our systems to enable informed business decisions, perform return filing and reconciling with the books of accounts on the Tax positions. Our focus is on improving our tax processes by using technology, which increases our responsibility for monitoring the completeness and accuracy in our indirect tax systems, striving for full tax automation, and driving the vision for improved and efficient tax compliance. What you will do Prepare EU VAT returns, EC Sales List, Intrastat, other indirect tax filings across multiple jurisdictions Prepare VAT related General ledger accounts ensuring correct balance sheet positions and approve related reconciliations Monitor regulatory developments across the EU and proactively translate changes into business processes and controls Maintain and enhance internal contol framework (SOX/Internal) Identify and implement automation, data quality improvements and system enhancements using ERP, tax engines and BI tools. Track, analyse and report compliance KPIs, operational metrics and risk indicators to senior leadership. Front-end set up of Indirect tax landscape for new entities Leverage technology to streamline and automate tax calculations, ensuring compliance and accuracy. Participate in special projects and ad-hoc tasks as required by the indirect tax team. What you will have Preferred
