Software Engineer - Lab Automation

Amgen · Hyderabad

About the role

Career Category Research Job Description The Research & Automation Technologies (R&AT) group within Amgen’s Automation, Research Informatics, and AI (ARIA) organization is seeking a highly creative candidate for a Software Engineer position. This person will be responsible for the development and delivery of full-stack solutions that accelerate the discovery and development of Amgen’s life-saving therapeutics. These solutions will require integration with multiple systems across our informatic ecosystem, using a variety of existing and emerging technologies, including source code repository and automated build system management, AWS infrastructure management, device driver development, and data integration . This Software Engineer will play a key role in developing solutions that enable the evolution of our Research labs into highly automated, highly integrated next-generation facilities. Our goal is to develop transformative, automated systems that fundamentally change the way science is executed in our Research labs. To effectively communicate and collaborate with a diverse group of stakeholders, an understanding of biology, chemistry and drug discovery is essential. Position

Responsibilities

Full ownership of projects, from client scientist interviews and requirements gathering, to system and functional specification, to development, testing, training, and support. Development of code using .NET (C#, WPF) and Python (desktop applications, web applications, AWS lambda functions, etc.) to: control lab automation instruments; take input from lab information management systems and transform it into automation system-specific instructions; data extraction, transformation, and loading from lab automation instruments to lab information management systems. Solutions will regularly require integration with our Benchling lab information management system via REST APIs, Mosaic compound inventory and management system using REST APIs and/or a direct Oracle database connection, and a variety of other informatic ecosystem services. Close communication and coordination with our engineers to ensure the output (CSV, XML, JSON) of the utilities developed will work with the lab automation systems that will consume them. Continuous outreach, collaboration, and communication with scientists across multiple research organizations and geographies to identify opportunities, ideate solutions, manage expectations, and deliver fit-for-purpose lab informatic systems and integrations. Identify emerging, transformative technologies and develop innovative solutions that improve existing processes or enable novel processes. Identify and establish best practices across the full spectrum of software development tasks, including DevOps, documentation, end-user training, delivery, and support. Basic

Qualifications

Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science OR Associate's degree in Computer Science and 5 years of in-industry experience Preferred

Qualifications

Master's in Computer Science, data science, bioinformatics, or a relevant field. Experience in device driver development and hardware communication and control. Expertise with Microsoft development technologies (Visual Studio, .NET Core and .NET framework, C#, WPF), and Python. Experience with cloud-based technologies, in particular AWS. Experience with database technologies (SQL Server, Oracle, NoSQL). Experience with DevOps. Demonstrated ability to work in teams to scale a process from a manual proof-of-concept to a fully automated, high-capacity, high-throughput unattended operation. Knowledge of a variety of therapeutic modalities. Monitoring technological developments in the field and evaluation of emerging trends and best practices. Proven troubleshooting and problem-solving skills. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail. Demonstrated successful interpersonal relationships with peers and colleagues in other engineering and scientific disciplines. High level of self-motivation. .
