Data Analyst

Redwood Software · Hyderabad

About the role

We are seeking a highly motivated and analytical Data Analyst to join our growing team. In this role, you will be responsible for analyzing sales data, creating insightful reports, and providing strategic recommendations to enhance sales performance and driving data-driven insights and initiatives to optimize sales performance and efficiency. This role involves interpreting complex data sets, identifying trends, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to optimize processes. Strong analytical skills, proficiency in data visualization tools, and the ability to communicate findings effectively are essential for this position. Additionally, experience with CRM systems and a deep understanding of sales processes contribute to success in this role.

Build and maintain dashboards and reports that track core KPIs: pipeline, conversion rates, revenue, and profitability.

Dig into the data to surface trends, patterns, and anomalies, then turn them into data-backed recommendations for improvement.

Build and manage Dataform models in BigQuery that curate data into clean, trusted datasets stakeholders can use on their own.

Deliver custom reports and ad-hoc analysis tailored to stakeholder needs.

Partner with Sales, Marketing, Finance, and other teams to put data behind decisions and strategic planning.

Find and implement automation that improves data quality and streamlines processes.

Maintain and improve our data infrastructure and pipelines, working with IT to protect data quality, integrity, and accessibility.

Work with the Revenue Operations team to keep data accurate and complete.

Flag risks and issues early, based on what the data is showing.

Translate complex insights into clear, concise recommendations for both technical and non-technical audiences.

Keep current on trends and tools in sales data analytics.

YOUR EXPERIENCE

5 years of hands-on experience with data analysis tools and techniques (e.g., Salesforce, BigQuery, SQL, Python, R, Qlik/Tableau, Excel). SQL is a MUST HAVE.

Experience in a sales and revenue operations or data analyst role.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Excellent communication and presentation skills.

Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Strong attention to detail and accuracy.

Passion for data and its ability to drive business results.

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Data Science, or a related field.

If you like growth and working with happy, enthusiastic over-achievers, you'll enjoy your career with us!
