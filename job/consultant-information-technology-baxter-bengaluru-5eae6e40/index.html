About the role

This is where your work makes a difference. At Baxter, we believe every person—regardless of who they are or where they are from—deserves a chance to live a healthy life. It was our founding belief in 1931 and continues to be our guiding principle. We are redefining healthcare delivery to make a greater impact today, tomorrow, and beyond. Our Baxter colleagues are united by our Mission to Save and Sustain Lives. Together, our community is driven by a culture of courage, trust, and collaboration. Every individual is empowered to take ownership and make a meaningful impact. We strive for efficient and effective operations, and we hold each other accountable for delivering exceptional results. Here, you will find more than just a job—you will find purpose and pride. Your Role at Baxter Lead the architecture, design, development, and delivery of secure, scalable Workday integrations across Baxter’s global People Technology landscape. Partner with HR, IT, business stakeholders, and vendors to translate business needs into reliable integration solutions, establish technical standards, and provide delivery governance. What You'll Be Doing Design and deliver end-to-end Workday integrations. Lead integration architecture, development, testing, deployment, and support. Integrate Workday with HR, Payroll, Benefits, Finance, Identity Management, and third-party systems. Evaluate and implement Application Programming Interface (API) and MuleSoft-based integration solutions. Establish integration standards, reusable frameworks and best practices. Drive automation and modernization initiatives across HR technology platforms. Partner with business stakeholders to translate requirements into scalable technical solutions. Perform impact analysis, risk assessment, and release planning. Provide technical leadership, code reviews, and mentoring to team members and vendors. Ensure compliance with Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) privacy, security, audit, and governance requirements. Lead Workday release testing and maintain technical documentation. What You'll Bring Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field. 8+ years of enterprise application integration and software development experience. 5+ years of hands-on Workday integration experience in complex enterprise environments. Demonstrated success leading complex Workday integration initiatives, enhancements, releases, and global vendor delivery. Experience supporting enterprise-scale HR technology implementations and integrations. Advanced expertise with Workday Studio, EIB, Core Connectors, Cloud Connect, RaaS, SOAP/REST APIs, web services, MuleSoft, XML, JSON, SFTP, and API integration patterns. Strong understanding of Workday Core HCM, Business Processes, Security Framework, and Data Model. Working knowledge of Talent Acquisition,
