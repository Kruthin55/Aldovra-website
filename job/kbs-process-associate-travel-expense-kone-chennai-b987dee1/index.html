KBS Process Associate – Travel & Expense

KONE · Chennai

About the role

Did you know KONE moves over one billion people every day? We employ over 60,000 professionals in over 60 countries worldwide joined together by a shared vision to ‘Deliver the best people flow experience’. As a global leader, we provide elevators, escalators, automatic building doors as well as solutions for maintenance and modernization to add value to buildings throughout their lifecycle. Through more effective People Flow®, we make people’s journeys safe, convenient and reliable, in taller, smarter buildings. Job Title – KBS Process Associate – Travel & Expense Job Location – Ambattur, Chennai Are you a detail-oriented professional who enjoys working with financial processes, accurate documentation, and continuous improvement? Do you thrive in a structured environment where timely processing, data accuracy, compliance, and smart use of digital tools are critical? If you are passionate about supporting finance operations while staying curious about AI, automation, and process efficiency, we’d love to meet you. Join KONE and play a key role in supporting Travel and Expense (T&E) operations by handling reimbursement claims received from multiple branch locations. In this role, you will process claims, verify documentation, maintain reliable records, and contribute to a more efficient, data-driven, and automation-ready finance operation. What will you be doing ? As a Process Associate – Travel & Expense , you will support finance operations by performing core claim processing and documentation activities, including: Receiving domestic and international reimbursement claims from branch locations Verifying submitted bills and supporting documents for completeness Checking submission deadlines in the Expense Anywhere (EA) tool Reviewing documentation received from branch administrative teams Sending follow-up communications for missing or incorrect documents Acknowledging claims once documentation requirements are met Performing step-wise verification of claims before forwarding for further processing Handling flagged claims and escalating issues when required Connecting with employees to resolve documentation or submission errors Exporting claim tickets and preparing documentation for further processing Supporting archiving activities and maintaining accurate claim records Coordinating with internal teams to ensure proper document handling and workflow completion Maintaining process documentation in accordance with organizational procedures Ensuring compliance with defined service level agreements (SLAs) Identifying repetitive or manual activities that could benefit from automation, better controls, or process simplification Using AI-enabled or automation-supported tools responsibly to improve productivity, documentation quality, and follow-up efficiency where approved Sharing practical process insights with team leads to support continuous improvement, automation discovery, and business value realization Are you the One ?
