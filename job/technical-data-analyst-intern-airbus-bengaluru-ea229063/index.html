Technical Data Analyst Intern

Airbus · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description: Feeling ready to spread your wings ? What if your adventure started with us ?! Whatever your dream job, we might have the internship for you ! Are you a student looking for an internship that will validate your degree and launch your career ? Imagine yourself at the heart of an internationally recognized company, established on every continent, and rich in diverse backgrounds, expertise, and cultures. Focused on engineering & services and at the forefront of research and innovation, you have the opportunity to be part of this exciting adventure. Your mission : An internship opportunity titled : Technical Data Analyst Intern has just opened at Airbus India located in Bangalore. You will join the "Customer Services Fleet Efficiency and Engineering" department. Ideally, this is a 3 months internship and it should start on 11 Jan 2027(date subject to flexibility). Your main missions will consist of : Write and update manuals: Author and maintain Aircraft maintenance documentation according to quality standards and schedules Improve processes: Find innovative ways to enhance technical data workflows and tools. Assess design changes: Review engineering updates (such as service bulletins) to determine their impact on technical documents. Coordinate with teams: Work closely with customer services, design offices, airlines, and suppliers. Ensure compliance: Maintain adherence to aviation regulations (EASA/FAA) and quality standards (EN9100). Support customers: Provide efficient assistance to all users and customers regarding maintenance deliverables. Your tutor will support you identify your professional objectives and develop your skills This internship will allow you to develop the following skills: Technical Skills Knowledge of aircraft Systems Aircraft maintenance Aircraft configuration Management Soft Skills Collaboration as a team Communication Skills

Qualifications

Currently pursuing AME/BTech (Aerospace/Mech). You possess the following competences: - Technical skills : - Engineer level with basic knowledge of Aircraft - Basic knowledge of Aircraft Systems - Good Analytical Skills - Soft Skills : - Team spirit - Being Curious & Proactive - Language skills : - English : fluent Our Recruitment Process : Your application will be reviewed by a recruiter. If your profile matches the key requirements of the job, your resume will be shortlisted and proposed to the manager for further review. If your application is shortlisted, we will proceed with the next steps. Feeling ready to be part of the Airbus adventure ? Join us !
