About the role

This is where your work makes a difference. At Baxter, we believe every person—regardless of who they are or where they are from—deserves a chance to live a healthy life. It was our founding belief in 1931 and continues to be our guiding principle. We are redefining healthcare delivery to make a greater impact today, tomorrow, and beyond. Our Baxter colleagues are united by our Mission to Save and Sustain Lives. Together, our community is driven by a culture of courage, trust, and collaboration. Every individual is empowered to take ownership and make a meaningful impact. We strive for efficient and effective operations, and we hold each other accountable for delivering exceptional results. Here, you will find more than just a job—you will find purpose and pride. Your Role at Baxter At Baxter, we are committed to advancing healthcare through innovative technology solutions that improve patient outcomes. As an Apprentice , you will contribute to the design, development, testing, and support of software applications and technology solutions. You will work closely with experienced engineers to build scalable, high-quality software while gaining exposure to modern technologies, automation frameworks, and product development methodologies. This role provides an excellent opportunity for early-career engineers to apply strong computer science fundamentals, collaborate with cross-functional teams, and contribute to the development of innovative healthcare solutions. What you'll be doing Design, develop, test, and support software applications based on business requirements. Participate in automation initiatives and integration of new technologies into existing solutions. Collaborate with team members to solve technical challenges and deliver quality solutions. Take ownership of assigned tasks and deliver with minimal supervision. Contribute to code reviews, debugging, and continuous improvement activities.

What you'll bring

Bachelor’s degree in computer engineering, Computer Science, or Electronics & Communication Engineering. Strong programming skills in Java, C, or C++. Solid understanding of Object-Oriented Programming, Data Structures, Algorithms, Operating Systems, and Complexity Analysis. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
