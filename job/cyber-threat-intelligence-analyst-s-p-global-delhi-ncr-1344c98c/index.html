Cyber Threat Intelligence Analyst

About the role

About the Role: Grade Level (for internal use): 11 The Team: The SGA Cyber Defense team is at the forefront of protecting S&P Global's critical assets through advanced threat intelligence and proactive security measures. We operate as a collaborative, high-performing unit that values technical excellence, continuous learning, and innovative approaches to emerging cyber threats. Our team culture emphasizes knowledge sharing, mentorship, and the pursuit of industry-leading security practices.

Responsibilities

and Impact: Focus on proactive threat research initiatives to identify emerging threats, attack patterns, and adversary tactics targeting the financial services sector and related industries Develop and execute comprehensive collection strategies aligned to Priority Intelligence

Requirements

(PIRs), focusing on strategic and tactical intelligence that anticipates future threat landscapes Conduct in-depth analysis of threat actor groups, their infrastructure, tools, and techniques to produce actionable intelligence that drives preventive security measures Create and maintain threat profiles, campaign tracking, and adversary attribution analysis that informs strategic security planning and investment decisions Collaborate with global security teams to translate research findings into practical defensive recommendations and threat hunting opportunities Produce high-quality intelligence assessments, briefings, and reports for senior leadership, providing strategic context on the evolving threat environment and its business implications What We're Looking For: Basic Required

Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, International Relations, or equivalent work experience in cyber threat intelligence Minimum 3+ years of experience in cyber threat intelligence with demonstrated focus on proactive research and analysis Strong analytical and research skills with experience in open source intelligence (OSINT) collection and analysis methodologies Proficiency with threat intelligence frameworks such as MITRE ATT&CK, Diamond Model, or Kill Chain analysis Excellent written and verbal communication skills with ability to present complex technical findings to diverse audiences Experience with threat intelligence tools and platforms for research, analysis, and reporting Additional Preferred

Qualifications

Advanced degree in relevant field or professional certifications such as GCTI, SANS FOR578, or equivalent threat intelligence credentials Experience with malware analysis, reverse engineering, or digital forensics techniques Knowledge of geopolitical factors influencing cyber threat landscapes and state-sponsored threat actor activities Familiarity with financial services regulatory requirements and industry-specific threat vectors What’s In It For You?
