Representative I, BPS Securities & Collateral Operations

FIS · Pune

About the role

At FIS, our technology and our people are moving forward. We advance the way the world pays, banks and invests. We believe in building inclusive, diverse teams. Together, we innovate to help our colleagues, clients and communities succeed. If you’re ready to grow your career and make an impact in fintech, we have one question: Are you FIS? About the Role You will support middle- and back-office services for clients investing in commercial and term loans. You will deliver accurate transaction processing, accounting, reconciliation and reporting across borrower-side credit fund portfolios. You will work closely with clients, internal teams and external stakeholders to resolve operational issues. Success requires strong control discipline, attention to detail and timely delivery. What You Will Be Doing Process and review borrowings, prepayments, trade bookings and settlements across multiple funds, currencies and credit facilities. Perform daily cash and position reconciliations, investigate breaks and resolve discrepancies to maintain accurate books and records. Maintain loan contracts, process rollovers and update rates supporting accurate interest, fee and facility calculations. Calculate and review monthly and quarterly interest accruals, fees and related cash movements. Prepare and validate wire instructions while maintaining appropriate operational controls and supporting documentation. Support month-end, quarter-end and year-end accounting and reporting activities for assigned client portfolios. Respond to client queries and operational escalations, collaborating with internal teams, external stakeholders and onshore counterparts. Monitor transaction processing against service levels, procedures and risk controls, escalating exceptions appropriately. Maintain process documentation and identify opportunities to strengthen controls, reduce operational risk and improve efficiency. Support changing client priorities and work flexibly across shifts when required to meet client requirements. Required

Qualifications

Experience in syndicated loan servicing, leveraged or credit funds, commercial lending, term loans or comparable financial operations. Working knowledge of loan transactions, borrowings, prepayments, interest accruals, fees, settlements and cash movements. Experience performing cash and position reconciliations and investigating transaction or accounting breaks. Understanding of middle- or back-office operations, financial transaction processing, controls and operational risk. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with demonstrated accuracy when reviewing financial and transactional data. Strong client-service and communication skills, with the ability to resolve queries across internal and external stakeholders. Ability to organise competing priorities, manage time-sensitive activities and consistently meet service-level commitments. Bachelor’s degree in finance, business, accounting or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience. Preferred

Qualifications

Experience supporting borrower-side credit funds, leveraged loans, syndicated loans or structured-credit portfolios. Knowledge of collateralized loan obligations or other structured-finance products. Experience with loan accounting, contract maintenance, rate resets, rollovers, wire processing and trade settlement. Proficiency with Microsoft Excel and other tools used for financial reconciliation, data analysis and reporting. Experience working within documented operating procedures, risk frameworks and control environments. What We Offer you At FIS, you can grow your career as far as you want to take it.
