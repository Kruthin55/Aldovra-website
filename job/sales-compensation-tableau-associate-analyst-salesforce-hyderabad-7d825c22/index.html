The Experience We are looking for an Analyst to join our Global Incentive Compensation (GIC) team in Hyderabad. The role will focus on designing, developing, and maintaining Tableau dashboards and reports for Sales and internal GIC teams. The candidate will also support Sales inquiries related to variable compensation while maintaining positive relationships with sales representatives and sales management. The ideal candidate will have strong analytical and problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and an interest in leveraging AI to improve operational efficiency and drive process innovation. The candidate should be comfortable working with cross-functional and global teams, investigating issues, and providing accurate resolutions while adhering to established policies and processes. What You'll Actually Be Doing Manage the monthly commission process for Salesforce direct sales, including understanding and applying commission plans, validating commission calculations, and managing exception requests in accordance with established policies. Design, develop, and maintain Tableau dashboards and reports to provide actionable insights and self-service capabilities for Sales and internal GIC teams. Identify opportunities to improve existing processes, reduce manual effort, and provide seamless support to Sales. Explore and implement AI-enabled solutions to improve case handling, data analysis, reporting, and operational efficiency. Use AI tools such as Claude to support data analysis, prompt development, workflow automation, and problem-solving. Validate AI-generated outputs against reliable source data, identify potential hallucinations, and apply appropriate checks to ensure accuracy and reliability. Explore AI integrations, including plug-ins and Model Context Protocol (MCP) connectors, to streamline workflows and connect AI tools with approved business systems, following company security and compliance requirements. Lead or contribute to innovation projects involving Tableau dashboards, reports, AI-assisted solutions, and self-service tools for Sales. Conduct Sales enablement sessions to explain incentive compensation policies, processes, and available reporting tools. Receive, investigate, and resolve inquiries related to variable compensation through commission cases, building trusted relationships with account executives and sales management. Coordinate with cross-functional teams to ensure requests are handled accurately and within established timelines. Process commission corrections and adjustments as required. Provide recommendations to Sales management and internal compensation leadership on exception requests, escalations, and critical issues. Provide backup support across the team and contribute to knowledge sharing and continuous improvement initiatives. You're Our Person If.. 3+ years of relevant experience in Tableau development, data analytics, business intelligence, reporting, or a customer-facing operational role, preferably within a SaaS organization. Tableau Data Analyst certification is mandatory. Hands-on experience developing Tableau dashboards, reports, and data visualizations. Working knowledge of SQL and data analysis techniques. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to investigate issues, identify root causes, and recommend solutions. Practical knowledge of AI tools, preferably Claude, or similar AI platforms for improving productivity, data analysis, and workflow automation. Understanding of prompt engineering, AI output validation, and techniques to identify and reduce AI hallucinations. Basic knowledge of AI plug-ins and Model Context Protocol (MCP), including their purpose and how they can connect AI tools with approved business applications and data sources. Ability to identify opportunities to apply AI to process improvement while maintaining data accuracy, security, and compliance. Even Better If... Experience with Salesforce, Python, and automated sales compensation platforms such as SPIFF. Hands-on experience using Claude Skills, creating or refining AI prompts, or developing AI-assisted workflows for business operations. Exposure to installing and configuring AI plug-ins or MCP integrations, with an understanding of permissions and troubleshooting. Experience using AI to automate repetitive tasks, validate data, analyze trends, or improve reporting processes. Basic understanding of sales compensation, commissions, or incentive compensation processes. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to business stakeholders. Advanced MS Excel skills and the ability to work with large datasets. Strong attention to detail and a process-oriented mindset. Ability to work independently and collaboratively with global and remote teams. Ability to work effectively in a dynamic, fast-paced environment, prioritize tasks, and meet tight deadlines. A continuous improvement mindset, curiosity to learn new technologies, and enthusiasm for AI-driven innovation.
