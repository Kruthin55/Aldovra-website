IN_RBAI_Senior Process Engineer Pick and Place

Bosch Group · Bengaluru

About the role

In India, Bosch is a leading supplier of technology and services in the areas of Mobility Solutions, Industrial Technology, Consumer Goods, and Energy and Building Technology. Additionally, Bosch has in India the largest development center outside Germany, for end to end engineering and technology solutions. The Bosch Group operates in India through twelve companies with a combined strength of over 31,500 associates which includes around 15,650 research and development associates. Bosch Automotive Electronics India Pvt. Ltd. (RBAI) is a 100% subsidiary of Robert Bosch GmbH . Established in 2009, in Naganathapura - Bengaluru, RBAI is primarily focused in manufacturing electronic products for the automotive sector. RBAI has consistently added an average of one product every year resulting in a wide range of products comprising – Engine Control Units (ECU) for both 4W and 2W, Body Control Modules (BCM), ECUs for ABS, DC-DC Converters, iTraMS (Intelligent communication control unit) to name a few. RBAI also produces products for  eMobility like MCU (Motor control Unit), C-Box (Communication Box), VCUs (Vehicle Control Unit), Central gateway modules and is geared up for other futuristic products.  RBAI with its diverse portfolio, not only caters to local markets, but also exports products to all major global customers. The Naganathapura Plant (NhP1)  is seen as a benchmark in Operational Excellence in AE and Bosch India , using an integrated approach of Bosch Production System (BPS) and I4.0. With a young and agile team, RBAI’s mission statement ‘Be RBAI’ (Be Reliable Benchmark Agile Innovative) is a testament to our commitment in providing best in class products and services for its customers. Ensure manufacturing process compliance with legal & safety requirements, engineering principles, customer requirements, Bosch standards and process specifications. Pick & Place Programming & Process Optimization - Develop, validate, and optimize Pick & Place programs, NC data generation, machine setups, and placement parameters to ensure efficient SMT production. New Product Introduction (NPI) & Engineering Change Management - Support new product launches and engineering changes through layout updates, component changes, depopulation activities, and placement/polarity validation. Machine Setup, Industrialization & Technical Ownership - Manage machine installation, qualification, calibration, capability studies, feeder setup, nozzle configuration, fiducial teaching, and production release activities. Technical Troubleshooting & Problem Solving - Provide second-level support for machine and process issues, perform root cause analysis using 8D, 5 Why, and RCCA, and implement sustainable corrective actions. SMT Line Optimization & Productivity Improvement - Drive cycle time reduction, line balancing, product allocation, bottleneck elimination, CPH improvement, and overall equipment utilization enhancement. Cross-Plant Collaboration: Drive knowledge sharing and best-practice exchange with global Bosch entities as a CN representative MES Integration, Digitalization & Traceability - Ensure seamless integration of Pick & Place equipment with MES systems to enable production control, traceability, and data-driven process improvements. Knowledge on ESD standards for machines, materials, and fixtures & impact on ESD. Responsible to convert the available data into usable data and visualize the data to define the actions  B.E in Electrical & Electronics Engineering/ Mechatronics 3 to 5 years of experience in Automotive electronics manufacturing industry Technical Competencies expected Pick & Place Process & Programming Expertise Hands-on expertise in SMT Pick & Place machines, preferably ASMPT  and Panasonic Knowledge of SMT components, package characteristics, placement requirements, and feeder/nozzle selection Defect Analysis & Technical Troubleshooting NPI / ECR Implementation & Process Validation MES & Manufacturing Process Understanding Awareness on IPC Standards (IPC-A-610), ESD control (ANSI/ESD S20.20) IATF 16949 / ISO 9001 awareness (process control) Knowledge on Digitalization & Industry 4.0  Behavioral Self-learner, adapt quickly for the changes, flexible to work in shifts based on the needs Take initiative to achieve the goal Maintains Good cooperation with Cross functional team Analytical Thinking & problem-Solving Mindset Communication & Influential presentation skills
