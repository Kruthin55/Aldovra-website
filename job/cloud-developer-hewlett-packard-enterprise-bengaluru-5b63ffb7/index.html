Cloud Developer

Hewlett Packard Enterprise · Bengaluru

About the role

Cloud Developer This role has been designed as ‘’Onsite’ with an expectation that you will primarily work from an HPE office. Who We Are: Hewlett Packard Enterprise is the global edge-to-cloud company advancing the way people live and work. We help companies connect, protect, analyze, and act on their data and applications wherever they live, from edge to cloud, so they can turn insights into outcomes at the speed required to thrive in today’s complex world. Our culture thrives on finding new and better ways to accelerate what’s next. We know varied backgrounds are valued and succeed here. We have the flexibility to manage our work and personal needs. We make bold moves, together, and are a force for good. If you are looking to stretch and grow your career our culture will embrace you. Open up opportunities with HPE. Job Description: In the HPE Hybrid Cloud , we lead the innovation agenda and technology roadmap for all of HPE. This includes managing the design, development, and product portfolio of our next-generation cloud platform, Green Lake. Working with customers, we help them reimagine their information technology needs to deliver a simple, consumable solution that helps them drive their business results. Join us redefine what’s next for you. What you will do: Private Cloud Infrastructure: Own validation and troubleshooting across compute, storage, networking, virtualization, operating systems, and integrated private cloud environments. End-to-End Validation: Design and execute functional, integration, interoperability, upgrade, scale, performance, resiliency, HA, and failure-recovery testing. Technical Ownership: Independently own complex features or subsystems from test strategy and environment readiness through execution, defect closure, and release qualification. Automation Leadership: Design and enhance automation frameworks using Python, REST APIs, scripting, and CI/CD technologies to improve coverage and reduce manual effort. Automation Adoption: Identify manual workflows and convert them into scalable automation for deployment, configuration, validation, regression, health checks, and troubleshooting. AI-Enabled Engineering: Apply AI and AI agents to test planning, test generation, automation development, log analysis, failure triage, root-cause analysis, and documentation. Cloud-Native Technologies: Develop strong expertise in containers, Kubernetes, orchestration, and modern cloud-native infrastructure. Complex Problem Solving: Lead debugging of cross-layer infrastructure and platform issues and collaborate with engineering teams to drive root cause and resolution. Quality & Release Readiness: Identify technical risks early, improve test coverage, assess product quality, and provide clear recommendations for release readiness. Customer-Focused Validation: Translate customer deployment patterns and operational scenarios into realistic validation strategies. Technical Mentoring: Guide TCP1/TCP2 engineers in infrastructure technologies, troubleshooting, automation, and validation methodology. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with development, architecture, QA, automation, support, and program teams to resolve dependencies and deliver quality releases. What you need to bring: Core Technical Expertise Infrastructure Compute/server platforms Enterprise storage technologies Networking and network troubleshooting Linux and Windows Virtualization and hypervisor technologies Infrastructure lifecycle management High availability and disaster/failure recovery Performance, scale, and resiliency Automation Strong Python and scripting skills REST APIs and API-based validation Test automation framework development Git and CI/CD pipelines Infrastructure/configuration automation Automated deployment and environment management Automation architecture and reusable framework development Private Cloud & Cloud Native Enterprise private cloud architecture Virtual machines and containerized environments Kubernetes and container technologies Infrastructure orchestration Observability and monitoring Lifecycle and upgrade management AI Good understanding of Generative AI, LLMs, and AI agents Use AI tools to accelerate coding and automation development Apply AI for test-case generation and optimization AI-assisted log analysis, failure triage, and root-cause analysis Develop AI-assisted workflows or agents that reduce engineering effort and improve validation efficiency TCP3-Level Expectations A TCP3 engineer should demonstrate the ability to: Own complex technical areas independently with minimal supervision. Move beyond test execution and define effective validation strategies for assigned components or features. Understand the interaction between infrastructure layers and troubleshoot complex cross-component issues . Proactively identify product, architecture, testability, and release risks. Drive automation-first engineering , improving both automation coverage and actual usage. Apply AI practically to improve engineering productivity rather than treating AI only as an experimental technology. Influence design and engineering decisions through technical analysis and validation findings. Communicate technical risks, defects, and recommendations clearly to engineering and leadership. Mentor junior engineers and enable them to become more technically independent. Build strong collaboration across teams and take accountability for delivering assigned areas to completion.
