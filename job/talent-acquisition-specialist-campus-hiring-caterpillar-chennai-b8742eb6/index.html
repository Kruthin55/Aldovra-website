Job Summary We are seeking a Talent Acquisition Specialist (Campus Hiring) to join our Recruiting Excellence team. Role Definition Ensures all responsibilities within the hiring and onboarding processes are completed accurately, timely and in compliance with country-specific governmental regulations and professional standards and practices. Recruits qualified applicants according to workforce needs, including support for Students & Graduates hiring programs, internships, campus recruitment and early talent initiatives.

Responsibilities

Placing advertisements, attending job fairs, campus recruitment events, university engagement activities, and performing other related duties to support recruitment processes. Conducting qualitative candidate pre-screens while providing exemplary candidate experience, providing informed recommendations, and extending offers to candidates, including interns, graduates and other early talent candidates. Sourcing candidates and evaluating resumes for jobs through internal and external tools and resources (social media, job boards, ATS and CRM etc.), as well as campus hiring channels and university partnerships. Delivering the most qualified candidates, building strong candidate pipelines, and monitoring and managing reports to measure efficiency and effectiveness, including maintaining talent pipelines for internship and graduate hiring programs.
