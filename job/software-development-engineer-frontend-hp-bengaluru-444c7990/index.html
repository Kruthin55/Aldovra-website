Software Development Engineer- Frontend

HP · Bengaluru

About the role

Software Development Engineer- Frontend Description - Position Background: In the Center of Excellence Pricing and Analytics organization our mission is to deliver impact by building machine learning products to optimize pricing and marketing investments and provide guidance to HP's commercial sales organizations. Our team consists of a diverse global team of economists, statisticians, data scientists, data engineers, software engineers, DevOps, and MLOps engineers, who are pushing the innovation frontier to bring new insights to our business.

Responsibilities

Use web-based programming languages and technologies to design, develop, test, debug, and deploy enterprise-grade applications on a cloud-based system in compliance with cyber-security requirements and with strong software engineering principles Troubleshoot production bugs Be proactive to enhance current processes Document & help other team apply best practices Automate operational processes as needed About You: Curiosity, explore new topics Rigorous, synthetic, methodical Self-starter with intellectual curiosity A strong profile in software design principles Job

Requirements

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 3-8 years of software development experience, with a strong focus on frontend engineering. Strong proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript and React, with experience building responsive, accessible, and maintainable web applications. Working knowledge of Node.js and backend development, including REST APIs, microservices, authentication, and authorization. Experience integrating AI capabilities into applications, including LLM APIs and agentic workflows. Experience with automated testing, code reviews, and practices that support application quality, performance, and security. Proficiency with Git, GitHub collaboration workflows, and CI/CD pipelines. Experience working in Agile/Scrum teams and collaborating with product managers, designers, and engineers. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts clearly. Ability to work independently, troubleshoot complex issues, and take ownership of features from development through deployment. Must have: Strong proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript and React, with experience building responsive, accessible, and maintainable frontend applications. Nice to have: Backend development experience with Node.js, REST APIs, microservices, authentication, and authorization.
