Digital Technology Intern

GE Vernova · Hyderabad

About the role

Job Description Summary We are seeking a motivated Power Systems intern to contribute to cutting-edge projects across Transmission and Distribution applications, including load flow, state estimation, and contingency analysis; Energy Market applications, such as unit commitment; and Wide-Area Monitoring Systems, including oscillation source detection and inertial response. This hands-on role offers practical experience supporting a range of customer projects.

Responsibilities

Assist in developing the Power System Models for various customers

Should have good programming knowledge in C++/Java to understand the existing product code and provide enhancements

Implement and test algorithms in the product stack

Support renewable energy integration and grid interconnection studies

Automate work flows for power system analytics Qualifications/Requirements Basic

Qualifications

Currently enrolled in a Master’s program at an accredited university on Power Systems & Electrical Engineering

CGPA 7& above

Eligibility

Requirements

Students should be physically available to attend this internship at the GE office and able to dedicate their full attention to the program without any concurrent coursework or project assignments.

Must be willing to work out of an office located in Hyderabad or Bengaluru. Location will be assigned according to business needs before actual internship start date.

Legal authorization to work in India is required. Job Description Desired Characteristics:

Prior intern, co-op, or research experience in software or relevant area.

Leadership experience inside and/or outside the classroom.

A strong commitment to a career in technology and passion/aptitude for software engineering.

Strong analytical and technical skills with experience in software industry standards and development tools.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills and ability to articulate clearly.

Demonstrated creative problem solving and proactive learning. Location: Hyderabad Please note that our internships are not full-time employment positions. However, we aim to provide potential candidates with the opportunity to transition to full-time employment roles based on business needs and individual performance. We believe that internships are a great way for students to gain experience and develop their skills. That's why we encourage you to apply for our internship program. We are confident that our program will provide you with a great learning experience and the opportunity to make a meaningful impact. Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: Yes
