Controllership Analyst

Job Description Responsible for supporting Revenue Accounting and Reporting activities, including revenue calculations, reconciliations, financial analysis, cash receipts, journal entries and reporting deliverables. The role will also support analysis of financial data, preparation of management and regulatory reporting information, process improvements and other Controllership initiatives. Key

Responsibilities

Revenue Accounting & Analysis Perform revenue calculations and related billing activities accurately and within established timelines. Analyze revenue movements and investigate material variances or exceptions. Perform reconciliations between revenue calculations, source systems and General Ledger balances. Support month-end, quarter-end and year-end Revenue Accounting activities. Respond to revenue-related queries and provide appropriate supporting analysis. Financial & Regulatory Reporting Support Support preparation and validation of financial, management and regulatory reporting information. Assess rules and how they apply to our Assets under management and fee calculations Analyze and transform financial data into meaningful information for reporting and stakeholder requirements. Perform checks and reconciliations to ensure completeness and accuracy of information provided. Investigate reporting exceptions and provide supporting analysis and commentary as required. Systems Support Help with maintaining and running systems such as Power BI, Fabric. Updating worksheets templates/reports Controls, Process Improvement & Projects Perform key controls and maintain appropriate supporting documentation. Identify opportunities to improve, standardize and automate existing processes and reporting. Support Finance transformation, system enhancements, testing and other Revenue/Controllership projects. Assess processes and look at if working with other teams or changes the process can lead to efficiencies Utilize tools such as Excel, Power Query, Power BI, SQL, Business Objects, Fabric and other available technologies to improve efficiency and reporting. Required Qualification Good understanding of accounting principles, Revenue Accounting, reconciliations, and financial reporting. 1-3 Years of total experience in Finance & Accounting. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with good attention to detail. Strong Microsoft Excel skills and ability to work with large financial datasets. Ability to analyses financial information and communicate key findings clearly. Good written and verbal communication skills. Ability to manage multiple activities and deliver within established timelines. Strong ownership, teamwork, and stakeholder-management skills. Preferred Qualification Experience within Revenue Accounting, Financial Reporting, Controllership, or a similar Finance function. Experience within Asset Management or Financial Services. Knowledge of Workday Financial Management or similar financial systems. Experience with Power BI, Power Query, SQL, VBA, Business Objects, Fabric, Report or other reporting and automation tools. Exposure to regulatory or controlled Finance reporting processes. Experience supporting process improvement, automation, or Finance transformation initiatives.
