Intern, Finance Operations

LSEG · Bengaluru

About the role

Position Summary The selected candidate for this role will be responsible for a range of activities supporting Finance Services Operations activities daily as directed by senior colleagues in the team. Role

Responsibilities

Processes financial transactions on a daily basis, ensuring that accounting processes and controls are adhered Processes and files correspondence and ensure that appropriate records are accurately maintained Manages the status of accounts and balances and identifies inconsistencies Supports the process for monthly, quarterly and year-end financial reporting as required Ensures adherence to reporting and regulatory requirements Ensures processes are followed in a timely manner and in line with agreed terms Support wider the finance teams where volumes are high/resolution complex to ensure group KPI targets and management reporting deadlines are met. Ad hoc reporting and requests from Group and/or local finance as required. Preferred Skills and Experience Entry level Finance degree/professional qualification from a reputed institute. Good knowledge on accounting concepts and principles. Good Knowledge of Microsoft Office Packages. Excellent oral and written English communication Responsible attitude and sense of teamwork Detail focus, and accuracy orientation. Ability to work according to UK work hours.
