Financial Analyst-Statutory Audits

HP · Bengaluru

About the role

Financial Analyst-Statutory Audits Description - The Statutory Audit COE is responsible for leading and delivering the statutory audits for legal entities around the world with the external audit partners. This role is responsible to apply developed subject matter knowledge to drive Statutory audits of different legal entities in compliance with local regulatory requirements; work on problems of diverse complexity and scope; exercise independent judgment within generally defined policies and practices to identify and select a solution; handle most unique situations. The role contributes to the development of innovative principles and ideas for long term roadmap of the COE.

Responsibilities

· End-to-end ownership of statutory audits for all the legal entities assigned with diverse natures by working closely with external auditors. · Preparation of Financial Statements as per accounting standards · Collaboration with Controllers and other stakeholders to ensure complete analysis of the Financial statements. · Standardization and automation of audit schedules and audit processes for the scope of entities · End-to-end knowledge of accounting processes, and accounting principles (IFRS, US GAAP, and other accounting frameworks) · Proficient in understanding of Statutory Reporting process, GAAP Bridging & Financial statement preparation. · Supports policy and process improvement and identifies opportunities. · Identifies issues and proposes solutions. · Trial Balance analysis year end close of Local GAAP books. Education Required: · Advanced degree or certification in accounting & audit with 1-2 years' experience (e.g., Chartered accountant, ACCA, CPA) preferred. Knowledge and Skills: · Strong understanding of accounting principles and financial analysis. · Strong technical aptitude relating to accounting systems, business applications (SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Office tools) preferred · Strong analytical skills. · Strong communication and influencing skills. · Excellent teamwork skills · Knowledge of US GAAP & IFRS · Ability to work independently with minimal supervision.
