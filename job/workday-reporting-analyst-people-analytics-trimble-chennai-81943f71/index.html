Workday Reporting Analyst (People Analytics)

Trimble · Chennai

About the role

Unlock the Power of Data as Our Next Workday Reporting Analyst (People Analytics)! Are you ready to transform HR data into actionable insights that shape organizational strategy? Trimble is seeking an analytical problem-solver to design, optimize, and scale our Workday reporting ecosystem, helping our leaders make data-driven decisions that impact teams worldwide. What Makes This Role Great: This position offers a unique opportunity to act as the core architect of our global People Analytics reporting infrastructure, where your technical solutions directly influence company-wide HR strategy. You will move beyond standard reporting by optimizing cutting-edge features like the Manager Insights Hub, Career Hub, and Workday Prism Analytics, giving you a platform to drive innovation and elevate the digital user experience for managers and employees alike. Key Exciting

Responsibilities

Design, build, and maintain high-impact Workday reports and dashboards, including Advanced, Matrix, and Composite formats.

Create and configure complex calculated fields to translate intricate business logic into reliable, scalable data outputs.

Enhance specialized Workday tools, such as the Manager Insights Hub and Career Hub, to deliver a highly customized experience for stakeholders.

Troubleshoot and optimize report performance, resolving data gaps and validating accuracy during system updates and data transfers.

Apply strict security domains, data privacy controls, and sharing restrictions to safeguard confidential workforce information. Essential Skills & Experience

Three or more years of hands-on experience developing and maintaining complex solutions in Workday Reporting.

Deep proficiency with Workday Prism Analytics, Report Writer, business objects, data sources, and reporting architecture.

Proven capability creating advanced calculated fields and executing multi-layered data analysis.

Strong foundational knowledge of HRIS and HCM business processes.

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Statistics, Analytics, or a related quantitative field. Bonus Points For:

Experience working directly within a specialized People Analytics or HR technology environment.

Hands-on exposure to advanced BI and data visualization tools such as Domo or Tableau.

Strong Excel skills, including expert-level knowledge of pivot tables and advanced lookup techniques.
