About the role

We are seeking a highly motivated and enthusiastic Intern to join Carrier in Hyderabad for a 6-month internship program. This opportunity is ideal for students who are eager to learn, passionate about building a strong foundation in a professional work environment, and looking to gain hands-on industry experience. Job

Responsibilities

· Assist in development, testing, and deployment of the Embedded software/Firmware applications. · Support the team in troubleshooting and resolving technical issues. · Participate in code reviews and contribute to documentation. · Conduct research and provide insights on emerging technologies.

Requirements

· Pursuing Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering, Electronics and Communication, Electrical and Electronics, Mechanical, Computer Science, or a related discipline from a reputed university. · Strong communication and interpersonal skills, demonstrate a positive attitude, and be willing to take initiative when working on assigned tasks. · Good analytical and problem-solving abilities. · Strong attention to detail and commitment to delivering quality work. · Ability to manage tasks effectively and meet deadlines.
