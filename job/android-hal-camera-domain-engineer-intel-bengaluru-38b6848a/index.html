Android HAL Camera Domain Engineer

Intel · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Details: Job Description: The role will serve as the Domain Lead for Android Camera within the System Software organization, responsible for architecture, requirements, development, integration, and debug of the camera software stack for Intel client Android platforms, including Client AOSP and Google book programs. Key

Responsibilities

Contribute to Camera domain architecture and technical requirements across the Android system software stack; participate in architecture and requirements reviews and translate platform/product requirements into implementable software requirements.

Lead development, integration, bring-up, and debug of Camera solutions across Android Framework/System Services, HAL/vendor components, native libraries, Linux kernel/drivers, firmware interfaces, and platform hardware.

Develop, enable, and debug MIPI, USB Camera IP on AOSP and Googlebook platforms.

Drive end-to-end issue triage, root-cause analysis, defect resolution, and performance/stability optimization across framework, HAL, kernel, driver, firmware, and hardware boundaries.

Work with platform architecture, SoC/IP, firmware, kernel, system integration, validation, Google, and other partner teams to deliver camera features through product milestones.

Review new Android dessert, CDD/VSR, AOSP, and platform requirements for Camera; assess architectural impact and drive implementation, integration, validation, and upstreaming as applicable.

Provide technical leadership for Camera domain design reviews, code reviews, debugging strategy, and cross-domain dependencies.

Qualifications

Bachelor's with 5+ years and Master's with 3+ years relevant experiences are preferred. Strong hands-on knowledge of Android Framework, System Services, AOSP architecture, Android HAL development and Camera Linux kernel drivers.

Strong understanding of Android vendor/BSP architecture and interfaces between Framework, HAL, native/user-space components, Linux kernel, device drivers, firmware, and hardware.

Proven development and system-debug experience in one or more camera technologies, with strong expertise in USB, MIPI Camera Stack.

Strong Linux kernel/device-driver debugging skills and ability to perform end-to-end root-cause analysis across the Android software stack.

Experience with architecture/requirements analysis, software design, implementation, integration, and productization on client platforms.

Strong C/C++ development, debugging, code-review, and problem-solving skills in complex system-software environments.

Ability to lead technical discussions across multiple organizations and drive issues to closure with architecture, firmware, kernel, validation, and external partner teams.

Working knowledge of additional Android HAL domains such as Graphics, Media, and Display, including their interactions with the broader Android framework and system stack.

Experience with Android compliance/compatibility requirements and validation such as CTS/VTS and related platform qualification flows.

Experience in performance, power, stability, and system-level optimization for Android client platforms.

Prior experience working with upstream/open-source development and multi-organization Android product development is desirable.
