Your key responsibilities Technical Excellence Demonstrated working knowledge of AI tools and their application to tax advisory and compliance workflows. Your key responsibilities Your main objective is to help the Transaction tax team deliver quality deliverables using tax knowledge gained from training and assistance from more experienced tax professionals. Contribute to the profitable growth by ensuring engagements are executed in an efficient manner. Work responsibilities o Conduct the engagement in a professional manner and in accordance with expectations o build productive working relationships with team People responsibilities o Exhibit positive attitude and demonstrate willingness to learn o Contribute and leverage knowledge from individuals, databases and other sources o Provide timely and high quality services and work products Operational Excellence / Day to day responsibilities o Research and analysis of transaction tax related laws (such as domestic and international tax, corporate laws, SEBI, FEMA, Competition Act, stamp duty laws etc.) on inbound and outbound deals, corporate reorganizations, securities market aspects, etc. o Research on tax theory and positions, and apply them to specific needs o Prepare deliverables capturing implications arrived on the basis of above research, in word, PowerPoint, excel or email form as required o Perform direct tax diligence (this involves scrutinizing past tax issues, record, compliance, etc. of the target) and capture the facts and analysis in a diligence report o Use and apply the right tax practice processes and tools to be as efficient as possible in your work o Be updated on latest developments and share articles on industry trends o Maintain and keep upto date records of internal documents / working papers o Assist in preparing training presentation on recent regulatory developments.
