Integration Analyst

athenahealth · Chennai

About the role

Join us as we work to create a thriving ecosystem that delivers accessible, high-quality, and sustainable healthcare for all. Position Summary: The Implementation Analyst will be responsible for building, maintaining and supporting a variety of integrations for our ambulatory clients by using various layers of technical integration components (HL7, networking protocols, VPN, SSH, SFTP, XML). As an Implementation Analyst, your job will include managing a large queue of projects for new interface build and proactively identifying workflow deficiencies. Additionally, the Analyst will maintain relationships with interface vendors and the clients. In the performance of these duties, the Analyst will actively collaborate with staff from athenahealth’s US-based teams including AM, PM or CSC teams. You have a desire to work in healthcare and make a difference. You are proactive. You are not afraid to get technical. You enjoy being exposed to various layers of technical integration components. (HL7, networking protocols, VPN, SSH, SFTP, XML). You are detail oriented, analytical, data-driven and are a problem solver. You enjoy working within a team and enjoy collaborating cross-functionally. You communicate and listen effectively. You are familiar with Agile product development and strive to operate within a LEAN environment. Experience and knowledge related to Hospital workflows and Healthcare information systems a plus! Essential Functions (Duties and Responsibilities): [Primary Function] Build, test and deliver Integrations Communicate with Vendors, Clients and athenahealth stakeholders to convey integration status and timelines Manage a high volume of projects and tasks with competing priorities Research and incorporate technical, functional and QA requirements to integration projects Identify operational inefficiencies and process breakdowns; contributing to the design of solutions to address the same Analyzing and resolving issues related to connectivity and electronic data interchange Collaborating with cross-functional teams to develop novel solutions to operations problems Collaborate with internal stakeholders (including Customer Success Managers, Program Managers, Product, and Development) to ensure ambulatory interface engineering engagements and implementations are successful. Continually reflect on team operations and processes to identify potential areas of improvement. Qualifications/Skills required · Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Science, Statistics or other quantitative fields. · 4+ years’ overall experience in Technical Support, Operations, IT, and/or US Healthcare. · 1 years of project management experience, including management of technical projects with significant cross-functional interdependencies. · Strong communication skills and an ability to effectively engage with members of geographically disparate teams in India and the US. · Ability to effectively communicate technical issues and resolve problems · Excellent logical, analytical and problem-solving skills · Mastery of MS Office and the ability and desire to advance one’s technical skill set · Ability to work in a collaborative environment to get the desired results. · Creative and inquisitive demeanor. · Open to working during U.S. business hours (5:30 PM–2:30 AM IST) Preferred Skills · Healthcare domain knowledge and knowledge on Interfaces and Interface Engines · Knowledge of HL7 standards · Knowledge of patient safety and HIPAA · Clinical Experience · EMR specific Certification · SQL Knowledge.
