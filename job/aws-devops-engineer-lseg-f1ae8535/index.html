AWS DevOps Engineer

LSEG · BLR-Divyasree Technopolis, India

About the role

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer (Senior Associate) who is excited to build, automate, and support modern cloud platforms! In this role, you will help design, implement, and operate cloud infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines that power microservices-based systems. This position is well suited for someone with a strong DevOps foundation who is eager to grow their skills, learn from experienced engineers, and make a real impact across the software delivery lifecycle. Role

Responsibilities

& Key Accountabilities Apply business and technical requirements to help design and implement DevOps and infrastructure solutions Contribute to Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and automation solutions, with support and guidance from senior team members Build, maintain, and improve CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation Support the reliability, performance, and availability of cloud-based systems Collaborate with developers, architects, and product teams to deliver practical, high-quality solutions Follow established DevOps practices, coding standards, and security guidelines Take ownership of assigned work and deliver outcomes with growing independence Participate in Agile ceremonies, including sprint planning, stand-ups, and retrospectives Assist with troubleshooting incidents and resolving deployment or infrastructure issues Communicate technical challenges clearly and escalate when support is needed Actively develop technical and domain knowledge through hands-on work, training, and certifications Key

Responsibilities

Understand and analyse business use cases and technical requirements Build and support infrastructure for microservices-based applications Assist with automating infrastructure deployments using IaC tools Support CI/CD pipelines used by multiple development teams Help implement monitoring, logging, and alerting to improve system reliability Contribute ideas to improve existing DevOps tools, pipelines, and processes Work within Agile and Lean delivery methodologies Support solutions throughout the product lifecycle, from development to deployment and operations Ensure solutions meet security, scalability, and resilience standards Capture requirements from stakeholders and translate them into actionable technical tasks

Qualifications

& Experience Professional qualification or equivalent practical experience 5-10 years’ experience in a DevOps, platform, or infrastructure engineering role Experience working in a software delivery environment Working knowledge of cloud platforms and DevOps tooling Understanding of SDLC and CI/CD concepts Curiosity and willingness to learn new technologies and best practices Technical Skills Hands-on experience with AWS or Azure cloud platforms Experience using Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform or CloudFormation Familiarity with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, or Azure DevOps Scripting experience using Python or Bash Basic to intermediate knowledge of Kubernetes (EKS experience is a plus!) Familiarity with Unix/Linux environments Experience using Git-based version control systems Understanding of monitoring, logging, and alerting concepts Exposure to security, governance, and compliance practices in cloud environments AWS certification is desirable but not required Soft Skills Strong problem-solving and analytical skills Clear written and verbal communication skills Ability to collaborate effectively within diverse teams Openness to feedback and a desire to continuously improve Organized, proactive, and delivery-focused approach Enthusiasm for automation, cloud technologies, and DevOps practices Equal Opportunity & Accommodations We are an equal opportunity employer and are committed to creating an inclusive workplace where everyone feels valued and supported.
