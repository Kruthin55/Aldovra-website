Campus-Beginner

HCLTech · India

About the role

Job Summary We are looking for an enthusiastic and customer-focused Service Desk Analyst to provide first-level IT support to end users. The role involves handling incidents and service requests through phone, email, chat, and ticketing tools, performing basic troubleshooting, and escalating issues to appropriate technical teams when required. Key

Responsibilities

Act as the first point of contact for users experiencing IT issues. Log, categorize, prioritize, and track incidents and service requests. Provide basic troubleshooting for: Windows desktop/laptop issues Microsoft 365 applications such as Outlook, Teams, Word, and Excel Password reset and account access issues VPN and network connectivity Printers and peripheral devices Basic software installation and application-related issues Perform password resets and basic user account support using Microsoft Active Directory / Microsoft Entra ID , based on defined procedures. Follow documented troubleshooting guides, SOPs, and knowledge articles. Resolve standard Level 1 incidents within defined SLAs. Escalate unresolved or complex issues to L2/L3 support teams with appropriate troubleshooting details. Keep users informed about the status and progress of their incidents. Update ticket records with clear troubleshooting steps and resolution details. Contribute to knowledge articles for frequently occurring issues. Maintain high levels of customer service and professionalism. Technical Skills Key
