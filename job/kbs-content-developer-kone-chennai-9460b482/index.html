KBS Content Developer

KONE · Chennai

About the role

Did you know KONE moves over one billion people every day? We employ over 60,000 professionals in over 60 countries worldwide joined together by a shared vision to ‘Deliver the best people flow experience’. As a global leader, we provide elevators, escalators, automatic building doors as well as solutions for maintenance and modernization to add value to buildings throughout their lifecycle. Through more effective People Flow®, we make people’s journeys safe, convenient and reliable, in taller, smarter buildings. Job Title – KBS Content Developer Job Location – Chennai, India What will you be doing ? Learning content development and quality Maintains, updates and improves existing digital learning content to ensure it remains accurate, relevant, engaging and effective. Develops and edits learning content using tools such as Articulate Rise, Storyline, Synthesia , PowerPoint, Word, and other multimedia tools as necessary. Applies instructional design, adult learning and learner-centered design principles when developing or updating learning materials. Simplifies complex technical and business information into clear, concise and engaging learning content for global audiences. Reviews and validates learning materials to identify content gaps, inconsistencies and opportunities to improve the learner experience. Proactively communicates opportunities for improvement in the learner experience. Ensures learning content meets established quality, accessibility, usability and brand standards. Localization and global learning Coordinates and supports the translation and localization of learning content into more than 30 languages, working with internal stakeholders and translation partners. Prepares content for translation, implements translated content and performs quality checks to ensure localized learning materials function correctly. Designs and maintains content with translation, localization, reuse and future updates in mind. Considers differences in learner roles, knowledge levels, cultures and geographies when developing learning for global audiences. Technical support and troubleshooting Troubleshoots and resolves issues with digital learning content, including functionality, publishing, formatting and learner-reported issues. Investigates content defects and implements fixes and updates within agreed timelines. Works with relevant stakeholders to identify root causes and resolve more complex technical or content-related issues. Builds knowledge of emerging learning technologies and approaches to continuously improve digital learning development. Business and learner focus Works with Learning Experience Designers, subject matter experts and business stakeholders to understand business needs, learner needs and desired learning outcomes. Supports the development and implementation of business-driven learning solutions by translating learning requirements and complex subject matter into effective digital learning content. Uses audience and learner insights to determine the appropriate content structure, format and level of detail. Creates practical, engaging and reusable learning experiences that support learner performance and business objectives. Proactively identifies opportunities to improve learning content, tools and ways of working. Collaboration and delivery Builds effective relationships with local and global stakeholders, subject matter experts and L&D colleagues. Uses strong interviewing and information-seeking skills to clarify requirements, gather content and validate information. Manages assigned learning development activities and delivers to agreed scope, quality and timelines. Proactively communicates progress, risks, dependencies and questions to stakeholders. Works effectively across functions, cultures and geographies in a global organization and contributes knowledge and good practices within the L&D team. Are you the One ? Bachelor’s degree in instructional design, education, English, communications or a related field preferred. Experience in Learning & Development, instructional design, digital learning or eLearning content development. Strong English language skills, including excellent writing, editing and content simplification. Strong working knowledge of Articulate Rise and/or Storyline and experience with Articulate 360. Experience developing digital learning using Synthesia, PowerPoint, Word or similar content development tools. Experience maintaining, updating and troubleshooting digital learning content. Experience with learning content translation or localization is an advantage. Strong stakeholder management, communication and collaboration skills. Ability to understand learner and audience needs and translate complex information into effective learning content. Good understanding of instructional design, adult learning and learner-centered design principles. Strong attention to detail and commitment to learning quality and learner experience. Ability to manage multiple assignments and deliver to agreed timelines. Curious, solution-oriented and comfortable learning new technologies and ways of working in a global L&D environment.
