What you’ll do

Lead and drive troubleshooting on Customer reported issues. Isolate Product issue at hardware/software level, replicate customer environments in lab, own and document communication among customers, engineering and provide final fix. Be a customer advocate for timely resolution of the problems reported, understand the environment/network and impact on business. Gain in-depth knowledge of the infrastructure and technologies that are present in customer’s network profile; including their network topology, features, configurations, and service history, which results in faster resolution. Partners internally with other Services teams to deliver focused support by participating in Customer reviews, Network trainings and driving critical issues to resolution. Keep the other support teams trained on the designated Premium customer network, practices followed. Provides technical expertise and guidance during testing, deployment and operational phases for Premium Customers. Participate in tasks such as new product development projects, Knowledge base creation, trainings, and other documentation activities. Mentor newly hired support engineers and peers in technical areas of specialty. Products to be supported - Switching Platforms (Juniper/Aruba), WLAN Platforms (Juniper/Aruba), Software Platforms (Juniper/Aruba) P rimary

Skills required

Excellent Customer handling and communication (verbal and written) skills What you need to bring: Strong understanding of SD-WAN architectures and technologies , including overlays/underlays, application-aware routing, path selection, service chaining, segmentation, WAN optimization, and centralized orchestration platforms. Working knowledge of SD-WAN deployment and operations , including branch onboarding, tunnel establishment, policy design, high availability, traffic steering, and troubleshooting across hybrid WAN environments. Good understanding of Layer 3 networking fundamentals , including IP addressing, routing concepts, QoS, NAT, VPN technologies (IPsec/GRE), and routing protocols such as BGP, OSPF, and IS-IS. Fundamental Layer 2 knowledge , including Ethernet, VLANs (802.1Q), LACP, STP/RSTP, ARP, and BFD to support branch, campus, and WAN connectivity troubleshooting. Experience with SD-WAN security and access services , including segmentation, Zero Trust concepts, secure internet breakout, firewall integration, security policies, and SASE/SSE fundamentals. Understanding of cloud and datacenter integration with SD-WAN , including connectivity to public cloud providers, SaaS optimization, multi-cloud networking, and hybrid network architectures. Knowledge of authentication and network services , including RADIUS/TACACS+, PKI, certificates, encryption, and policy-based access control within enterprise networking environments. Prefer to have understanding/Exposure/Expertise in one or more areas : Advanced scripting knowledge such as Perl/Python/Unix/Expect scripting Requires B.E. in Electronics/Engineering or Computer Science ( or equivalent) with 10+ years’ experience in Networking Industry. Following candidates having any of following experience will be preferred and minimum experience criteria may not apply - 7+ years extensive Juniper or Aruba experience in SD WAN technologies OR minimum 7+ years’ experience in Troubleshooting Datacentre networks, Enterprise core networks. Platform Knowledge: Experience with SD-WAN management and orchestration platforms (e.g., Aruba Edgeconnect Orchestrator and Aruba Central and/or Juniper Contrail Networking, CSO) Technical Knowledge: Firewalls, IDS/IPS, DDOS protection, SASE architectures, routing policies, network monitoring Preferred Certifications – JNCIS/JNCIP/JNCIE/ACA/ACP/ACSX /CCNA/CCNP/CCIE and other equivalent. Shift Coverage Requirement Mandatory weekend and night shift coverage only as part of the team's operational support model. Flexibility to work outside standard business hours to support global customers and critical service operations.
