AI/ML Intern

LSEG · Bengaluru

About the role

AI/ML Intern Location: Bengaluru (Onsite) Duration: Semester Internship (Full-Time) Company: London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG) Role Overview At LSEG, we are building AI solutions that help teams solve complex operational and business challenges. We are looking for an AI/ML Intern who is excited to learn, experiment, and apply machine learning and generative AI in a business environment. As part of our Innovation & Intelligence team, you will work with data scientists, engineers, and business teams to develop solutions using machine learning, information retrieval, and generative AI technologies. You will contribute throughout the development lifecycle, from research and prototyping to deployment and performance improvement. We value curiosity, collaboration, and continuous learning. Whether you have gained experience through academic projects, research, hackathons, or personal work, we encourage you to apply and bring your ideas to the team. About the Team We apply data science, artificial intelligence, and automation to improve how work gets done across LSEG. Our team partners with business and technology teams to deliver practical solutions that improve efficiency, support decision-making, and enhance customer outcomes. We believe the best solutions come from diverse perspectives. Everyone on the team is encouraged to share ideas, ask questions, and contribute to solving real-world problems. Key

Responsibilities

As an AI/ML Intern, you will: Develop and improve machine learning and AI solutions that address real business challenges. Build and maintain data processing and machine learning pipelines. Evaluate emerging machine learning and generative AI approaches and apply them where appropriate. Analyse data and identify opportunities to improve model performance and business outcomes. Create prototypes to test ideas and validate approaches. Work with engineers, data scientists, and business stakeholders to deliver solutions. Participate in technical discussions, code reviews, and collaborative problem-solving sessions. Document findings and share knowledge with the wider team. Support deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement activities. Professional Skills We are looking for someone who: Enjoys solving problems and working with data. Is curious and open to learning new tools, technologies, and ways of working. Communicates ideas clearly with both technical and non-technical audiences. Works well with others and values different perspectives. Takes ownership of assigned work and follows through on commitments. Can adapt to changing priorities and learn quickly in a dynamic environment. What You Will Gain During your internship, you will have the opportunity to: Work on Real Business Challenges Contribute to AI and machine learning projects that support operational and strategic decisions across a global organisation. Learn from Experienced Practitioners Work alongside data scientists, engineers, and business professionals who will provide guidance, feedback, and mentorship throughout the internship. Build Practical Skills Gain hands-on experience with machine learning frameworks, generative AI platforms, software engineering practices, and cloud technologies. Grow Your Professional Network Collaborate with teams across LSEG and build relationships with colleagues from different disciplines and backgrounds. Explore Future Opportunities Successful interns may be considered for future opportunities within LSEG based on performance and business needs. Inclusion at LSEG At LSEG, we know that people bring different experiences, skills, and perspectives. We welcome applications from candidates of all backgrounds. If you do not meet every requirement listed in this description, we still encourage you to apply. Research shows that some candidates are less likely to apply unless they meet every qualification. We are interested in your potential, your willingness to learn, and how you can contribute. If you require adjustments or accommodations at any stage of the recruitment process, please let us know. We are committed to providing an accessible and inclusive experience for all candidates. Career Stage: Associate LSEG unlocks the power of markets to create opportunities across the global economy. Through our connected network of market infrastructure, data and analytics, we help customers access capital, manage risk and make confident decisions. Our insights bring clarity to complex markets, and our trusted services ensure resilience and stability. With deep expertise and global scale, we partner with customers to drive innovation and shape the future of finance. Few organisations operate at the scale, complexity and reach of LSEG. Here, you’ll work alongside experts, be inspired to deliver your best work and help shape what’s next for our business and the global economy. Guided by our values of Integrity, Partnership, Excellence and Change, we bring together diverse perspectives, encourage fresh thinking, and create an environment where learning never stops. Join more than 26,000 colleagues across 60 countries, supporting customers in over 170 countries.
