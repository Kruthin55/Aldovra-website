The opportunity : Associate-National-TAX-TAX - Indirect Tax - Core - Mumbai National : National comprises of sector agnostic teams working across industries for a well rounded experience. TAX - Indirect Tax - Core : Our globally coordinated tax professionals offer connected services across all tax disciplines to help the clients thrive in an era of rapid change. We combine our exceptional knowledge and experience with the people and technology platforms to equip clients in making better business decisions by bringing insights to the forefront. We help companies understand the tax policies and tax laws of governments around the world to plan and comply accordingly. Our teams leverage transformative technologies to deliver strategy and execution, from tax and regulatory obligations to operations and workforce management, to reduce risk and drive sustainable value. We have competencies in Business Tax Services, Global Compliance and Reporting, Global Law, Indirect Tax, International Tax and Transaction Services. Your key responsibilities Technical Excellence Demonstrated expertise in interpreting and applying indirect tax laws, including GST, customs, and excise regulations, to advise clients on compliance obligations. Strong working knowledge of indirect tax legal frameworks, including provisions, notifications, circulars, and judicial precedents relevant to transactional and advisory matters. Proficient in drafting and reviewing legal opinions, representations, and responses to indirect tax notices and assessments with precision and legal accuracy. Experienced in analysing complex tax positions and litigation risks to support structuring, documentation, and dispute resolution strategies. Proven ability to apply general consulting methodologies to translate technical tax law into practical, well-documented recommendations for clients. Skills and attributes To qualify for the role you must have Qualification CA / LLB Experience 0 to 6 years What we look for People with the ability to work in a collaborative manner to provide services across multiple client departments while following the commercial and legal requirements. We look for people who are agile, curious, mindful, and able to sustain positive energy, while being adaptable and creative in their approach.
