Responsibilities

Develops and maintains technical learning solutions, including instructor-led training, digital learning, hands-on labs, technical exercises, demonstrations, and supporting materials. Develops and modifies code, scripts, automation, configuration files, and other technical assets used to provision environments, demonstrate product capabilities, automate workflows, and support hands-on learning experiences. Uses programming and scripting to interact with APIs, automate deployment and configuration tasks, manipulate data, integrate systems, and create repeatable technical scenarios. Builds technical proficiency with assigned software, cloud, virtualization, infrastructure, and IT operations technologies to accurately represent product capabilities, configuration, administration, integration, troubleshooting, and operational workflows. Configures, tests, validates, and troubleshoots lab environments and technical exercises, including investigation of issues involving software, operating systems, networks, APIs, automation, and infrastructure. Uses AI-assisted development and productivity tools to support coding, scripting, automation, technical research, troubleshooting, content development, and other technical workflows. Evaluates and validates AI-generated code, technical information, and content to ensure accuracy, security, technical correctness, and suitability for the intended use. Executes established content and technical validation processes; identifies, documents, troubleshoots, and resolves issues within learning content, scripts, automation, lab environments, and exercises. Translates technical product information, documentation, engineering input, and real-world use cases into clear and effective learning experiences for technical audiences. Participates in the maintenance and modernization of learning solutions as products, platforms, APIs, features, and recommended practices evolve. Collaborates with product engineering, product management, technical subject matter experts, instructors, and internal and external development partners to ensure technical accuracy and relevance. Participates as a member of cross-functional project teams developing reliable, scalable, and high-quality technical enablement solutions. Incorporates feedback from instructors, learners, subject matter experts, and stakeholders to improve learning content and technical experiences. Contributes reusable code, automation, lab assets, technical content, and learning components that can be leveraged across multiple delivery formats, audiences, and technologies. What You Need to Bring Education and Experience Required Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent combination of education and relevant technical experience. Typically 0–2 years of relevant experience in software development, scripting, automation, cloud or infrastructure technologies, technical enablement, technical content development, or related fields. Knowledge and Skills Experience or demonstrated proficiency with at least one programming or scripting language, such as Python, PowerShell, JavaScript, Bash, or similar. Understanding of software development fundamentals, including source control, debugging, testing, APIs, data structures, and basic software development practices. Experience or understanding of scripting and automation for deployment, configuration, system administration, infrastructure management, or similar technical workflows. Familiarity with REST APIs, JSON, YAML, Git, command-line tools, and common automation techniques. Foundational understanding of cloud computing, virtualization, infrastructure, networking, operating systems, containers, or IT operations technologies. Ability to learn complex technical products and translate technical concepts into clear and structured learning experiences. Experience or understanding of configuring, administering, testing, and troubleshooting software and infrastructure technologies. Ability to diagnose and resolve issues involving software, operating systems, networks, scripts, APIs, automation, and infrastructure components. Experience using generative AI and AI-assisted development tools to improve productivity across software development, scripting, technical research, troubleshooting, automation, and content creation. Ability to effectively prompt, evaluate, refine, and validate AI-generated code, technical information, and content rather than relying on generated output without technical verification. Understanding of responsible use of AI technologies when working with proprietary information, source code, customer information, and other sensitive data. Understanding of technical documentation, hands-on labs, demonstrations, or technical learning content. Familiarity with Linux and/or Windows operating systems. Good analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills. Strong attention to technical accuracy, consistency, and quality. Good written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to communicate technical concepts clearly to different audiences. Ability to collaborate effectively with engineers, technical subject matter experts, instructors, product teams, and content development partners.
