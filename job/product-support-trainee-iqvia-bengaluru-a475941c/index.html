Product Support Trainee

IQVIA · Bengaluru

About the role

Application Testing & Quality Assurance Develop and execute test plans, test scenarios, test cases, and test data for Functional, Integration, Regression, and System Testing. Validate application, database, interface, and end-to-end business process functionality against approved requirements and acceptance criteria. Log, track, retest, and close defects with clear impact analysis, reproducible steps, and supporting evidence. Perform release validation, deployment verification, smoke testing, and post-production checks. Maintain test evidence, traceability matrices, execution results, and quality status reports. Identify opportunities for test automation and contribute to reusable automation scripts and frameworks. Application & Production Support Provide L1/L2 application support for production applications and automation solutions. Monitor applications, troubleshoot issues, perform recovery actions, and escalate incidents when required. Own incidents from acknowledgment through resolution, including impact assessment, root cause investigation, workaround implementation, status communication, and documentation. Fulfill service requests within defined SLAs and maintain accurate implementation and closure records. Coordinate with development, infrastructure, business, and resolver teams for complex issue resolution. Create and maintain knowledge base articles, runbooks, SOPs, and workaround documentation. Participate in shift handovers, operational reviews, major incident management, and on-call/weekend support. Monitor ticket aging, SLA compliance, recurring issues, and support trends, and recommend improvement initiatives. ITIL & Change Management Follow ITIL-aligned processes including Incident, Service Request, Problem, Change, Release, Event, Knowledge, and Configuration Management. Prepare change requests with implementation plans, risk assessments, impact analysis, test evidence, communication plans, and rollback procedures. Present changes for CAB review and approval, coordinate implementation, and complete post-deployment validation. Support problem management activities, root cause analysis (RCA), and preventive action tracking. Ensure compliance with organizational procedures, audit requirements, and segregation-of-duties controls. Knowledge, Skills &

Requirements

Functional & Professional Skills Strong programming and coding experience. Good understanding of application functionality, defect management, troubleshooting, and testing methodologies. Excellent verbal, written, and interpersonal communication skills. Strong documentation, presentation, analytical, and multitasking abilities. Experience in project management and operational delivery. Strong customer-focused approach and stakeholder management skills. Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment. Strong leadership, teamwork, adaptability, and problem-solving skills. Demonstrated commitment to process improvement and operational excellence. High level of curiosity and willingness to learn new technologies, tools, and processes. Willingness to work outside regular business hours based on operational requirements. Adherence to IQVIA values, processes, and procedures. ITIL & Infrastructure Knowledge Knowledge of ITIL v3 Foundation concepts. Understanding of Event, Incident, Change, Problem, and Configuration Management processes. ITIL v3 Foundation Certification preferred. ITIL Intermediate (Service Operation) certification is an added advantage. Basic understanding of IT infrastructure, Windows Servers, Operating Systems, and Networking concepts. Technical Skills Good knowledge of JavaScript . Strong SQL and database querying skills. Hands-on experience with Selenium Automation Testing . Basic knowledge of PowerShell scripting . Good proficiency in Python . Understanding of CI/CD pipelines and deployment processes. Experience with test automation frameworks and automation best practices.

Qualifications

A bachelor’s degree in computer science or equivalent (BCA/ BSc CS/ BSc IT) is required, with knowledge of software development processes preferred.
