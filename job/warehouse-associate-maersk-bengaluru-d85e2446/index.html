Warehouse Associate

Maersk · Bengaluru

About the role

Position Title: Warehouse Associate Department: Contract Logistics – Warehouse Operations Job Purpose To execute warehouse operations efficiently by performing receiving, put-away, picking, packing, inventory handling, loading/unloading, and dispatch activities while ensuring safety, quality, productivity, and customer service standards are met. Key

Responsibilities

Inbound Operations Unload incoming shipments safely and accurately. Verify received quantities against documents. Perform GRN-related activities as per process. Complete put-away within defined TAT. Immediately report damages, shortages, and discrepancies. Outbound Operations Pick orders accurately according to system instructions. Pack material as per customer requirements. Conduct order verification before dispatch. Support loading and ensure vehicle dispatch readiness. Adhere to shipment cut-off timelines. Inventory Maintain inventory accuracy. Participate in cycle counts and physical inventory audits. Report stock variances and damaged inventory. Follow FIFO/FEFO and inventory-control processes. Safety & Compliance Follow Maersk EHS and warehouse safety standards. Use PPE and material-handling equipment appropriately. Maintain 5S and housekeeping standards. Report near misses, unsafe conditions, and incidents. Comply with customer and warehouse SOPs. Quality & Productivity Meet daily productivity and accuracy targets. Minimize picking, packing, and shipment errors. Handle products properly to prevent damage. Participate in continuous-improvement initiatives. Required Skills Basic warehouse operations knowledge Basic SAP knowledge Excel knowledge Teamwork and communication skills Ability to work staggered and night shifts Education & Experience PUC / Graduate 0–2 years of warehouse/logistics experience Freshers may also be considered depending on operational requirements.
