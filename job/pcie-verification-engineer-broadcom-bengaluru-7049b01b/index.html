Job Description: PCIe Verification Engineer Seeking an experienced PCIe Verification Engineer to join Broadcom ASIC Product Division to be part of PCIe subsystems and SoC development. Candidate should meet the below requirements Bachelors/Masters in Electronics/VLSI Engineering with 14+ years of experience in verification domain Expertise in UVM based verification methodology and testbench development Domain expertise in PCIe Gen5/6/7 and PIPE protocols Knowledge of AMBA AXI/AHB/APB protocols Expertise in design spec reviews, testplan creation, testbench architectures, and test scenario development Functional coverage and code coverage management Ability to work through technology challenges with high-performance design implementations and verification.
