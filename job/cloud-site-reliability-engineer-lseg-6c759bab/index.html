Cloud Site Reliability Engineer

LSEG · BLR-Divyasree Technopolis, India

About the role

We have an immediate opening for a Cloud Site Reliability Engineer. We are looking for passionate and enthusiastic problem-solvers with an interest in capital markets data to join us as we continue providing technology innovation and support to the world’s leading global capital markets companies. Global capital markets are an ocean of fast-moving, interrelated, and complex data. We are building the next generation of capital markets data technology. Current strategic pursuits The Cloud Site Reliability Engineer will research, plan, and collaborate with our development and infrastructure teams to implement the integration of our production systems and its related tools / systems as we seek to harmonize our processes across the organization. This will include meticulous planning, documentation, testing and will necessitate a deep understanding of our interconnected systems. You will play a critical role in shaping and delivering solutions and will at times interface directly with our clients. Required
