Mechanical Engineer, Thermal & Packaging This role has been designed as ‘’Onsite’ with an expectation that you will primarily work from an HPE office. Job Description: What You Will Do: Design, analyze, develop, modify, and evaluate mechanical and thermal hardware, electronic enclosures, and production tooling. Develop and implement test plans for new and existing designs, including tolerance analysis, form-fit-function, shock and vibration, EMI, safety, reliability, acoustics, fan curves, and system power measurements. Conduct feasibility studies, design margin analysis, validation, and empirical testing. Use CFD analysis and thermal mockups to validate thermal solutions. Collaborate with management, internal teams, and external development partners on design status, project progress, and issue resolution. Lead mechanical engineering project teams to deliver reliable, cost-effective, and high-quality solutions. Represent the mechanical and thermal engineering team throughout complex product development projects. Provide technical guidance and mentoring to junior engineers. What You Need to Bring: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering. Typically 4–6 years of relevant mechanical engineering experience. Proficiency in ProEngineer (ProE/Creo) or other 3D CAD software. Strong understanding of electronics packaging and mechanical hardware design. Experience designing sheet metal and plastic components, including production tooling and manufacturing processes. Knowledge of CFD, thermal simulation, and thermal mockups for thermal validation. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and design validation skills. Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and the local language. Ability to communicate product architectures and design proposals, negotiate technical options with management, and coordinate cross-functional teams.
