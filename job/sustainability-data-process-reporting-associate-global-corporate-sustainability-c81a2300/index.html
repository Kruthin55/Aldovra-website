Sustainability Data, Process & Reporting, Associate – Global Corporate Sustainability Controllers

BlackRock · Mumbai

About the role

About this role Your Team The Global Corporate Sustainability Controllers (GCSC) team is responsible for the governance, management, and reporting of sustainability data and metrics that support internal decision-making, external disclosures, assurance activities, and evolving reporting requirements across the firm. The team partners closely with stakeholders across Finance & Strategy, Corporate Sustainability, Technology, Operations, and other business functions to enhance the quality, consistency, governance, and accessibility of sustainability-related information. As sustainability reporting requirements continue to evolve, the team is focused on strengthening controls, improving data quality, increasing reporting efficiency, and building scalable processes that support future business needs. This role sits within the Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Reporting pillar of GCSC. The pillar is responsible for the development, governance, reporting, and continuous improvement of the firm's operational GHG inventory and associated reporting processes. The team plays an important role in delivering trusted emissions data, supporting assurance activities, and helping the firm monitor progress against its operational sustainability objectives. Your Role and Impact The Sustainability Data, Process & Reporting Associate will play a key role in both the delivery and modernization of the firm's operational GHG reporting processes. This role combines participation in GHG inventory reporting, controls, and assurance activities with dedicated responsibility for identifying, designing, and implementing process improvements. The successful candidate will help improve how sustainability data is collected, managed, validated, and reported while supporting the team's efforts to enhance reporting efficiency, scalability, timeliness, and data quality. The role provides a unique opportunity to apply data analysis, process optimization, automation, and technology-enabled problem solving within a rapidly evolving sustainability reporting environment. Working closely with business and technology stakeholders, the individual will help challenge existing ways of working, identify opportunities for improvement, and support the development of more efficient and scalable reporting capabilities. Identify opportunities to improve reporting efficiency, strengthen controls, re duce manual effort, and enhance data quality through data-driven process improvements. Partner with stakeholders across sustainability, enterprise services, finance, data, and technology teams to deliver reporting improvements and support implementation of enhanced processes and tools. Evaluate emerging technologies and artificial intelligence-enabled capabilities that can enhance sustainability reporting, management information, operational decision-making, and process efficiency. Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities with the ability to translate business requirements into practical and scalable solutions. You will work at the intersection of sustainability, data, process improvement, and technology, helping build more scalable and timely reporting capabilities that support the firm's operational sustainability objectives and evolving reporting requirements.
