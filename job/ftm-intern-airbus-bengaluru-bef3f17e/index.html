Airbus · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description: Internship Job Description Duration : 6 months Job title : FTM Intern. Your main missions will consist of : A) Accountabilities ● The Jobholder is responsible for the deliverables and accountabilities as defined under main activities by the respective trade / department domain heads. B) Dimensions ● There is no team or budget responsibility linked to this position. C) Main activities ● Data collection and analysis of the delays and defects ● Reviewing the Technical publications such as AD, SB and VSBs ● Coordinating with various FTM (CAMO) divisions for smooth induction / acceptance of the major assemblies such as landing gear, Engines etc. ● Basic data review / collection from component shop reports (SFR) & other miscellaneous project ● Defining and maintaining customer aircraft configuration data in ERP / MIS ● Supporting the ARC and CMR related documents preparation and standardization of the associated processes ● Updation of structural damage/repair data in the required reporting templates ● WGR management & data sanity checks. ● Managing Engineering Statistical Report (ESR) ● Creating and managing RCP reports for all FTM contracts ● Review and preparation of the AMP / OMP ● Supporting trade engineers on day to day engineering functions 2.Technical Records Based at FHS-TSP Central site, FHS-TSP Technical Recorder Intern will associate in managing the following activities : ● Review for completeness of all maintenance and operational documentation (including maintenance checks, work orders, Technical LogBook…). ● Continuously input data into AFHS Maintenance Information System (MIS). ● Ensuring that the records are maintained and updated to the highest quality standard in both documentation and in the Maintenance Information System (MIS) with regards to Continuing Airworthiness Management.
