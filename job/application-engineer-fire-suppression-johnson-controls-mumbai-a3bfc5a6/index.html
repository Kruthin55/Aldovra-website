Application Engineer -Fire Suppression

Johnson Controls · Mumbai

About the role

About India Sales Support CoE India Sales Support CoE organization entails of 792+ engineers supporting Johnson's Controls branches across the world for 20+ years. We are a global organization with field experiences across the different markets right from Asia to North America, Europe to Latin America, and the Middle East. The collected diverse experience in one single organization enables us to be an effective partner for our JCI branches. This engineering organization offers controls, Security & Fire and Energy solution. About Fire & Security Business 5-8 years of wide JCI/Tyco and third-party system knowledge for design, detailed engineering and implementation of Fire Protection and Suppression system. Team provides the services and value-added solutions for the system and provides onsite & remote engineering support across the globe for engineering. What you do The primary responsibility for this role is to work on end-to-end presales engineering activities of Fire protection & Suppression opportunities from various verticals (e.g., Transportation, Data Center, Commercial, Industries, Fit-outs, Key Accounts, etc.). You will be handling techno-Commercial activities of the projects covering various such as Fire Sprinkler, Hydrant, water spray, foam and gas suppression Systems etc. You should own the technical consideration to win the job and should be part of HOTO process to get the D&E and OPS approval for booking. How you will do it: · Review the project inputs and project intake form shared by the Front desk/Sales. · Analyze the requirement and initiate the Kick-off call with Front Desk after getting CoE SFDC Code and time frame. · Prepare designs on given floor plans to arrive with the BOQ; in case BOQ is unavailable. · Identify potential issues/risks and proactively bring them to the customer's knowledge and help to mitigate the identified risks. · Once the deliverables are ready, perform Self QC and get the 2nd level QC done by the Proposal Lead. Eventually, submit the deliverables to the Proposal lead. · Update the SFDC regularly, and complete timesheet & assigned training on time. · Align with Organization KPIs such as Quality, Cycle Time, OTD, Win rate, Productivity, etc. What we look for · BE / Diploma (Mechanical / Electrical) · Must have 5-8 years for BE & 10 years for Diploma of relevant experience in Fire Protection & Suppression Systems domains. · Strong knowledge of Fire Protection & Suppression Systems (Fire Sprinkler, Pre-action system, Water spray, standpipe & hose system, Hydrant network, Fire water storage, Stationary pumps, Gas suppression, Extinguishers, etc.) · Must have presales and estimation experience on various verticals (Transportation, Commercial, Fit-outs, Industries, Data Centers, etc.). · Capable of performing hydraulic and flow calculation and doing product selections along with the deviation/compliance statements. · Must be a quick learner & keen on problem-solving techniques. · Proficient in written and verbal communication. · Must be a good team player. Preferred · Experienced in designing Fire Protection (fire hydrant, sprinkler, HVWS, MVWS, Foam, Water Mist, Suppression) per NFPA, BSEN, Vds, TAC, and NBC norms. · Proficient in hydraulic and flow calculations using SprinkCAD, Fire-CAD, ANSUL, and HYGOOD. · Preparing the bill of quantity (BOQ) & material track sheet as per approved / shop drawings and material technical data sheet. · Knowledge of ACAD, Sprink CAD, Fire CAD, Navis, and Excel.
