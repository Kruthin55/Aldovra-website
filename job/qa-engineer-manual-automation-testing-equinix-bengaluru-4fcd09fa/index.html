QA Engineer – Manual & Automation Testing

Equinix · Bengaluru

About the role

Who are we? Equinix is the world’s digital infrastructure company®, shortening the path to connectivity to enable the innovations that enrich our work, life and planet. A place where bold ideas are welcomed, human connection is valued, and everyone has the opportunity to shape their future. Help us challenge assumptions, uncover bias, and remove barriers—because progress starts with fresh ideas. You’ll find belonging, purpose, and a team that welcomes you—because when you feel valued, you’re empowered to do your best work. Job Summary The QA Engineer will be responsible for ensuring product quality, reliability and performance through a balanced combination of manual and automated testing. The role requires approximately 50% manual testing and 50% automation testing , with hands-on experience in designing, developing and executing test cases, alongside building and maintaining automated test scripts using TypeScript and Selenium WebDriver . You will work closely with engineering, product and QA teams to define test strategies, identify potential risks, improve test coverage and integrate automated testing into the software development lifecycle. The ideal candidate should have strong fundamentals in manual testing, practical automation experience and the ability to independently drive quality across the development lifecycle. Key

Responsibilities

1. Manual Testing (50%) Perform functional, regression, integration, exploratory and smoke testing. Analyze business and technical requirements and translate them into comprehensive test scenarios and test cases. Execute manual test cases to validate application functionality, usability and reliability. Identify, document, track and validate defects, working closely with development teams for resolution. Participate in test planning, requirement reviews and defect triage discussions. 2. Test Automation (50%) Design, develop and maintain automated test scripts using TypeScript and Selenium WebDriver. Build and enhance reusable automation components, frameworks and test suites. Identify suitable test scenarios for automation and continuously improve automation coverage. Develop and maintain regression and end-to-end automation suites. Debug automation failures and ensure test scripts remain reliable and maintainable. 3. API & Integration Testing Perform API testing using Postman or equivalent tools. Validate backend workflows, integrations, data processing and application responses. Collaborate with engineering teams to investigate and resolve integration-related issues. 4. Continuous Testing & Collaboration Integrate automated test suites into CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins or GitHub Actions. Work within Agile development teams and actively participate in sprint planning, reviews and retrospectives. Collaborate with developers, product managers and other QA engineers to ensure quality throughout the SDLC. Leverage AI-assisted tools to improve test case generation, automation development and overall testing efficiency.

Qualifications

& Experience Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or an equivalent discipline. 5+ years of hands-on professional experience in software testing, covering both manual and automation testing. Strong practical experience in manual testing, including functional, regression, integration, exploratory and smoke testing. Hands-on experience with TypeScript and Selenium WebDriver is mandatory. Experience developing and maintaining automation frameworks, reusable components and automated test scripts. Good understanding of API testing using Postman or equivalent tools. Strong understanding of SDLC, STLC, test case design, defect lifecycle and Agile methodologies. Experience with source code management tools such as Git/GitHub. Exposure to CI/CD tools such as Jenkins or GitHub Actions. Good to Have Working knowledge of JavaScript, HTML, JSON, SQL and Unix/Linux environments. Exposure to modern automation frameworks such as Playwright or Cypress. Familiarity with performance testing tools such as JMeter, Gatling or LoadRunner. Experience using AI-assisted tools for test case generation, test automation and debugging. Understanding of continuous testing practices and quality engineering principles. Desired Competencies Strong analytical, debugging and problem-solving skills. Ability to independently own testing activities across manual and automation tracks. Strong attention to detail and a quality-first mindset. Effective communication and collaboration skills. Ability to balance functional testing requirements with automation objectives.
