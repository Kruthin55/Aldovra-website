Finance Executive

JLL · Bengaluru

About the role

JLL empowers you to shape a brighter way . Our people at JLL are shaping the future of real estate for a better world by combining world class services, advisory and technology for our clients. We are committed to hiring the best, most talented people and empowering them to thrive, grow meaningful careers and to find a place where they belong. Whether you’ve got deep experience in commercial real estate, skilled trades or technology, or you’re looking to apply your relevant experience to a new industry, join our team as we help shape a brighter way forward. Job Description What this job involves: This role serves as a key member of JLL's Regional Finance Shared Services team, specializing in client accounts payable processes and financial operations support. You will be responsible for ensuring accurate and timely invoice processing while contributing to process improvement initiatives that enhance operational efficiency. The position directly contributes to JLL's business objectives by maintaining strong vendor relationships, supporting cash flow management, and implementing best practices in financial operations. This is an excellent opportunity to develop expertise in corporate finance while working with diverse clients and contributing to continuous improvement projects in a collaborative, technology-driven environment. What your day-to-day will look like: Meticulously verify, record, and authorize invoices for payment, ensuring accuracy and adherence to established payment schedules to maintain vendor relationships Communicate clear timelines, ownership, objectives, and guidelines to stakeholders while addressing payment disputes promptly and professionally Establish consistent communication protocols, personalizing messaging and implementing tracking systems to ensure smooth invoicing processes Participate in process improvement reviews and contribute to ad hoc projects aimed at optimizing accounts payable operations and financial workflows Utilize Microsoft Office applications and finance system software to maintain accurate records and generate reports for management review Work autonomously on assigned projects while demonstrating strong attention to detail and excellent problem-solving capabilities under limited supervision Support cash flow management initiatives and ensure compliance with JLL's financial policies and client-specific requirements. Required

Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Commerce (B.Com) or equivalent degree in Finance, Accounting, or related field 0-2 years of experience in accounting, finance, or accounts payable processes Strong knowledge of accounts payable processes and procedures with practical application experience Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and other Microsoft Office applications for financial analysis and reporting Excellent communication skills with ability to interact professionally with vendors, clients, and internal stakeholders Strong attention to detail and accuracy in financial data processing and verification Ability to work independently under limited supervision while meeting strict deadlines and quality standards. Preferred

Qualifications

Post-graduation degree in Finance, Accounting, or related business field Working knowledge of finance system applications such as SAP, Oracle, or similar enterprise software platforms Experience with invoice processing software and automated accounts payable systems Understanding of cash flow management principles and vendor relationship management Basic knowledge of financial reporting and analysis techniques Experience with process improvement methodologies and project management Familiarity with multi-client finance operations and shared services environments.
