Internship- Structures

Airbus · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description: Feeling ready to spread your wings ? What if your adventure started with us ?! Whatever your dream job, we might have the internship for you ! Are you a student looking for an internship that will validate your degree and launch your career ? Imagine yourself at the heart of an internationally recognized company, established on every continent, and rich in diverse backgrounds, expertise, and cultures. Focused on digital and at the forefront of research and innovation, you have the opportunity to be part of this exciting adventure. Your mission : An internship opportunity titled Intern- Structures has just opened at Airbus India located in Bengaluru. You will join the Structures department. Ideally, this is a 6 months internship and it should start in Jan 2027 (date subject to flexibility). Internship Title: Continuum Damage Mechanics (CDM) based fatigue initiation prediction model integrated to Abaqus Brief: - Continuum Damage Mechanics (CDM) is a promising analysis methodology for better fatigue life prediction & currently has less maturity within Airbus - Objective to further mature the fatigue initiation prediction models developed previously for uniaxial and multiaxial models including aspects of plasticity and variable loading treatment. Job scope (core activities): Improve the finite element implementation of the previously developed models with required user inputs to integrate with Abaqus Adapt CDM models to higher complexity such as multiaxial loading, non-localization phenomenon, complex spectra, plasticity and analyze the results Validate the developed methods with PoC specimens and correlate with physical test results Summary of findings and preparation of report Pre-requisites: Sound knowledge of solid mechanics, finite element methods, plasticity and hardening principles Basic knowledge on structural fatigue Ability to derive broad conclusions from research articles Hands on experience with Abaqus along with experience with creating user subroutines preferably UMAT, USDFLD, UEXTERNALDB Good programming skills Good analytical and reasoning skills Tools: Abaqus, Fortran, Python Qualification: Graduate/Post graduate (Mechanical / Civil / Aerospace / Material Science / Applied Mechanics Engineering) Soft skills: Ability to work independently Good communication and interpersonal skills Flexibility & adaptability This job requires an awareness of any potential compliance risks and a commitment to act with integrity, as the foundation for the Company’s success, reputation and sustainable growth.
