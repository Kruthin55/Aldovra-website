Social Media Intern

Swiggy · Mumbai

About the role

About Swiggy: Founded in 2014, Swiggy is India’s leading tech-driven on-demand delivery platform with a vision to elevate the quality of life for the urban consumer by offering unparalleled convenience. The platform is engineered to connect millions of consumers with hundreds of thousands of restaurants and stores across 500+ cities. Our phenomenal growth has come on the back of great technology, incredible innovation and sound decision-making.   About Dineout Mag We are an F&B media arm of Dineout where we create original content that highlights and recommends the best experiences in the city’s food and beverage landscape. From restaurant reviews and chef interviews to collaborations, events, and more.   Dineout Mag is where food lovers come to discover trusted restaurants, chefs, unique events and exciting culinary experiences in cities across the country. Our aim is simple: help people discover good food, centre the stories of the people behind the food, and create unique experiences to build food culture.   At its heart, Dineout Mag is rooted in Indian food culture yet speaks to a global audience. We take pride in being a source of discovery, backing places for not only their food, but also their backstories, the context and the community they are a part of.    To get a better grasp of the work we do, take a look at our city pages:   → @mumbaifoodie → @letsfigrr → @dineout.pune → @dineout.bengaluru → @dineout.hyderabad → @dineout.goa → @dineout.delhi → @dineout.chennai → @dineout.kolkata → @dineout.jaipur → @dineout.ahmedabad → @dineout.mag What will you work on? Social Media Management: Assist in managing Dineout Mag’s social media pages and maintaining a consistent posting schedule  Content Calendar & Scheduling: Help plan, organise, schedule, and publish content across platforms. Content Strategy & Execution: Support the execution of social media strategies and content series to improve reach and engagement. Page Management: Keep pages updated by managing stories, bios, highlights, comments, DMs, and broadcast channels. Audience Engagement: Create interactive stories, engage with followers, and help build an active online community. Performance Tracking: Monitor content performance, track key metrics, and share insights to help improve engagement and reach. Trendspotting: Stay updated on social media trends, formats, and relevant F&B conversations to identify content opportunities. LinkedIn Support: Assist in creating and publishing content that strengthens Dineout Mag’s professional presence. Creator Collaborations: Identify potential creators and influencers for collaborations and audience growth. Who are we looking for? 3-6 months of experience in social media management or a related role; relevant internship experience is welcome. Hands-on familiarity with Instagram and other social media platforms. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with an eye for detail. A good understanding of social media trends, content formats, and audience engagement. Basic knowledge of social media analytics and performance metrics. An interest in food, restaurants, and the F&B landscape is a plus. A proactive, organised, and creative individual who can work well in a fast-paced environment. We are an equal opportunity employer.
