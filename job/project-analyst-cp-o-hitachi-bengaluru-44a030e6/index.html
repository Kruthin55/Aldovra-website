Project Analyst - CP&O

Hitachi · Bengaluru

About the role

Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India Job ID: R0151838 Date Posted: 2026-11-17 Company Name: HITACHI ENERGY TECHNOLOGY SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED Profession (Job Category): Finance Job Schedule: Full time Remote: No Job Description: Key

Responsibilities

Project Financial Management - Support project setup and financial administration activities. - Monitor project costs, commitments, revenues, margins, and cash flow. - Validate project financial transactions and month-end activities. Forecasting and Planning - Prepare project forecasts, EAC and ETC analyses. - Perform variance analysis and recommend corrective actions. Financial Analysis and Reporting - Develop financial reports, dashboards, and management presentations. - Monitor KPIs including Revenue, Gross Margin, Cash Flow, Working Capital, Cost Performance, and Forecast Accuracy. Stakeholder Collaboration - Partner with Project Managers and cross-functional teams including Procurement, Accounting, Tax, Treasury, Risk, and Operations. Project Closure - Support project closeout, reconciliations, documentation, and lessons learned reviews.
