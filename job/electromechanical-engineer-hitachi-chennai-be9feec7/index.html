Electromechanical Engineer

Hitachi · Chennai

About the role

Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India Job ID: R0144227 Date Posted: 2026-09-16 Company Name: HITACHI ENERGY TECHNOLOGY SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED Profession (Job Category): Engineering & Science Job Schedule: Full time Remote: No Job Description: The opportunity: We the Power Conversion (PC) Solutions team at Indian Operation Center, Hitachi Energy Technology Services Pvt Ltd, Chennai help our clients with our state-of-the-art power converter solutions in a varied range of applications. With our competence in Power Conversion technology, we have a leading position in the development of solutions for Utility STATCOM's, Rail Power Supply Converters as well as static frequency converters for industrial applications. We have also built the world’s largest drive for a hydro pump station. To strengthen this motivated team, we are looking for an experienced and dedicated Control design Engineer with high interest in power electronics and control technology. As the system being continuously evolving, becoming more complex and customized, great deal of test & verification are required. Thus, the job description would be as given below: How you’ll make an impact STATCOM/ Static Frequency Convertor/ Static Var Compensator/ Series Compensation Plant design & modelling in 3D Environment Preparation of FACTS plant layouts (Yard Arrangement – Layout & Plan, Electrical HV / EHV Clearance Check) Preparation of civil conceptual design & interface drawings (Foundation plans, trench, and other civil requirements, foundation load calculations) Preparation of control building layout (electrical cubicles, thyristors, pump skid arrangement) Preparation of cable trench & conduit layout Preparation of grounding & lightning protection calculations and layouts Preparation of Installation drawings / Erection Key Diagrams Review of equipment drawings (Reactor, Capacitor, SA, LA, CT, PT, CB, Isolator, WB / Insulator, Cubicle, Cooling Skid etc.,) Steel design calculations (Member size & foundation bolt selection) Preparation of detailed steel drawings (for manufacturing / procurement & installation) Heat & ventilation system data preparation (Input to HVAC design) Review of Tender / Bidding Specification Technical data sheets verification of primary equipment Adherence to quality system and contribution to its improvement Vendor evaluation by visiting their facilities Responsible for obtaining customer approvals for the designs and drawings As-built drawings preparation etc. Co – ordination with different stakeholders during project execution. Technical co-ordination with sub suppliers and multiple stakeholders in different locations & discussion with other departments / stakeholders to prepare the Division of works. Technical support at site during commissioning phase of the project Should be willing to travel to sites in India and Abroad at short notice for support work.
