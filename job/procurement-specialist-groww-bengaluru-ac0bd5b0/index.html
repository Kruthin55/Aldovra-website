About the Role

We seek an experienced, results-driven purchasing manager to join our team at Groww. As a purchase manager, you will be responsible for handling the procurement process, sourcing suppliers and managing vendor relationships to ensure a seamless supply chain and optimise costs. You will also collaborate with cross-functional teams, make strategic decisions and implement best practices within our procurement function.

This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to our company's success and be part of a dynamic team in a competitive market.

Key

Responsibilities

Develop and implement procurement strategies and policies for cost optimization and supply chain efficiency

Identify, evaluate, and select suppliers based on quality, cost-effectiveness, and ethical standards

Negotiate contracts and terms with suppliers to ensure favorable pricing and mutually beneficial agreements

Manage supplier relationships, including performance evaluation, issue resolution, and long-term partnership building

Conduct market research and analysis to stay updated on industry trends and pricing fluctuations

Monitor inventory levels, analyze demand patterns, and implement inventory management strategies

Collaborate with internal stakeholders to understand requirements and align procurement with organizational goals

Implement procurement systems and tools to streamline processes and enhance efficiency

Analyze and present procurement reports, performance metrics, and cost-saving opportunities to management

Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and promote ethical and sustainable sourcing practices

Develop procurement metrics to track performance and identify areas for improvement

Manage Admin, HR, IT, and Marketing spends

Preferred skills and qualifications

Bachelor's degree in supply chain management, business administration, engineering or a related field.

2+ years in procurement/purchasing in a fast-paced, competitive environment in the Indian market.

In-depth knowledge of procurement principles, strategies and best practices.

Strong skills in budgeting, cost optimisation and negotiation techniques.

Knowledge of vendor management and inventory management systems.

An analytical mind with excellent communication and interpersonal skills.

Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams.

Proficiency in using procurement software and systems.

Knowledge of local and national laws and regulations related to procurement.

Willingness to work in a fast-paced and competitive environment.

Knowledge of Admin , HR, IT , Marketing Spends.
