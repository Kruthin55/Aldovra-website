Project Management Analyst

athenahealth · Chennai

About the role

Join us as we work to create a thriving ecosystem that delivers accessible, high-quality, and sustainable healthcare for all. Position Summary: The Project Management Analyst is responsible for supporting workflows and projects aimed at initiating, scoping and authorizing documents from clients. The Project Management Analyst will maintain relationships with vendors, clients and other athena teams to validate, triage and assign tasks to internal teams based on the clients’ requirements. This position reports directly to the Associate Manager, Project Management in Chennai. Essential Functions (Duties and Responsibilities): · Analyzing and working on related work queues, including requests from clients for integrations with medical facilities and healthcare vendors, and moving the requests to the appropriate downstream teams · Communicating with clients, labs, hospitals, IT vendors, pharmacies and athena stakeholders to convey project status and timelines · Managing a high volume of projects and maintaining priorities · Developing a comprehensive understanding of athenahealth’s healthcare transactions and how relationships between teams and their workflows influence complex problem solving · Scoping, analyzing and validating data for project requirements · Triaging Tasks / Projects to internal teams for further processing · Working on additional ad hoc projects as business needs arise Education & Experience Required: · Graduate in engineering, science, or commerce stream · Freshers or candidates with 1-2 years of project coordination experience, including stakeholder communication, requirements gathering, request tracking, and cross-functional team coordination. · Willingness and ability to work flexible hours to ensure substantial overlap with US-based teams (Evening/Night) shift timings Knowledge & Skills: · Good communication skills and an ability to effectively engage with peers, stakeholders & clients across geographies · Excellent customer service skills and experience with MS Office Suite · Shows Job to Be Done (JTBD) curiosity and proven aptitude towards identifying and resolving problems · Comfortable in reaching out to stakeholders from multiple teams · Follows appropriate communication channels to provide regular updates to the team · Develops appropriate awareness of team goals. Plans daily work; Executes work according to SOPs · Participates in department and company culture · Develop new relationships with colleagues to support the diversity, collective spirit, and contributions of our people · Careful, detail-oriented, and efficient in work · Comfortable identifying concerns with daily tasks and actively looking for ways to streamline processes · Proven ability to meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment · Flexibility to adapt to change in a rapidly expanding and fast paced company · Enthusiasm for working in a collaborative environment About athenahealth Our vision: In an industry that becomes more complex by the day, we stand for simplicity. We offer IT solutions and expert services that eliminate the daily hurdles preventing healthcare providers from focusing entirely on their patients — powered by our vision to create a thriving ecosystem that delivers accessible, high-quality, and sustainable healthcare for all. Our company culture: Our talented employees — or athenistas, as we call ourselves — spark the innovation and passion needed to accomplish our vision. We are a diverse group of dreamers and do-ers with unique knowledge, expertise, backgrounds, and perspectives. We unite as mission-driven problem-solvers with a deep desire to achieve our vision and make our time here count. Our award-winning culture is built around shared values of inclusiveness, accountability, and support. Our DEI commitment: Our vision of accessible, high-quality, and sustainable healthcare for all requires addressing the inequities that stand in the way. That's one reason we prioritize diversity, equity, and inclusion in every aspect of our business, from attracting and sustaining a diverse workforce to maintaining an inclusive environment for athenistas, our partners, customers and the communities where we work and serve. What we can do for you: Along with health and financial benefits, athenistas enjoy perks specific to each location, including commuter support, employee assistance programs, tuition assistance, employee resource groups, and collaborative workspaces — some offices even welcome dogs. We also encourage a better work-life balance for athenistas with our flexibility. While we know in-office collaboration is critical to our vision, we recognize that not all work needs to be done within an office environment, full-time. With consistent communication and digital collaboration tools, athenahealth enables employees to find a balance that feels fulfilling and productive for each individual situation. In addition to our traditional benefits and perks, we sponsor events throughout the year, including book clubs, external speakers, and hackathons. We provide athenistas with a company culture based on learning, the support of an engaged team, and an inclusive environment where all employees are valued.
