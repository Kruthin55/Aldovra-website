Associate Operations Processor

Wells Fargo · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role Wells Fargo is seeking an Associate Operations Processor to support day-to-day operational activities while delivering accurate, timely, and quality service. In this role, you will perform routine operational tasks, follow established procedures and controls, and collaborate with team members to resolve issues and meet business needs. This opportunity offers the chance to build operational expertise while contributing to Wells Fargo’s focus on operational excellence, effective risk management, consistent execution, and a strong customer experience. In this role, you will: Perform routine operational tasks using established procedures and workflows Receive, review, track, and complete assigned work accurately and within established timelines Follow applicable policies, procedures, controls, and compliance requirements Maintain accurate and organized records to support the consistency and traceability of work Identify errors, discrepancies, and exceptions, and escalate them to more experienced team members or supervisors Organize daily assignments to meet established service-level expectations and deadlines Work with team members to support consistent process execution and operational goals Provide routine information and operational support to internal partners Required

Qualifications

6+ months of operations support experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired Qualification: Experience in operations, transaction processing, data entry, customer service, or a similar process-driven environment Ability to follow established procedures, workflows, controls, and compliance requirements with attention to accuracy Strong attention to detail and organizational skills, with the ability to manage routine assignments and meet established deadlines Experience identifying errors, discrepancies, or exceptions and escalating issues appropriately Ability to maintain accurate records and documentation to support operational requirements Effective verbal and written communication skills when working with team members and internal partners Ability to work effectively in a team-oriented, structured operational environment Basic proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, including Outlook, Word, and Excel Posting End Date: 15 Nov 2026 *Job posting may come down early due to volume of applicants.
