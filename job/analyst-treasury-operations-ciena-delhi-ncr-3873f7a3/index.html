Analyst, Treasury Operations

Ciena · Delhi NCR

About the role

As the global leader in high-speed connectivity, Ciena is committed to a people-first approach. Our teams enjoy a culture focused on prioritizing a flexible work environment that empowers individual growth, well-being, and belonging. We’re a technology company that leads with our humanity—driving our business priorities alongside meaningful social, community, and societal impact. We are seeking a Treasury Analyst to support Global Treasury Operations function in Gurgaon, India. The Treasury Analyst will be responsible for day-to-day treasury operations across EMEA and APAC, while also supporting Americas as needed. Reporting to the Manager, Treasury Operations, this role is a member of the team responsible for delivering efficient and well-controlled treasury operations across multiple countries. Prior treasury experience is desired but not required. How You Will Contribute: Third-Party Payments: Process or assist with all payment types — electronic batch, manual, critical payments (payroll, taxes, import duties), cross-border payments, including required documentation for foreign-exchange control approvals. Intercompany Settlements: Process or assist with regular intercompany settlements, including netting and cash-pool settlements Cash Planning & liquidity management : Perform daily cash positioning to ensure country-level liquidity while minimizing idle cash; prepare short-term cash forecasting. Banking Operations: Assist with operating global cash pool and managing bank accounts, including account openings, closures, maintenance, KYC requirements. Treasury Management System : Operate cash reporting, payments, netting, in-house banking modules of the treasury management system. Treasury Controls : Assist with compliance with global Treasury policies; support internal and external audit requirements; support cash applications and account reconciliations. The Must Haves: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, or a related discipline. 1 or 2+ years of experience in Treasury, Banking, Accounting, Finance or a related field. Experience working in a multinational, multi-currency and multi-country environment. Working knowledge of cash management, international payments, bank-account administration, cash forecasting and treasury controls is a plus. Experience with Treasury Management Systems and electronic banking platforms is a plus. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, attention to detail and a high degree of control awareness. Effective written and verbal communication skills in English. Willingness to work across different time zones. Professional qualifications such as CTP, CA, CPA, MBA are a plus. Assets: The successful candidate will be a treasury, accounting or finance professional who is in their early stage of career. The individual should demonstrate a mentality for progressive ownership and accountability while being comfortable working across countries, functions and time zones.
