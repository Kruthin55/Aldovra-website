Associate Analyst, Delivery Strategy Services

Invesco · Hyderabad

About the role

About Invesco As one of the world’s leading independent global investment firms, Invesco is dedicated to rethinking possibilities for our clients. By delivering the combined power of our distinctive investment management capabilities, we provide a wide range of investment strategies and vehicles to our clients around the world. If you're looking for challenging work, intelligent colleagues, and exposure across a global footprint, come explore your potential at Invesco. What's in it for you? As an organization we support personal needs, diverse backgrounds and provide internal networks, as well as opportunities to get involved in the community and in the world. Our benefit policy includes but not limited to: Competitive Compensation Flexible, Hybrid Work 30 days’ Annual Leave + Public Holidays Life Insurance Retirement Planning Group Personal Accident Insurance Medical Insurance for Employee and Family Annual Health Check-up 26 weeks Maternity Leave Paternal Leave Adoption Leave Near site Childcare Facility Employee Assistance Program Study Support Employee Stock Purchase Plan ESG Commitments and Goals Business Resource Groups Career Development Programs Mentoring Programs Invesco Cares Job Description Your Role The Associate Analyst will be responsible for assisting in all phases of the due diligence process including repeatable low-level client due diligence questionnaires (DDQs), quarterly client questionnaires and digital platform support. This progressive role is responsible for helping with researching, writing, editing, and responding to due diligence questionnaires related to Invesco’s global suite of products. The Associate Analyst must be highly motivated and intellectually curious, with the ability to take on more responsibility and independence as they gain experience and expertise with the DDQ process and Invesco’s products. Key Responsibilities/Duties: Delivery of high-quality, accurate, and timely submissions for various clients, including the digital due diligence platforms Invesco supports, repeatable low-level client DDQs and quarterly client questionnaires, as required. Following processes and procedures (i.e., saving resource files correctly to project folders, closing out projects, etc.). Taking ownership for your projects. Ensure you have quality checked your deliverable in its entirety for relevance, accuracy, and formatting prior to sending it to your Delivery Manager for peer review. Pursue process improvements through automated features in our content system, Responsive, and online submission platforms. Building and maintaining strong investment knowledge and a credible understanding of different asset classes and capabilities offered by Invesco. Gain and maintain in-depth knowledge of Invesco's structure and operations. Sharing knowledge with other team members. Peer review and checking of work produced by other team members once required level of expertise has been reached. Contribute towards the review and development of processes within the team, to ensure that the team is working as efficiently and effectively as possible. The Experience You Bring Bachelor’s degree, Economics/Communications/English/Liberal Arts; some exposure to Economics or Finance courses preferred. Knowledge of Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook required. Understanding of different asset classes/strategies and how they function is preferred. Strong writing and editing skills; an excellent communicator. Strong project management skills with high attention to detail. Ability to juggle and prioritize multiple projects simultaneously. Ability to work well under pressure in a deadline-oriented atmosphere. Self-starter, highly motivated. Critical thinker and a quick learner; intellectually curious. Able to work well both independently and as a team member. Interest in exploring a career in financial services. Full Time / Part Time Full time Worker Type Employee Job Exempt (Yes / No) No Workplace Model At Invesco, our workplace model supports our culture and meets the needs of our clients while providing flexibility our employees value. As a full-time employee, compliance with the workplace policy means working with your direct manager to create a schedule where you will work in your designated office at least four days a week, with one day working outside an Invesco office.
