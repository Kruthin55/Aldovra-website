Accounting Associate - OTC(Billing/Collections) US Shift

Maersk · Pune

About the role

PURPOSE OF ROLE: Would be responsible for some or all of the accounts receivable processes, i.e. sales invoice generation, sales invoice processing, booking incoming payments, customer account reconciliation, collections, dunning, dispute management. The role is responsible to coordinate with other teams including other functions to ensure timely resolution of customer issues. Executes day to day processing of transactions within defined standard operating procedures with a purpose of achieving service delivery goals for the organization and providing first level support for both technical and functional issues raised by end users. Key user in OTC tasks. PRINCIPAL ACCOUNTABILITIES:

Perform customer analysis on the trending, payments, disputes, on account etc. and proactively drive Collections using this information.

Coordinate with cash applications, invoicing, dispute, D&D, Sales etc. teams on actions pending to ensure timely resolution of customer issues

Execute outbound telephone calls and other contact to customers (Customer queries/requests/disputes logging)

Communicating with stakeholders/customers via email, telephone, VC, etc.

Highlight risks on customer account and proactively have action plan to mitigate the risk

Reconcile customer accounts

Follow up with customers regarding past-due payments

Update client billing information

Billing invoice generation and processing

Perform timely Self -Assessment for OTC controls and ensure 100% control effectiveness.

Maintain documentation for compliance reporting and audits

Revenue analysis by customer, by service, by volume mix

Posting of Tax entries on collection

MDM support to create new customers.

Training and coaching new team members.

Participate and support testing and implementation of improvement initiatives CRITICAL QUALIFICATIONS/SKILLS/EXPERIENCE

Bachelor’s degree in accounting or finance (preferred)

For a JL2 1-4 years of ‘Accounts Receivables’ experience.

Prior experience of working on IFS system would be an added advantage.

Good knowledge of E2E AR process and procedures.

Good knowledge of MS office, Word, Excel, etc.

Familiar with MS Outlook & Internet.

Good communication skills.
