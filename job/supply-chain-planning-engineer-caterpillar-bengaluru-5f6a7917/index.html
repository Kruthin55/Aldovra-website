Job Summary Processes job requisitions and provides support for supply chain, sourcing, cost, price analyses and contract/purchase order generation. What You Will Do:

Working with master planners on outsourcing needs; establishing blanket purchase orders and vendor managed inventory (VMI).

Identifies and leads initiatives to improve the supply-chain process.

Supporting customers and leading customer proposal efforts with pricing information and supplier selection.

Accountable for maintaining supply chain application systems.
