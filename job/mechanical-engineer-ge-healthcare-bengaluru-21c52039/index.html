Mechanical Engineer

GE HealthCare · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary Activities contributing to the design and development of products, solutions and systems. Includes activities linked to technical improvement of existing products and components Impacts quality of own work and the work of others on the team. Executes standard operational/technical tasks typically subject to instructions and work routines. There is latitude to rearrange the sequence to complete task/duties based on changing work situations. Job Description Key

Responsibilities

Own and execute mechanical engineering activities for new product development, installed-base, and VCP projects . Develop and evaluate mechanical designs using tolerance analysis, material selection, and design-for-manufacturing considerations. Create and modify 3D CAD models, drawings, specifications, and engineering documentation . Perform engineering analysis, risk assessment, root-cause investigation, and design reviews. Plan and execute prototype builds, testing, verification, and design validation activities. Apply DFMEA, tolerance analysis, GD&T, and Design for Six Sigma/design control principles as appropriate. Work with manufacturing, quality, sourcing, regulatory, systems, and other engineering teams to resolve technical issues. Support technical investigations and drive issues to closure using structured problem-solving methodologies. Ensure designs and engineering documentation comply with applicable quality systems, regulatory requirements, and design-control processes. Clearly communicate technical recommendations, risks, trade-offs, and project status to stakeholders.

Qualifications

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related engineering discipline . Approximately 2+ years of relevant mechanical engineering experience , Hands-on experience with 3D CAD and engineering drawings , preferably Creo or equivalent. Good understanding of GD&T, tolerance stack-up, materials, manufacturing processes, and mechanical testing . Experience with structured problem solving, design reviews, verification, and engineering change processes. Experience in medical devices or another regulated industry is preferred. Exposure to simulation/CAE, DFSS, reliability engineering, or MBD would be an advantage. Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: No
