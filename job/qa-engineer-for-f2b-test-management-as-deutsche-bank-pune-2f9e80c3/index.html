QA Engineer for F2B Test Management, AS

Deutsche Bank · Pune

About the role

Job Description: Job Title: QA Engineer for F2B Test Management, AS Location: Pune - Magarpatta, India Corporate Title: Associate Role Description Quality Assurance (QA) is responsible for ensuring testing strategies, plans and deliverables are executed for all applications and programs of work within their domain. Work includes: Designing and conducting testing procedures and frameworks to verify software and other technical products to ensure expected functional performance Investigating instances where software and technical products do not perform as expected Designing and conducting quality assurance practices to support business development activities Driving the adoption of recognized best practice and policy and contributes to the ongoing improvement of methodologies and assets for the business Working with their counterparts across the business to raise the standard of QA across the bank

Recording of defects and the collation of metrics and KPI information for reporting purposes Testing disciplines include: Performance Testing, Test Automation, Test Processes, Testing Products What we’ll offer you As part of our flexible scheme, here are just some of the benefits that you’ll enjoy Best in class leave policy Gender neutral parental leaves 100% reimbursement under childcare assistance benefit (gender neutral) Sponsorship for Industry relevant certifications and education Employee Assistance Program for you and your family members Comprehensive Hospitalization Insurance for you and your dependents Accident and Term life Insurance Complementary Health screening for 35 yrs. and above Your key responsibilities End-to-end test management and delivery for the Cards domain, from requirements analysis and test planning through execution, closure and release-readiness assessment. Define and maintain risk-based test strategies, scope, plans, estimates, resource requirements, schedules and entry and exit criteria, aligned with program milestones and business priorities. Work with business analysts, developers and stakeholders to identify critical Cards business journeys, integration dependencies and functional and non-functional requirements; maintain traceability from requirements to test cases, results and defects. Coordinate test environment and test data readiness across dependent applications and teams, ensuring prerequisites are met and escalating constraints that could impact test delivery. Plan and coordinate functional, integration, end-to-end and regression testing across Cards applications and interfaces; coordinate performance and other applicable non-functional testing with specialist teams. Drive test automation priorities and reusable regression coverage with the testing team, balancing hands-on test execution with overall delivery coordination and continuous improvement. Manage the defect lifecycle, including triage, prioritization, ownership tracking, retesting and closure; work with delivery teams to resolve blockers and escalate quality risks promptly. Track and communicate test progress, requirements coverage, execution results, defect trends, resource needs and delivery risks through clear metrics and stakeholder reporting; contribute to sprint planning, reviews and retrospectives. Coordinate user acceptance testing with business stakeholders, consolidate test evidence and assess exit criteria; provide a documented release-readiness recommendation, highlighting outstanding defects and residual risks for authorized stakeholder acceptance. Complete test closure activities, capture lessons learned and drive improvements to test processes, reusable assets and quality practices in collaboration with the broader QA & Testing community. Your skills and experience Experience in end-to-end test management, including risk-based test strategies, planning, estimation, requirements traceability, test environment and test data readiness, defect management and release-readiness assessment. Hands-on experience designing, developing, executing and maintaining automated API and end-to-end regression tests using Karate or a related test automation framework, with a focus on reusable test assets and reliable coverage of Cards business journeys. Strong experience testing REST APIs and validating JSON and XML payloads, supported by practical knowledge of Postman and SQL Developer for API testing and data validation. Strong communication and stakeholder coordination skills, with the ability to report test progress, explain defects and quality risks, resolve dependencies and collaborate effectively across business and technology teams; good English language skills. Knowledge of the Cards domain and experience working in Agile/Scrum delivery teams, including requirements analysis, test case design, sprint ceremonies and coordination of functional, integration, regression and user acceptance testing. Bachelor’s degree with a focus on IT or data evaluation, or comparable professional experience. Practical experience with Java and related automation tools such as Selenium or REST Assured; experience using Quality Centre/ALM, Jira and Confluence for test management, defect tracking and documentation, and Performance Centre or a related tool for performance testing. Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimise workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs.
