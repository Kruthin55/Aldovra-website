Technical Courseware Developer

Hewlett Packard Enterprise · Bengaluru

About the role

Technical Courseware Developer This role has been designed as ‘’Onsite’ with an expectation that you will primarily work from an HPE partner/customer office. Who We Are: Hewlett Packard Enterprise is the global edge-to-cloud company advancing the way people live and work. We help companies connect, protect, analyze, and act on their data and applications wherever they live, from edge to cloud, so they can turn insights into outcomes at the speed required to thrive in today’s complex world. Our culture thrives on finding new and better ways to accelerate what’s next. We know varied backgrounds are valued and succeed here. We have the flexibility to manage our work and personal needs. We make bold moves, together, and are a force for good. If you are looking to stretch and grow your career our culture will embrace you. Open up opportunities with HPE. Job Description: What You'll Do Design, develop, deliver, and maintain customer-facing, best-in-class technical courseware and learning content. Create comprehensive training materials, including concepts, tasks, installation guides, illustrations, video scripts, course modules, and in-product documentation. Develop customer-focused learning content for HPE Networking cloud services, automation, and networking solutions. Translate complex networking and software technologies into easy-to-understand educational content. Collaborate with product managers, engineers, and subject matter experts to gather requirements and validate technical accuracy. Analyze product specifications and recommend the most effective learning approach. Gain hands-on experience with HPE networking products to understand customer workflows and learning needs. Follow established content development standards, style guides, and documentation processes. Lead or contribute to courseware development projects while working in a collaborative Agile environment. Utilize AI-powered tools to improve content creation efficiency while maintaining high-quality deliverables. What You Need to Bring 5+ years of experience creating technical documentation, courseware, or customer education content. Bachelor's degree in Technical Writing, English, Computer Science, or a related field, or equivalent networking certifications/experience. Strong technical writing skills with the ability to produce accurate, high-quality, user-friendly content. Good understanding of cloud computing, networking, automation, data centers, security, and enterprise software technologies. Hands-on experience with networking technologies, including: Routing & Switching Network Security Network Automation Virtual Machines Containers Cloud Services Ability to independently research and simplify complex technical concepts. Experience with XML, Markdown, content management systems, and courseware authoring tools. Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder collaboration skills. Experience leveraging AI tools for content creation and productivity. Self-driven mindset with the ability to thrive in a fast-paced technical environment. Preferred Skills Proficiency in Markdown and Microsoft PowerPoint for technical content creation. Experience working with Agile methodologies and tools such as JIRA. Familiarity with HPE Aruba Networking and/or HPE Juniper Networking products. Industry-recognized networking certifications (e.g., Aruba, Juniper, Cisco, or equivalent). Experience leading technical content or courseware development initiatives.
