GPU Design Verification Engineer

Intel · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Details: Job Description: The Role and Impact: We are looking for a GPU Design Verification Engineer to ensure the functional accuracy of cutting-edge graphics logic components, including 3D graphics, media, and display. In this role, you will develop and execute verification plans, build scalable test benches, and perform system simulations to uncover bugs and ensure design compliance. Your work will be integral in delivering high-quality designs that power Intel's advanced graphics solutions. Business Group: Join Intel's Data Center Group (DCG), a team that drives innovation in computing and accelerates solutions for data-centric workloads. This group is focused on advancing technologies that support Intel's leadership in the data center domain, from performance optimization to scalability. As part of this team, you will contribute to Intel's mission to transform data into actionable insights and enable breakthrough innovations. Key

Responsibilities

- Perform functional verification of graphics logic components to ensure they meet specifications. - Define and develop scalable and reusable verification plans, test benches, and verification environments. - Execute system simulation models to verify design functionality, analyze power and timing, and identify bugs. - Root cause and debug issues in the presilicon environment and implement corrective measures. - Collaborate with GPU architects, RTL developers, and physical design teams to improve verification processes and meet design goals. - Document test plans and drive technical reviews with design and architecture teams. - Maintain and enhance verification infrastructure and methodologies.

Qualifications

Minimum

Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electronics or Computer Engineering, or any related STEM degree, OR Master's degree in a relevant field OR PhD in a relevant field with no prior professional experience with 4+ years of hands on experience in IP Verification. - Proficiency in Verilog, System Verilog, and UVM for pre-silicon verification. - Hands-on experience with test bench development, coverage/assertion writing, and closure. - Exposure to GPU or CPU architecture and debugging experience with industry-standard frontend design flows and tools. - Programming expertise in scripting languages such as Python, Perl, or Shell scripting. Preferred

Qualifications

- Familiarity with Graphics architecture and GPU verification processes. - Expertise in RTL verification and validation, testbench development, and functional modeling. - Experience with coverage-driven verification methodologies. - Strong analytical and debugging skills, with the ability to work independently and mentor junior engineers. We invite you to be part of a team that shapes the future of graphics innovation. Take this opportunity to grow, learn, and make a meaningful impact on the next generation of technology solutions.
