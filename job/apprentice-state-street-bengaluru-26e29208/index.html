About the role

Who We Are Looking For : We are seeking motivated and detail-oriented interns/apprentices to join the IM ERM, India team. This role offers exposure to risk monitoring, reporting, and governance within a global financial services environment, working with cross-functional teams. Key Responsibilites: Support preparation of risk reports, dashboards, and presentations using Excel/PowerPoint. Assist in collection and validation of Key Risk Indicator (KRI) data for reporting. Help track and document operational risk incidents, errors, and action items. Perform basic data analysis to identify trends, anomalies, and risk themes. Support development and maintenance of reporting tools (Excel / Tableau dashboards). Assist with process documentation, procedure updates, and maintaining risk-related records. Monitor workflows and flag exceptions or threshold breaches for review. Support team initiatives related to automation and process improvements. Collaborate with internal teams (Business, Operations, Compliance) for data collection and clarification. Candidate Profile (Education, Skills & Attributes): Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Finance, Business, Economics or related field with strong academic performance. Proficiency in MS Excel, PowerPoint & Word; familiarity with Tableau / Power BI / Python or AI concepts is a plus. Strong communication, attention to detail, and willingness to learn in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment. What We Value: Positive attitude, ownership mindset, and curiosity to understand risk concepts. Strong teamwork and ability to collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders. Basic analytical thinking and problem-solving ability. Program Details: Location: Bangalore Duration: Internship / Contract Role Experience: Fresh graduates / Final-year students.
