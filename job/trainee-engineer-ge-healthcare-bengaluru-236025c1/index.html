Trainee Engineer

GE HealthCare · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary Supporting Sourcing activities for Category Team that includes supplier price updates, contract management, RFQ execution, payment-term updates, COOR compliance, supplier issue resolution, and coordination with cross-functional teams to ensure uninterrupted supply and operational efficiency. GE HealthCare is a leading global medical technology and digital solutions innovator. Our purpose is to create a world where healthcare has no limits. Unlock your ambition, turn ideas into world-changing realities, and join an organization where every voice makes a difference, and every difference builds a healthier world. Job Description Roles &

Responsibilities

Managing and updating approved supplier price changes (Increase/Decrease) in Blanket Purchase Agreements (BPAs) within the workflow system, based on specific plants (Inventory Organizations) or supplier locations Contracts Updating in OCDB ( Approvals ) / Renewal of Contracts (documentations ) Pay terms changes/updates in WFF Updating Leadtime, minimum order quantity, and standard packaging quantity information in the system (Supplier requests) Supplier Change Requests Follow-ups (Quality issues/Delivery Challenges/ EOL issues) with cross-functional teams Creating or updating the COOR (Country of Origin) information for Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA), of the supplied parts in the system. Bank details Updates from Suppliers (Supplier request on Name Change Etc.,) Drawing Downloads from My workshop for RFQs (Request for Quotation) Service Parts price / LT / MOQ (minimum order quantity) changes RFQ Support ( Launching RFQs for Transfer BC ) Support on Supplier fulfillment issues ( Components Follow-ups ) New Supplier Additions in SAM Workflows / SQR Updating Payment follow-ups with Finance Team ( Overdue payment issues) Required

Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Mechanical/Production/Electrical/Electronics/ Medical Electronics Engineering Recent graduate or up to 1 year of relevant internship or industrial experience. Basic understanding of manufacturing processes and engineering fundamentals. Good analytical and problem-solving skills. Strong communication and teamwork skills. Good interpersonal skills Desired Characteristics Internship or academic project experience in manufacturing, production engineering. Should be able to read and interpret mechanical/engineering drawings Eagerness to learn in a fast-paced manufacturing environment. Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications for reporting and documentation. Positive attitude with strong ownership and collaboration skills.
