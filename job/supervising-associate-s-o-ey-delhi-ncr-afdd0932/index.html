About the role

At EY, you’ll have the chance to build a career as unique as you are, with the global scale, support, inclusive culture and technology to become the best version of you. And we’re counting on your unique voice and perspective to help EY become even better, too. Join us and build an exceptional experience for yourself, and a better working world for all. GDS Enablement Services | Strategy & Operations Job Description Supervising Associate About the Team GDS Enablement Services Strategy & Operations partners with ES leadership and Service Functions to advance strategic priorities, operational excellence, and business performance. The team provides data-led insights and business advisory support, while positioning key priorities and performance for leadership forums. Role Purpose The Supervising Associate will serve as a trusted business advisor and delivery lead, partnering with senior leadership and Service Functions to advance strategic and operational priorities. The role encompasses business advisory, program management, reporting and analytics, and financial and operational governance, with responsibility for managing complex workstreams and delivering measurable outcomes. Key

Responsibilities

Governance, Reporting & Business Insights Own recurring and on-demand MIS and KPI reporting across ES and Service Functions, ensuring accurate, consistent, and decision-ready outputs. Partner with senior SF leadership and the Core Leadership Team (CLT) to deliver data-driven analysis, insights, and executive-level reporting. Analyse business, financial, people, client, compliance, and operational KPIs to identify trends, risks, opportunities, and areas requiring management attention. Translate complex data into meaningful insights, dashboards, storyboards, and executive-level presentations using Excel, PowerPoint, Power BI, and AI-enabled tools. Develop agendas and content for leadership meetings, and track key deliverables, actions, and decisions through to closure. Maintain effective governance over SharePoint repositories, dashboards, reporting trackers, and key performance information. Strategic Program & Project Management Lead the planning and implementation of strategic initiatives and transformation programs across ES. Manage programs and projects end-to-end, developing business cases, project plans, KPIs, governance structures, milestones, and leadership reporting to support accountability and successful delivery. Facilitate stakeholder discussions, governance meetings, workshops, brainstorming sessions, and leadership updates. Coordinate with GDS Finance, Talent, Procurement, Payroll, Service Excellence, BMC, Real Estate, and other enabling teams to deliver project outcomes. Identify risks, issues, dependencies, and delivery challenges early and work with stakeholders to establish appropriate mitigation plans. Lead or support change impact assessments, stakeholder mapping, communication planning, and adoption activities for transformation initiatives. Business & Operational Advisory Act as a trusted advisor to Service Function leadership on strategic, operational, and financial priorities. Translate performance insights into actionable recommendations that support informed decision-making and course correction. Collaborate with Service Functions and enabling teams to resolve operational issues and drive business priorities. Support workforce and headcount governance, including cost centres, departments, workforce pyramid, location strategy, and the alignment of headcount with organizational structures. Provide guidance on engagement codes, expense-code governance, and related operational requirements. Monitor compliance requirements, highlight gaps and risks, and work with relevant stakeholders to drive timely closure. Support real estate and workplace planning through analysis of seat requirements and utilization. Financial Planning & Performance Lead or support ES planning, budgeting, and forecasting activities, including coordinating Service Function inputs and submissions in Hyperion. Provide analysis and insights on P&L, utilization, hiring, headcount, billability, indirect costs, and other financial drivers. Partner with Finance and Service Functions on rate-card inputs, cost optimization, workforce planning, and performance reviews. Review unbilled FTEs and hours, non-billable costs, and other financial indicators to identify potential revenue leakage and optimization opportunities. Support accounts receivable governance and timely resolution of outstanding items. Leadership, Stakeholder & People Management Build trusted relationships with senior leaders, Service Functions, regional stakeholders, and enabling teams, influencing alignment and outcomes. Provide strategic, evidence-based recommendations, offer constructive challenge where appropriate, and proactively identify and escalate risks. Represent Strategy & Operations in leadership forums and cross-functional initiatives, combining strategic thinking with effective execution. If direct reportees are assigned, manage goal setting, ongoing feedback, and performance review cycles, while supporting team learning, career development, and overall growth through regular coaching and guidance. Knowledge, Skills & Experience Relevant experience in strategy and operations, program management, analytics, finance, transformation, or a comparable field, supported by strong business acumen and problem-solving skills. Strong project and stakeholder management capabilities, with the ability to manage competing priorities and deliver high-quality executive-level outputs. Should have Sound understanding of planning, budgeting, forecasting, P&L, headcount, utilization, billing, and cost management. Advanced proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint, and Power BI, with familiarity in Copilot, EYQ, and other AI-enabled tools considered desirable.
