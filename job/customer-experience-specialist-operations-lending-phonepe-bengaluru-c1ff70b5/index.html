Customer Experience Specialist • Operations | Lending

PhonePe · Bengaluru

About the role

PhonePe Limited (Formerly PhonePe Private Limited) is a technology company that builds digital platforms for Payments, Digital Distribution Services and Financial Services. Headquartered in India, the PhonePe digital payments app was launched in 2016. As of April 2026, PhonePe has over 70 Crore life-till-date registered users and a digital payments acceptance network spread across over 5 Crore merchants. PhonePe’s products and services include Consumer Payments (including Digital Distribution Services), Merchant Payments, Lending and Insurance Distribution services, and New Platforms, which comprise Share.Market (stock broking and mutual funds distribution platform), and Indus Appstore (Android-based mobile app marketplace). Culture: At PhonePe, we go the extra mile to make sure you can bring your best self to work, Everyday!. And that starts with creating the right environment for you. We empower people and trust them to do the right thing. Here, you own your work from start to finish, right from day one. PhonePe-rs solve complex problems and execute quickly; often building frameworks from scratch. If you’re excited by the idea of building platforms that touch millions, ideating with some of the best minds in the country and executing on your dreams with purpose and speed, join us! About the Role: We are looking for a highly capable In-House Specialist for our Customer Support team. Your primary responsibility will entail running system queries and generating accurate resolutions for complex cases escalated by L1 agents or automated BOT systems, ensuring timely end-to-end ticket closures. In this role, you will solve problems that our customers encounter and continuously seek outstanding ways to improve their experience. You will also contribute to consistently bettering and streamlining our support systems by identifying patterns, tracking fraud governance, and managing high-priority escalations to optimize the overall customer experience. Key

Responsibilities

Query Execution & Escalation Resolution: Act as the primary resolution driver for tickets escalated by L1 agents and BOT systems. Run necessary system queries, generate effective resolutions, and ensure the complete and accurate closure of customer tickets. Core Ticket Handling: Manage BAU L2 ticket resolutions by providing context-based solutions. Handle critical tasks such as sharing in-house generated foreclosure links, resolving NDC and Statement of Account (SOA) issues, and processing specific tickets routed from L1, such as autopay cancellations. System Diagnostics: Perform additional L2 checks and diagnostics at the L1 stage using internal tools (such as fabricator and echo) according to detailed SOPs to ensure accuracy before further escalation. Escalation Management: Take ownership of high-level escalation cases, including Grievances, Social Media (SM) complaints, Leadership escalations, lending-grievance DL cases, and HITS. This includes performing outbound calls to customers to bring about resolution. Driving Resolution & TAT: Proactively monitor aging and breached tickets. Conduct daily connects and follow-ups with L2 teams, flag high-aging cases, and push for timely resolutions and Root Cause Analyses (RCAs). Fraud Governance: Oversee daily tracking of fraud cases, share requested details based on tracker comments, check for L2 resolutions, and flag cases that are breaching Turnaround Time (TAT) limits. Lender Coordination: Manually review and provide QRC (Query & Resolution Clarification) details required by lenders for flagged cases on a monthly basis Customer & Process Excellence: Act with integrity and a customer-first mindset in every interaction. Recommend process improvements, leverage internal resources to drive resolution, engage and educate customers so they can leverage PhonePe to the fullest, and consistently meet hourly and daily productivity goals. Ideal Candidate Profile: Education & Experience: Graduation (10+2+3) is strictly mandatory. Requires 0 - 2 years of work experience in a customer-facing role. Communication: Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Must be fluent in English and Hindi. Multilingual skills (spoken and written) in South Indian languages are highly preferred. Problem-Solving: Strong customer orientation with the ability to adapt to different scenarios, deal well with objections, and leverage internal processes or relevant teams to drive resolutions. Work Ethic: High learnability, active listener, flexible, open to feedback, and a proven team player. Must possess the ability to multitask, prioritize effectively, and manage time efficiently in a fast-paced environment. PhonePe Full Time Employee
