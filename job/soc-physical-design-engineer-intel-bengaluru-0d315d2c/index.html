SoC Physical Design Engineer

Intel · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Details: Job Description: The Role and Impact As a Physical Design Engineer at Intel, you'll be at the forefront of cutting-edge semiconductor innovation, designing next-generation Client SoCs to redefine computing. In this role, you will implement complex structural physical designs and methodologies, ensuring high-performance, efficient, and reliable products. Your contributions will directly influence Intel's ability to deliver world-class solutions for laptops and desktops, driving advancements in technology and enriching lives globally. Business Group Join Intel's Devices Development Group, a highly regarded organization focused on creating Client SoCs that enable the future of computing. This team works on groundbreaking technologies, designing world-class products with an emphasis on power, performance, and efficiency. By joining, you'll contribute to Intel's mission of creating world-changing technology that enriches the lives of every person on Earth. Key

Responsibilities

Perform physical design implementation of SoCs from RTL to GDS, creating manufacturing-ready design databases. Execute physical synthesis, floor planning, place and route, clock tree synthesis, and power grid design using industry-standard tools such as Synopsys or Cadence. Conduct verification and signoff processes, including formal equivalence verification, static timing analysis, reliability verification, layout verification, and noise analysis. Analyse design results, identify violations, and recommend optimizations to improve product architecture and performance. Drive tool, flow, and methodology enhancements to streamline design processes. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define innovative design methodologies and ensure seamless integration across development workflows. Provide technical consultation on design trade-offs and engage with stakeholders to establish and refine design objectives.

Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Electronics, Computer Engineering, or a related field with 10+ years of relevant experience; master’s degree with 8+ years of relevant experience; or PhD with 6+ years of relevant experience. Proficiency in physical design tools and methodologies, including clock tree synthesis, static timing analysis, floor planning, noise analysis, and RTL to GDS workflows. Expertise in scripting languages such as Python, Perl, or TCL for automation and flow improvements. Experience in verification and signoff processes, including equivalence verification, timing analysis, and reliability verification. Preferred

Qualifications

Strong problem-solving and organizational skills with attention to detail. Ability to collaborate effectively in dynamic, fast-paced environments. Experience in driving tool and methodology enhancements or leading specific domain improvements (e.g., power optimization, formal equivalence verification). Familiarity with developing and defining physical design methodologies, engaging with design automation teams, vendors, and stakeholders. Be part of Intel's journey to shape the future of semiconductor design and technology. Apply today to contribute to delivering innovative solutions that power the next generation of computing devices.
