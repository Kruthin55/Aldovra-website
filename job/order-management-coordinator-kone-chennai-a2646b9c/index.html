Order Management coordinator

KONE · Chennai

About the role

Position Summary (Order Management coordinator) We are seeking a detail-oriented Order Management professional to manage elevator and escalator equipment orders from project booking through procurement handover. The role includes technical validation, SAP order processing, BOM management, vendor coordination, and logistics support to ensure accurate and timely order fulfillment. Key

Responsibilities

Receive elevator, escalator, equipment, and component orders from Frontline (FL) and Customer Solutions Engineering (CSE) teams and book them into the FL Order Book Verify order information, initiate order-related engineering activities, and confirm orders with Supply Line (SL). Review project documents, specifications, order forms, XML files, layouts and surveys. Compare tender and order specifications and resolve discrepancies. Process orders in SAP KTOC and SAP Network. Create and release BOMs and Purchase Requisitions. Manage equipment reservation and vendor allocation activities. Coordinate effectively with Project Management, Logistics, Procurement, Engineering, and other stakeholders. Generate and distribute BOM documentation packages. Update SAP milestones and maintain order records. Perform quality checks to ensure First-Time-Right delivery and process compliance. Communicate delivery deviations and operational issues to FL/CSE teams in a timely manner. Drive continuous process improvement and operational excellence initiatives Performance Measures Order turnaround time First-Time-Right quality BOM accuracy SAP milestone compliance On-time procurement handover Stakeholder/customer satisfaction Required skills Strong attention to Detail, SAP/ERP System proficiency BOM Management, Stakeholder Management, Technical Documentation and specification review, Process Compliance Time management and prioritization skills Continuous Improvement mindset. · Basic Project management Analytical and problem-solving skills · Effective communication and collaboration Qualification and experience · Higher vocational diploma or university degree (B.Sc. or M.Sc.) in technical or commercial sciences. · Fluent level of English · Good IT and digital tool proficiency 2-6 years’ experience in order management, Frontline/supply chain, procurement support or engineering operations. Experience working with ERP systems, preferably SAP. Knowledge of BOM, PR and procurement workflows. Elevator and Escalator industry experience preferred Exposure to engineering drawings and technical specifications.
