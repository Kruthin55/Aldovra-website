Banking - Investment Banking, Summer Analyst, India – APAC, 2027

Citi · Mumbai

About the role

We want to hear from you if… You are in your penultimate year of university pursuing a degree in any discipline Who we think will be a great fit... We are looking for motivated individuals, who are eager to grow their careers in Investment Banking. Successful candidates take pride in all aspects of their work and recognize that it is a reflection not just of the firm, but of themselves. Openness to diversity is something we highly value, and so should you. We will also be looking for the following: Ability to win and sustain the loyalty of the most demanding institutional clients with confidence and good judgment Intellectual curiosity and proactive approach to searching for new and creative ideas to implement innovative investment banking strategies Enthusiasm for contributing to the Citi community, through teamwork, leadership, and mentoring Commitment to their own personal success and the success of the institution as a whole We provide you with the knowledge and skills you need to succeed. We are committed to teaching you the ropes of Investment Banking. The 10-week Summer Internship Program will commence with the one-week intensive training program. Depending on country, your program may be rotational or assigned to a particular team. In both cases, your allocations are based on your interests and our needs. Summer Interns are assigned to an industry or product coverage group, where you’ll focus on financial analysis and business development. Your time here will look something like this… Collecting & analyzing company information to assist in transactions, including conducting industry research, revising, and organizing financial data You're the brains behind our work. If you are ready to bring your knowledge from the classroom to the board room, then Citi can help you get there! Whether in honing your skills or building your network, success will not come easy without growth. Our programs will equip you with the knowledge and training that you will need to play a valuable role in a team and to establish a long-term career here.
