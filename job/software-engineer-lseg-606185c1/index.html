Software Engineer

LSEG · BLR-Divyasree Technopolis, India

About the role

LSEG Data & Analytics is one of the world’s largest providers of financial markets data and infrastructure. With over 40,000 customers and 400,000 end users across approximately 190 markets, we are an essential partner to the global financial community and redefining the future of data in financial services. We enable customers to draw crucial insights through data, feeds, analytics, AI and workflow solutions. Our engineers design, develop and deliver the products and services that reimagine how our industry works. Driving innovation through digitalisation and AI, we deliver accelerated impact and excellence for customers every day. The Opportunity We are seeking passionate software engineers from mid-level to lead to help us build robust, enterprise-grade solutions that drive real-world impact. Our problems are complex from building AI-powered analytics that are constantly evolving, to challenges that define modern finance, giving engineers the opportunity to stretch their craft and shape solutions that matter. Our software engineers have a strong appetite for innovation, new technologies, and AI, both in how we accelerate engineering and in how we create smarter, more valuable experiences for our customers. We encourage curiosity, experimentation, and continuous learning, giving our teams the opportunity to solve complex challenges at scale. What We Look For We place great value on technical excellence and a drive for continuous learning. Experienced Software Engineers with one of the following technologies – Java, Python, C++ or C# Strong experience with CI/CD, unit testing framework and automation (testing/deployment) in cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure Curiosity about AI and a strong interest in how emerging technologies can be applied to solve complex software engineering problems. Prior Financial Services or Big Tech experience preferred Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering and related field or relevant experience Excellent problem-solving skills and a proactive approach to identifying and resolving issues Willingness to learn and work with new technologies across the full stack Strong teamwork and communication skills, with the ability to collaborate optimally with cross-functional teams across multiple regions and timezones Career Stage: Senior Associate London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG) Information: Join us and be part of a team that values innovation, quality, and continuous improvement.
