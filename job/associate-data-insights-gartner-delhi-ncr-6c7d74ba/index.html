Associate, Data Insights

Gartner · Delhi NCR

About the role

About this role: This role supports the Digital IQ team in collecting high-quality data, investigating data-related issues, documenting findings, and contributing to continuous data quality improvements under the guidance of senior team members. The role requires a strong sense of ownership and accountability for the accuracy and quality of collected data. The ideal candidate will independently develop end-to-end data collection reports while collaborating with senior colleagues and stakeholders to understand requirements and deliver high-quality outcomes. The individual will assess the overall health, completeness, and consistency of data from third-party and proprietary sources. What you will do: Accurately record observations, evidence, and notes in internal platforms following established validation rules. Identify and promptly flag website bugs, data errors, or usability concerns to research leads or managers for further action. Use available information resources to build understanding of data points and contribute to overall data quality improvement efforts. Review assigned data sets for accuracy; share findings with colleagues as part of routine quality checks. Investigate and document questions or issues related to brand site content and configuration under the guidance of senior team members. Complete assigned project tasks on schedule while maintaining high standards for quality in deliverables. Collaborate closely with teammates in a fast-paced environment; seek feedback from peers and supervisors to support continuous learning. Participate actively in knowledge sharing sessions as a learner; apply new concepts or methods as directed.

What you’ll need

Graduate with excellent record in any stream 0-1 years of experience in data collection/extraction/entry or marketing support functions such as list building, lead qualification Basic proficiency in Microsoft Excel (data entry, sorting, filtering) Familiarity with PowerPoint for reporting or sharing findings Ability to use internal platforms/tools for documentation and data validation Understanding of digital marketing concepts (introductory level) · Interest in learning and responsibly applying Gartner approved GenAI-enabled tools. undefined
