Job Summary: This role coordinates and oversees key workload activities to ensure materials are available when needed.

Responsibilities

include tracking orders, expediting shipments, managing stock issues, resolving record discrepancies, making decisions on sourcing changes, and providing training and support for Material Availability Analysts toward departmental objectives. Engage and discuss with Global Purchasing whenever a lack of supplier capacity/capability is identified. Take ownership to lead the appropriate action plans for process improvements with Purchasing and other Process Partners including Warehouse and Transportation teams. What You Have People: - Works effectively with colleagues, customers, and suppliers at all levels. Leadership: No direct reports.
