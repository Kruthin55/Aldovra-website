Analytics Consultant

Wells Fargo · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Analytics Consultant. In this role, you will: Consult with business lines and enterprise functions on complex research and analytical initiatives. Use functional knowledge to assist in developing non-model quantitative tools that support strategic decision-making. Perform analysis of findings and trends using statistical techniques and document analytical processes. Present recommendations to increase revenue, reduce expenses, and maximize operational efficiency, quality, and compliance. Identify and define business requirements and translate data and business needs into research and recommendations to improve efficiency. Participate in group technology efforts, including the design and implementation of database structures, analytics software, data storage, and processing solutions. Develop customized reports and ad hoc analyses to provide recommendations and guidance to less experienced staff. Understand compliance and risk management requirements for the supported area. Ensure adherence to data management and data governance regulations, standards, and policies. Participate in company initiatives and processes to support risk and capital objectives and other strategic goals. Collaborate and consult with more experienced consultants and partners across technology and other business groups. Required

Qualifications

2+ years of Analytics experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, or education. 2+ years of experience in Data Analytics, Reporting, or Capacity Planning. Desired

Qualifications

Strong hands-on experience in SQL, including joins, CTEs, aggregations, window functions, and data validation. Good working knowledge of Python for data analysis, data manipulation, and automation. Strong proficiency in Excel; experience with Power BI or similar visualization tools is preferred. Ability to analyze historical workload, volume trends, productivity, and operational data to generate actionable insights. Ability to support capacity planning and workforce forecasting by translating workload drivers into resource requirements. Job Expectations: Identify trends, seasonality, demand changes, and potential capacity gaps or surpluses. Ability to build and maintain analytical reports, dashboards, and recurring capacity-planning outputs. Work with business and operations stakeholders to understand demand drivers, productivity assumptions, and staffing needs. Demonstrate strong analytical and problem-solving skills with close attention to data accuracy and quality. Ability to clearly communicate analytical findings and recommendations to business stakeholders. Posting End Date: 14 Nov 2026 *Job posting may come down early due to volume of applicants.
