CA Internship

Procter & Gamble · Mumbai

About the role

Job Location MUMBAI GENERAL OFFICE Job Description Finance & Accounting at P&G plays a key role in business strategy, decision-making, planning and performance management . As a CA Intern , you will join P&G for a 1-year internship and work on meaningful Finance assignments across brands, businesses, functions or key projects. From Day 1, you will take ownership of defined responsibilities, work with Finance leaders and cross-functional teams, and use financial analysis and business insights to support decision-making. The internship provides practical exposure to Finance in a leading FMCG organization while building your financial, analytical, business partnering and leadership capabilities . Key

Responsibilities

Support financial analysis, planning, forecasting and performance management . Analyze financial and business data to identify trends, risks, opportunities and actionable insights . Partner with Finance and cross-functional teams to support business and strategic decisions . Take ownership of assigned Finance projects and deliverables . Gain exposure to areas such as Commercial Finance, Accounting, Internal Controls, Tax and Treasury , depending on the assignment. Support improvements in financial processes, reporting and controls. Communicate financial insights clearly to relevant stakeholders. Demonstrate ownership, integrity and sound financial stewardship . Job

Qualifications

CA Intermediate qualified in the first attempt. Eligible to undertake the 1-year CA internship/articleship as per applicable ICAI requirements. Strong academic record. Strong analytical, financial and problem-solving skills . Good written and verbal English communication skills. Strong interpersonal and collaboration skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities and take ownership of deliverables. Strong interest in Finance, business and decision-making . Demonstrated initiative, learning agility and willingness to take on challenging assignments. What We Offer Meaningful Finance responsibilities from Day 1. Exposure to real business and financial decision-making. Continuous coaching and mentoring from experienced Finance professionals. Opportunities to work with Finance and cross-functional teams. On-the-job learning to develop technical Finance, analytical, business partnering and leadership skills . Job Schedule Full time Job Number R000162371 Job Segmentation Internships
