Associate, Marketing Technology

Elastic · Bengaluru

About the role

Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI.

What is The Role

We need a Marketing Technology Specialist for our APJ region. You will be the main contact for regional marketing teams. You will make sure they can use our tools for campaigns, events, and webinars.

Your work will cover three main areas

Fix technical issues quickly.

Set up virtual events, live events, and mail campaigns.

Train users on our platforms so they can do tasks on their own.

What You Will Be Doing

Regional Contact: Be the main tech contact for the APJ region. Answer questions and fix system problems.

Events & Campaigns: Build and assist with virtual events and live events. You will be using tools such as ON24, Splash, Cvent, and Sendoso.

User Training: Teach regional teams how to use our tools. Show them best practices so they can work independently .

System Setup: Learn our different marketing tools, like Demandbase and Adobe Experience Manager. Work with the Marketo and Salesforce teams. Make sure data moves correctly between all systems.

Global Teamwork: Meet with global peers. Spot common issues in your region, share solutions, and create standard processes.

What You Bring

Hands-on experience with major marketing tools. You need to know event platforms (ON24, Splash, Cvent), giftin tools (Sendoso), and content systems (Adobe Experience Manager).

Knowledge of Marketo and Salesforce. You must know how outside tools send data to them.

Problem-solving skills. You like finding out why a process stopped or why data did not sync between systems.
