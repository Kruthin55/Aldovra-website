SDR Intern

Weekday · Bengaluru

About the role

SDR Intern | Weekday AI Role overview Remote | 6-Month Paid Internship 11 AM – 7 PM (flexible) | 6 days/week Stipend: ₹15,000 fixed + ₹5,000 performance incentive Learn Sales the Way It Actually Happens. At Weekday AI, you won't spend your internship shadowing someone else. You'll be on the phone from week one. As a Sales Development Representative (SDR) Intern on our Contingency team , you'll reach out to companies, start conversations with decision-makers, and book demos for our recruitment-as-a-service offering. Every demo you book is a direct contribution to the company's revenue pipeline You don't need to come in knowing sales. If you communicate well, can handle a "no" and keep going, and want to get better every day, we'll teach you the rest About Weekday AI Weekday AI is building a better way for companies to hire engineering talent. Our platform helps companies find high-quality, peer-recommended candidates through intelligent sourcing, automated outreach, and instant reference checks. Through our Contingency service, we work as an extension of a company's hiring team and deliver vetted candidates for its open roles. We're a lean, fast-moving, Y Combinator-backed startup where people take ownership early and see the impact of their work What You'll Do Make outbound calls to prospective customers, about 100 dials a day. Have first conversations with founders, hiring managers, and HR leaders about their hiring needs. Pitch Weekday's Contingency service clearly and briefly. Book qualified demos for the sales team, with a target of 2 a day. Handle objections and follow up with prospects until a meeting is set. Keep your activity, conversations, and pipeline up to date What You'll Learn Cold calling: how to open a conversation with a stranger and earn the next two minutes Communication: how to speak clearly and confidently with decision-makers Objection handling: how to respond to "not interested," "send me an email," and "we already have a vendor." Pipeline discipline: how volume, consistency, and follow-ups turn into results Resilience: how to stay sharp and motivated in a role with clear daily targets These skills are a strong foundation for careers in sales, business development, account management, growth, and entrepreneurship. Who We're Looking For You don't need a perfect resume or previous sales experience. We're looking for someone who: Communicates clearly and confidently on the phone Enjoys talking to people and doesn't mind rejection Is target-driven and enjoys measurable goals Is consistent and disciplined about daily activity Learns quickly and acts on feedback has recently completed a Bachelor's degree, preferably 2025/2026 graduates Prior sales or calling experience is a bonus, not a requirement. What matters most is your communication, energy, and willingness to put in the reps Why Weekday? Real conversations. You'll talk to real decision-makers at real companies every day. Real impact. Every demo you book feeds directly into revenue. Real learning. You'll learn by doing, with coaching from the people who do it every day. Real rewards. On top of the fixed stipend, you'll earn a performance incentive for hitting your targets. If you want an internship where you learn fast, own clear targets, and build skills that stay with you long after it ends, apply. Weekday AI | Y Combinator-backed
