Developer Advocate

Salesforce · Hyderabad

About the role

To get the best candidate experience, please consider applying for a maximum of 3 roles within 12 months to ensure you are not duplicating efforts. Job Category Sales Job Details About Salesforce Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together. Here, ambition meets action. Tech meets trust. And innovation isn’t a buzzword — it’s a way of life. The world of work as we know it is changing and we're looking for Trailblazers who are passionate about bettering business and the world through AI, driving innovation, and keeping Salesforce's core values at the heart of it all. Ready to level-up your career at the company leading workforce transformation in the agentic era? You’re in the right place! Agentforce is the future of AI, and you are the future of Salesforce. Who We Are Trailblazer Relations APAC is a team of creative, highly technical advocates, community builders, and program leaders. We inspire, enable, engage, and support the ever-growing Trailblazer community across Asia Pacific. We help Trailblazers succeed on the Salesforce Platform and use cutting-edge technologies like AIforce, Agentforce and Data Cloud. Our work goes well beyond developer advocacy. We grow and support Trailblazer Community Groups, MVPs, and Group Leaders. We run community events and executive engagements. We also partner with customers, partners, and local ecosystems across the region. For developers, we write blog posts , produce video content, host workshops , and engage in person and virtually at Salesforce and third-party events. We work closely with Trailblazer Community, Architect Evangelists, marketing, product management, engineering, and product marketing. We are the "voice of the Trailblazer" in APAC, bringing regional perspectives into the company to shape the Salesforce experience, especially around AI and data. Who You Are We're looking for an experienced technologist who embraces enterprise software development and is passionate about the transformative power of AI and data. You're a creative thinker who understands when to choose visual builder tools or when building with code from scratch. You're equally comfortable working with front-end and back-end programming languages, APIs, and are eager to explore new paradigms in AI-driven development. You love going broad and deep, creating inspirational and technical open source demos, and complementing those demos with accessible written and video content. You're comfortable sharing your knowledge in front of a camera, and you're looking forward to domestic (sometimes international) travel to present to live audiences of hundreds of people. You Are Excited About This Role Because You Will: Develop and maintain your expertise with the Salesforce Platform, AIforce, Agentforce, and Data Cloud, and create content that integrates them with other Salesforce technologies. Build end-to-end apps and integrations that support the programs and initiatives run by the team. Create code samples, prototypes, and visually compelling demos that inspire Trailblazers and help them succeed when adopting Salesforce, with a focus on AI-powered solutions, intelligent agents, and scalable data architectures. Write technical content and produce on-demand and live-streamed videos that showcase practical applications across the Salesforce portfolio, tailored to APAC audiences and languages where relevant. Support virtual and in-person events , including Trailblazer Community Group meetups, with technical talks, demonstrations, and workshops. Work with Trailblazer Community Group Leaders, MVPs, and other community members to help them grow and succeed. Work closely with product, engineering, and other cross-functional teams as the "voice of the Trailblazer" in APAC. We're Excited About You, Because You Have: 5+ years of experience implementing on the Salesforce Platform, with a creative technical mindset. Deep understanding of and passion for the needs of enterprise developers who build on Salesforce, especially when using AI and data for business solutions. Proven technical expertise in web applications, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Apex, and APIs, and a willingness to learn and apply new technologies related to AIforce, Agentforce and Data Cloud. Certifications or equivalent hands-on technical experience in Salesforce development. Excellent presentation, interpersonal, and communication skills, and experience making complex technical concepts accessible and understandable. A solid understanding of how quickly and constantly the ways people consume content and share their experiences are shifting. Drive to be a self-starter and, at the same time, a highly collaborative team player. Passion for learning and working with new technologies. A strategic mind and strong problem-solving and analytical skills. Ability to travel up to 30%. Unleash Your Potential When you join Salesforce, you’ll be limitless in all areas of your life. Our benefits and resources support you to find balance and be your best , and our AI agents accelerate your impact so you can do your best . Together, we’ll bring the power of Agentforce to organizations of all sizes and deliver amazing experiences that customers love. Apply today to not only shape the future — but to redefine what’s possible — for yourself, for AI, and the world.
