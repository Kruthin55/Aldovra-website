Salesforce Developer - Production Support

Caterpillar · Bengaluru

About the role

Career Area: Technology, Digital and Data Job Description: Your Work Shapes the World at Caterpillar Inc. When you join Caterpillar, you're joining a global team who cares not just about the work we do – but also about each other. We are the makers, problem solvers, and future world builders who are creating stronger, more sustainable communities. We don't just talk about progress and innovation here – we make it happen, with our customers, where we work and live. Together, we are building a better world, so we can all enjoy living in it.

Responsibilities

This is a delivery leadership role, with the following responsibilities: Designing and developing custom solutions using Salesforce platform technologies such as Apex, Visualforce, Lightning Aura and Lightning web components. Configuring and customizing Salesforce features, including workflow rules, validation rules, process builder, Flows and custom objects. Complete Data Migration, Security and Maintenance. Developing and implementing integrations between Salesforce and other systems using SOAP/REST services. Participating in requirements gathering and analysis with business stakeholders and translating business needs into technical solutions. Writing clean, efficient, and maintainable code and following best practices for development and deployment. Creating and maintaining technical documentation, such as design documents, testplans, and user guides. Troubleshooting and resolving issues in Salesforce solutions, including identifying and resolving bugs, performance issues, and data quality issues. Collaborating with other developers, business analysts, and project managers to ensure successful delivery of projects. Conduct research into clients’ use of Salesforce, producing a report of findings with recommended steps and a roadmap for Salesforce solutions, Review solutions and recommend improvements, ensure success and acceptance criteria are defined for each story, Describe and illustrate designs with appropriate justifications, ensure documentation of the steps for testing, Collaborative project and workstream planning guide the internal team for any technical help (Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, B2B, CPQ, Experience Cloud). Review solutions and recommend improvements. SFDC Technical Lead (5+ Yrs Experience)
