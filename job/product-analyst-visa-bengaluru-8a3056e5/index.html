Product Analyst

Visa · Bengaluru

About the role

About Us Visa is a world leader in payments technology, facilitating transactions between consumers, merchants, financial institutions and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories, dedicated to uplifting everyone, everywhere by being the best way to pay and be paid. At Visa, you'll have the opportunity to create impact at scale — tackling meaningful challenges, growing your skills and seeing your contributions impact lives around the world. Join Visa and do work that matters – to you, to your community, and to the world. Progress starts with you. Job Description Role Summary: The AI Platform Product team is building a scalable, enterprise‑grade platforms that enables teams across the organization to develop, deploy, operate, and govern AI/ML solutions at global scale. The team focuses on providing reliable, secure, and reusable platform capabilities that support the full AI/ML lifecycle - from data and feature enablement to model training, deployment, monitoring, and optimization. The team partners closely with engineering, data science, product, and business teams to ensure AI capabilities can be delivered efficiently and responsibly across multiple use cases, including fraud, risk, authorization, and ecosystem protection. We are seeking a hands‑on Product Analyst with experience across AI/ML platforms, data‑driven product development, and GenAI prototyping. This role is ideal for someone who is technically grounded, product‑oriented, and adaptable, and can work across multiple problem spaces as priorities evolve. Key

Responsibilities

Drive and support product requirements for AI/ML and Generative AI platform capabilities, partnering closely with engineering, data science, and MLOps teams. Translate business, data science, and platform needs into well-defined features, user stories, technical requirements, workflows, and acceptance criteria. Contribute to platform capabilities across AI/ML lifecycle management, Generative AI enablement, feature engineering and feature platforms, developer tooling, and observability. Develop a strong understanding of end-to-end AI/ML workflows, including data preparation, feature engineering, model development, inference, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring. Apply hands-on technical skills using Python, SQL, APIs, notebooks, and AI/ML tools to analyze data, validate solutions, troubleshoot issues, and develop prototypes. Support Generative AI capabilities involving Large Language Models (LLMs), RAG, embeddings, evaluation, and agentic AI patterns, working with engineering teams to translate prototypes and use cases into scalable platform capabilities. Work with technologies such as Databricks and cloud environments to understand AI/ML workloads and incorporate relevant technical considerations into product requirements and solution trade-offs. Develop an understanding of feature engineering and feature platforms, including how features are computed, transformed, stored, discovered, reused, and consumed by AI/ML applications. Understand large-scale data processing across cloud and on-premises environments, including batch and real-time/streaming workloads, as they relate to AI/ML platform capabilities. Conduct product and technical acceptance validation prior to release and support successful adoption of AI/ML platform capabilities by user and stakeholder teams. Collaborate effectively with Product, Engineering, Data Science, MLOps, Infrastructure, Security, and Governance teams within an Agile environment to accelerate delivery and time-to-market. Own assigned platform initiatives from requirements definition through delivery, release, and post-launch validation. Create and maintain product and technical documentation, including requirements, workflows, release notes, technical guidance, FAQs, and runbooks, to support stakeholders and platform adoption. Remain flexible and adaptable, supporting multiple product initiatives and evolving AI/ML platform priorities. Visa requires at least 3 days in office, expectations of these days will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager.

Qualifications

Basic

Qualifications

2+ years of relevant work experience with a bachelor's degree, or 1+ years of relevant work experience with an Advanced degree (e.g., Master's, MBA). Preferred

Qualifications

2+ years of experience in Product Management, Product Analysis, Technical Product Management, Business Analysis, AI/ML platforms, software development, data science/engineering, or a related technical role. Good understanding of AI/ML concepts and the model lifecycle, including data preparation, feature engineering, model development, inference, deployment, and monitoring. Understanding of Generative AI technologies, including Large Language Models (LLMs), RAG, embeddings, evaluation approaches, and agentic AI. Hands-on experience with Python and/or SQL for data analysis, automation, prototyping, or AI/ML use cases. Exposure to Databricks and/or cloud environments AI/ML workloads. Understanding of large-scale data processing concepts, including batch and real-time/streaming processing in support of AI/ML use cases. Exposure to feature engineering and feature platforms/stores, with an understanding of how ML features are created, managed, discovered, reused, and consumed. Hands-on exposure to Generative AI technologies such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face, or equivalent technologies, through professional work, academic projects, or prototypes. Familiarity with MLOps, model and platform monitoring, observability, or production support is desirable. Familiarity with software development lifecycle methodologies, Git/version control, Agile delivery models, and Scrum-based development teams. Experience working with cross-functional teams to launch and support technical products or AI/ML platform capabilities within complex organizations. Exposure to payments, fraud, risk management, decisioning systems, or real-time AI/ML systems is highly desirable. Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, documentation, and stakeholder management skills.
