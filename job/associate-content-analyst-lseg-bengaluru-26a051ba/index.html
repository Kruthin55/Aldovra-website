Associate Content Analyst

LSEG · Bengaluru

About the role

Good knowledge of Financial Data like Financial Data (balance sheet / income statement/ cash flow) Deep understanding of Content set like Fundamentals, Events and Main Statements like Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow etc Processes data in an accurate and timely manner using the knowledge of the tools and financial markets - Uses the approved sources to identify the data, convert / add value to the data, or use the data as is defined in the collection policy​ Understanding of Sql, python and Copilot and be able to use for continuous improvement. Ability to handle complexity and think logically with incredible attention to details Communicates and works with internal and external teams as required to support sourcing and collection of data Adheres to the quality metrics and prioritizes activities to support business and customer requirements ​ Supports specific projects assigned and meets or exceeds the KPIs defined Suggests process improvement ideas to improve efficiency and customer experience.
