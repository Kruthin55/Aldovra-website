Data Engineer

3M · Bengaluru

About the role

3M has a long-standing reputation as a company committed to innovation. We provide the freedom to explore and encourage curiosity and creativity. We gain new insight from diverse thinking, and take risks on new ideas. Here, you can apply your talent in bold ways that matter. Job Description: Collaborate with Innovative 3Mers Around the World Choosing where to start and grow your career has a major impact on your professional and personal life. With a wide variety of people, global locations, technologies and products, 3M is a place where you can collaborate with other curious, creative 3Mers. The Impact You’ll Make in this Role As a Data Engineer, you will have the opportunity to tap into your curiosity and collaborate with some of the most innovative people around the world. Here, you will make an impact by: Design, develop, and maintain data pipelines, ETL/ELT workflows, and data integration solutions. Build and support trusted data products, including curated datasets, reusable data assets, semantic layers, and analytics-ready data models. Support data lake, data warehouse, and analytics platform configuration, optimization, and operations. Apply DevOps practices, including CI/CD, version control, automated testing, and deployment automation for data engineering solutions. Monitor data quality, pipeline performance, platform reliability, and production support processes. Your Skills and Expertise To set you up for success in this role from day one, 3M requires (at a minimum) the following qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or higher (completed and verified prior to start) 5+ years of experience in data engineering, data warehouse development, or analytics platform support. Experience with SQL, Python, ETL/ELT development, data modeling, and data quality practices. Databricks experience preferred, including notebook development, data engineering workflows, or Lakehouse architecture. SAP HANA & SAP ERP knowledge helpful. Experience with data platforms and tools such as Snowflake, AWS , Power BI, or similar technologies. Additional qualifications that could help you succeed even further in this role include: Strong communication and collaboration skills Excellent stakeholder management skills Ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams Learn more about 3M’s creative solutions to the world’s problems at www.3M.com or on Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn @3M.
