Cloud Engineer - AWS

Fidelity Investments · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description: Job Title – Cloud Operations Engineer / Cloud Ops Platform Support ( lead Cloud Operations from the India side) Shape the reliability, security, and performance of cloud infrastructure that powers critical business capabilities. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys improving platform stability, enabling engineering teams, and delivering efficient cloud operations on a scale. The Purpose of This Role The Cloud Operations Engineer plays an important role in maintaining and improving the cloud infrastructure that supports business applications, platform services, and engineering delivery teams. This team exists to ensure cloud environments are secure, resilient, cost-conscious, and operationally efficient. You will work closely with application development teams, infrastructure teams, security partners, architecture teams, and service providers to support day-to-day operations and long-term platform improvements. The role helps connect business needs with dependable cloud service delivery across multiple stakeholders. In addition to strong technical Cloud Operations expertise, the candidate should also demonstrate the capability to lead and coordinate the Cloud Operations function from the India side, ensuring effective stakeholder alignment, operational governance, and service delivery excellence. The Value You Deliver Supporting cloud infrastructure operations across development, test, and production environments. Monitoring platform health, availability, capacity, and operational performance to improve service reliability. Coordinating incident response, problem analysis, and service restoration for cloud-based platforms and workloads. Automating routine operational tasks to improve efficiency, consistency, and deployment quality. Managing cloud resource provisioning, access, configuration updates, and environment maintenance. Partnering with engineering and security teams to strengthen platform governance, compliance alignment, and operational readiness. Demonstrating the capability to lead and coordinate the Cloud Operations function from the India side, including stakeholder management, operational oversight, and driving service excellence across teams. Improving observability, alerting, backup, recovery, and operational documentation for business-critical services. Contributing to cost optimization efforts by reviewing usage patterns and recommending infrastructure improvements. The Skills that are Key to this role Technical You have strong experience supporting cloud environments within the AWS platform. You have hands-on experience with infrastructure automation, scripting, and configuration management. You understand monitoring, logging, alerting, and incident management practices in cloud operations. You have experience working with containers, orchestration platforms, and deployment pipelines. You are comfortable supporting Linux and/or Windows server environments in cloud-based ecosystems. You understand identity and access management, security controls, patching, and operational compliance practices. You have experience with disaster recovery, backup processes, and platform resilience planning. You have the technical depth required to support and guide Cloud Operations activities while contributing to the overall effectiveness of the function from the India side Behavioral You demonstrate the ability to take initiative and improve operational processes. You work effectively across teams and build strong working relationships with technical and non-technical partners. You communicate clearly during operational updates, issue resolution, and planning discussions. You are comfortable prioritizing work in a fast-moving environment while maintaining attention to detail. You bring a continuous learning mindset and look for ways to simplify, automate, and improve outcomes. You demonstrate the capability to lead and coordinate the Cloud Operations function from the India side, including stakeholder communication, operational oversight, and delivery alignment. You are able to influence cross-functional teams and drive operational priorities effectively in a distributed global environment. The Skills that are Good To Have for this role You have experience supporting DevOps or Site Reliability Engineering practices. You have exposure to cost management and cloud optimization tools. You are comfortable presenting operational updates, recommendations, and risk considerations to stakeholders. You work well in team-based environments and contribute to shared goals. You have effective documentation skills for processes, runbooks, and support knowledge. You have experience working in regulated or enterprise-scale environments. You have familiarity with service management tools and structured change management processes. You demonstrate the potential to take on broader operational ownership and support the leadership of the Cloud Operations function from the India side. You are comfortable coordinating with multiple stakeholders and contributing to operational decision-making in a global delivery model. How Your Work Impacts the Organization Your work helps create stable, secure, and scalable cloud environments that support business operations and application delivery. By improving operational reliability, automating support processes, and strengthening infrastructure governance, you help teams deliver services with greater confidence and efficiency. Your contributions also help reduce operational risk, improve user experience, and support long-term technology growth. In addition, your ability to lead and coordinate the Cloud Operations function from the India side will help strengthen operational ownership, improve stakeholder alignment, and enhance service delivery across global teams. The Expertise We’re Looking For 3-5 years of IT experience Graduate Relevant cloud certifications are a plus, such as AWS Certified Solutions Architect, AWS Sys-Ops Administrator Company Overview Fidelity Investments is one of the world's largest providers of financial services. Headquartered in Boston, US, Fidelity's goal is to make financial expertise broadly accessible and effective in helping people live the lives they want. Privately held for nearly 70 years, Fidelity employs 45,000 associates who are focused on the long-term success of our customers. FMR (Fidelity Management & Research) India is the Global Inhouse Center of Fidelity Investments. Headquartered at Bangalore, where operations commenced in 2003, FMR India has another fully-functional unit at Chennai.
