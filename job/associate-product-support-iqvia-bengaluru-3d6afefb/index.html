Formulates and defines systems scope and objectives through research and fact-finding combined with an understanding of applicable business systems and requirements.

Manages sprint backlog, including planning, prioritization, grooming and readiness.

Works collaboratively with users, designers and engineers to understand requirements, solution features, and slice work into vertical stories.

Ensures stories collectively result in useable software that meets the needs of the users.

Performs portions of reports and data analysis based on understanding of the function’s business context, requiring general domain knowledge and developing business experience.

Evaluates and prioritize scope trade-offs as new work emerges.

Reviews development work for quality and completeness.

Facilitates effective defect management.

Communicates status and manages stakeholder expectations when required.

Contributes to product discovery work.
