Position Overview: Boeing Digital Services Technology India is currently looking for an Associate Software Application Developer – Java Full Stack to support end-to-end software delivery within our digital aircraft maintenance product suite. This role is grounded in strong software development fundamentals, with an emphasis on leveraging modern AI-assisted tools to build cloud-native systems at the cutting edge of aerospace technology. You will work closely with Developers, Engineering, Greenfield Leadership, Data Science, and Architects to deliver product features and capabilities that keep AI and ML at the center of our solutions. You will be modernizing systems that have been running the business for years, while building new ones designed to run it for years to come. Position

Responsibilities

Building production applications from scratch and modernized legacy systems; comfortable with both greenfield and brownfield approaches, and know when to apply patterns like strangler fig or lift-and-refactor Building full-stack, production-grade applications; frontend interfaces, backend services, REST and GraphQL APIs, and the integrations that tie them together in complex enterprise environments Integrating AI, ML, or LLM capabilities directly into client applications; including agentic workflows, RAG pipelines, AI-augmented developer tooling, and intelligent automation that works reliably in production Use AI-assisted development tools (e.g., Codex) as a natural part of your daily workflow to move faster and produce higher-quality output Participate in planning, definition, and high-level design of the solution and exploration of solution alternatives and contribute to the Backlog refinements by asking relevant questions Raise any impediments/risks (technical/operational/personal) they come across and approaches Scrum Master/Technical Architect/PO accordingly to arrive at a solution Lead, Coach and contribute to the development of technical team members through consistent, empathetic, and kind guidance, mentoring and learning sessions Demonstrated ability to create positive impact on customers by developing polished, cohesive, effective, and user-friendly applications for large and complex aviation-related systems Deliver working software in accordance with expected quality standards Designs for testability: Design and evolve the system to support testability and test automation Support experimentation, monitoring, and data pipelines that ensure reliable application behavior in production Basic

(Required Skills/Experience): Bachelor’s degree is required as basic qualification. 4+ years of relevant experience in software engineering or application development Hands on knowledge in Designing and Developing full stack modules and components for various applications. This position is for a full stack developer who has excellent programming skills in the latest tools & technology frameworks like JavaScript, JQuery, Java/J2EE, Azure App Services / Functions, Microservices etc. Must have experience with Spring Boot Framework, REST based Web Services and SOA Preferred

Also, should be able to write scripts that will fire off and orchestrate the complete deployments of DEV, QA and Production environments via tools such as Chef.
