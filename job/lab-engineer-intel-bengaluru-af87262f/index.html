Lab Engineer

Intel · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Details: Job Description: This role requires regular onsite presence to fulfill essential job responsibilities. Drives lab systems engineering including the development of solutions to improve lab quality, compliance, and throughput. Drives monitoring, record keeping, and reporting for lab equipment including maintenance, service, calibration, rework, and testing activities. Creates and develops test plans for new features on each product and recommends improvements to enable automated scripting for testing and archiving of results. Drives lab execution including engineering analysis, instrumentation, data analysis, and result reporting. Resolves reported bugs and root causes issues that could impact product quality and experience. Develops plans and cost estimates based on requirements and assesses projects to analyze risks and rewards, drive procurement of equipment and other lab materials, and development of lab infrastructure. Ensures lab preparedness including bench set-up, procurement of test equipment and other lab materials, and infrastructure requirements. Collaborates with functional teams such as IT, security, and facilities to maintain a successful lab. Develops and coordinates the design, development, and implementation of new ideas, process, and systems for lab improvements needed to meet program deliverables.
