Client - Research, Summer Associate, Mumbai - India, 2027

Citi · Mumbai

About the role

You're the brains behind our work. You're ready to bring your knowledge from the classroom to the boardroom, and Citi wants to help you get there. Whether it's honing your skills or building your network, we know that success can't come without growth. Our programs equip you with the knowledge and training you need to play a valuable role on your team and establish a long-term career here. At Citi, we value internal mobility, and career growth is not a question of if, but when. Citi’s Client organization is looking for Summer Associate to join the Research team in Mumbai. Citi provides world-class products and financing solutions for corporations, governments, and institutional and retail investors through our dominant underwriting capabilities. Our global platform, broad product knowledge and on-the-ground local markets expertise coupled with complementary strengths in technology and distribution allows us to offer clients a wide range of comprehensive financial solutions. We address their needs by leveraging our products in all discipline currencies, sectors, geographies and asset classes; given the unparalleled breadth and depth of our global platform, Citi is able to consistently provide clients with innovative solutions regardless of complexity. Your time here will look something like this... -Working with a team covering a specific industry or macro area including involvement in the day-to-day responsibilities of an analyst. -Develop a thorough understanding of financial markets. Learn to identify and analyze trends with the goal of producing cutting edge research and actionable recommendations. -Learn to collect, outline and explain information giving insights that drive creative solutions. -Take on various projects the team assigns, including economic data and analysis, pricing surveys, industry trends, valuation studies and company-specific due diligence. -Create, update and maintain economic or financial spreadsheets, quantitative models and databases of assigned companies. -Refresh marketing handouts and assist with ad-hoc client requests. -Shadow Research Analysts by attending company and industry meetings as well as industry conferences. We provide you with the knowledge and skills you need to succeed. Our Summer Associate Program is 8 weeks long. You'll start the Program with a week of training, which introduces you to Citi as a whole. The training gives you: -An overview of the key processes you should know as a new Citi employee -An introduction to Global Financial Markets to help you discover the main business areas and the "ins and outs" of an investment bank; you'll also get to know Citi's products and services, as well as simulated trading and portfolio management -A chance to try out your presentation skills -Speaker series -Networking events across businesses -Access to the most senior leaders in the firm As we go through the summer together, we'll keep providing training and networking opportunities, so you explore and develop an in-depth knowledge of the markets and what it's like to work at Citi. There are plenty of internal resources to help you (and us) get the most value from your time here. We want to hear from you if... -You are a penultimate year student in any discipline, from a top academic institution working towards a competitive GPA and graduating between Dec 2026 - April 2027. Who we think will be a great fit... -A highly ethical and honest individual who is prepared to provide accurate and independent analysis -A quick learner, who assimilates facts quickly, processes a great deal of detailed information, organizes it, puts in into useful form, and remembers it well -Enthusiasm, eagerness to take off, advance quickly, and assume more responsibility -Extremely entrepreneurial, with exceptional attention to detail -A natural communicator, both verbal and written, who builds strong relationships -An ability to stay calm and work under pressure -A creative thinker who is also highly numerate -A teamwork ethic that drives success from every individual ------------------------------------------------------ Job Family Group: Research ------------------------------------------------------ Job Family: Research Analysis ------------------------------------------------------ Time Type: Full time ------------------------------------------------------ Most Relevant Skills Please see the requirements listed above. ------------------------------------------------------ Other Relevant Skills For complementary skills, please see above and/or contact the recruiter. ------------------------------------------------------ Citi is an equal opportunity employer, and qualified candidates will receive consideration without regard to their race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, status as a protected veteran, or any other characteristic protected by law.
