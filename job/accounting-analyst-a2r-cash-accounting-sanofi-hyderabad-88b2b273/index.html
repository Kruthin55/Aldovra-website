Accounting Analyst - A2R Cash Accounting · Location: Hyderabad

About the job

Global Finance Services (GFS), one of the pillars of Sanofi’s Business Operations organization, is setting up a Global Hub in Hyderabad to provide various Finance services. The Accounting Analyst - A2R - Cash Accounting reports to Team Lead- Cash Accounting and may be assigned a specific sub-process. He / She works closely with Finance (Controlling, Treasury, Tax, Internal Control), other BO departments, Customer Service and the A2R team. Our Hubs are a crucial part of how we innovate, improving performance across every Sanofi department and providing a springboard for the amazing work we do. Build a career and you can be part of transforming our business while helping to change millions of lives. Ready? As Accounting Analyst – Cash Accounting within our Account to Report, you’ll be working in line to Deliver treasury/cash accounting services to the Group from Hyderabad. This will include bank reconciliations, clearing of transitory accounts etc. and participate in continuous improvement initiatives. We are an innovative global healthcare company with one purpose: to chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives. We’re also a company where you can flourish and grow your career, with countless opportunities to explore, make connections with people, and stretch the limits of what you thought was possible. Ready to get started? Main responsibilities: · Operation: Deliver Cash accounting services. Reconcile A2R GL transit accounts, book manual postings to clear them. Perform bank reconciliations and book related adjustments. Book transactions relating to financial instruments, debts, and loans. Calculate Forex net positions. Manage month end closure relating to treasury accounting and prepare related reporting · Contribute to business reviews. Execute in compliance of processes, transactions and reports with Sanofi accounting policies, internal control guidelines and audit / SOX requirements · Complete all activities as per the global Core Model and operational KPIs. Develop expert level knowledge of the activities in scope as well as local knowledge of entities in scope · Build, document and transfer A2R Core Model process knowledge (Level 6/7) relating to treasury accounting activities. Pro-actively anticipate operational issues and coach junior analysts. · Continuous improvement: Identify process improvement opportunities through elimination of redundant activities. Participate in process automation initiatives and ensure seamless adoption and transition. About you · Experience : First hands on experience in a GL accounting role. · Functional : Understanding of IFRS and local GAAPs (essential) · Broad understanding of GL Accounting, monthly closure and reporting activities, bank reconciliation process (essential) · Good business acumen (advantageous) · Strong problem solving, deductive and analytical skills (advantageous) · Technical : Computer literate and familiar with accounting applications, as e.g. SAP FICO (advantageous) · Knowledge of Microsoft Office and expertise of MS Excel (essential) · Interpersonal : Ability to interact with internal and external clients in a professional manner (essential) · Team player, able to work collaboratively transversally and with A2R front line teams with an end-to-end mindset (essential) · Good communication skills (advantageous) · Attentive to customer needs and feedback (advantageous) · Leadership : Organized and structured to execute tasks as per deadlines (essential) · Attentive to detail and works with precision (advantageous) · Action oriented, delivery driven, change agent (advantageous) · Education : Graduate degree in Accounting or Finance · Languages : Fluent in English (essential) Why choose us?
