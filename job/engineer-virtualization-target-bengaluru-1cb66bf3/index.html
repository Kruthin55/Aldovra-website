Engineer - Virtualization

Target · Bengaluru

About the role

About us: Working at Target means helping all families discover the joy of everyday life. We bring that vision to life through our values and culture. Learn more about Target here . Position Overview: As an Engineer – Compute Virtualization, you will join the engineering team responsible for building, supporting, and improving compute and virtualization capabilities. Approximately 6 months or more of apprenticeship, internship, training, or equivalent hands-on experience in infrastructure, software engineering, cloud, virtualization, or related technologies. You should understand core concepts across compute infrastructure, virtualization, operating systems, networking, storage, automation, and cloud technologies. A successful engineer progressively builds a strong foundation in compute virtualization and infrastructure, becomes more confident troubleshooting technical problems, contributes to automation and engineering work, takes increasing ownership of assigned responsibilities, and becomes a dependable member of the team. We value curiosity, accountability, teamwork, and a strong desire to learn and grow. What You Will Do:

Build practical knowledge of compute, server virtualization, and infrastructure through deployment, configuration, testing, and support.

Troubleshoot Linux/Ubuntu, virtualization, networking, infrastructure, and application issues with guidance from experienced engineers.

Gain hands-on experience with Ansible, infrastructure APIs, automation, and configuration management.

Develop Go engineering practices, including Git, code reviews, testing, debugging, documentation, and design discussions.

Use metrics, logs, dashboards, and alerts to monitor services and participate in production support.

Document and share technical findings, procedures, troubleshooting steps, and lessons learned.

Collaborate across teams, communicate progress and blockers, and take on increasingly complex responsibilities as skills grow. Technical

Qualifications

Familiarity with some of the following areas is expected

Server virtualization concepts and terminology, including virtual machines, hypervisors, hosts, clusters, CPU, memory, and resource allocation.

Linux, preferably Ubuntu, including common commands and basic administration and troubleshooting.

Fundamental compute, storage, and networking concepts, including IP addressing, DNS, HTTP/HTTPS, load balancing, high availability, and persistent versus ephemeral compute.

Git and basic software-development practices; exposure to at least one low level programming language. Go/Golang experience is an advantage, not a requirement.

REST APIs; familiarity with gRPC is an advantage.

Automation or configuration-management concepts; exposure to Ansible is preferred.

Testing concepts such as unit, integration, and automated testing.

Cloud infrastructure concepts and how compute resources are provisioned and managed.

Container technologies such as Docker, containerd, or Kubernetes, and monitoring/logging tools such as Grafana, InfluxDB, or the ELK stack, are advantages. What We Are Looking For:

Curiosity & learning: You investigate how systems work, ask thoughtful questions, learn from experienced engineers, and apply feedback.

Accountability: You take ownership of assigned tasks, communicate when blocked or when help is needed, follow through on commitments, and learn from mistakes.

Teamwork: You collaborate respectfully with engineers and partner teams, share what you know, learn from others, and contribute where needed.

Problem solving: You approach problems logically, gather evidence from logs, documentation, error messages, and system behavior, and break larger problems into manageable pieces.

Communication: You communicate technical information clearly in written and verbal form, document what you learn, and openly share progress, questions, and blockers. Education & Experience:

Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field, or equivalent practical/industry experience. Know More About Us here: Life at Target : https://india.target.com/
