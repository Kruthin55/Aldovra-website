IT Developer

Medtronic · Hyderabad

About the role

Careers that change lives start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a Mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact. A Day in the Life This role is part of the IT function supporting global web application platforms that enable critical business processes and customer-facing digital experiences. The team is responsible for the design, development, enhancement, and support of enterprise web applications hosted on cloud-based infrastructure, with a focus on scalable, secure, and compliant solutions. The position partners closely with Information Technology Business Analysts, Project Managers, business stakeholders, and technical teams to deliver web application capabilities that support global and cross-functional business needs. The IT Developer is responsible for designing, developing, testing, implementing, and supporting enterprise web applications with a focus on Oracle database technologies and modern web development frameworks. This role partners with business and technical stakeholders to translate requirements into scalable, secure, and user-focused solutions while supporting all phases of the software development lifecycle.

Responsibilities

may include the following and other duties may be assigned: Design, develop, test, and deploy web application functionality that supports global business processes. Partner with business stakeholders, Information Technology Business Analysts, and end users to gather requirements and define technical solutions. Translate functional specifications into technical designs and development deliverables. Develop and maintain front-end and back-end application components using modern web technologies and frameworks. Collaborate with cross-functional development teams to deliver integrated and scalable solutions. Create and maintain technical documentation, including functional specifications, design documents, and architecture artifacts. Support cloud-hosted applications and contribute to application performance, reliability, and security improvements. Ensure compliance with information protection, data governance, and system compliance requirements throughout the development lifecycle. Required Knowledge and Experience: Bachelor's degree and 5+ years of experience in web application development. Experience with Java, JavaScript, J2EE, SQL, HTML, and modern web development frameworks. Experience with React, Angular, Next.js, Spring Framework, and Hibernate. Experience developing, testing, and deploying end-to-end web applications. Experience working with Oracle databases and cloud-hosted application environments. Experience with Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Oracle Relational Database Service (RDS). Experience with WebLogic and/or Apache Tomcat application servers. Experience with Groovy, Grails, or additional web development technologies. Experience creating functional specifications, technical specifications, and solution documentation. Nice to Haves: Experience supporting system integrations, interfaces, and cross-functional data flows within enterprise environments. Physical Job
