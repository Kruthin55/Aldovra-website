Commercial Lending Servicing Representative

Wells Fargo · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Commercial Lending Servicing Representative. This role supports accurate and timely loan servicing while helping deliver reliable customer outcomes. The role applies commercial lending knowledge to resolve servicing needs, maintain strong controls, and contribute to operational excellence, risk management, and customer-focused service. In this role, you will: Complete standard and moderately complex commercial loan servicing activities accurately, within established service levels and assigned authority. Review loan instructions, approvals, and supporting documentation for completeness, accuracy, and adherence to established requirements. Process loan maintenance and servicing requests, perform reconciliations, and resolve routine discrepancies while escalating complex or high-risk matters. Maintain complete and accurate loan records, approvals, and supporting evidence in accordance with bank policies and controls. Provide timely updates, collaborate with internal partners, and meet defined quality, productivity, risk, service, and confidentiality expectations. Required

Qualifications

1+ year of Commercial Loan Servicing support experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education 1+ year of experience in banking, financial services, commercial lending, loan operations, or loan servicing. 1+ year of experience supporting banking or loan operations across the loan lifecycle Desired

Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in commerce, Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant experience. Exposure to commercial lending, loan servicing, reconciliations, banking controls, risk management, or customer service. Ability to apply business rules, exercise sound judgment, and resolve routine operational issues within established procedures. Job Expectations: Candidates must be flexible and willing to work night shifts or other assigned schedules based on project and business requirements.
