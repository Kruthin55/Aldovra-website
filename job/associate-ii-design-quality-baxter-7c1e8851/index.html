Here, you will find more than just a job—you will find purpose and pride. Your Role at Baxter As a Quality Subject Matter Expert (SME), you will provide quality oversight and strategic support for formulation development, technology transfer, new product development, sustaining engineering activities, and post-approval products. You will ensure compliance, regulatory expectations, and Baxter quality standards while partnering with cross-functional teams to support product development, commercialization, and lifecycle management. What You'll Be Doing Serve as the Quality SME for formulation development, technology transfer, and post-approval products, providing expert guidance to internal and external stakeholders. Review and approve development and manufacturing documentation, including Master Formula Records (MFRs), protocols and reports, stability programs, decision notes, process engineering documents, and submission-related documentation. Review and approve Product Development Lifecycle Management (PDLM) deliverables, including risk management files, human factors studies, accelerated aging studies, distribution studies, design verification, and design validation documents. Review and approve Product Introduction Management (PIM) documents to support launch readiness activities. Review and approve Product Development Reports (PDRs) and agency query responses for regulatory submissions. Review and approve Quality Management System (QMS) records, including change controls, investigations, deviations, and related quality documentation. Partner with Project Management, Regulatory Affairs, Formulation Development, Sustaining Engineering, and Contract Manufacturing Organizations (CMOs) to resolve quality and compliance-related issues. What You'll Bring Bachelor's or Master's degree in Pharmacy, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Chemistry, Life Sciences, Biomedical Engineering, or a related discipline. Strong 8+ years’ experience in Quality Assurance, Product Development, Formulation Development, Technology Transfer, or New Product Development within the pharmaceutical, medical device, or combination-product industry. Experience reviewing and approving technical, quality, validation, qualification, and regulatory documentation. Experience conducting internal audits, compliance assessments, and remediation activities. Excellent collaboration, stakeholder management, communication, and problem-solving skills.
