Product User Research, Intern

Sarvam AI · Bengaluru

About the role

ABOUT SARVAM AI Sarvam AI is building India's sovereign AI stack, spanning foundational models, infrastructure and applications. Our mission is to make powerful AI accessible and useful for India, building technology that works across languages, industries and real-world use cases. As we continue to build and scale our products, understanding how people interact with them becomes increasingly important. We're looking for a curious and research-oriented intern to help us understand our users better and shape the experiences we build. THE ROLE As a Product User Research Intern, you'll work closely with our product, design and engineering teams to understand user behaviour, identify friction points and uncover opportunities to improve our products. You'll interact directly with developers, product managers and founders, study how they use Sarvam's products, and translate your findings into actionable insights that influence product and design decisions. This is an opportunity to work on a new user research function at Sarvam, helping establish research practices and processes from the ground up. You'll also get ownership of a primary product area and the opportunity to see your research translate into real product improvements. WHAT YOU'LL WORK ON - Understand our users: Conduct user interviews, surveys and usability studies with developers, PMs and founders to understand their needs, behaviours and challenges. - Improve product experiences: Research user journeys across Sarvam's developer console, playground, API documentation and applications. Identify friction points across onboarding, API key creation, first API calls, billing and other key workflows. - Study developer journeys: Analyse the developer funnel, from sign-up to making the first API call and eventually becoming a paying user. Understand where users drop off and, more importantly, why. - Test and evaluate designs: Conduct usability testing on existing interfaces and new prototypes, run preference and A/B tests, and benchmark experiences against other developer products. - Turn research into insights: Analyse qualitative and quantitative data, identify patterns and present clear recommendations to product, design, growth and support teams. - Build the research practice: Help establish research playbooks, interview templates, participant recruitment processes and a research repository that can scale with the team. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR - Strong curiosity about user behaviour and a genuine interest in understanding why people use products the way they do. - Comfortable speaking with users, conducting interviews and asking thoughtful, unbiased questions. - Technical fluency — you should be comfortable reading API documentation, making basic API calls using tools like Postman, curl or Python, and understanding what might have gone wrong when an API fails. - Comfortable working with data, analysing spreadsheets and interpreting product funnels or dashboards. - Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to translate research findings into clear insights and recommendations. - Familiarity with AI tools and developer products, and an interest in exploring how they can improve research workflows. GOOD TO HAVE - Experience with SQL or product analytics tools such as Mixpanel, Amplitude or Metabase. - Familiarity with user research methodologies, usability testing and survey design. - Experience reviewing Figma prototypes or conducting user interviews. - Previous research projects, internships or personal projects involving products you've built or shipped. - Fluency in one or more Indian languages. WHO CAN APPLY? - Final-year students or graduates who have completed their studies within the last 12 months. - Candidates from engineering, psychology, economics, design or social science backgrounds. - Individuals who can commit to a full-time, 6-month, in-office internship in Bengaluru. WHAT YOU'LL GET - The opportunity to help build Sarvam's user research function from the ground up. - Direct exposure to product, design and engineering teams working on India's sovereign AI ecosystem. - Hands-on experience researching developer products and real-world AI applications. - Ownership of meaningful research projects with findings that directly influence product decisions. - An opportunity to work closely with experienced product leaders and learn how research translates into better products. - Potential for a pre-placement offer (PPO) based on performance.
