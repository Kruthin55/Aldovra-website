Fitout Executive

JLL · Mumbai

About the role

Tailored residential property management, with Brightstone. Managing housing complexes, residential townships and residential communities requires subject matter expertise, customised services and a commitment to creating a seamless yet enjoyable living experience. At Brightstone - we are setting a new standard for excellence in residential property management, focused on enhancing resident satisfaction and property value for housing societies nationwide. Position: Fit-out Executive, Mumbai Business: Property and Asset Management, Mumbai What this job involves You will be responsible to ensure all necessary utilities are being given to the occupants/tenants. You will be responsible for all drawing submissions and approvals to be maintained for fitout work. The contractor will coordinate with you for all minor execution issues. Building maintenance and upkeep will be one of your KRA. Your key deliverables will be: Maintain records of all building drawing. Make sure all instruction / guidelines are being followed as per the fitout manual. Provide necessary permits (Night work, hot work, height work). Coordinate for all necessary security arrangements for execution of fitout activities. Monitor and control work in progress as per approved plan by the client. Ensure completion of Snagging and De-snagging work of flats / Common area. Ensure all safety requirements are being followed at the site. Advise contractor on minor execution issues. Ensure that all work meets client design control specifications. Daily visit to ongoing fit-out work for checking of civil work layout. Working closely with management to help improve and maintain the building in clean and maintenance free condition. Meet owners who are about to start the fit-out work to resolve queries regarding the working procedures, instruction and for material handling as per approved drawings and other documents related to fit out work and operation activities. Maintain a fitout tracker. Providing the clients with suitable energy conservation plans. Coordinate and maintain the as-built drawing and transition document library. Work closely with the regional RO / HO / SME/ Training teams and ensure closures of all required reports. Facilitate the services under the Slogan “No Safety No Work”. Client: You will be working on Narang Privado, which is a residential , located at Thane. Reporting: You will be working with our operations team and reporting to the Property Manager. Location: On-site –Thane, MH Scheduled Weekly Hours: 48 If this job description resonates with you, we encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet all of the requirements. We’re interested in getting to know you and what you bring to the table! At Brightstone, we’re committed to redefine residential property management across India through innovation, expertise and our drive for excellence. Our people are our greatest asset when it comes to achieving this – professionals who bring passion and precision to every aspect of residential property management.
