About the Team The People & Purpose Reporting Services Analyst will be a key member of the People Operations team, providing Workday Reporting support for all employees Join our People & Purpose Reporting Services team, part of People Operations, where we help every employee and manager across Workday get the data they need. We work side-by-side with our People & Purpose Centers of Excellence — Recruiting, Talent Experience, Total Rewards, HR Partners, Learning, Compliance, Belonging and Diversity, and Global Mobility — to close gaps in data and build reports that help our leaders make informed, real-time decisions. We also build self-service tools so our partners can find answers faster on their own. If you enjoy connecting the dots across many parts of HR and turning complex data into something people can act on, you'll feel right at home here. About the Role As a Reporting Analyst , you will be the person people turn to when they need HR data they can rely on, whether that's building a new report, updating an existing one, or digging into a one-off question. You will own high-impact, ad-hoc reporting requests from start to finish, helping requesters shape raw data into a clear story they can bring to their own audience. This role touches nearly every area of HR, including recruiting, compensation, diversity, learning, talent, audits, compliance, and transfers, so you will build broad knowledge of how HR data connects across the business. You will also look for ways to make reporting simpler and more enjoyable for everyone who relies on it. In this role, you will: Respond to HR data requests spanning recruiting, compensation, diversity, learning, talent, audits, compliance, and transfers Be responsible for audits requested by any team across the business, ensuring the underlying data is accurate, complete, and compliant Build new reports and update or troubleshoot existing ones in Workday Provide attentive, high-touch support for high-impact, ad-hoc reporting requests, helping requesters interpret and present their data Apply strong data security, accuracy, governance, and standardization practices to every report you deliver Prioritize and manage a high volume of requests with competing deadlines Create self-service resources that help HR partners find answers faster on their own About You Basic

Qualifications
