Connector Design Engineer – Data Center Materials

3M · Bengaluru

About the role

3M has a long-standing reputation as a company committed to innovation. We provide the freedom to explore and encourage curiosity and creativity. We gain new insight from diverse thinking, and take risks on new ideas. Here, you can apply your talent in bold ways that matter. Job Description: Collaborate with Innovative 3Mers Around the World Choosing where to start and grow your career has a major impact on your professional and personal life, so it’s equally important you know that the company that you choose to work at, and its leaders, will support and guide you. With a wide variety of people, global locations, technologies and products, 3M is a place where you can collaborate with other curious, creative 3Mers. This position provides an opportunity to transition from other private, public, government or military experience to a 3M career. The Impact You’ll Make in this Role As a Connector Design Engineer you will lead the design and development of next-generation optical connector solutions that bring 3M innovation to AI, cloud, networking, and data center applications. You will drive product architecture and technology differentiation across EBO optical interconnect, TwinAx copper interconnect, and broader 3M value add solutions, while influencing technology roadmaps and strengthening the technical capabilities of our global team. Here, you will make an impact by: Leading development of advanced high-speed I/O (HSIO) connector products, including TwinAx copper and EBO optical interconnect, from concept through qualification and production launch. Developing product architectures, specifications, design objectives, and validation plans that leverage 3M material science to differentiate performance. Solving complex design, manufacturing, reliability, and customer application challenges in partnership with data center ecosystem customers. Collaborating with Product Management, Signal Integrity, Application Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, and key customers to deliver on customer commitments. Driving innovation and technology differentiation in high-speed connector solutions while contributing to technology roadmaps, intellectual property, and strategic initiatives. Mentoring engineers and elevating the technical capabilities of our global engineering organization. Your Skills and Expertise To set you up for success in this role from day one, 3M requires (at a minimum) the following qualifications: Bachelor's degree or higher (completed and verified prior to start) with Twelve (12) years of mechanical engineering experience developing connectors, electrical components, or electromechanical products OR - Master's degree with Eight (8) years of mechanical engineering experience developing connectors, electrical components, or electromechanical products OR - PhD with Five (5) years of mechanical engineering experience developing connectors, electrical components, or electromechanical products Additional qualifications that could help you succeed even further in this role include: Knowledge of high-speed I/O connectors, TwinAx cable assemblies, or optical interconnect technologies and system architectures, including high-speed electrical testing and connector performance. Expert-level 3D CAD skills with extensive electromechanical product design experience (Creo preferred). Advanced proficiency with FEA tools and simulation, including thermal management and thermal simulation experience. Strong GD&T, tolerance analysis, and specification-writing skills, with deep knowledge of machining, stamping, molding, plating, and assembly processes and proven Design for Manufacturing (DFM) experience. Green Belt or Black Belt certification, executive-level presentation and customer communication skills, and the ability to work effectively across global teams and time zones with limited supervision.
