Operations Analyst, AS

Deutsche Bank · Jaipur, India

About the role

Job Description: Job Title: Operations Analyst Corporate Title: Analyst Location: Jaipur, India Role Description The person will be responsible for day-to-day review of voice logs between Client Relationship Managers (RC) and clients where trade details are discussed and agreed. They need to ensure that all key trade-related details are clearly discussed, accurately captured, and documented in the applicable product-specific template. The role requires strong attention to detail, adherence to defined review standards, timely completion of assigned production duties, and appropriate escalation of exceptions, missing information, or first-level queries within the pre-specified timelines. The candidate should also act as a backup for peers when required and share best practices with the team to support consistent quality delivery. What we’ll offer you As part of our flexible scheme, here are just some of the benefits that you’ll enjoy, Best in class leave policy. Gender neutral parental leaves 100% reimbursement under childcare assistance benefit (gender neutral) Sponsorship for Industry relevant certifications and education Employee Assistance Program for you and your family members Comprehensive Hospitalization Insurance for you and your dependents Accident and Term life Insurance Complementary Health screening for 35 yrs. and above Your key responsibilities Review voice logs between RCs and clients to validate that trade details have been discussed and agreed as per the required standards. Listen to and understand client and RMs discussions and accurately document the agreed details or the prescribed business template, as applicable. Complete the relevant product-specific voice log review template with accurate details, observations, and outcome of the review. Identify and highlight missing, incomplete, inconsistent, or unclear trade details and escalate exceptions to the relevant stakeholders within agreed timelines. Ensure accurate and timely completion of daily production activities in line with defined SLAs, cut-off times, and quality expectations. Perform quality checks on completed reviews to support consistency, audit readiness, and control effectiveness. Assist in preparing and reviewing daily control / production reports, including tracking of open items, exceptions, and follow-ups. Support follow-up with RC / business teams for clarification or resolution of deficiencies noted during voice log review. Maintain proper documentation and evidence of review outcomes to support internal control, audit, and management reporting requirements. Coordinate with team members and stakeholders on complex cases, escalations, reporting requirements, and process improvements. Act as backup for peers during absence and support knowledge sharing, cross-training, and continuous improvement within the team. Perform other administration, reporting, and control-related tasks as assigned. Your skills and experience Minimum educational qualification: University graduate with a Bachelor’s Degree. Good command of business English for accurate documentation, exception reporting, and stakeholder communication. Understanding of trade lifecycle, banking / wealth management products, or control review processes will be an added advantage. Strong listening, comprehension, documentation, and analytical skills with high attention to detail. Ability to identify gaps, inconsistencies, missing information, and potential control issues from recorded conversations. Candidates are required to be proficient in Microsoft Office applications, especially Excel, Word, and PowerPoint. Highly motivated, quality-focused, and able to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team environment. Ability to multi-task and work under pressure to meet production timelines, cut-off times, and quality standards. Good communication skills and ability to manage routine queries / first-level escalations professionally. Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimise workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs. How we’ll support you Training and development to help you excel in your career. Coaching and support from experts in your team. A culture of continuous learning to aid progression. A range of flexible benefits that you can tailor to suit your needs.
