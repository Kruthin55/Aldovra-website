Software Engineer

Thomson Reuters · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description As a Software Engineer, you will design, build, and maintain high-quality software in an agile, collaborative environment. You will translate business requirements into technical solutions, deliver performant and resilient features, and deepen your technical expertise while helping shape how the team uses modern development tools, including AI-assisted coding. Exceptional attention to detail, strong analytical skills, and excellent communication are essential to success in this role. Key

Responsibilities

Partner with product and business teams to gather and analyze requirements, and translate them into technical designs and functional/technical specifications. Research and evaluate emerging software technologies that can improve application performance and development efficiency while reducing cost and maintaining quality. Develop creative, elegant solutions to complex technical problems. Prepare technical specifications and documentation. Participate in team meetings, status updates, and project planning sessions to analyze development requirements, propose design options, and provide work breakdowns and estimates. Build and maintain RESTful Web APIs and related services. Participate in software releases and support production stability. Collaborate with Quality Assurance to prepare test plans and test cases, execute tests, and identify and resolve defects. Work with the system operations team to ensure system stability and high availability. Use AI-assisted development tools to support coding, testing, refactoring, debugging, and documentation, and critically review all generated code for correctness, maintainability, security, and performance. Required

Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience. 2–4 years of professional software development experience. Hands-on experience with C#/.NET Core, Node.js, or Python. Experience building and maintaining RESTful Web APIs. Experience with cloud-based development, preferably AWS. Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments. Familiarity with CI/CD, Git, code reviews, debugging, logging, and production support. Working knowledge of common software design patterns. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with technical staff, business analysts, management, and business stakeholders. Strong attention to detail and the ability to prioritize work effectively. Customer-first approach to software development. Interest in AI-assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, or ChatGPT, with the ability to validate their output. Preferred

Qualifications

Experience with AWS services in production environments. Experience with both C#/.NET Core and Node.js or Python. Hands-on experience using AI-assisted development tools in day-to-day coding, testing, or documentation work. Experience with test automation and collaborating closely with QA teams. Background in transaction tax, tax technology, or other enterprise software environments.
