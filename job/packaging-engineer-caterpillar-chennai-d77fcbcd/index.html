Packaging Engineer

Caterpillar · Chennai

About the role

Career Area: Supply Chain and Logistics Job Description: Your Work Shapes the World at Caterpillar Inc. When you join Caterpillar, you're joining a global team who cares not just about the work we do – but also about each other. We are the makers, problem solvers, and future world builders who are creating stronger, more sustainable communities. We don't just talk about progress and innovation here – we make it happen, with our customers, where we work and live. Together, we are building a better world, so we can all enjoy living in it. Job Summary Work with manufacturing team or end customer and packaging design team to bring out a best packaging design to meet New Model launch metrics such as Safety, Quality, Velocity, Cost as well Sustainable. What You Will Do

Coordinates meetings and partners with cross-functional team for packaging approvals between design and end user.

Solidifying agreement with Supply Chain on Returnable packaging loop size/inventory and drives procurement through standard process.

Leading design approval meetings and conducting proto validation to get sign-off from stake holders.

Governing and meeting new model launch metrices, score card etc.

Independently identifying cost reduction and sustainability opportunities.
