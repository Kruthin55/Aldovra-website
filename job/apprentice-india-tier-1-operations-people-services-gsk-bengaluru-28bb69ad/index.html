Apprentice, India Tier-1 Operations – People Services

GSK · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Purpose This position is a member of the People Services & Support India (Rx and GCC) and plays a key role in the ensuring data quality, enablement of downstream applications and processes to perform as expected and maintaining a compliant People Services organization. This position is essential to support in the Workday for all Tier 1 Transactional Support related to Employee Life cycle and support of PS Service delivery obligations. This position requires an action oriented professional, who will provide essential support in the processing of employee data via manual data entry in Workday. The position will ensure high quality customer service delivery, meeting all KPI’s and other Metrics as determined by GSK globally, and where applicable, locally Key

Responsibilities

Describe the key deliverables to be achieved by the post holder and the ongoing responsibilities of the role. Supporting the life cycle of an employee through routine Workday/Local transactional processing/approval Hire Processing Employee Documentation management Confirmation process Personal Information / Legal Name Change Clearing Workday inbox actions Transactions related to Employee life cycle Support employees with policy queries through chat and servicenow tickets. Follow the process audit framework and help the effectiveness team in data driving quality. Support other team members with reviewing and drafting policies. Skills Case Management Software, Continual Improvement Process, Critical Thinking, Data Analysis, Data Management, Document Management, Escalation Management, Human Resource Information Systems (HRIS), Prioritization, Problem Solving, SLA Management, Standards Compliance Why GSK?
