Implementation Engineer

Broadridge · Hyderabad

About the role

At Broadridge, we've built a culture where the highest goal is to empower others to accomplish more. If you’re passionate about developing your career, while helping others along the way, come join the Broadridge team. We are seeking an Implementation Consultant to join the Conversions Team, supporting client onboarding and migration activities through test, UAT, and go-live phases. This role combines technical implementation, environment coordination, and client-facing delivery, ensuring successful setup, testing, migration, and handover of solutions into live operation. The successful candidate will work closely with clients, internal delivery teams, and support functions to manage environments, coordinate releases, triage issues, oversee migration rehearsals, and support production go-live. deployment Act as a point of contact between the client and internal teams throughout the conversion lifecycle Ensure implementation activities are documented clearly and tracked to completion
