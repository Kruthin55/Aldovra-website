Content Analyst - German Language

LSEG · Bengaluru

About the role

Entity Content maintains a central database that connects and unlocks the true value of our content across our products and internal services. We are the starting point for all organization information. The data we provide helps our customers run their business successfully and in accordance with the law and regulations of the markets they operate in. We provide Entity data and Qualified financial content as services to the Industry. A Content Analyst – German Language is responsible for researching, analyzing, translating, and maintaining business and entity information from German-language sources while ensuring the highest standards of data quality, accuracy, and timeliness. The role requires strong language proficiency, analytical thinking, and the ability to interpret complex information from company disclosures, regulatory filings, and other authoritative sources. Roles &

Responsibilities

Process and maintain data accurately and in a timely manner onto databases. Ensure data accuracy, timeliness and completeness are maintained as per company standards. Analyze, interpret and process information from German-language sources, including company websites, regulatory filings, annual reports, press releases and other public sources. Translate and validate business information from German to English while maintaining data quality and consistency. Develop expertise in database and financial knowledge through continuous learning and development. Build up knowledge of financial regulations and market practices/conventions in relevant markets. Widen usage of available tools/support to best advantage to maintain/improve content quality during daily operations. Adhere to dynamic process/Method of Work changes, data operations standards and current data policies & procedures. Responsible for delivering high productivity and quality data as per expected performance standards. Proactively identify, call out and resolve data problems. Communicate & liaise with internal and external departments for resolution of data queries as per established process. Test new database/application releases where appropriate, providing constructive user feedback. Perform in-depth analysis on queries, identify root causes and drive action plans to improve quality and the overall customer experience. Ensure awareness of customer views on content & recommend improvements/raise problems as needed. Support the team by providing language-specific expertise and guidance on German-language content and research. Research and validate entity information across German-speaking markets using reliable and authoritative sources. Ensure that allotted tasks are completed within agreed SLA's and meet the service metrics of the team. Contribute to process improvement initiatives that enhance productivity, quality and operational efficiency. Required Skills and Experience Professional proficiency in German with strong reading, writing, speaking and comprehension skills. Strong written and verbal communication skills in English. Technical / Professional skills at an Intermediate level in using Excel & SQL. Strong research and analytical skills with attention to detail. Ability to interpret and analyze information from German-language corporate and regulatory sources. Should be a very quick learner and should display a high level of agility. Should have good communication skills – Written and Verbal. Should be able to take multiple tasks and be flexible to extend support as per business requirements. Should be flexible enough to work in different shifts & Hybrid model of 3 days in Office & 2 days working from Home. Ability to operate with minimal guidance. Coach and train analysts on language-specific data issues, databases & products. Proven analytic and problem-solving skills, innovation and taking initiative in continuous process improvements. Knowledge of German-speaking markets, corporate structures and business terminology will be an added advantage. Experience in data management, content operations, market intelligence or financial services environments is preferred. Demonstrated commitment to quality, accuracy and customer focus.
