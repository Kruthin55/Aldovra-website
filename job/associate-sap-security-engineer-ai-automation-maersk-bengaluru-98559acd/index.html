Associate SAP Security Engineer - AI & Automation

Maersk · Bengaluru

About the role

Company: Maersk is going through times of unprecedented change. As we aspire to secure sustainable growth of our businesses, we need to re-think the way we engage with our customers and partners. If you are passionate about working in an entrepreneurial and ever-changing environment, developing new products that engage and provide an enhanced customer experience – you maybe what we are looking for. We Offer: Joining Maersk will embark you on a great journey with career development in a global organization. As an SAP Security Technical Engineer working with the new age SAP Platform you will gain broad business knowledge of the company’s activities globally, as well as take part in the transformation of the transport and logistics industry. You will work and communicate across geographical and cultural borders that will enable you to build a strong professional network. We will provide you with opportunities that broaden your knowledge and strengthen your technical and professional foundation. Professional

Requirements

3 to 5 years of core Security experience with SAP at its core and main skill Bachelor’s or master’s degree or equal by experience Key Professional Skills: Build, test and deploy SAP solutions based on the design from the solution architects and at the same time be able to progress into detailed design for a successful implementation and transition to application support team Solve complex business problems in the areas of finance, operations, human capital, customer, and governance, risk and compliance whilst using SAP technologies primarily. Has experience in more than 1-2 Full Lifecycle SAP Implementation project Excellent communication skills as the job requires a high degree of verbal and written communication Be highly motivated, hardworking and possess good time management and communication skills Knowledge and experience of delivering projects in an Agile framework Core SAP Security skills: Ensure governance and compliance through effective and accurate analysis, design, development and quality assurance of information security controls Can directly build and manage roles / authorisations and its maintenance along with user access in SAP systems and is willing to learn admin controls for peripheral systems including cloud SAP security controls Have strong experience in SAP GRC AC, S4HANA, FioriBW4HANA & SAC, HANA DB Security Respond timely to business requests requiring security control changes and solicit accurate and complete business and functional requirements from Solution Architects and Subject Matter Experts Have familiarity with security framework controls as it applies to SAP system access e.g Enterprise Segregation of Duties, EAM, Access controls Support & manage different SAP internal & external audit activities. Manage and define security processes. Implements proper security controls to secure SAP Systems resources. Assists with periodic security reviews, critical transaction reviews, and segregation of duty reviews to ensure access within the system is appropriate based upon job responsibilities. Adheres to change control rules and methodology with regard to break fix and project support. Provides SAP security project support for site deployments, implementations and related functional enhancements. Engages in knowledge transfer and training of SAP Security team members, IT team members and business users as applicable Has knowledge of Enterprise level threat detection tools such as SIEM Working experience in Enterprise Identity & Governance tools would be added advantage Maersk is committed to a diverse and inclusive workplace, and we embrace different styles of thinking.
