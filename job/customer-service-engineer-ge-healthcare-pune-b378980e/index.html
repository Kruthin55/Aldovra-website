Responsibilities

Perform installation, inspection, repair, maintenance, and life-extension activities on equipment at customer sites. Support construction and commissioning of new and existing equipment. Carry out technical activities such as machining, NDT inspection, welding, generator diagnostics, and equipment troubleshooting. Make engineering decisions and solve technical problems using established procedures within a defined framework. Apply engineering knowledge to complete assigned tasks and continuously develop technical expertise. Resolves technical issues using established procedures and sound engineering judgment. Escalates complex or non-standard problems to senior team members when needed. Work closely with internal teams to ensure successful project execution. Communicate technical information clearly to customers and team members. Build and maintain strong customer relationships as a key point of contact. Support and guide junior team members when required. Understand business objectives and contribute to team and project goals. Required
