Fraud & Claims Operations Representative

Wells Fargo · Hyderabad

About the role

About this role: At Wells Fargo, a Fraud & Claims Operations Representative is a front-line role in a high-volume call center, supporting customers through potential fraud situations. You will protect customers and the organization by identifying, investigating, and resolving fraud and claims in a fast-paced, high-volume call center environment. You’ll engage directly with customers to handle sensitive situations, analyze account activity, and make informed decisions across multiple claim types. In this role, you will: Support fraud and claims investigations by reviewing customer information, account activity, and transactions Manage moderately complex fraud and claims cases across multiple claim types and prioritize work effectively in a high-volume, call center environment Oversee assigned cases, using critical thinking and sound judgment to determine next steps and ensure accurate, timely resolution through voice and non-voice functions Communicate clearly and professionally with customers, especially in sensitive situations, to build understanding and trust Navigate escalated or challenging interactions with professionalism while balancing customer experience, risk, and regulatory requirements Partner with internal teams and stakeholders to resolve cases and support effective, end-to-end investigations Maintain a strong understanding of fraud risk and consistently apply that perspective to make informed, accurate decisions Required
