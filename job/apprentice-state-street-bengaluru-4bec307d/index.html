Apprentice

State Street · Bengaluru

About the role

Who we are looking for Join a dynamic global team that supports client-facing functions by creating high-quality proposals and consultant database responses. This role offers hands-on experience in asset management, exposure to global markets, and an opportunity to develop professional skills in writing, data analysis, and client engagement. What you will be responsible for As an Apprentice you will Assist Proposal and Presentation Specialists in preparing client proposals, RFPs, and consultant questionnaires. Support projects involving quantitative data collation and integration for proposals. Identify and source accurate responses from internal databases for proposal questionnaires. Generate reports using internal tools to support proposal specialists. Serve as a backup for urgent proposal requests. Ensure compliance with company standards and contribute to continuous improvement of proposal processes. What we value These skills will help you succeed in this role with MUST and NICE to have skills Strong written and oral communication skills; proficiency in English. Good analytical and interpersonal skills. Excel skills. Ability to multitask and manage multiple projects under tight deadlines. Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook). Flexible to work in shifts and adapt to changing priorities. Education & Preferred

Qualifications

Graduate or above, preferably with exposure to finance or asset management. 0–12 months of experience in financial services or related fields. Additional requirements Basic understanding of asset management concepts, including portfolio management and investment strategies. Familiarity with equity investing principles and market dynamics. Awareness of mutual funds, their structure, and role in investment portfolios. About State Street Across the globe, institutional investors rely on us to help them manage risk, respond to challenges, and drive performance and profitability. We keep our clients at the heart of everything we do, and smart, engaged employees are essential to our continued success. We are committed to fostering an environment where every employee feels valued and empowered to reach their full potential. As an essential partner in our shared success, you’ll benefit from inclusive development opportunities, flexible work-life support, paid volunteer days, and vibrant employee networks that keep you connected to what matters most. Join us in shaping the future.
