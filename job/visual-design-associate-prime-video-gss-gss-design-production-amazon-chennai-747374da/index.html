Visual Design Associate, Prime Video - GSS, GSS - Design Production

Amazon · Chennai

About the role

Amazon Prime Video is changing the way people watch movies and TV shows, offering multiple ways to instantly stream content across the globe. Our mission is to build the widest selection of digital video content and make it easy for customers to enjoy great content wherever and whenever they want. With a deep connection to the broader Amazon family of brands and a passionate, diverse global team, Prime Video is a place where creators, makers, and builders of all kinds can do the best work of their careers. If you want to operate at start-up speed, be a part of the team that is solving some of the hardest problems and help build a service that customers love, Amazon Prime Video might just be the place for you. The Global Shared Services (GSS) at Prime Video is an operationally-driven team of designers, producers, QA specialists and operations managers responsible for creating and delivering high volumes of digital assets. GSS is seeking a Visual Design Associate with the ability to deliver clean, digital-ready designs based on existing style guides and brand guidelines in a fast-paced production environment. The ability to manage multiple projects and large volume is essential. Key job responsibilities The candidate will have a comprehensive understanding of graphic design processes and software, specifically for graphic production, along with experience and a successful track record in digital marketing at fast-paced environments. Additionally, this candidate should have a keen eye for detail, and an excellent ability to communicate.

Create on-brand digital design solutions that meet business goals and project requirements.

Create best in class layouts and visuals that support and reinforce business objectives while partnering with business/marketing teams to ensure that designs meet brand and business needs.

Ensure design requirements are met in adherence with campaign/project guidelines.

Able to optimize digital images for web, mobile, big screen and other devices.

Follow and apply graphic templates and style guides.

Able to maintain quality on their own creative production

Able to fulfil multiple projects requests while simultaneously meeting tight deadlines. A day in the life · The associate should be able to create innovative, multi-platform graphics based on the key art and other inputs provided by the partners/producers. · The associate has to follow SOP/guidelines to complete the production task · Able to partner with design leadership and product management partners to consistently deliver high-quality creative that meets placement specs.

Basic qualifications

- Knowledge of Adobe creative suite (Photoshop, InDesign, Illustrator) - Bachelor's degree, or experience as technical specialist in design and architecture - Experience working independently with minimal supervision - Experience prioritizing and handling multiple assignments at any given time while maintaining commitment to deadlines, or experience with end-to-end project management - Have an online portfolio demonstrating strong visual design skills and advertising experience across a range of media and platforms, along with work that reflects your design process - Experience that includes strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and effective communication abilities, or experience working with customers with a passion for delivering exceptional service

Preferred qualifications

- Experience working as a producer, creative executive, or a similar role - Experience working with global teams and diverse partners - Experience in advertising Our inclusive culture empowers Amazonians to deliver the best results for our customers.
