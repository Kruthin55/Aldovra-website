Planning Process System Consultant

HP · Bengaluru

About the role

Planning Process System Consultant Description - Job Description for Planning Process System Expert and IBP Consultant Business Environment HP creates new possibilities for technology to have a meaningful impact on people, businesses, governments and society. The world’s largest technology company and ranked 10 on the Fortune 500 list for 2012, HP brings together a portfolio that spans printing, personal computing, software, services and IT infrastructure to serve more than 1 billion customers in over 170 countries on six continents. HP invents, engineers, and delivers technology solutions that drive business value, create social value, and improve the lives of our clients. About the Engagement The Print Planning Excellence (PPE) team is responsible for managing and executing production planning systems, data, and processes supporting global and regional Print and Personal Systems businesses within HP. The team focuses on ensuring that demand and supply plans are complete, reliable, accurate, and available on time to support effective business planning and execution. The Planning Process System Expert and IBP Consultant would work closely with business planning teams, IT, and cross-functional stakeholders to support the execution of demand and supply planning processes. The consultant would monitor daily and weekly planning activities, analyse planning data, identify process and system issues, and coordinate with relevant teams to drive timely resolution. The role would also support master data accuracy, planning metrics and reporting, business-user queries, and planning cycle readiness. The consultant would act as a key connection point between the planning community and IT, supporting issue resolution, process improvements, testing, and implementation of planning system enhancements. The objective of the role is to ensure stable and timely planning execution, high-quality planning data, effective issue resolution, and continuous improvement of planning processes . Responsibilities

ü Support and manage end-to-end Demand and Supply Planning activities across Print, S&G, and Personal Systems businesses. ü Execute daily, weekly, and periodic planning processes, ensuring planning activities are completed accurately and on time. ü Analyse planning data and reports to identify demand, supply, forecast, and planning-system issues and highlight potential business impacts. ü Monitor key supply chain and planning KPIs, including forecast accuracy, demand-supply alignment, and other operational planning indicators. ü Identify planning gaps and operational risks, perform initial root-cause analysis, escalate critical issues, and coordinate actions toward resolution. ü Partner closely with Business SMEs, IT, and cross-functional teams to troubleshoot planning issues and ensure demand and supply alignment. ü Communicate planning issues, business impact, corrective actions, and resolutions to relevant stakeholders and support/lead regular SME discussions. ü Identify opportunities for process improvement, standardization, automation, and cost reduction across demand and supply planning processes. ü Participate in cross-functional projects and planning transformation initiatives, including business requirements, testing, validation, and implementation activities. ü Support the implementation, stabilization, and continuous improvement of planning tools and systems, while maintaining process documentation, controls, and planning best practices. Planning Process System Expert and IBP Consultant – Skills Required ü Demand & Supply Planning Fundamentals Good understanding of demand planning, supply planning, forecasting, inventory, and demand-supply balancing. ü Planning Systems Knowledge Working knowledge of SAP IBP, SAP HANA , ARIBA , Enterprises Business Wearhouse or similar supply-chain planning systems. ü Data Analysis Strong analytical skills with the ability to review planning data, identify exceptions, trends, gaps, and potential issues. ü Planning KPI & Performance Monitoring Understanding of planning metrics such as forecast accuracy, demand-supply match, inventory, backlog, and service-level indicators. ü Problem Solving & Root-Cause Analysis Ability to investigate day-to-day process, data, and system issues and support root-cause identification and resolution. ü Excel & Reporting Skills Strong knowledge of Microsoft Excel ; exposure to Power BI or other reporting/analytics tools is preferred. ü Master Data & Data Quality Basic understanding of master-data dependencies and the importance of data quality within planning processes. ü Stakeholder Collaboration Ability to coordinate effectively with planners, Business SMEs, IT/support teams, and other cross-functional stakeholders. ü Communication Skills Ability to clearly communicate planning issues, business impact, risks, corrective actions, and resolution status. ü Testing & Implementation Support Ability to support requirements gathering, UAT, validation, implementation, go-live, and stabilization activities.
