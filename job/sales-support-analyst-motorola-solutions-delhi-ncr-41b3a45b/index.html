Sales Support Analyst

Motorola Solutions · Delhi NCR

About the role

Department Overview Silvus Technologies, a Motorola Solutions company, is seeking a Sales Support Analyst to serve as an operational and analytical resource to the Regional Territory sales leadership across India. This position plays a key role in supporting opportunity management, maintaining commercial pipeline visibility, tracking and analysing business activities for Silvus' industry-leading StreamCaster Mobile Ad-Hoc Networking (MANET) and multi-antenna MIMO radio systems to enhance business potential and support the Territory Sales Leadership.. The Sales Support Analyst exercises independent judgment in supporting sales operations activities across India, including maintaining sales and opportunity tracking data, monitoring procurement and tender portals, coordinating public procurement and defence bid documentation and coordinating all online and offline sales activities. Operating in a dynamic environment, this role balances opportunity tracking, bid coordination, reporting, and cross-functional support with minimal supervision. The position reports to Regional Territory Sales Leadership and works closely with all Sales operations activities so as to work closely with Pre sales, Engineering, Finance, and authorized Partners to support commercial activities across India's defence, government, public safety, and commercial sectors. This position is based out of our Gurgaon, India office. Job Description Monitor relevant procurement portals (including Government e-Marketplace / GeM and defence portals), industry platforms, and official public channels to identify tenders, customer requirements, and emerging prospects. ● Maintain engagement with industry associations, including the Society of Indian Defence Manufacturers (SIDM), and monitor relevant industry announcements, events, and membership activities. ● Support end-to-end tender evaluation, bid coordination, compliance matrix alignment, and preparation of required submission documentation in close collaboration with internal stakeholders. ● Coordinate with legal, technical, sales, and partner teams to align tender deliverables, submission timelines, and compliance requirements. ● Coordinate with authorized partners on partner registrations, onboarding activities, documentation submissions, and opportunity-related administrative requirements. ● Maintain sales trackers, CRM records, and opportunity databases to monitor pipeline status, deal milestones, and commercial activities. ● Support customer engagement initiatives, defence exhibitions, aerospace expos, trade shows and other field business development and demonstration activities. ● Review, configure, and audit Bills of Materials (BOM) for StreamCaster tactical radios, OEM transceiver modules, antennas, accessories, and firmware feature licenses. ● Track and monitor compliance deliverables, End-User Certificates (EUC), and import/export documentation in alignment with trade compliance policies. ● Support process improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing sales support operations, reporting accuracy, data quality, and operational efficiency. ● Follow up on Opportunity pipeline with fortnightly engagement with customers.for requirements and related activities. ● Offline customer engagement and meetings with the Sales Team for Sales and Trade show activities. ● Business Development activities by tracking opportunities on the Govt E- Marketing portals and MoD websites ● Perform other related duties as assigned. Requirements

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS ● Bachelor’s degree in Electronics and Communication or Information Technology or Computer Science or Business Administration with reasonable understanding of technology and related discipline. ● Practical exposure supporting defence opportunity tracking, tender monitoring, CRM updates, sales operations, partner coordination, and procurement-related activities. ● Familiarity with CRM platforms (such as Salesforce, Zoho, or Workday) and advanced Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets skills (Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, data reconciliation, tracker automation). ● Working knowledge of Indian public procurement procedures, tender evaluation workflows, and portals such as the Government e-Marketplace (GeM). ● Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English to interface with channel partners, commercial teams, and internal stakeholders. ● Strong organizational skills, structured analytical thinking, and meticulous attention to detail. ● Preferably an experience of at least 6 months of internship or active job with similar profile. ● Must be based in or willing to relocate to Gurgaon, Haryana, India. PREFERRED KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, AND ABILITIES ● Familiarity with tactical communications, radio frequency (RF) networks, defense electronics, or telecommunications hardware. ● Experience supporting industry trade shows, defence exhibitions, aerospace events, customer demonstrations, or partner engagement activities. ● Understanding of government procurement processes, defence acquisition programs, or public sector tendering environments in India. ● Working knowledge of End-User Certificates (EUCs), import/export documentation, trade compliance requirements, or related commercial documentation processes. ● Awareness of the Indian defence ecosystem, including government stakeholders, procurement agencies, industry associations, and defence- focused events.
