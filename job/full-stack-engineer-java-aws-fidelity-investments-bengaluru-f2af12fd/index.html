About the role

Job Description: Job Title - Full Stack Engineer - Java, AWS Build a data platform to support Asset Management’s data analytics and discovery needs. If you have a passion for working with data using multiple new technologies on the cloud, this might be the right opportunity for you! The Purpose of Your Role The Full Stack Engineer with expertise and experience in designing and developing applications including new developments, enhancements, maintenance and support. The role involves continuous collaboration with partners, iterative understanding of requirements, following Fidelity standard practices methodologies,detailed understanding of the technology roadmap, advancement to design / development process and providing on-call production support on rotation basis. The Value You Deliver Good Object oriented programming skills Building quality solutions that align with the technology blueprint and standard methodologies to solve business problems by driving design, development and ongoing support. Work with our distributed team and provide technical direction in building solutions. Actively participating in sharing the knowledge sessions, code and design reviews etc. The Skills that are Key to this role You are involved in development and delivery of high quality, timely and maintainable software solutions in an Agile environment which meet functional and non-functional business requirements You demonstrate ability to contribute towards multi-functional tasks as part of a robust Agile team You have experience with matured Agile SCRUM practices You keeps current with IT blueprint, frameworks and technologies that would benefit us and improve our productivity You demonstrates software engineering excellence through continuous integration, continuous deployment, and Acceptance Test Driven Development You gain domain expertise for the functional and technical areas of focus. Shows eagerness to broaden understanding and knowledge of domain and technology You follow practices and contribute towards innovative solutions to increase efficiency You are highly engaged with the team and a good teammate You are proficient in following technologies Must Have: Java 8 and related frameworks like Spring Framework, at least one ORM tool (Hibernate or myBatis), JMS and/or REST/Web Services, and/or Angular JS or any equivalent JavaScript based UI framework like jQuery, node JS SQL/PL SQL Hands-on experience of any test automation tools Willingness and Ability to work as a full stack engineer proficiency in one or more these Dev Ops – Jenkins, uDeploy Exposure to Cloud services like AWS, Azure Queing systems like Kafka You keeps current with the newest technology trends in the industry You understand and applies fundamental design principles behind a scalable, low latency, high throughput distributed applications You are familiar with usage of design patterns and their application You understanding of Finance/Asset management business domain will be an advantage You have excellent written and verbal communication skills You continuous out-of-the-box assessment of status-quo and opportunities for improvement You are self-motivated with proven ability to effectively work with diverse personalities in a dynamic and fast paced environment How Your Work Impacts the Organization Asset Management Technology (AM Tech) is the business unit that is responsible for delivering innovative, scalable, industry-leading investment tools that enable Fidelity’s Investment & Asset Management business to achieve competitive advantage globally. AM Tech is an integral partner in setting Asset Management's strategic direction and has shared accountability for achieving Asset Management goals. AM Tech is global organization of technologists based in the United States, London, India and Ireland. Supporting multiple business function in Investment Management and Advisory domain like Portfolio Management, Research & Trading.
