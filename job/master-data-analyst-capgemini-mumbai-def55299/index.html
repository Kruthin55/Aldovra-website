Master Data Analyst

Capgemini · Mumbai

About the role

Choosing Capgemini means choosing a company where you will be empowered to shape your career in the way you’d like, where you’ll be supported and inspired by a collaborative community of colleagues around the world, and where you’ll be able to reimagine what’s possible. Join us and help the world’s leading organizations unlock the value of technology and build a more sustainable, more inclusive world. Your Role The CI Master Data Analyst sits within the Insights Analysis pod and is responsible for ensuring external and internal commercial data is accurately mapped, enriched and maintained for reporting and analytical use. The role supports data onboarding, master-data alignment, data-quality monitoring and vendor coordination to ensure trusted, reporting-ready datasets. Working closely with Commercial Insights, Data Engineering and syndicated data providers, the analyst helps maintain consistent and governed data foundations across markets and platforms. In this role you will play key role in Data Enrichment & Master Data Mapping Enrich commercial datasets to ensure they are reporting-ready and aligned to business requirements. Map external syndicated and third-party data sources to the client's internal product master-data structures. Maintain mapping rules, hierarchies and documentation to support consistent reporting outputs. Data Quality & Validation Design and operate data-quality reporting processes to identify inconsistencies, mapping gaps and data anomalies. Perform validation, reconciliation and quality-control checks on newly onboarded and existing datasets. Escalate and coordinate resolution of technical data issues with Data Engineering and D&T teams where required. Data Onboarding & Change Management Support onboarding of new syndicated and commercial data sources. Validate source structures, attributes and mappings before production use. Coordinate testing and controlled implementation of approved data changes.
