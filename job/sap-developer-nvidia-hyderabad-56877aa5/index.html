SAP Developer

NVIDIA · Hyderabad

About the role

NVIDIAns work, think and learn as a team and thrive in a very inspiring environment and are motivated by a culture that demands innovation and the highest standards. Our employees are passionate about parallel and visual computing. Our technology impacts the visual experience in video game development, film production, space exploration, medicine, computational finance and automotive design and we've only scratched the surface of what we can accomplish when we apply our technology to it. We need passionate, hard-working and creative people to help us pursue some of these unusual opportunities. Our Application Development team is looking for a highly motivated ABAP Developer. The ideal individual for our team will have in-depth knowledge of full spectrum of SAP Development domain, ranging from basics to advanced skills and techniques. This resource will be working with equally motivated team of Developers and will support our amazing SAP Functional team. What you’ll be doing: Work closely with Business Systems Analyst team to determine optimal solutions addressing the functional requirements Translate requirements and functional specifications into technical specifications documentation Design, Develop and Maintain a diverse set of SAP Objects based on the business needs Conduct design reviews, code reviews as per established SDLC guidelines Proactively monitors and supports the production landscape to address issues What we need to see: 5+ years of experience in SAP ABAP analysis, design, and programming experience developing custom SAP solutions Bachelors or higher Degree in related field, or equivalent experience Proficiency in conventional ABAP development tools and techniques including but not limited to Classic ABAP programming, ABAP Workbench, ABAP Objects, IDocs, SAPScripts, Smartforms, Web-Dynpro, Web Services, Workflows, User Exits / BADIs / Enhancements Working experience in OData, SAPUI5, Fiori and Screen Personas, SAP certification is helpful Deep understanding of SAP Authorizations Ability to understand functional specifications and convert them to technical specifications, come up with estimates based on various levels of specifications at various stages of a project and accomplish tasks within estimated time targets Expertise in troubleshooting and resolving technical issues. Debug, tune and optimize programs for performance. Ability to document / explain / escalate issues appropriately through to the Management Experience with working in a distributed multiple SAP systems landscape Exposure to latest AI tools and applications of AI in SAP. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Within SAP systems, knowledge of the following modules is desirable for this position: Sales and Distribution Materials Management Finance Supply Chain Management Logistics execution Experience in Automation tools, development of Automation and AI applications in SAP would be a definite plus.
