Junior Finance Analyst - Cost Accounting

Flex · Chennai

About the role

Flex is the diversified manufacturing partner of choice that helps market-leading brands design, build and deliver innovative products that improve the world. A career at Flex offers the opportunity to make a difference and invest in your growth in a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment. If you are excited about a role but don't meet every bullet point, we encourage you to apply and join us to create the extraordinary. Job Summary

To support our extraordinary teams who build great products and contribute to our growth, we’re looking to add Junior Financial Analyst - Cost Accounting Solutions in Chennai. What a typical day looks like:

Perform all cost accounting activities that are defined.

Understand the corporate policy and follow-up of key cost accounting metrics.

Implement time saving/cost reducing initiatives through pocket kaizens & process improvements.

Work along with operations team & track reasons for metric non-conformances through regular follow ups.

Posting month end journal entries related to accruals and reclass in ERP.

Prepare & maintain SOP in line with the latest process and document them in DMS.

To learn & serve as a backup for cost accountant. The experience we’re looking to add to our team:

Basic level knowledge in MS applications.

Excellent oral & written communication skills in English.

Good team player with analytical skills.

Willingness to learn & deploy the skills learnt in day to day process.

B.Com / BBA (Finance) / M.Com / MBA (Finance).

Freshers / 0-2 years of experience in relevant field.

MS Office good knowledge.

Knowledge in accounting principles.

Understand & interpret accounting policies. What you’ll receive for the great work you provide:

Health Insurance.
