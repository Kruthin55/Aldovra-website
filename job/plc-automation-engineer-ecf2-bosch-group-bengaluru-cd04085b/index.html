PLC Automation Engineer-ECF2

Bosch Group · Bengaluru

About the role

Bosch Global Software Technologies Private Limited is a 100% owned subsidiary of Robert Bosch GmbH, one of the world's leading global supplier of technology and services, offering end-to-end Engineering, IT and Business Solutions. With over 27,000+ associates, it’s the largest software development center of Bosch, outside Germany, indicating that it is the Technology Powerhouse of Bosch in India with a global footprint and presence in the US, Europe and the Asia Pacific region. Expected skill set : Handled any one of PLC programming tools (TwinCAT, CODESYS, Simatic STEP 7). Knowledge on IEC1131-3 PLC programming languages - SFC, Structured Text. Reading and understanding of customer documents like E-Plan, CTS, and Mechanical drawings. Knowledge of Industrial Automation protocols like EtherCat, Profibus. Experience working in Discrete Manufacturing domain preferably in Assembly line and packaging machines . Tasks /

Responsibilities

Development of PLC and HMI programs. Testing the machine working as per requirements. Commissioning the machine in customer location.Ensuring safety of the operator on the machine usage. Good to Have skills: Experience in interfacing with external devices like Servo Drives, Motion Controllers, Screwers Hands-on experience in any HMI building tools like Factory Talk View, InTouch, WinCC Experience in implementing any Manufacturing Execution System (MES) UAT (User acceptance Testing) and plant rollouts

Qualifications

BE in EC/EE/IA or any equivalent Additional information 3 - 6 years of related experience Candidate should be willing to travel to customer locations based on requirements.
