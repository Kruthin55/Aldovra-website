Overview of role

The HR Intern (Pune – All 5 days WFO, 6-month Internship) is an entry-level role responsible for supporting HR Operations and Employee Lifecycle Management activities. This individual will gain hands-on exposure to various HR functions, including pre-onboarding, background verification, employee onboarding, employee engagement, HR operations, post-onboarding, and admin-related activities. The ideal candidate brings strong communication and coordination skills, a keen eye for detail, and the ability to manage multiple tasks while ensuring a seamless employee experience.

Responsibilities

Pre-Employment Verification & Background Verification (BGV): Initiate UAN (Universal Account Number) verification requests via email based on Talent Acquisition requirements and review UAN reports against candidate records to identify discrepancies or dual employment prior to offer release.

Document Collection & Vendor Coordination: Collect candidate documents (including Aadhaar and passport copies) from Greenhouse, initiate background verification on the Integritas portal, obtain Letters of Authorization (LOA), and conduct weekly review meetings with the BGV vendor to track pending cases and resolve queries.

Employee Onboarding & HRIS Management: Create employee profiles and initiate onboarding workflows in HRIS upon hiring confirmations from ATS, process offer rescind requests for dropouts/backouts, and guide new joiners in completing Google Forms and Rippling profiles.

Candidate Experience & Day 1 Readiness: Maintain regular communication with candidates from offer rollout until joining, coordinate with IT, Admin, and Talent Acquisition teams for a smooth Day 1, assist new joiners with PF Form 11 and LinkedIn updates, and maintain confidential, employee-specific document folders in Google Drive.

Post-Onboarding & Lifecycle Management: Schedule and coordinate new-hire check-in calls, track probation completion dates with HRBPs for timely reviews and confirmations, schedule quarterly employee connect calls with PODs and practices, and maintain the headcount sheet and KRA tracker for all full-time employees.

HR MIS, Attendance &

Benefits

Track and update the weekly attendance dashboard for Pune-based employees, monitor hybrid employee physical presence, and maintain the employee referral tracker while verifying eligibility for quarterly payout cycles with HRBPs.

Pune Office Administration & Engagement: Coordinate employee ID card creation/printing, collaborate with Pune IT to ensure laptops, IT assets, and access cards are assigned on or before Day 1, manage welcome kit inventory, and partner with Admin for monthly birthday celebrations and engagement activities.

Key Skillsets

Communication & Interpersonal: Good verbal and written communication skills paired with strong coordination, follow-up, and interpersonal abilities.

Organization & Attention to Detail: Strong organizational and time-management abilities with high accuracy in maintaining employee records and HR trackers.

Technical & Workspace Tools: Proficiency in Google Sheets and Google Workspace, alongside a willingness to learn HRMS platforms, tools, and other HR systems.

Discretion & Ownership: Ability to handle confidential employee information with discretion, backed by a proactive approach, positive attitude, and willingness to take ownership of responsibilities.

Qualifications

Freshers or candidates with 0–1 year of experience passionate about building a career in Human Resources.

Ability to work full-time from the Pune office (all 5 days WFO) for a 6-month internship duration.

Demonstrated proficiency in Google Workspace (especially Google Sheets) and strong interest in HR Operations, Background Verification, HRMS tools, and Employee Engagement.
