Digital Buildings Analyst

FPC Global · Remote, India

About the role

To support the delivery of consultancy and systems engineering projects focused on Digital Building technologies, applications, processes, and standards. Representing FPC in all client and partner interactions, the Digital Buildings Analyst must maintain high standards of technical and professional performance, and conduct themselves with unwavering honesty and integrity at all times.

Key responsibilities

include: Conduct technical analysis and feasibility assessments for Digital Building solutions and implementations Develop and maintain comprehensive project documentation, specifications, and technical reports Collaborate with cross-functional teams, clients, and external partners to ensure project objectives are met Support the design and deployment of Digital Building systems, ensuring compliance with industry standards and best practices Identify, troubleshoot, and resolve technical issues and system challenges in a timely manner Contribute to continuous process improvement initiatives and knowledge sharing within the organisation Perform quality assurance reviews to ensure deliverables meet agreed specifications and client expectations Provide technical guidance and support to project teams and stakeholders throughout project lifecycles Key

Responsibilities

Programme and Project Management Deliver defined tasks and workstreams under the direction of senior team members in accordance with the agreed Scope of Work and Engagement Letter. Produce accurate analysis, data outputs, and reports to a high standard. Support project teams reliably across multiple concurrent assignments. Client Management Support senior consultants in client-facing activities under supervision. Prepare data, analysis, and materials to support client deliverables. Conduct yourself professionally in all interactions with clients and stakeholders. Services and Solutions Delivery Assist with the production of technical reports, documentation, and project outputs. Support the team in delivering Digital Building consulting services. Carry out research and technical analysis to inform project work. Business Development Support proposals with research and background data. Prepare data and analysis to support business development activities. Assist with bid preparation activities as directed. Industry Expertise Build foundational knowledge of Digital Building technologies and industry trends. Engage with internal training, mentoring, and knowledge-sharing initiatives. Develop a growing understanding of FPC's service offerings and client sectors. Innovation & Culture Contribute to improving project delivery processes, tools, and frameworks Support lessons learned, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement initiatives Encourage adoption of best practices and efficient ways of working Promote a collaborative and high-performance team culture FPC Corporate

Responsibilities

Represent FPC professionally in all client and internal interactions Lead and support project teams, fostering collaboration and performance Adhere to company policies, governance standards, and reporting requirements Support continuous improvement across delivery operations Skills and Experience Essential: Education: Bachelor's degree (or working toward one) in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline. Foundational understanding of building systems, controls, or IoT technologies. Strong analytical skills with the ability to produce accurate, well-structured reports. Proficiency in Google Suite and Microsoft Office applications. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Desirable: Any exposure to Digital Building technologies, BMS, or IoT systems. Familiarity with design or documentation tools such as AutoCAD or Visio. Experience in a consulting, engineering, or technology environment (including internships or placements). Values Balance and Inclusion: We celebrate diversity, foster collaboration, and ensure every voice is heard—while maintaining a strong commitment to work-life balance.
