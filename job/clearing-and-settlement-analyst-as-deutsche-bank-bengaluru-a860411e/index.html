Clearing and Settlement Analyst, AS

Deutsche Bank · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description: Job Title: Clearing and Settlement Analyst, AS Location: Bangalore, India Role Description This role is supporting for Commodities Pre and Post settlement processes of Interbank /Corporate clients. The responsibility of this role is to ensure all the Commodities Trades to be settled on value date before the respective currency cut off's to avoid any financial risk to the bank. The role also needs to support all client queries and manage client exceptions on real time basis. The nature of the process is dynamic, it requires to deal with Energy and Emission settlements which are critical in nature and timely action required to avoid risks and escalations. You will be responsible to ensure that all tasks assigned to the team are done accurately and all the exceptions items are followed up to resolution. You will also facilitate projects, group initiatives and exception processing resolution. Our team of experts will be there to coach and support your development to ensure you excel in this role.
