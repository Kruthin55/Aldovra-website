Conference Content Executive

RELX · Chennai

About the role

Are you a content professional looking to develop your career? Do you have conference content management experience? About our Team: The team are passionate, collaborative, and high-performing, dedicated to the development and growth of these exceptional conferences in a booming and vibrant industry. We value creativity, autonomy, and accountability, working together to deliver exceptional conference programs. The environment is supportive, with a strong focus on teamwork and innovation, ensuring that each member's contributions are valued and impactful. About the Role: Come and join our vibrant team to help delight our customers by providing exceptional service and establishing our conferences as most effective direct customer touchpoints for our journals. We are offering a creative role with independence and responsibility for a conference Content Executive.

Responsibilities

Manage conference preparation timelines to keep activities on track. Coordinate with internal and external teams to meet deadlines and provide authors and team members with all required information. Confirm the proposed program structure with the Project Lead and Chair, ensuring it fits time, venue and poster-space constraints. Track author registrations against accepted abstracts, update replacement speakers in the system as needed, and follow up with authors to register by the deadline. Compile all required files for online and print versions. Respond to all email queries within 48 hours. Support the Conference Project Lead onsite with setup, registration, delegate queries and all activities needed to run a successful conference. Travel to approximately eight conferences annually.

Requirements

Demonstrate a strong passion for customer service. Have customer service experience. Have project management experience. Demonstrate meticulous attention to detail and strong analytical skills. Possess exceptional communication skills and an understanding of cross-cultural communication. Manage challenges with agility and flexibility to deliver timely, quality services. Hold a valid passport. Have international travel experience, whether for business or personal purposes. Work in a way that works for you: We promote a healthy work/life balance across the organisation. We offer an appealing working prospect for our people. With numerous wellbeing initiatives, shared parental leave, study assistance and sabbaticals, we will help you meet your immediate responsibilities and your long-term goals. Working for you: We know that your wellbeing and happiness are key to a long and successful career.
