Associate Employee Relations

IQVIA · Mumbai

About the role

Key

Responsibilities

Advanced Employee Support: Address complex employee inquiries and issues, providing expert guidance and support. Conflict Resolution: Lead the resolution of escalated employee conflicts and grievances, ensuring fair and timely outcomes. Policy Compliance: Ensure strict adherence to company policies and employment laws, advising management on compliance matters. Investigations: Conduct in-depth investigations into employee complaints, including harassment, discrimination, and other workplace issues. Documentation: Maintain detailed and accurate records of employee relations cases and actions taken. Training and Development: Develop and deliver advanced employee relations training programs for managers and employees. Strategy Development: Assist in the development and implementation of employee relations strategies and initiatives. Reporting: Prepare comprehensive reports on employee relations activities, trends, and outcomes. Key Tasks: ER advice & guidance, casework support, draft letters review. Producing complete investigation documentation Own & maintain ER SOP’s, HR/Manager training, Reporting of ER matters, harmonization activities, project work. Counsel business managers to help them understand and proactively address employee issues. Investigations of harassment and discrimination claims Termination as a result of disciplinary action Identify, investigate, collect evidence, analyze findings and recommend solutions/ closure actions for ER issues, in consultation with HR lead, Legal, ECO and other COEs. Analyzing data from various reporting systems to ensure swift progress on cases Management of stakeholders as related to the case ensuring appropriate closure of the matter.
