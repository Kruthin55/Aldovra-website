SHE Executive

Unilever · Mumbai

About the role

Job Tite: Safety, Health & Environment (SHE) Executive Location: Mumbai HURC The Opportunity The R&D Centre brings together laboratories, pilot plants, engineering systems and a diverse technical community working with chemicals, enzymes, solvents, fragrances, scientific equipment & instruments, engineering projects, pressure systems and emerging technologies. This role will translate SHE strategy into disciplined daily execution, strengthen risk controls and help teams innovate safely, responsibly at R&D centre and satellite sites. Role Purpose Drive end-to-end Safety, Health and Environment performance across the R&D Centre and associated locations. The role will embed robust risk management, regulatory compliance, process and laboratory safety, site security and surveillance systems, contractor safety, emergency readiness and a proactive safety culture, while using digital tools and data to improve assurance and decision-making. The position is expected to convert plans into visible field action, personally drive priority interventions and ensure disciplined closure of commitments with clear owners, timelines and evidence of effectiveness. Key Accountabilities 1. Risk Assessment, Laboratory Safety & Process Safety

Lead and facilitate HIRA, task hazard analysis, environmental risk assessment, HAZOP and other fit-for-purpose risk studies for laboratories, pilot plants, utilities, R&D as well as Infrastructure projects and new technologies.

Strengthen controls for chemical handling, storage, segregation, labelling, dispensing and disposal, including flammables, compressed gases, enzymes and other sensitising or hazardous materials.

Maintain risk registers and ensure actions are prioritised, owned, verified for effectiveness and closed on time.

Support process hazard analysis, pre-start-up safety reviews, safe operating limits and critical control verification for pilot-scale and engineering systems. 2. SHE Management Systems & Compliance

Implement applicable Unilever SHE standards and site procedures covering occupational safety, health, environment, chemical management, contractor safety and process safety.

Maintain legal and other requirement as per Factory Act and Shop & Establishment act as applicable, coordinate inspections, licenses, returns and statutory examinations with relevant authorities and competent persons.

Support compliance with applicable factory, fire, environmental, electrical, pressure-system and radiation-safety requirements, where relevant to the site.

Plan and execute internal audits, workplace inspections and compliance reviews; drive timely corrective and preventive action closure.

Support BSC,ISO 45001, ISO 14001 and relevant quality/GMP management systems, as applicable. 3. Incident Prevention, Investigation & Learning

Promote timely reporting of incidents, near misses, unsafe conditions and safe/unsafe behaviours.

Lead or support evidence-based incident investigations using appropriate root-cause analysis methods; define CAPA and verify sustainable closure.

Identify high-potential events and deploy lessons learned across laboratories, pilot plants, projects and support teams.

Use leading and lagging indicators to identify trends, anticipate risk and focus preventive action. 4. Emergency Preparedness, Fire Safety & Crisis Readiness

Maintain site emergency response plans, scenario-based response protocols, evacuation arrangements and mutual-aid interfaces.

Coordinate emergency response team capability, drills and exercises covering fire, chemical spill, medical emergency, utility failure and severe weather scenarios.

Assure readiness and maintenance of fire detection, alarm, hydrant, sprinkler, suppression and portable firefighting systems.

Conduct fire and loss-prevention reviews for laboratories, pilot plants, chemical stores and project activities. 5. Contractor, Project & Permit-to-Work Safety

Own or govern contractor SHE prequalification, induction, competency verification, field supervision and performance review.

Ensure effective permit-to-work controls for hot work, confined space, work at height, electrical isolation, excavation, lifting and other high-risk activities.

Provide SHE input to design, procurement, installation, commissioning and handover of new or modified facilities and equipment.

Embed safety-by-design, Management of Change and pre-start-up assurance in capital projects and operational changes. 6. Occupational Health, Ergonomics & Wellbeing

Partner with Occupational Health to identify and control exposure to chemicals, dusts, vapours, noise, biological agents and ergonomic risks.

Coordinate exposure monitoring, health surveillance inputs and workplace improvement plans based on risk.

Promote inclusive workplace safety, accessible emergency arrangements and fit-for-task controls. 7. Safety Culture, Capability & Stakeholder Partnership

Coach leaders, line managers, safety committee members and safety champions to strengthen visible ownership and proactive risk leadership.

Design and deliver engaging inductions, toolbox talks, role-based training, campaigns and emergency-response capability programmes.

Partner with scientists, technical project leaders, engineering, facilities, procurement, HR, security and external specialists to integrate SHE into everyday work.

Facilitate safety committees and cross-functional governance forums, ensuring decisions and actions are documented and followed through. 8. Digital SHE, Data & Continuous Improvement

Use digital systems for permit-to-work, incident and CAPA tracking, inspections, training, chemical inventory and compliance assurance.

Build clear dashboards covering leading indicators, critical controls, audit findings, action ageing, training and incident trends.

Apply data analysis and practical automation to reduce manual dependency, improve visibility and enable predictive risk management.

Drive continuous improvement using PDCA, Kaizen or equivalent structured problem-solving approaches. 9. Environmental Performance & Resource Stewardship

Support environmental aspect-impact assessments and controls for waste, effluent, emissions, water, energy and hazardous-material management.

Promote waste minimisation, responsible disposal and sustainability improvements without compromising product, process or people safety.

Monitor environmental performance and support regulatory reporting and improvement plans. 10. Site Security & Business Continuity Interface

Coordinate with site security to align access control, surveillance, visitor/contractor management and protection of critical or sensitive areas with SHE risk controls.

Support business continuity, crisis response and high-profile visit readiness from a safety and operational-risk perspective. 11. Operational Execution, Field Assurance & Closure

Translate annual and monthly SHE priorities into practical execution plans with defined deliverables, owners, milestones and measurable outcomes.

Maintain a strong field presence and directly lead routine safety walks, critical-risk verifications, permit checks, contractor interventions and follow-up reviews across laboratories, pilot plants, utilities and project areas.

Coordinate day-to-day execution of statutory inspections, audits, drills, training, exposure monitoring, equipment testing and compliance activities, ensuring readiness of people, systems and records.

Drive actions to closure through structured tracking, regular review and escalation of overdue or ineffective controls; validate completion through field evidence rather than administrative closure alone.

Support safe execution of shutdowns, installations, commissioning, high-risk work and operational changes by aligning stakeholders, resolving constraints and assuring controls at the point of work.
