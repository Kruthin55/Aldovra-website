Business Development Representative

ServiceNow · Bengaluru

About the role

It all started in sunny San Diego, California in 2004 when a visionary engineer, Fred Luddy, saw the potential to transform how we work. Fast forward to today — ServiceNow stands as a global market leader, bringing innovative AI-enhanced technology to over 8,100 customers, including 85% of the Fortune 500®. Our intelligent cloud-based platform seamlessly connects people, systems, and processes to empower organizations to find smarter, faster, and better ways to work. But this is just the beginning of our journey. Join us as we pursue our purpose to make the world work better for everyone. We’re looking for a motivated, and results-driven Business Development Representative (BDR) to join ServiceNow’s Global Business Acceleration team.   As a BDR, you’ll play a critical role in driving pipeline growth by identifying, engaging, and qualifying potential customers. Through strategic outreach, inbound lead follow-up and engagement with prospects from marketing campaigns, you’ll help connect organizations with solutions that address their business challenges.   In this role, you’ll engage prospective customers, uncover business needs, assess interest using established qualification criteria, and collaborate with Account Executives to advance qualified opportunities. You’ll develop foundational skills in communication, prospecting, and relationship building.   Whether you're launching your career or bringing experience from another industry, GBA is more than your first role at ServiceNow. It's an opportunity to discover your potential, accelerate your growth, and build a career with a company shaping the future of work.  What you get to do in this role:  Generate new business pipeline through a combination of outbound prospecting, inbound lead qualification, and follow-up on marketing campaigns and events.   Engage current and prospective customers through phone, email, and social—and connect with key contacts using approved messaging and defined qualification criteria.  Research companies, industries, and business challenges to identify opportunities where ServiceNow can deliver value.   Apply basic problem-solving techniques to assess client needs and determine appropriate next steps based on defined criteria.  Qualify opportunities and partner with account executives to build strong sales pipeline.   Take ownership of individual performance goals, embrace coaching and feedback, and contribute to a collaborative, high-performing sales culture.  What you gain from this role:  An immersive four-week new hire training program designed to build your sales skills, confidence, and knowledge of the ServiceNow platform.   A collaborative global community that celebrates successes, encourages innovation, and invests in your future.   A strong foundation in technology sales, with real-world experience and ongoing development opportunities to help shape your career at ServiceNow.   To be successful in this role you have:  High school diploma or GED equivalent required  Strong verbal and written communication skills and the ability to engage professionally and confidently with customers and internal stakeholders.   Demonstrate initiative, curiosity, and a results-oriented mindset, with a willingness to take ownership of goals and responsibilities.  Strong interpersonal and relationship-building skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams.   Resilience, adaptability, and growth mindset.   Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple priorities.   Willingness to learn AI-native tools and innovate on how they’re used to enhance outreach, prioritization, and daily workflows.  A basic understanding of cloud computing and SaaS concepts, or the willingness to learn quickly.  Ability to participate in development simulations, in-person messaging activities, and meet defined performance and metrics expectations.   Commitment to full participation in Global Business Acceleration development tracks to launch an enduring career at ServiceNow within 48 months.    Work Personas We approach our distributed world of work with flexibility and trust.
