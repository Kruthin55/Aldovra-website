Tax Analyst

Wells Fargo · Hyderabad

About the role

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Tax Analyst , this role supports the maintenance of a robust indirect tax control environment across the international region (primarily EMEA), helping achieve the primary objective of ensuring the accuracy of the GST / VAT returns and records across entities within EMEA and the timely and accurate financial reporting to internal and external stakeholders. In this role, you will: Perform moderately complex to complex tax accounting activities related to the preparation of federal, state and local income tax returns or the financial statement accounting for income taxes Prepare comprehensive tax work papers, perform account analysis, and prepare responses to audit requests Research memos or correspondence and embody and model compliance and risk management requirements for supported area Exercise proficiency in accounting and multi-jurisdictional income tax laws, rules and regulations Develop knowledge of federal and state income tax laws, rulings, regulations and judicial decisions Perform basic income tax research Maintain records related to income tax returns and reports Work with moderate to minimal supervision Provide functional advice to less experienced staff Consult on income tax related matters with lines of business and other control functions Develop relationships with assigned business units related to the collection and analysis of information important to income tax accounting or compliance Work with business partners to collect internal financial or tax data, perform data analysis, test for accuracy, identify and resolve data problems Participate in projects Communicate and provide consultation regularly with mid-level stakeholders companywide Performance of legal entity related financial control and EMEA VAT reporting tasks, including: Initial preparation of UK and EMEA VAT returns in Excel and Alteryx. Performing period-end tax accounting functions such as creating journal entries, monitoring tax accounts positions, performing tax account reconciliations, substantiation of GL balances, initiating payment/refund processes etc. Assist with data collection from a variety of sources, ensure the data is accurate, reconciled to the GL and arrives on time for the report to which it is used. Demonstrate clearly a good understanding of international indirect tax principles. Running the monthly and quarterly Alteryx processes for VAT filings. Ensuring that any running errors are dealt with in a timely fashion. Ensuring that internal error reports are clear and running the process to a strict timetable. Use a variety of technologies, such as Alteryx, PowerBI and A.I. tools (Co-pilot) etc to improve any given process and assist team with IT automation projects. Completing the internal controls checklist to ensure all controls are run accurately and on a timely basis. Assisting with RCSA (risk and control) implementation and updates. Managing delivery of responses to internal and external VAT accounting audits. Collating VAT return analytics and trend analysis for technical reviews etc. Updating control procedure docs. Explaining Alteryx or IT process in a simplified manner and preparing documents/backup as may be required. Constantly challenge the way processes are run within the wider tax team and assist on making changes to those processes where required. Required

Qualifications

2+ years of Corporate Tax, Finance, Accounting, Analytics, Reporting experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Experience working on EMEA VAT/GST accounting and other tax reporting requirements in a financial services organization. Knowledge of and experience in indirect tax concepts and tax accounting treatment. Advanced Microsoft Office skills (specifically Excel) and ability to manipulate and review large data sets. Experience with and working knowledge of Alteryx (Core User certificate if possible). Desired

Qualifications

Qualified accountant: 2 yr+ PQE. Attention to detail and accuracy. Strong communication skills and not afraid to ask questions. Good time management and organizational skills. Self-motivated/proactive approach, with the ability to work independently within a team structure. Innovative mindset, with a track record of process enhancement (enhancing efficiency, control, transparency). Strong analytical and problem solving skills Knowledge of tools such as Power BI and A.I. tools and similar considered an advantage.
