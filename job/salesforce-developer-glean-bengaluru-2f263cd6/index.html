About Glean

At its core, Glean is redefining how enterprises find, use, and act on knowledge. Its Enterprise Graph and Personal Knowledge Graph map the relationships between people, content, and activity, delivering deeply personalized, context-aware responses for every employee. This foundation powers Glean’s agentic capabilities - AI agents that automate real work across teams by accessing the industry’s broadest range of data: enterprise and world, structured and unstructured, historical and real-time. The result: measurable business impact through faster onboarding, hours of productivity gained each week, and smarter, safer decisions at every level.

Recognized by Fast Company as one of the World’s Most Innovative Companies (Top 10, 2025), by CNBC’s Disruptor 50, Bloomberg’s AI Startups to Watch (2026), Forbes AI 50, and Gartner’s Tech Innovators in Agentic AI, Glean continues to accelerate its global impact. With customers across 50+ industries and 1,000+ employees in more than 25 countries, we’re helping the world’s largest organizations make every employee AI-fluent, and turning the superintelligent enterprise from concept into reality.

You'll be deeply embedded where people get things done. About the Role

Glean is seeking a Salesforce Developer to design, develop, and continuously improve our Salesforce platform and its ecosystem of integrations. You will partner closely with Sales, Finance, Operations, and Engineering to build scalable, secure, and reliable solutions that support the full quote-to-cash and go-to-market lifecycle.

This role is ideal for an experienced hands-on developer who can translate complex business requirements into maintainable Salesforce solutions, independently own technical delivery, and help establish strong engineering, testing, and release practices as Glean scales.

You will

Drive the technical implementation of Salesforce enhancements, from technical design and development through testing, deployment support, documentation, and post-release issue resolution.

Design and develop scalable solutions using Apex, Lightning Web Components, Salesforce Flow, validation rules, asynchronous processing, and platform automation.

Build and maintain integrations between Salesforce and internal and third-party systems using REST/SOAP APIs, platform events, middleware, and integration patterns appropriate to the use case.

Contribute to the evolution of Salesforce as the system of record for opportunity, quote, order, customer, and other go-to-market data.

Support quote-to-cash capabilities, including Revenue Cloud or CPQ-related processes, product and pricing workflows, order management, approvals, and downstream fulfillment integrations.

Translate business requirements into clear technical designs, data models, user experiences, and implementation plans.

Establish and maintain high standards for code quality, test coverage, security, performance, observability, and long-term maintainability.

Follow and contribute to deployment and release practices across environments, including source control, CI/CD, code reviews, release planning, and rollback readiness.

Investigate production issues, lead root-cause analysis, and implement durable fixes that prevent recurrence.

Partner with administrators, analysts, architects, and business stakeholders to balance speed, usability, technical debt, and platform governance.

Contribute to Salesforce architecture, integration standards, documentation, and operating procedures as the platform and team grow.

Collaborate with administrators, developers, and business stakeholders to deliver reliable solutions and continuously improve the platform.

About you

7+ years of hands-on Salesforce development experience, including ownership of complex implementations in a fast-growing or enterprise environment.
