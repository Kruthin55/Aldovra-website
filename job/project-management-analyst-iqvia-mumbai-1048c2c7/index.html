Project Management Analyst,

IQVIA · Mumbai

About the role

Job Posting Title: Project Management Analyst, Real World Evidence Location: Home-based/Hybrid RWE Solutioning & Project Leadership (S&PL) Overview: As S&PL, we design and deliver innovative, technology-enabled RWE evidence programs for the pharmaceutical industry. We apply scientific rigor and advanced analytics to real world data in order to help our clients improve patients’ health. Our team works globally, partnering with regional colleagues throughout IQVIA to diagnose critical business issues and deliver evidence-based solutions. We are strategic thinkers, innovative technologists, deep subject matter experts and data specialists passionate about driving better performance in healthcare. We are collaborative, intellectually curious, entrepreneurial and disruptive.

Responsibilities

As an RWE Project Management Analyst, you will support the delivery of technology-enabled real-world evidence programs for pharmaceutical and life sciences clients. You will be working with Project Managers and cross-functional scientific, operational and technical teams to coordinate project activities, maintain documentation and financial information, and support reporting and communications. You will gain experience across site-based, direct-to-patient, database, survey and hybrid studies while developing project management, analytical and client-facing skills.

Requirements

Bachelor's degree in life sciences, public health, healthcare, business, or a related field with 0-2 years of relevant professional experience. Knowledge of project management principles, with an interest in healthcare, life sciences and evidence generation. Strong planning, organization, time-management and prioritization skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities. Proactive, adaptable and accountable approach to learning, ownership, collaboration, innovation and growth. Knowledge of applicable clinical research regulations, including ICH-GCP and relevant local requirements, is a plus where relevant to assigned projects. Why Join IQVIA: Join a recognized global leader in RWE that challenges the status quo to improve patient care. Grow with an organization that encourages and invests into continuous professional and personal development. Shape evidence generation strategies and deliver a broad range of RWE studies with the most cutting-edge technology, the largest data sets and best in class experts. Build your career by gaining exposure to diverse geographies, capabilities and vast therapeutic, information and technology areas. Work in a flexible mixed model that allows working from home or your closest IQVIA office. IQVIA is a leading global provider of clinical research services, commercial insights and healthcare intelligence to the life sciences and healthcare industries. We create intelligent connections to accelerate the development and commercialization of innovative medical treatments to help improve patient outcomes and population health worldwide. We appreciate your honesty and professionalism.
