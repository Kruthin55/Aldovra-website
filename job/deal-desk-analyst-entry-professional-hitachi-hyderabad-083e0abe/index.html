Job description

Good to have knowledge of O2C cycle & SOW, Contract Management Experience on project creation, Contract types. Oracle or any ERP for Project Code creation will be Plus advantage. Preparing Reports and Dashboards for leadership team. Hence, working knowledge of Adv. Excel is required. Driving month end and PRC Cost rate exception Process. Responsible for Yearend activities for OMF updates, Cost rate Updates and other Project extension activities. Operational Support for handling various tasks like Bill Rates changes, transferring time of resources. Good communication and analytical skills. Self-motivator and interested in taking initiatives and automating processes. Punctual and disciplined. Open for UK shifts #LI-BJ1
