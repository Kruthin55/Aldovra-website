After Sales Service Engineer

Hitachi · Remote, India

About the role

Location: Maneja, Gujarat, India Job ID: R0153485 Date Posted: 2026-11-26 Company Name: HITACHI ENERGY INDIA LIMITED Profession (Job Category): Engineering & Science Job Schedule: Full time Remote: No Job Description: Demonstrate a strong commitment to Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) standards, polices and guidelines. Perform installation, testing, commissioning, and service activities for power transformers, OLTCs, and related equipment at customer sites and Hitachi Energy projects. Maintain and regularly update Service Engineer movement records, deputation details and site activity records. Prepare and maintain service activity reports for visibility of team performance and ongoing activities. Maintain COPQ data and coordinate with the Quality team for regular reporting and updates. Manage company service assets and equipment, including allocation, movement tracking, inventory, calibration, repair, maintenance and proper storage. Coordinate TPC engineer’ invoices, contractor bills and customer invoicing for ETC projects and supervision activities, including timesheet verification, man-day calculations and invoice documentation. Coordinate vendor purchase orders, commercial offers, price comparisons, vendor finalization, ARC renewals and vendor qualification activities in coordination with SCM. Maintain and verify project site documentation, service records and other required project documents, ensuring proper e-filing and audit readiness. Follow-up and actions for free supply of materials for closure of pending activities at site. Maintain and update ISO documentation for Global and internal audits, including tracking audit observations and ensuring timely compliance. Coordinate safety requirements at project sites, including safety equipmentS, ABRA preparation and approvals from HSE, night work permissions and contractor’s safety compliance. Maintain safety training and certification records of Service Engineers, including PICW, Confined Space, First Aid and other mandatory training programmes. Conduct periodic site visits to review safety compliance, contractor activities, equipment storage and overall site conditions. Handle SAP/Reiwa-related activities for After Sales & Services activities and ensure compliance with applicable company processes and procedures. Coordinate with Project Managers, Service Engineers, Finance, SCM, Quality, HSE, vendors and contractors for timely completion of assigned activities.
