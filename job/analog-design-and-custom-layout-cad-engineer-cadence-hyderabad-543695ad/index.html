Analog design and Custom Layout CAD engineer

Cadence · Hyderabad

About the role

At Cadence, we hire and develop leaders and innovators who want to make an impact on the world of technology. Cadence Design Systems is seeking Analog CAD engineers to support the growing analog design and custom layout team building IP using the Cadence design tools (Virtuoso, Pegasus, Quantus, etc.) to build high speed interfaces used in chips found in cars, cell phones, data centers, etc. You will get the opportunity to collaborate with circuit implementation experts to contribute to the development of exciting chips, work with the best-in-class EDA tools and interact with the Cadence engineers using developing those tools to improve those tools. Analog CAD tasks The Analog CAD team is a part of the Cadence Design IP development group responsible for supporting the design and layout teams as they design analog IP circuits. They support the design teams in the different steps of the design and layout flow: design capture, pre and post-layout simulation, optimization, layout, EM/IR analysis, thermal analysis, reliability, 2.5D and 3D IC, etc. We also collaborate with the best in class tools developed by Cadence to improve them and tailor them to our needs. We also build tools to help increase the efficiency of the teams and the quality of the circuits. We collaborate with the teams to improve their flows and design methodologies. Additional BSEE, MSEE Previous experience in Analog CAD support Very good understanding of circuit design and layout design Exposure to Cadence Virtuoso Layout and physical verification tools is a plus Team player, driven, self-motivated, innovative and autonomous Excellent communication, presentation and customer service skills We’re doing work that matters. Help us solve what others can’t.
