Data Analyst - GA4, SQL, Snowflake, Tablue/PowerBI

Thomson Reuters · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description The Data Analyst, Audience & Product Analytics turns audience, product and marketing data into trusted insights and reusable analytical products that improve decision-making across Reuters. Working within the unified Reuters Data & Analytics function, the role partners with Product, Editorial, Marketing, Commercial and Technology teams to define success measures, strengthen data quality and deliver measurable audience, product and commercial outcomes. The analyst uses governed data, shared KPI definitions and approved AI tools to automate routine work, accelerate analysis and enable effective self-service. Key

Responsibilities

Turn business questions into analytical approaches and measurable outcomes. Analyse audience behaviour, app and site experience, subscriptions, and marketing performance. Build reusable metrics and decision-ready reporting on governed datasets. Contribute to the semantic layer, KPI catalogue, and self-service tools. Validate analytics for launches across GA4, Segment, and Snowflake; monitor data quality. Automate QA and recurring analysis with approved AI tools, with human review. Communicate findings and recommended actions clearly. Required

Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative or related field, or equivalent experience. 5+ years in audience, web, product, or digital analytics. Hands-on GA4, tag management, and event tracking experience. Strong SQL (ideally Snowflake) and working Python. Tableau or Power BI for reporting. Understanding of KPI design and metric governance. Experience using AI-assisted workflows, with sound judgement on validating outputs and data privacy. Strong communication, stakeholder management, and critical thinking. Preferred

Qualifications

Customer data platforms (Segment, Tealium, Adobe Audience Manager). Engagement platforms (Braze, Sailthru). Mobile app debugging tools (Charles, Proxyman, Firebase DebugView). Experimentation, A/B testing, or causal measurement. Experience in media, publishing, or subscription businesses. #LI-KP1 What’s in it For You?
