Prophet Actuarial Analyst in Life Insurance Domain – EMEA Shift - Pune

FIS · Pune

About the role

Prophet Actuarial Analyst in Life Insurance Domain – EMEA Shift - Pune Are you curious, motivated, and forward-thinking? At FIS you’ll have the opportunity to work on some of the most challenging and relevant issues in financial services and technology. Our talented people empower us, and we believe in being part of a team that is open, collaborative, entrepreneurial, passionate and above all fun.

About the team

FIS Prophet is an actuarial production platform consisting of a suite of applications covering model development through to results and for automating end-to-end production workflow. Provide senior actuarial and functional expertise for Prophet, supporting clients and internal teams with complex life insurance modelling and actuarial questions.The role should be particularly strong in Prophet modelling, life insurance, reinsurance and pricing , providing the actuarial expertise necessary to investigate and resolve complex functional queries. What you will be doing Your primary responsibilities will be to provide a high level of client experience in form of support and technical expertise for helping clients get the best use out of the applications and to further develop the product.

Key responsibilities

Provide actuarial and functional support for client queries related to Prophet models and calculations under client services team. Analyze complex client questions from life insurance and actuarial perspectives. Determine whether issues stem from model design, actuarial assumptions, or configuration, or require further product investigation. Collaborate with technical support and product teams on cases requiring both actuarial and technical expertise. Clearly explain complex actuarial and Prophet concepts to clients and internal stakeholders. Share actuarial expertise across the broader Prophet support team. Support client presentations and knowledge-sharing sessions. Participate in client calls and escalations involving actuarial or product-specific questions, as needed. Communicate with clients on actuarial issues by using Service Now ticketing tool. Key

Requirements

Essential 5–10 years of hands-on IT experience. Strong actuarial background within Life Insurance. Strong hands-on Prophet modelling knowledge. Good understanding of life insurance products and actuarial calculations. Strong knowledge of reinsurance and/or pricing. Ability to analyse complex models and actuarial results. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Ability to communicate complex actuarial concepts clearly to both clients and non-actuarial colleagues. Comfortable working in a client-facing environment and taking ownership of complex functional queries. Highly desirable Experience in experience analysis and underwriting. Previous experience providing functional/actuarial support for Prophet users. Progress toward, or completion of, a recognized actuarial qualification, such as IFoA. Experience working across actuarial, product and technical teams.

Qualifications

Bachelor’s Degree in Actuarial Science [honours or 2.1/high level degree] – or – one year post-graduate actuarial experience and Masters Degree or higher in technology related field, or other relevant field of study, or the equivalent in education and experience.
