The Business Intelligence Analyst sits within the Media Sciences team, reporting into the Head of Media Sciences. This role requires a combination of technical expertise, data and analytical skills, working on data sourcing, analysis, reporting, and integration efforts. The ideal candidate will have a strong understanding of business intelligence principles, data warehousing concepts, and experience working with BI tools. The Media Sciences team leads the transformation of Citi’s media investment and measurement, while delivering positive return on marketing investment and future-proofing our business. The team works in close partnership with US and Global Digital Media, Analytics, Acquisitions, Advertising and Product Management teams. In this role, you’re expected to: Data Analysis and Reporting: Analyze business data, generate reports, and create compelling data visualizations using business intelligence tools like Power BI or Tableau. Develop and maintain dashboards to track key performance indicators (KPIs) and provide actionable insights to stakeholders. Convert business questions about key metrics to relevant views on dashboards, and design dashboards to drive relevant insights Data Integration: Work with external vendors and internal technology teams to navigate data landscape and regulations, sharing agreements, and integrate data from multiple data sources into a centralized data warehouse or reporting environment. Data Structure and QA flows : QA data flows and data structures from different systems to production reporting environments. Implement data validation procedures to ensure data integrity and accuracy throughout the data pipeline. Data Analysis and Interpretation: Conduct in-depth data analysis to identify trends, patterns, and insights that can inform business decisions. Communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders in a clear and concise manner. Accountability: Own and lead the dashboard and tech roadmap with the BI lead and oversee day-to-day reporting and deliverables. Operational Excellence: Utilize BI tools and platforms to create interactive dashboards and data visualizations. Support the end-to-end project lifecycle, from data collection, data structure and framework, QA. As a successful candidate, you’d ideally have the following skills and exposure: Minimum 3-5 years’ experience within data analysis, business intelligence, or a related field, either on the client side, agency side or publisher side Highly analytical, with a proven ability to distill data and numbers into business outcomes, derive and communicate strategic insights and implications for senior leadership Strong proficiency in BI tools and platforms such as Datorama, Tableau Microsoft Power BI. Solid understanding of data warehousing concepts, data modeling techniques, and ETL processes. Clear understanding of benefits and limitations of reporting platforms in terms of breadth of visualization options, customizability, interactivity, connectivity to databases, near real-time updates etc. Experience working with large datasets and relational databases (e.g., SQL Server, Oracle, MySQL). Outstanding data storytelling skills, and ability to package and present key trends from data into compelling insights Should be able to independently support and project manage workstreams involving multiple stakeholders, show strong sense of accountability and decisiveness in strong work ethic Must be process oriented and organized, operate efficiently to deliver high-quality work in a fast paced and dynamic environment across different teams Experience with cloud-based data platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP) is a plus.
