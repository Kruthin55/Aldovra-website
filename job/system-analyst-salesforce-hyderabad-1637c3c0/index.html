Responsibilities

Escalation Management & Incident Response Own escalations on cases, intake, and investigations across teams and time zones: set priority, route to the right engineering, SRE, or vendor owner, drive them to closure, and keep stakeholders informed. Run follow-the-sun handoffs so escalations keep moving between Mexico City, Dublin, and Hyderabad without losing context. Track escalation trends (volume, repeat causes, time to engage, time to resolve), and bring them to leads and platform engineering to drive fixes. Be the escalation point for Tier-2 analysts on complex cases and investigations. Be a frontline responder in incidents: connect first, assess impact, and use judgment on when to pull in Site Reliability Engineers (SREs). Manage the case, intake, and stakeholder communication workstream alongside Incident Command. SREs own incident resolution. Meet individual and team lifecycle SLAs: First Response Time (FRT), Next Response Time (NRT), and Average Resolution Time (ART), and case satisfaction (CSAT) targets while keeping backlog health. You are measured against the same targets as the team. Help L7 refine the intake and investigation lifecycle SLAs: mentor Systems Analysts on hitting them, and raise challenges where targets, routing, or processes make them hard to meet. Spot patterns in SLA misses and satisfaction feedback, and bring recommended fixes to the L7 and the team. Problem Management & RCA Ownership Own root-cause analysis and resolution for application bugs and recurring failures, and close RCAs with verified fixes. Troubleshoot complex declarative and low-code issues, and recommend code-level fixes to engineering partners. Prioritize technical debt against the backlog to improve stability, and recommend fixes. Reliability & Observability Design and implement proactive monitors, alerts, and automations for platform health. Contribute to design reviews and operating-model improvements (for example Operational Readiness practices). Use automation and AI to reduce manual work and move the team toward self-healing operations. Platform Administration & Enhancement Deliver complex declarative solutions (Flows, validation rules, permission models) and manage enhancement projects from inception through support transition. Own complex access and role-hierarchy design, bulk data loads and migrations, and sandbox management. Reliably handle standard administration so the team can focus on higher-value work. Process Engineering, SOPs & Support Reference Manuals Own process engineering for your platform area: templates, process design, capture, and lifecycle (create, review, update, retire) of lightweight SOPs and Support Reference Manuals (SRMs). This is process design, not technical writing. Request SRM inputs from Platform Engineering and create or manage them as needed, so support teams have the reference material they need before launch. Keep SOPs and SRMs current and findable, and use them to reduce repeat escalations. Write knowledge articles and business process maps that let Tier-1/Tier-2 teams resolve issues without escalation. Act as SME and point of contact for development and product partners. Security, SOX & Compliance Maintain compliance with security policies and change controls. Support technology compliance activities, audit reporting, and SOX controls, and help close findings. Follow the SDLC (Agile) and change, deployment, incident, and problem management processes. Collaboration & Growth Partner effectively with vendors, engineers, and business teams across time zones. Mentor Systems Analysts and new joiners, and share knowledge
