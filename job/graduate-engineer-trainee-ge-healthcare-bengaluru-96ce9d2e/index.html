GE HealthCare · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary Ensure an uninterrupted supply of materials to support production and sales. Work to resolve Materials issues while achieving and maintaining inventory business metrics. GE HealthCare is a leading global medical technology and digital solutions innovator. Our purpose is to create a world where healthcare has no limits. Unlock your ambition, turn ideas into world-changing realities, and join an organization where every voice makes a difference, and every difference builds a healthier world. Job Description Roles and

Responsibilities

The Manufacturing / Materials Planner (also known as on site as a Production Controller) is responsible for generating a Master Production Schedule (MPS) to meet required output with available capacity and releases job cards in line with the plan. The post holder will also manage shortages to maximize enablement and clear to build in order to drive on time starts and completes to MRP and collaborates closely with Flow Leaders/Production Line Managers to ensure buy-in and adherence to the plan. Generate daily and weekly production schedule Manage shortages to maximize clear to build and on time starts/finishes Release job cards in line with MRP and action exception messages Collaborate with ops team to provide output commits Define daily/weekly production goals to track adherence to the plan Collaborate with ops team to ensure resources are appropriately allocated and aligned to achieve the plan The applicant must be a good team player flexible and be able to work to tight schedules. Monitor the integrity of job card data to ensure MRP signals are correct Required

Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Mechanical /Electrical/Electronics/Biomedical Engineering Recent graduate or up to 1 year of relevant internship or industrial experience. Basic understanding of manufacturing processes and engineering fundamentals. Good analytical and problem-solving skills. Strong communication and teamwork skills. Strong interpersonal and leadership skills Strong problem solving skills Desired Characteristics Internship or academic project experience in manufacturing, production engineering. The successful candidate will be a motivated and enthusiastic individual Eagerness to learn in a fast-paced manufacturing environment. Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications for reporting and documentation. Positive attitude with strong ownership and collaboration skills.
