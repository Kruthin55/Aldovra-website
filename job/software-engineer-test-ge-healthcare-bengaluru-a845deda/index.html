Software Engineer (Test)

GE HealthCare · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary As a Automation Engineer (Test) ,you will be responsible for automation testing to deliver a high quality product, platform or sub system and ensuring it is released as per GE Healthcare Quality Management Systems. You will be defining, developing, and evolving software for testing in a fast paced and agile development environment using the latest software development technologies and infrastructure. GE HealthCare is a leading global medical technology and digital solutions innovator. Our purpose is to create a world where healthcare has no limits. Unlock your ambition, turn ideas into world-changing realities, and join an organization where every voice makes a difference, and every difference builds a healthier world. Job Description Role and

Responsibilities

Responsible for planning and implementing verification and validation strategies for PET-CT systems across system and subsystem levels. Build automated testing for product verification ensuring compliance with functional and nonfunctional requirements. Design, develop, and maintain automation frameworks using Python for scalable testing. Perform automation testing using Selenium and Playwright to ensure high-quality product releases. Develop and manage Docker-based test environments for reproducible and efficient execution. Perform testing and debugging in Linux-based environments to validate system reliability. Determine appropriate test environments, tools, and automation strategies aligned with product requirements. Collaborate closely with development, system, technical leads & architects and global teams to ensure end-to-end product quality. Participate in Agile ceremonies and contribute to continuous quality improvement initiatives. Support development of test infrastructure, instrumentation, and verification tools. Develop strong understanding of system behavior and contribute to improving testing efficiency and product quality. Work in global programs requiring cross-location collaboration, including extended or flexible work hours. Educational

Qualifications

B.E/B.Tech in Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or an equivalent engineering discipline. Desired Characteristics: Minimum 3+ years of experience in verification and validation of healthcare or medical device products. Strong expertise in Python programming and automation framework development. Hands-on experience with Selenium, Playwright for test automation. Experience with Docker for containerized test environments. Strong working knowledge of Linux systems. Domain expertise in Healthcare / Medical Devices. Good understanding of Software QA processes and Agile methodologies. Strong documentation skills for test design, scripts, and requirements traceability. Quality-focused mindset with high attention to detail. Good organizational, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Strong oral and written communication skills. Ability to document, plan, market, and execute programs.
