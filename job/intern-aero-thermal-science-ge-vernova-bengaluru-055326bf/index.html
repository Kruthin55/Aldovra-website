Intern – Aero-Thermal Science

GE Vernova · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary We are seeking a motivated and analytical Engineering Intern to join our research team. In this role, you will contribute to the advancement of aerothermal technology across our diverse portfolio of power generation and electrical products. You will work alongside world-class researchers to solve complex technical challenges, push the boundaries of current designs, and contribute to the innovation pipeline at GE Vernova. Job Description Essential Functions (Responsibilities): The primary responsibilities of Intern will be: Apply sound engineering principles to solve complex aerodynamic and thermal challenges for stationary and rotating components across gas, wind, and steam turbine platforms. Partner with local and global research teams to execute assigned projects, leveraging cross-functional expertise to deliver high-quality results. Collaborate with senior researchers to document findings and contribute to technical reports, internal white papers, or research publications. Interpret analysis results to identify performance bottlenecks and propose innovative design improvements that enhance product efficiency and reliability. Analyze current industry research trends to identify and document intellectual property (IP) opportunities, contributing to our innovation strategy. Ensure all technical data is documented rigorously and remain consistent with internal documentation standards, safety guidelines, and quality requirements. Required

Qualifications

M. Tech./ M. E. in Mechanical Engineering with specialization in Aerodynamics/Thermal/Thermal Science with excellent academic records Interest in aerodynamics analysis, heat transfer & flow analysis of any turbo machinery Experience with ANSYS CFX, ICEMCFD, UG-NX, ANSYS-THERMAL, HYPERWORKS, Fluent, STARCCM+, Open-Source Software etc. Must have excellent verbal and written communication skills Must have excellent analytical skills Desired Characteristics: Ability to work in a team environment with a positive “can do” attitude. Self-starter and ability to own work scope with excellent commitment Results oriented, ability to learn quickly, and strong interpersonal skills Good understanding of turbo machinery design Proficiency in any of the programming or scripting language Fortran, Visual Basic, Python will be added advantage etc. Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: No
