About the role

Total Number of Openings 1 Chevron Engineering and Innovation Excellence Center (ENGINE) in Bengaluru India brings together the resources and expertise of the Chevron global network with talent in India to enhance agility and technological innovation to optimize solutions for the world’s current and future energy challenges. As one of the leading energy providers worldwide, Chevron is involved in the production of crude oil and natural gas, manufacturing of transportation fuels, lubricants, petrochemicals, and additives, and the development of enabling technologies. Chevron's vision is to be the global energy company most admired for its people, partnerships, and performance. With a clear purpose to develop affordable, reliable, ever-cleaner energy that enables human progress, we believe human ingenuity has the power to solve any challenge and overcome any obstacle. Meeting the world’s growing energy needs requires the pursuit of innovations and advancements that deliver a better future for all. We are looking for an experienced Senior Java Developer with 8+ years of hands-on experience in designing and building enterprise-scale applications. The ideal candidate should be an innovative thinker with strong expertise in Java, Microservices, Docker, Kubernetes, Azure, and Enterprise Content Management (ECM) platforms such as Documentum, SharePoint. Key Skills & Experience 3-5 yrs years of Java development experience or any Programing Language Strong expertise in Spring Boot and Microservices Architecture Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes Experience designing, developing, and consuming REST APIs Strong knowledge of Azure Services and Azure REST API integrations Experience with Azure Service Bus, Event Grid, Key Vault, Azure AD/Entra ID Hands-on experience with anyone ECM platforms such as Documentum, Alfresco, Box, SharePoint, or FileNet Understanding of content management, workflows, metadata, and document lifecycle management Experience with CI/CD, Git, and cloud-native development practices Exposure to Azure AI Foundry, Generative AI, or AI-powered document/content solutions is highly desirable What We Are Looking For Out-of-the-box thinker with strong problem-solving skills Ability to drive technical design and architecture discussions Strong communication and stakeholder management skills Experience leading complex enterprise application development initiatives Self-driven individual capable of working independently and mentoring team members Chevron ENGINE supports global operations, supporting business requirements across the world. Accordingly, the work hours for employees will be aligned to support business requirements. The standard work week will be Monday to Friday. Working hours are 8:00am to 5:00pm or 1.30pm to 10.30pm.
