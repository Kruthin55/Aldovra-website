Intern

GE HealthCare · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary We are looking for an Intern to support the development of an early-stage prototype for a new product or technology solution. The role involves working with cross-functional teams on system design, requirement definition, prototype development, integration, testing, and documentation. This internship is ideal for candidates interested in systems engineering, innovation, and hands-on prototype development. GE HealthCare is a leading global medical technology and digital solutions innovator. Our purpose is to create a world where healthcare has no limits. Unlock your ambition, turn ideas into world-changing realities, and join an organization where every voice makes a difference, and every difference builds a healthier world. For more information visit: https://www.gehealthcare.in/ Job Description Role &

Responsibilities

Support system design for new proof of concept/prototype Assist in translating user needs into high-level system requirements Contribute to prototype development, integration, and testing activities Create and maintain design documentation, block diagrams, interface definitions, and use cases Participate in design discussions, feasibility analysis, and technical reviews Help identify technical risks, assumptions, and design trade-offs Strong documentation skills — ability to produce structured test reports, experiment logs, and result summaries Document results, learnings, and recommendations from prototype evaluations Qualifications/Requirements B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech in Systems, Mechatronics, Aerospace, or Systems Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, or related field. 70% or 7 CGPA aggregate in the current degree. No active backlogs at the time of application. Willing to work in GE HealthCare premises during the internship. Willing to work on any projects as assigned based on fitment and business need. Familiarity with Excel/Google Sheets, Python, or MATLAB for experiment logging, data analysis, and plotting Desired Characteristics Strong interest in system design, prototyping, and product development Academic/project experience in prototype building, integration, testing, or multidisciplinary engineering preferred Ability to work in a collaborative and fast-paced environment Exposure to testing, debugging, and performance evaluation Hands-on experience with programming is a plus. Willingness to learn new software tools and platforms based on project needs. Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and teamwork skills. Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: No
