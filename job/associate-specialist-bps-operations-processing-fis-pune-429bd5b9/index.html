Associate Specialist, BPS Operations Processing

FIS · Pune

About the role

The future of money is being built right now. At FIS, we help the world’s businesses, banks and communities move, manage and grow money with confidence. Join a team that values fresh thinking, works together to solve complex challenges, and creates technology that powers everyday life.

About the role

As an Account Services Associate Specialist, you will process account openings, closures and changes. You will answer client questions and keep account data accurate. You will follow set steps, meet service goals and raise risks or issues when needed. Your key responsibilities Process account openings, closures, changes and service requests on time. Review system reports, fix account issues and complete control checks. Answer client and team questions, share updates and raise open issues. Track work, prepare reports and suggest ways to improve quality and speed. Keep procedures current and help junior team members with daily issues. What you bring Required qualifications Nine or more years of experience in account services, investment operations or a related role. Knowledge of investment products, such as shares, bonds, mutual funds, derivatives, foreign securities and exchange-traded funds. Experience with audits, risk checks and controls in a regulated operations team. Strong client service, communication, review and time management skills. A bachelor’s or master’s degree in accounting, business management, business administration or a related field.

Preferred qualifications

Experience using the Global Plus system. Experience improving controls or automating manual work. Skills in Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Teams. The impact you’ll make You will join a team that provides back-office services to stockbrokers and wealth managers in the United Kingdom and Europe. As an Account Services Associate Specialist, you will help keep data accurate and meet client service goals. Your work will support safe, timely account service and a strong client experience. What we offer you Bring your ideas. Build your future. At FIS, you'll find the support, resources and opportunities to develop your career while helping shape the future of financial technology.
