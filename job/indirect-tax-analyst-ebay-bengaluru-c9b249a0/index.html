Indirect Tax Analyst

eBay · Bengaluru

About the role

At eBay, we're more than a global ecommerce leader — we’re changing the way the world shops and sells. Our platform empowers millions of buyers and sellers in more than 190 markets around the world. We’re committed to pushing boundaries and leaving our mark as we reinvent the future of ecommerce for enthusiasts. Our customers are our compass, authenticity thrives, bold ideas are welcome, and everyone can bring their unique selves to work — every day. We're in this together, sustaining the future of our customers, our company, and our planet. Join a team of passionate thinkers, innovators, and dreamers — and help us connect people and build communities to create economic opportunity for all. This is an exciting opportunity to launch your career in a highly visible role where you'll have real ownership from day one. As part of eBay's Americas Indirect Tax team, you'll partner with teams across GCX, Product, and Engineering to solve complex business challenges that directly impact millions of buyers and sellers. From investigating tax issues and improving customer experiences to analyzing data and influencing product enhancements, you'll gain valuable insight into how one of the world's leading ecommerce platforms operates while developing expertise at the intersection of tax, technology, and analytics. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys problem-solving, working with data, and collaborating across functions in a fast-paced environment. You'll build valuable skills in data analysis, technical research, and process improvement while contributing to high-impact initiatives that improve the customer experience and drive operational excellence. With opportunities to work alongside experienced professionals, influence scalable solutions, and continue growing within a purpose-driven, innovative company, this position is an excellent next step that will allow the successful candidate to take on meaningful responsibility and build a long-term career at eBay. Job Description: Ownership and main point of contact for escalated sales tax refunds related to taxability issues and buyer exemptions Collaborate with customer service & to resolve customer-initiated sales/VAT tax inquiries Engage with the Tax Product team to investigate root causes for incorrect application of taxability and provide recommendations solutions in collaboration with the broader IDT team. Review Monthly Vertex Data patches prior to application. Assist US-based Indirect Tax Team in with audit support by gathering and analyzing large data sets and other support documentation Assist US-based Indirect Tax Team with other special projects or miscellaneous duties as assigned Perform ad-hoc tax technical research Collaborate with the indirect tax team & finance innovation & transformation (FIT) team to identify opportunities to design and implement scalable solutions to streamline indirect tax processes.

Requirements

Knowledge of U.S. sales & use taxes, Canadian GST and additional global VAT systems Experience with Vertex and/or other tax engines a plus Capable of working independently while acting as part of a global tax team Strong issue identification and mitigation skills Ability to articulate complex concepts in a concise manner both written and verbally Proficiency with Excel, Google Suite, and tax research tools Experience with ChatGPT, Gemini, Tableau, Alteryx and other data wrangling tools a plus Basic

Qualifications

1-3 years of related corporate finance and/or accounting experience Bachelor’s degree or equivalent with emphasis in Finance, Information Systems, or Accounting. Advanced degree a plus Fluent in English – other languages helpful
