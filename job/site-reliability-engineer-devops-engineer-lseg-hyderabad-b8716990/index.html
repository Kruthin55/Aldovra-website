Site Reliability Engineer / DevOps Engineer

LSEG · Hyderabad

About the role

Future Talent: Site Reliability Engineer / DevOps Engineer (Talent Community – India) The Opportunity We are always looking for exceptional Site Reliability Engineers (SRE) and DevOps Engineers across India to help us build resilient, highly available, cloud-ready platforms that power mission‑critical systems. While we have a number of roles open with very specific requirements, this dedicated SRE/DevOps Talent Community allows us to connect and engage with strong engineers early. By joining our engineering community, you'll gain access to insights into our cloud architecture, engineering culture, reliability practices, and events—and be among the first to move directly into advanced interview stages when a matching role opens. What You’ll Do (When You Join Us) Architect & Build Design, implement, and optimize reliable, scalable infrastructure and cloud environments using Kubernetes, Docker, microservices, CI/CD, IaC, and automation frameworks. Build resilient distributed systems, high‑throughput pipelines, observability frameworks, and automated operational tooling. Optimize & Enhance Improve system performance, reliability, and availability by leveraging SLOs, SLIs, monitoring, chaos engineering, performance testing, and proactive incident prevention. Implement automation across deployments, scaling, monitoring, and incident response using Terraform, Helm, GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, or Jenkins. Collaborate Work closely with product owners, architects, platform engineers, backend teams, and security teams to build and operate production‑grade systems. Participate in solution designs, blameless post‑incident reviews, capacity planning, and operational readiness assessments. Mentor & Share Guide junior engineers, contribute to engineering forums, and participate in internal tech talks or knowledge‑sharing sessions around DevOps/SRE topics. What You’ll Bring Build With Purpose – Start with why. Solve real operational problems with measurable impact. Customer Driven – Prioritize reliability, uptime, and real‑world performance. Technically Courageous – Dive deep into failures, distributed systems, and complex infrastructure challenges. Learn and Be Curious – Stay ahead in cloud, automation, observability, and reliability engineering. Act as Owner – Treat production as your responsibility. Drive long‑term value and operational excellence. Lead with data – Use metrics, logs, traces, and experiments to guide improvements. Dive deep – Investigate issues across systems, networks, containers, and cloud layers. Build responsibly, deliver incrementally – Prioritize secure, scalable, reliable solutions. Collaborate to achieve – Communicate openly, work respectfully, and uphold engineering excellence. Why Connect Now? Skip the Line – Talent community members are prioritized for fast‑track evaluation. Exclusive Insights – Receive invites to SRE roundtables, cloud architecture sessions, and engineering roadmap previews. Career Alignment – We reach out only for roles aligned to your strengths—SRE, DevOps, Cloud, Platform Engineering, or Automation. How to Join Upload your CV along with a short note about: Your areas of strength: SRE, DevOps, Cloud, Kubernetes, Observability, Automation, IaC, CI/CD, Performance Engineering. The type of reliability or automation challenges you enjoy. Your preferred focus areas: reliability engineering, cloud operations, infrastructure automation, observability, platform engineering, or deployment pipelines.
