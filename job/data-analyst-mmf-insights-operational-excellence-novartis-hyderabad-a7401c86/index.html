Data Analyst, MMF - Insights & Operational Excellence

Novartis · Hyderabad

About the role

Job Description Summary Location: Hyderabad, India #LI-Onsite

About the role

Turn data and automation into smarter ways of working. In this role, you’ll partner closely with Finance teams to identify operational challenges and build scalable solutions that improve efficiency, accuracy and decision-making. You’ll combine your expertise in Python, Selenium and robotic process automation with data transformation and emerging artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities, giving you the opportunity to shape intelligent automation solutions and continuously improve how Finance operates. Job Description Key

Requirements

Design and implement automation solutions using Python, Selenium and robotic process automation to improve Finance processes. Integrate scripts and automation platforms to create efficient, intelligent workflows and support data-driven decision-making. Embed artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities into automated workflows to enable predictive and cognitive solutions. Optimize automation pipelines to strengthen scalability, reliability and performance. Develop processes to extract, transform and integrate clean, actionable data into automated workflows. Explore emerging automation, artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies to continuously improve solutions. Communicate technical concepts clearly to stakeholders and maintain robust documentation throughout the automation lifecycle. Essential

Requirements

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Technology or a related discipline. Two to four years of hands-on experience delivering automation solutions using Python and Selenium libraries. Practical experience developing solutions using robotic process automation technologies. Strong Python programming skills, including experience using Selenium libraries for process automation. Experience working with Microsoft Power Apps to support business solutions. Working knowledge of Structured Query Language and databases for managing and transforming data. Desirable

Requirements

Ability to communicate technical concepts clearly to non-technical stakeholders and document automation solutions effectively. Professional proficiency in English, with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams and stakeholders. Exposure to artificial intelligence and machine learning, particularly integrating predictive or cognitive capabilities into automated workflows. Experience using PowerShell or VBScript to integrate scripts with automation platforms and enhance process efficiency. Skills Desired Artificial Intelligence (AI), Controls Compliance, Critical Thinking, Database Normalization, Data Cleansing, Data Reporting, Data Visualization, Employee Engagement, Financial Management, Give Feedback, Insight Generation, Interpersonal Relationships, Key Driver Analysis (KDA), Managing Ambiguity, Market Understanding, Planning Analysis, Problem Solving, Process Optimization, Stakeholder Influence
