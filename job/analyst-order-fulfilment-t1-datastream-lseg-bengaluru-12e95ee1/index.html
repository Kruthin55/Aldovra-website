Analyst - Order Fulfilment, T1 & Datastream

LSEG · Bengaluru

About the role

The Global Center OFS team's purpose is to work on orders/activities raised by the OMS teams in Global Center and the regional centers for fulfillment. The OFS Team also performs key compliance functions for the order fulfillment, maintains product administration and constantly strives to improve the way that we run the external and internal order process to help improve our service to customers, to consistently enhance customer experience through improving timeliness in service delivery by meeting the guaranteed average turnaround time, based on agreed product/process guidelines. To mail, speak and interact with functions (Customer Service, Order Administration, Sales, segments, cross functional teams, regional partners) to expedite the resolution. To consistently meet average productivity targets on transactions daily To handle simple and complex requests, products, systems and accounts with advanced knowledge of process and systems. To display strong process and system knowledge to independently resolve difficult requests and urgent requests. Analyze data and reports related to transactions. Troubleshoot and resolve queries and issues. Display proficiency and knowledge to handle sophisticated requests and build better understanding of business, upstream and downstream processes.

of the role include: Handle the order process for select products; this includes entering orders into specialist permissioning or electronic fulfilment systems and working with internal customers to ensure the order is fulfilled according to agreed turnaround times and with accuracy. Bring up all delays to internal groups including to the senior members of the team as appropriate. Accountable for meeting and maintaining defined standards for quality and productivity. Perform licence assignment and service account creation activities. Meet agreed volume, service and quality targets.
