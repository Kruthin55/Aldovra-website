Associate Service Finance Analyst (

GE HealthCare · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary The Associate Service Finance Analyst supports the regional Finance team in delivering high-quality financial analysis, reporting, and operational finance support for the after-sales services business (the "Service organization"). The role reports into the Global Markets Service Income Leader with a dotted line to the regional Service Senior Finance Manager. The role provides strong exposure to forecasting, budgeting, closing activities and cost analysis. It offers an excellent opportunity to develop core FP&A skills in a dynamic, international environment within GE HealthCare. GE HealthCare is a leading global medical technology and digital solutions innovator. Our purpose is to create a world where healthcare has no limits. Unlock your ambition, turn ideas into world-changing realities, and join an organization where every voice makes a difference, and every difference builds a healthier world Job Description

Roles and responsibilities

As the Associate Service Finance Analyst, you will partner with finance managers to support financial planning, analysis, reporting, and closing activities for the Service organization. Your responsibilities include, but are not limited to, the following: Financial reporting & data extraction Extract, validate and analyze financials on a regular basis from multiple systems. Provide insights via reports and dashboards to the Service Finance teams and cross-functional stakeholders. Cost tagging & performance analysis Tag and classify actual fixed costs and SG&A expenses by nature. Prepare structured summaries to support monthly and quarterly variance analysis and financial reviews. Prepare data summary of O&U profitability order by order and by P&L line. Headcount tracking & reporting Prepare monthly headcount movement reports. Support financial planning through accurate tracking of workforce-related cost drivers. Closing & accounting support Prepare and book Manual Journal Entries (MJEs) for a predefined set of recurring transactions during closing cycles. Support finance teams in ensuring accurate and timely financial close processes. Business Intelligence & Power BI Maintain and enhance Power BI reports and dashboards. Support report automation and process simplification initiatives. · Ensure reporting models and datasets remain accurate and up to date. Ad hoc analysis & projects Support zone-specific initiatives and finance projects. Respond to business requests such as historical account trending and performance analysis. Contribute to continuous improvement of reporting and analytical processes. Required

Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or Business Administration or related field from a university or college preferred. Financial work experience. Fluency in English. Desired Characteristics Strong analytic skills: able to clearly link financial results to operational performance drivers and provide insights into opportunities to improve the business. Clear thinking / problem-solving: successfully lead projects / process improvements within operations / finance functions; able to quickly grasp new ideas, breakdown problems, identify solutions and implement solutions. Adaptable / Flexible: open to change in response to new information, different or unexpected circumstances, and / or to work in ambiguous situations. Solid PC skills: experience with financial systems / applications, strong Excel skills in data compilation and aggregation. Power BI: experience with building / updating / using Power BI dashboards Strong oral and written communication skills: experience working across multiple levels (including executive level), functions and regions.
