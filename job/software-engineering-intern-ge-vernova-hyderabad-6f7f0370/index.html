Software Engineering Intern

GE Vernova · Hyderabad

About the role

Job Description Summary If you are passionate about energy transition and software innovation - GE Vernova is looking for a motivated Software Engineering Intern to join our Grid Software team. In this role, you will contribute to the development and enhancement of GridOS Connect and GridOS Fabric—the backbone of our digital energy platform. You will work alongside experienced engineers to build scalable, secure, and high-performance software components that help utilities orchestrate complex energy grids, integrate renewables, and accelerate decarbonization. Job Description Key

Responsibilities

Feature Development: Assist in designing, coding, and developing new flows for the GridOS platform. Infrastructure & Integration: Gain hands-on experience with GridOS Fabric by contributing to the connectivity, data ingestion, and service orchestration layers that enable real-time grid operations. Deployment: learn and perform DevOps actions to deploy the Grid Software in the customer environments. Documentation: Assist in maintaining technical documentation to support internal development workflows. Required

Qualifications

Currently enrolled in a bachelor’s or master’s degree program in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field. Proficiency in one or more programming languages (e.g., Java, Python, Go, or C++). Understanding of modern software development life cycles (SDLC) and version control tools (e.g., Git). Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. A collaborative mindset and ability to work effectively within a cross-functional team. Preferred Skills (Bonus) Experience or interest in distributed systems, microservices architecture, or cloud-native technologies (e.g., Kubernetes, Docker). Familiarity with data streaming or messaging platforms (e.g., Kafka). Basic understanding of power systems or energy grid operations. Academic projects or previous internships demonstrate exposure to backend development. Why Join GE Vernova? At GE Vernova, we are building the software that powers the world’s most critical infrastructure. As an intern, you won't just be writing code—you will be contributing to projects that impact grid reliability and sustainability on a global scale. You will receive mentorship from industry experts, gain exposure to complex enterprise-grade systems, and grow your skills in a supportive, innovative environment. Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: Yes
