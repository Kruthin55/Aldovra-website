Customer Resolution Representative

Wells Fargo · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role: At Wells Fargo, a Customer Resolution Representative plays a critical role in resolving complex customer issues involving financial products, disputes, and life events. You will conduct thorough research, evaluate risk, and exercise sound judgment to determine the most appropriate resolution path. You’ll navigate sensitive situations, collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders, and act as an intermediary to resolve disputes while ensuring adherence to policies, regulatory requirements, and internal controls. In this role, you will: Serve as the primary point of contact for moderately complex and sensitive customer issues such as estate care, life events, remediations, settlements, and related resolution activities Conduct thorough research, identify root causes, and determine appropriate resolutions while delivering exceptional customer experience Partner with customers, internal teams, and external stakeholders to resolve concerns, mitigate risks, escalate issues when appropriate, and achieve positive customer and business outcomes Manage customer cases from intake through resolution, including research, documentation, transaction processing, communication of outcomes, and adherence to regulatory, compliance, and risk requirements Support issue management activities and customer resolution efforts while balancing customer needs, business objectives, and compliance requirements Support process improvement opportunities that enhance customer satisfaction, operational effectiveness, and compliance. Required

Qualifications

1+ year of customer contact support in a financial services environment, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired

Qualifications

Experience handling death/deceased claims, including claim investigation, processing, and settlement. Strong verbal and written communication and cross-team collaboration. Customer service experience handling complex or escalated issues Experience with financial products, transactions, and regulatory compliance Experience in a fast-paced call center/contact center environment Strong investigation, documentation, and problem-solving skills Ability to navigate multiple systems accurately and efficiently Posting End Date: 22 Nov 2026 *Job posting may come down early due to volume of applicants.
