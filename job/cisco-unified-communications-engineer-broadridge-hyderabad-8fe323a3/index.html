Cisco Unified Communications Engineer

Broadridge · Hyderabad

About the role

At Broadridge, we've built a culture where the highest goal is to empower others to accomplish more. If you’re passionate about developing your career, while helping others along the way, come join the Broadridge team. Cisco Unified Communications Engineer responsible for the design, implementation, management, and support of Cisco Collaboration solutions. The role requires hands-on expertise in Unified Communications Manager (CUCM), Contact Center (UCCX), Webex Control Hub, WebEx cloud calling, Microsoft Teams, Video conferencing, Cisco Unity Connection, and related Cisco UC technologies. Key

Responsibilities

Design, implement, and maintain Cisco UC solutions (CUCM, UCCX, Webex, Video, WebEx Cloud Calling, WebEx Cloud Contact Center, Unity, CER). Configure and manage CUBEs, SIP trunks, dial plans, and QoS. Develop UCCX call flows per business requirements. Administer UCS and VMware environments (vCenter, ESXi). Perform system upgrades, patches, and integrations with third-party services. Provide Level 2/3 technical support for voice and video issues. Monitor system performance and ensure solution scalability, security, and reliability. Stay updated on Cisco UC technologies and recommend improvements. Experience Required: Strong background in Cisco Collaboration technologies. Cisco UC Suite (Unified Communications Manager, Unity Connection, Emergency Responder) Cisco Webex Calling (Cloud Calling) Microsoft Teams (Phone and Meeting Rooms) Contact Center (Cisco Unified Contact Center Express and Webex cloud Contact Center) Cisco RoomOS Devices.
