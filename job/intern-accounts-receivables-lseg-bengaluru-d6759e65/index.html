Intern, Accounts Receivables

LSEG · Bengaluru

About the role

Role Profile As an Intern in the Accounts Receivable team at London Stock Exchange Group, you will gain hands-on experience in core finance operations, particularly around invoicing, contract tracking, and revenue assurance. This internship offers a unique opportunity to learn within a global financial services environment, working alongside experienced professionals. Key

Responsibilities

Under guidance and supervision, the intern will: Assist in the setup and invoicing of new business contracts. Help monitor the inter-sales inbox and log contract stages to support billing calculations. Track contract progress between sales, legal, and customers. Support the completion of new business contract checklists. Help prepare credit notes and re-bills, and assist in distributing billing documents. Respond to ad-hoc requests from the Finance Team and business units. Assist in preparing month-end revenue accruals. Support debt tracking and provide updates on credits and re-bills. Participate in revenue assurance activities and reporting. Help respond to internal and external audit queries. Provide support to finance teams during high-volume or complex resolution periods. Contribute to process improvement initiatives. Engage in cross-functional learning and team collaboration. Assist in system testing and upgrades related to billing processes. Candidate Profile The ideal intern should: Be pursuing a degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field. Demonstrate basic knowledge of accounting and bookkeeping principles. Show eagerness to learn and contribute in a collaborative environment. Have good time management and organizational skills. Be proficient in Microsoft Office, especially Excel. Communicate effectively in English, both written and verbal. Be detail-oriented and accurate in work. Be open to learning financial systems (e.g., Oracle) and tools.
