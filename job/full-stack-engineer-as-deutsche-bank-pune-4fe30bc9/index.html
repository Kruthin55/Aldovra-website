Full stack Engineer, AS

Deutsche Bank · Pune

About the role

Job Description: Job Title: Full stack Engineer, AS Location: Pune, India Role Description: We are seeking a hands-on Full Stack Engineer whose primary skill set is React, Next.js, Java with Spring Boot, REST APIs, and microservices. The ideal candidate should have practical experience designing, developing, and deploying scalable applications on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), with good knowledge of CI/CD practices. This role requires strong front-end and back-end engineering capability, cloud-native development experience, and the ability to deliver secure, reliable solutions in a collaborative Agile environment. What we’ll offer you As part of our flexible scheme, here are just some of the benefits that you’ll enjoy Best in class leave policy. Gender neutral parental leaves 100% reimbursement under childcare assistance benefit (gender neutral) Sponsorship for Industry relevant certifications and education Employee Assistance Program for you and your family members Comprehensive Hospitalization Insurance for you and your dependents Accident and Term life Insurance Complementary Health screening for 35 yrs. and above Your key responsibilities: Build responsive, intuitive, and reusable user interfaces using React, Next.js, JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3. Develop and maintain Java Spring Boot based REST APIs and microservices, ensuring clean API design, scalability, security, and maintainability. Develop, and deploy high-quality, scalable, and maintainable full-stack applications using modern engineering best practices. Implement solutions on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), using relevant cloud services for compute, storage, databases, security, monitoring, and deployment. Collaborate closely with product owners, UX/UI designers, business analysts, and other engineers to define, design, and ship new features. Ensure the performance, scalability, security, and reliability of applications. Support and maintain CI/CD pipelines for automated build, test, and deployment processes, including deployments to Google Cloud Platform. Participate in code reviews, promote knowledge sharing within the team. Stay up to date with emerging technologies and industry best practices, particularly within the GCP ecosystem. Contribute to technical documentation and architectural decisions. Adhere to Deutsche Bank's stringent security, compliance, and regulatory standards. Your skills and experience 7+ of professional experience in full-stack software development. Strong hands-on proficiency in Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, and microservices architecture. Strong hands-on experience with front-end technologies, primarily React and Next.js, along with JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3. Hands-on experience designing, developing, deploying, and supporting applications on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Experience with relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL, Oracle) and/or NoSQL databases (e.g., MongoDB, Cassandra, Firestore). Experience with containerization technologies (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes, GKE). Good knowledge of CI/CD concepts and experience with tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI, Cloud Build, or similar platforms. Experience in Agentic AI with knowledge of context windows, token usage Strong skills in prompt engineering, structured outputs, function/tool calling, and schema validation to connect agents securely with enterprise APIs and services. Experience implementing retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), embeddings, vector databases, semantic search, reranking, and source-grounded responses. Experience testing and evaluating AI agents using representative datasets, task-completion metrics, groundedness checks and regression testing. Experience deploying and operating AI services on GCP with versioned prompts and configurations, agent tracing and monitoring Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills. Ability to work effectively in an Agile/Scrum development environment. Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field. Highly Desired Skills (Bonus Points): GCP Professional Certification (e.g., Professional Cloud Developer, Professional Cloud Architect). Experience with infrastructure-as-code tools (Terraform, Cloud Deployment Manager). Knowledge of data streaming technologies (e.g., Kafka, GCP Pub/Sub). Familiarity with financial services domain knowledge and regulatory requirements. Experience with test-driven development (TDD) and behavioral-driven development (BDD). Open-source contributions or active participation in developer communities. Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimise workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs. How we’ll support you Training and development to help you excel in your career Coaching and support from experts in your team A culture of continuous learning to aid progression A range of flexible benefits that you can tailor to suit your needs
