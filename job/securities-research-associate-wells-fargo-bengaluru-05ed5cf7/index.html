Securities Research Associate

Wells Fargo · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Securities Research Associate In this role, you will: Provide support in the editing and publishing functions within the Global Research and Economics Group in Wells Fargo Securities Generate research packet products, post research documents to market data vendors, update and maintain mailing lists, and provide client access to web sites Assign reports to Editors and Supervisory Analysts to ensure the most efficient use of resources Maintain databases Receive directions from leaders Exercise independent judgment while developing the knowledge to understand function, policies, procedures, and compliance requirements Collaborate and consult with peers, colleagues and managers to resolve issues and achieve goals Required

Qualifications

2+yrs of Securities Investment research, financial services experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education 2+ years of relevant Securities Research experience Deep experience in DCM, Leveraged Finance, including leveraged loans (TLB), high yield bonds, LBO / sponsor-backed financing, refinancing and liability management transactions Proven track record in debt origination and syndication across US and EMEA markets, working closely with global LevFin / DCM teams Strong capability to lead end-to-end deal execution , including preparation of pitch books, RFPs, information memoranda, investor materials, term sheets, pricing analysis, and transaction documentation. Advanced ability to analyze credit and capital structures , including leverage assessment, cash flow evaluation, comparable transactions, and market positioning Demonstrated expertise in structuring complex debt transactions across instrument, pricing, tenor, covenant packages, currency, and investor strategy Deep understanding of leveraged products and markets , including institutional loans, high yield, hybrids, and liability management solutions Strong collaboration across Syndicate, Sales & Trading, Corporate Finance, ESG, and Coverage teams , with ability to operate seamlessly in global deal teams Excellent ability to manage senior stakeholders across US and EMEA, with clear communication and strong ownership mindset Proven leadership in mentoring and managing junior teams , including training, guidance, and rigorous quality review of deliverables Ability to generate differentiated ideas and financing solutions , contributing to client strategy and transaction origination Key role in building and scaling the India Leveraged Finance franchise , with a focus on deeper integration and partnership with US teams MBA / Master’s in Finance from a premier institution Strong knowledge of accounting, financial concepts, and credit markets , with familiarity in tools such as Tableau, Power BI, Python, and VBA considered an advantage Desired qualification: High proficiency in PowerPoint and Excel , with ability to deliver client-ready materials under tight timelines Strong familiarity with LevFin market databases , including FactSet, Capital IQ, Bloomberg, LCD, LevFin Insights, and Factiva Excellent communication and interpersonal skills , with confidence interacting with senior professionals globally Ability to operate in a fast-paced, deal-driven environment , managing multiple priorities under pressure Strong attention to detail and commitment to high-quality output Proactive mindset with focus on innovation, efficiency, and continuous improvement. AI & Future-Ready Capabilities Practical use of AI tools for summarization, content drafting, and data analysis with appropriate human oversight Strong critical thinking to interpret and validate AI-generated insights Awareness of responsible AI use , including ethics, data privacy, and compliance Commitment to continuous learning and adaptability in a rapidly evolving technology landscape Posting End Date: 17 Nov 2026 *Job posting may come down early due to volume of applicants.
