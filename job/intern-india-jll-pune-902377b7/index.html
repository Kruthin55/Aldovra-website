Intern - Architecture About the Role We are looking for a motivated Architecture Intern to support our design and project teams. This role offers hands-on exposure to real estate design projects, space planning, and coordination with cross-functional teams in a professional corporate real estate environment. Key

Responsibilities

Assist in preparing architectural drawings, layouts, and presentations using AutoCAD/Revit/SketchUp. Support space planning and design concept development for client projects. Coordinate with design, project management, and MEP teams for project execution. Conduct site visits and assist in documentation of as-built conditions. Help prepare mood boards, material boards, and design presentations for client reviews. Research design trends, materials, and sustainability practices relevant to commercial interiors/architecture. Support in preparing BOQs (Bill of Quantities) and basic cost estimates under supervision. Assist with documentation, reports, and project trackers. Required

Qualifications

Pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's/Master's degree in Architecture. Proficiency in AutoCAD; working knowledge of Revit, SketchUp, or similar design software is a plus. Basic understanding of space planning, building codes, and design principles. Good communication and presentation skills. Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment. Preferred Skills Familiarity with sustainability/green building concepts (LEED, WELL, etc.). Knowledge of MS Office (Excel, PowerPoint) for reporting and presentations. Strong attention to detail and willingness to learn. What You'll Gain Exposure to live commercial real estate and workplace design projects. Mentorship from experienced design and project management professionals. Opportunity to build a strong foundation in corporate real estate and design practices.
