Engineer - Mechanical Component

GE Vernova · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary Position Overview: We are seeking a talented and detail-oriented Mechanical Engineer to lead inhouse design development of internal mechanical components for wind turbine towers. This role focuses on ensuring the functionality, reliability, and efficiency of components such as access systems, cooling mechanisms, cable management, and service platforms. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in mechanical design and an innovative mindset to optimize internal systems for cost, durability, and performance. Chosen candidate will become owner of a Product Breakdown Module (PBM) owner of a mechanical components as part of this role Job Description R5057689 Engineer - Mechanical Component Every wind turbine tower needs more than a strong structure — it needs the ladders, platforms, and cable systems that keep technicians safe and the turbine running. As our Mechanical Component Engineer, you'll lead the in-house design of these internal systems — from access and cooling systems to cable management and service platforms — owning their performance, cost, and durability from concept through to the field. You'll take designs from first concept to validated, working components, running the analyses that prove they're reliable and solving the problems that come up on the shop floor or out at a turbine site. As owner of your assigned Product Breakdown Module (PBM), your decisions shape how these systems perform across the whole fleet. What You'll Do Design internal mechanical systems for wind turbine towers, including ladders, elevators, service and entrance platforms, cable trays, handrails, and tie-off points. Run engineering analyses — including structural and dynamic simulations — to confirm your designs are reliable, efficient, and built to last. Select materials and manufacturing processes that balance durability, weight, and cost while meeting industry standards, and develop the technical specifications, drawings, and documentation that bring designs to life. Oversee prototype development, testing, and validation , and troubleshoot design challenges that arise during manufacturing, assembly, and installation. Own your assigned component as PBM owner — resolving field cases, Root Cause Analysis (RCA) action items, and safety cases to keep the current fleet running well. Partner with technical leaders and principal engineers to standardize how the team solves common field issues, and support the supplier qualification and quality process. Collaborate across teams — System Integration Leaders, project managers, suppliers, and stakeholders — to keep designs aligned and on schedule. About You You're an engineer who likes owning a design end to end — from first sketch through validated hardware — and who's energized by solving the practical problems that come up when components meet the real world. Required: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related engineering discipline. A formal education is valuable, but we're most interested in your total experience and practical problem-solving ability. Experience with structural and/or dynamic simulation tools for mechanical component design. Working knowledge of material selection and manufacturing processes for mechanical components. Strong technical documentation skills, including specifications and engineering drawings. Comfortable collaborating with cross-functional and supplier teams to resolve design and quality issues. Preferred: Experience in wind energy or another heavy industrial/renewable energy sector. GE Vernova employees rise to the challenge of building a world that works. We offer a robust, competitive benefits package designed to support your health and your goals, along with ongoing opportunities for on-the-job learning and career growth. Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: No
