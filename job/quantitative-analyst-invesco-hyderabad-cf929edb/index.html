Your Team You will join the Hyderabad-based Separately Managed Accounts (SMA) team within Invesco Solutions & Custom Strategies. SMAs are among the fastest-growing segments in asset management, and the team delivers the analytics, data, controls, and reporting capabilities required to support equity and fixed-income SMA strategies at scale. You will collaborate with portfolio managers and colleagues across Performance, Operations, Product, Investment Technology, and business leadership. Your Role Build and enhance scalable datasets, analytics, dashboards, and reporting for SMA performance, after-tax, composite, and tax-lot data. Design and automate production workflows, reconciliations, and controls to improve accuracy, efficiency, and scalability. Own month-end analytical processes, including exception investigation, results validation, and timely delivery. Analyse portfolio, transaction, benchmark, performance, and tax-lot data to identify trends and explain results. Partner with investment, operations, product, and technology teams to translate business requirements into production-ready analytical solutions. Apply disciplined testing, documentation, code-review, and change-management practices. The Experience You Bring 3+ years of experience in investment management, quantitative analytics, financial services, or a comparable data-intensive environment. Strong proficiency in Python and SQL, with experience in relational data modelling and production data pipelines. Experience implementing data-quality controls, reconciliations, exception monitoring, and automated workflows. Proven ability to investigate complex data issues, communicate findings clearly, and deliver accurately within time-sensitive deadlines. Knowledge of SMAs, portfolio accounting, investment performance, tax-managed strategies, equity, or fixed income is preferred. Academic requirements Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Engineering, Finance, Economics, or another quantitative discipline.
