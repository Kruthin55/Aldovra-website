About the role

Solution Validation Engineer for Campus Fabric / Enterprise Networking This role has been designed as 'Hybrid' with a requirement that you will work on average 2 days per week from an HPE office. Job Description: What You 'll do Design, develop, execute, and troubleshoot test plans, test scripts, and validation solutions for Campus Fabric and Enterprise Networking solutions. Perform end-to-end testing and validation of VXLAN/EVPN, fabric workflows, network segmentation, and multi-node deployments. Test and troubleshoot 802.1X, access security, DHCP/DHCP security, multicast, and endpoint authentication workflows. Develop and enhance automation scripts to expand test coverage, improve efficiency, and streamline validation processes. Leverage AI-driven approaches to optimize test automation and improve testing efficiency. Collaborate closely with development teams to identify, troubleshoot, and resolve product issues, ensuring improved product quality. Investigate customer escalations, perform root-cause analysis, and recommend effective solutions or workarounds for issue mitigation. Provide technical recommendations and support in resolving common and complex networking issues. Take ownership of assigned projects, exercise independent judgment, and provide technical guidance to junior team members when required. What You Will Bring Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or an equivalent discipline. 6+ years of experience in Network System Testing, Quality Assurance (QA), or related networking roles. Strong hands-on experience with VXLAN/EVPN, Campus Fabric, and Enterprise Networking deployments. Solid understanding of network segmentation, fabric architectures, 802.1X, access security, DHCP/DHCP security, multicast, and endpoint authentication. Proficiency in developing test automation scripts using Python or similar programming languages. Strong troubleshooting, debugging, and root-cause analysis skills, preferably with hands-on real-world testbed experience. Experience working with network traffic generators, packet analysis tools, and log debugging utilities. Ability to independently investigate complex technical issues and recommend effective solutions. Strong collaboration and communication skills to work effectively with development teams and cross-functional stakeholders. Ability to manage assigned deliverables independently and provide technical guidance to junior professionals.
