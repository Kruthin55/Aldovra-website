Zero Trust Engineer

Johnson Controls · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Title: Zero Trust Network Security Engineer Region: India Bangalore/Pune What you will do: As part of Johnson Controls’ global Network Security Operations function, the Zero Trust Network Operations Engineer will be responsible for the day-to-day operation, support, and lifecycle management of our enterprise Zero Trust platforms. This role is heavily focused on Forescout Network Access Control (NAC) operations and device classification (approx. 70-80% of the role), with secondary responsibilities supporting our Infoblox DDI infrastructure. How you will do it: Primary Focus: Forescout NAC, Device Classification & Segmentation · Daily Operations: Serve as the primary operational SME for the Forescout NAC platform globally. Perform daily monitoring, health checks, and troubleshooting. · Incident Management: Respond to and resolve network access control incidents, including 802.1X/RADIUS authentication failures, MAB, and Guest Captive Portal (JCI-Internet) loading issues. · Device Visibility & Classification: Leverage data analytics to identify, classify, and manage the lifecycle of over 30,000 unmanaged, IoT, and OT devices across the global network. · Zero Trust Enforcement: Manage MAC address repository (MAR) whitelisting and exceptions. Implement approved access control rules, "Semi-Trust" policies, and network segmentation changes (VLAN assignments/ACLs) in production environments. Secondary Focus: Infoblox DDI & Automation · Platform Ownership & Infrastructure Management: Serve as the platform owner for the Infoblox Universal DDI (BloxOne) infrastructure. Deploy, maintain, and manage the health of virtual appliances across multi-cloud (Azure, AWS) and on-premises environments, including the implementation of a proactive 3-tier monitoring strategy. · DNS & Security Operations: Execute DNS configuration changes and operationalize advanced native DNS security features, such as Response Policy Zones (RPZ) and DNS Traffic Control (DTC). · Cross-Team Collaboration: Strictly adhere to the shared-platform RACI model, focusing exclusively on the platform foundation and DNS operations, while supporting the Global Network Team as the consumer for DHCP and IPAM services. What we look for: Required Skills (Must-Haves): · Strong hands-on operational experience with Forescout NAC is required. · Working knowledge of network authentication protocols (802.1X, RADIUS, MAB) and core networking concepts (TCP/IP, VLANs, routing/switching). · Experience working within ITIL-based operational processes (Incident, Change, Problem management). · Strong troubleshooting and operational documentation skills for L1/L2 networking support teams. Desirable Skills (Nice-to-Haves): · Forescout Certifications (FSCA / FSCP) are highly preferred. · Direct experience deploying and operating the Infoblox Universal DDI (BloxOne) platform. · Experience managing enterprise DNS architecture and DNS security features (RPZ, DTC). (Note: DHCP and IPAM business operations are out of scope for this role). · Exposure to Zero Trust Network Architecture and IoT/OT device classification from an operational perspective. · Experience with ServiceNow CMDB and building automated workflows (e.g., PowerApps, Python scripting, API integrations)..
