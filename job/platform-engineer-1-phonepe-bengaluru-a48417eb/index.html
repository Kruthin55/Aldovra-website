Platform Engineer 1

PhonePe · Bengaluru

About the role

PhonePe Limited (Formerly PhonePe Private Limited) is a technology company that builds digital platforms for Payments, Digital Distribution Services and Financial Services. Headquartered in India, the PhonePe digital payments app was launched in 2016. As of April 2026, PhonePe has over 70 Crore life-till-date registered users and a digital payments acceptance network spread across over 5 Crore merchants. PhonePe’s products and services include Consumer Payments (including Digital Distribution Services), Merchant Payments, Lending and Insurance Distribution services, and New Platforms, which comprise Share.Market (stock broking and mutual funds distribution platform), and Indus Appstore (Android-based mobile app marketplace). Culture: At PhonePe, we go the extra mile to make sure you can bring your best self to work, Everyday!. And that starts with creating the right environment for you. We empower people and trust them to do the right thing. Here, you own your work from start to finish, right from day one. PhonePe-rs solve complex problems and execute quickly; often building frameworks from scratch. If you’re excited by the idea of building platforms that touch millions, ideating with some of the best minds in the country and executing on your dreams with purpose and speed, join us! Job Summary: We are seeking an entry-level Platform Engineer to focus mainly on patching, monitoring, and day-to-day operations across our Windows and Linux server environments. The ideal candidate will assist in maintaining operational health, executing routine maintenance tasks, and handling initial troubleshooting under supervision.

Responsibilities

& requirements: Perform system monitoring, tracking alerts, and managing day-to-day operational status metrics. Execute regular patching, server updates, and basic OS operational tasks. Manage user access requests and basic Active Directory / DNS administrative tasks. Handle routine daily operational activities, system health checks, and basic maintenance task automation. Document incidents, knowledge base articles, and standard operating procedures (SOPs). 0–2 years of experience or relevant internship/projects in IT infrastructure support. Basic knowledge of Linux/Windows OS, networking concepts (DNS, DHCP), and shell scripting. Strong willingness to learn platform tools, cloud platforms, and automation frameworks. Any Graduation. PhonePe Full Time Employee
