Pitch Bearing Design Engineer

GE Vernova · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary This role will support design activities for the Pitch Bearing Engineering team within the Mechanical Subsystem Engineering segment, working under the guidance of senior engineers.

Responsibilities

will include assisting with new product (NPI) design and development, total lifecycle cost, validation, productivity, and fleet support efforts for the OneWind organization. Job Description Roles and

Responsibilities

Assist with design expertise and FE analysis for the pitch bearing component within the OneWind organization, under guidance from senior engineers. Support design activities for NPI, RCA/Quality, validation, and total lifecycle cost initiatives of pitch bearings. Collaborate with cross functional teams, including Systems, Sourcing, Sourcing Quality, Materials, Services, Fleet Management, Lab Testing, and GE Suppliers to support optimum rotor and turbine lifecycle cost. Support the Multi Generation Product Plan (MGPP) and assist with initiatives that drive site specific customer value with pitch bearings. Support technology needs and assist in bringing new technology into products. Assist in delivering cost out via component, subsystem, and system level redesigns. Learn and apply GD&T standards to create technical drawings and specifications. Support testing activities that resolve design validation/field issues/PAC activities, working with the Fleet Support team and Regional Customer Solution teams under guidance. Responsible for well-defined assignments with guidance on approach, contributing to the products' technical success. Communicate and provide input through analysis, preparation, and presentation of technical material to internal audiences, with review from senior team members. Support hub mechanical subsystem problem solving and risk mitigation activities under supervision. Work with the Controlled Title Holders community across the business and global team, with guidance, to support quality and consistency on systems design and trade-off decisions. Required

Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Structural Engineering, Civil Engineering, or similar from an accredited College or University Minimum 0 to 2 years of relevant engineering experience Developing problem identification and solving ability Ability to work well within a cross matrixed, global organization in a dynamic, ever-changing environment Developing interpersonal skills and a positive energy Strong oral and written communication skills Developing ability to analyze and resolve problems with guidance Developing project management skills Self-driven learner who is proactive and completes tasks with oversight from senior engineers Familiarity with GD&T and fundamental technical drawing guidelines Able to understand technical drawings and specifications with support Desired Characteristics Exposure to pitch bearing engineering (through coursework, internship, or early career experience) FEA coursework or early hands-on experience Exposure to structural design (component or part design, mechanical systems, etc.) Developing knowledge of wind turbine component design criteria and wind turbine systems integration Great Team Player - active listener, reliable and responsible, supports and respects team members - a positive influence Continuous improvement mindset - eager to learn productivity gains, technology development, cost reduction, etc. Clear Thinker - developing ability to work through uncertainty with support Developing ability to communicate to various levels of the organization Good verbal and written English communication Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: No
