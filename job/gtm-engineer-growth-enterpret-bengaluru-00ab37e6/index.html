GTM Engineer, Growth

Enterpret · Bengaluru

About the role

About Enterpret

Enterpret collects a company's customer feedback in one place, organizes it with an adaptive taxonomy, and gives that context to the people and AI agents deciding what to fix next. Customers include fast-growing AI-native companies such as Canva, ElevenLabs, Notion, Superhuman, and Zapier.

The role

Our sales team, growth lead, and founders generate a steady stream of outbound ideas. You'll turn the best of them into production systems within days, keep them running, and bring plenty of ideas of your own.

You'll be our first GTM Engineer. You'll work hand in hand with our growth and sales team and run the daily growth standup. When someone asks "can we build this?", you're the one who says yes and ships it.

This is a builder role with one scoreboard: more of the right accounts turning into booked meetings.

What you'll own

Building the outbound engine

Design, build, and run the systems behind outbound: account and contact sourcing, enrichment, signal detection, scoring, routing, and sequencing

Ship LLM-powered agents and automations that research accounts, draft first-touch messaging, and hand reps context they'd otherwise spend an hour collecting

Write the code. Scripts, services, integrations, and internal tools that other people depend on every day

Own deliverability. When emails land in spam, you diagnose it and fix it without calling in an outside consultant

Instrument every system so we know which ones produce meetings and which ones only produce activity

Running growth with the team

Run the daily growth standup and own the build queue that comes out of it

Take ideas from the growth team, the AEs, and leadership, and come back with how you'd build them, what it costs, and what you'd do instead

Bring your own ideas. If you see ten things worth trying, say so and rank them

Work in our stack, including Salesforce and Monaco, and replace pieces of it when a tool stops earning its seat

Pull Enterpret's own product into the motion where it helps; we sell customer intelligence, and our outbound should use it

What success looks like

Day 30: Sit with the BDRs and AEs, run the current outbound stack end to end, find where it leaks, and ship your first system to production.

Day 90: Run the daily growth standup. Several systems are live, instrumented, and owned by you, with a baseline for meetings booked from outbound.

Day 180: Outbound meetings are up by a multiple over that baseline, and you can show which systems drove it. The team brings you ideas instead of waiting for engineering time.

Who you are

We care about two things more than your title or background. You have built or run GTM systems, and you have pushed code to production that other systems rely on. If both are true, we want to talk. You might be an engineer who drifted toward growth and cares more about pipeline than system design. You might be a BDR or SDR who taught yourself to build and now automates everything around you. You might be a former founder who wrote the product and ran the outbound.

What you bring

A track record of building outbound or growth systems (email, paid, LinkedIn, product-led, anything) and the numbers they moved

Code you shipped to production that people or systems depended on. Prototypes, demos, and weekend vibe-coding projects don't count on their own

Hands-on experience building LLM-powered agents, copilots, or automations that run in production

Daily, fluent use of AI coding tools such as Claude Code or Cursor, as a habit you built long before this job search

Working knowledge of the GTM data stack: CRM (Salesforce), enrichment and orchestration tools (Clay, n8n, Make, or plain scripts), and sequencing platforms

Enough sales experience, or proximity to it, to understand a rep's day and what makes a cold email get a reply

Email deliverability knowledge: domains, warmup, authentication, and what to do when reply rates fall off a cliff

How you work

You ship first and polish later. A rough system running on Tuesday beats a perfect one in a doc

When you disagree with an idea, you come back with a better one

You measure in meetings and pipeline, not in tasks completed

You like working in the same room as the sales team

Nice to have

Experience at a B2B SaaS company selling to US mid-market and enterprise buyers

Early-stage experience where you were the only person doing this work

Experience selling to product, CX, or insights teams

You've done outbound yourself, carrying a quota or booking your own meetings

Work sample (required)

Send links or a short write-up covering at least one GTM system you built (what it did, how it worked, what it changed) and at least one piece of code you shipped to production. Redact anything confidential and tell us what you left out.

How we hire

Alongside conversations with the growth and revenue team, you'll do a live build session in person with us. In one to two hours, we'll pick a real GTM problem and you'll prototype a working system for it. It can be rough. We want to see how you think, what you reach for, and whether something runs at the end.

Why Enterpret?

High Impact: Own systems that are already in production and need to scale well — the core data systems, models, and pipelines where Enterpret's platform comes together.

Ownership: End-to-end responsibility for features and systems.

Complex Challenges: Work on the knowledge graph, distributed data tiers, model hosting, and high-throughput enrichment pipelines where a single change can touch storage, query paths, reliability, and cost.

Growth: Learn and grow with a high-caliber team, with a clear path from owning components to shaping larger slices of Graph Platform and into L5.

Culture: Open, collaborative, and values-driven environment with autonomy.

Benefits

Competitive salary, equity, hybrid work setup, premium healthcare, and more.
