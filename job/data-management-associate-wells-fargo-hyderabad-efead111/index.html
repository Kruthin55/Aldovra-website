Data Management Associate

Wells Fargo · Hyderabad

About the role

About this role: We are seeking a motivated Data Management Associate to join the Commercial Banking and Data for CB and CIB Strategy & Planning Analytics & Data Management & Product. The role will support data initiatives covering CIB trades and positions, including business requirements, data analysis, data quality, governance, controls, and delivery coordination. The analyst will work closely with business, technology, operations, and data governance teams to deliver reliable, well-controlled, and business-aligned data solutions. In this role, you will: Analyse, assess, and test data controls and data systems to support quality and risk compliance standards Perform routine data quality, metadata, governance, and remediation activities using established procedures Prepare basic documentation of requirements, design decisions, issue closure, and remediation updates Monitor data governance, data quality, and metadata policies, standards, tools, processes, and procedures Support managers in executing tasks across the data issue remediation life cycle Process data from third-party providers and coordinate routine questions or issues Gather, analyse, and document business and data requirements. Translate requirements into user stories, process flows, data mappings, and acceptance criteria. Develop an understanding of CIB products, trade lifecycle, positions, and related data flows. Perform data analysis, profiling, validation, and reconciliation to identify data quality issues. Support investigation and remediation of data defects, data quality, control gaps, and production issues. Coordinate with business, technology, operations, and governance stakeholders throughout delivery. Support Agile ceremonies, backlog refinement, testing, implementation, and post-production validation. Maintain clear documentation covering data definitions, lineage, mappings, controls, and business rules. Assess new data sources for suitability, completeness, quality, and business use. Track risks, dependencies, issues, and action items, and escalate them appropriately. Contribute to product enablement and enhancements in Marketplace. Required

Qualifications

6+ months of data management, business analysis, analytics, or project management experience Desired

Qualifications

Experience in data management, including governance, data quality, metadata, data access, and remediation practices Skill in business analysis to clarify needs, evaluate options, and inform data solutions Experience supporting data implementation activities from requirements through validation Understanding of compliance requirements and risk controls used to identify, document, and remediate data-related gaps Knowledge of software and system testing methods used to validate data flows, controls, requirements, and expected outcomes Proven success in producing clear, complete documentation for requirements, controls, decisions, and remediation Ability to build productive stakeholder relationships and coordinate decisions across business and technology partners 6 months of experience in data management, business analysis, data analysis, or project delivery, preferably within financial services. 6 months of experience in CB products lending, reference data and its domain knowledge Understanding investment banking products, trade lifecycle, positions, or front-to-back business processes. 6 months of experience in requirements gathering user stories, process documentation, data mapping, and acceptance criteria. Knowledge of data quality, data governance, metadata, lineage, data controls, and issue remediation is a plus. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to data accuracy and completeness. Experience supporting Agile or Scrum-based delivery. Ability to work effectively across business and technology teams. Strong written and verbal communication, documentation, and stakeholder coordination skills. Basic to intermediate SQL and data analysis skills are preferred. Exposure to ETL, data lakes, cloud platforms, S3, Parquet, Python, or real-time data processing is a plus. Exposure to AI or LLM-based tools is an added advantage. Job Expectations Deliver accurate and well-structured business and data analysis. Support initiatives from requirements through testing and implementation. Maintain high standards for data quality, governance, controls, and documentation. Proactively identify data issues, risks, and dependencies. Build effective partnerships across business, technology, operations, and governance teams.
