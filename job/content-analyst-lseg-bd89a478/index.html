Content Analyst

LSEG · BLR-Divyasree Technopolis, India

About the role

Position Overview: To maintain the Exchange Traded Instrument data like Real time, Reference and End of day pricing data for Equities on database by adhering to all efficiency, quality & compliance requirements, and handle Customer Queries for the process handled. Key

Responsibilities

· Process and Validate the accuracy of data received from various sources. · Developing expertise in data related issues. · Building knowledge of financial regulations and market practices/conventions in relevant markets. · Responsible for delivering projects efficiently. · Ensure that this information is stored in databases is accurately reflected on products by crafting or running data quality checks and standards. · Ensure quality and time efficient production of financial information to products. · Respond to data queries and provide high level support to our clients. · Analyse client cases to form patterns and proactively improve data accuracy. · Consolidate information around the dataset leading to the establishment of standard processes. · Monitor market events to anticipate changes in financial instruments and take actions efficiently. · Improve usage of available tools to best of advantage to maintain/improve content quality during daily operations. · Mentor and train analysts on data issues, databases & products. · Frequently run automated/semi-automated checks to ensure accurate data is provided on our Products with high quality of content. · Work on simplifications and innovations. · Support specific projects, as assigned by manager. · Implement change control procedures, data operations standards and current data policies and procedures. Key

Requirements

· Good Financial Market Knowledge. · Knowledge of Refinitiv products. · Excellent verbal and written communication skills. · Candidate should be flexible to work in shifts. · Experience in migrating data from one system to another would be an advantage. · Required Excel knowledge · Be resilient while prioritising multiple tasks · Problem solving and attention to detail Career Stage: Associate London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG) Information: Join us and be part of a team that values innovation, quality, and continuous improvement.
