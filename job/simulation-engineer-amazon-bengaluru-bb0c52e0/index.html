Simulation Engineer

Amazon · Bengaluru

About the role

Are you interested in working on the next generation of Amazon buildings? Do you want to be part of the ongoing growth and expansion of Amazon’s infrastructure? Do you want to be a Subject Matter Expert concerning simulations for multiple miles and countries? Are you reluctant to apply because you’re concerned you don’t have the right qualifications? The Global Engineering Services Team has a full training plan and support network to help you grow and succeed within this role. The International Engineering Programs team is seeking an experienced Amazon Simulations and Optimization engineer to join the team to support the expansion of simulations engineering. The Simulations and Optimization engineer is a key player in the end-to-end delivery of the complex challenge of launching new buildings – or optimizing existing buildings, driving improvements and standards to enable new sites to achieve benchmark rates while ramping to meet network capacity. In this role, you will influence a team comprising of Site Leads, Senior Operations Managers, Operation Managers, Project Managers, Process SMEs, Design Engineers and as well as representatives of various key stakeholder. Furthermore, the team is expanding to support international expansion, working across EU, MENA, India, LATAM, Australia. We collaborate with colleagues in North America to maintain global excellence in simulation services and models. This role calls for an individual who is knowledgeable concerning Yard and Under The Roof operations, structured, confident and professionally and socially adept to earn credibility and trust with customers and stakeholders at various levels of the organization. The role requires to travel up to 20%. Key job responsibilities

Will have the ability to deal and deliver results in an ambiguous environment concerning sites Operations and Yard

Is able to support infrastructure and flow related simulations to derive infrastructure requirements – softwares: Siemens Plan Simulation X, Flexsim

Is able to provide design inputs based data driven methodology

Interpersonal skills, to motivate and lead expectations and outcomes to project team.

Communication skills to build relationships and identify with stakeholders across the organization.

Is able to use their initiative and make decisions under pressure.

Has the ability to seek opportunities to drive year on year savings and performance/process improvements.

Will compile, manage and report on weekly project metrics and be responsible for leading many high-profile meetings to align on project requirements.

Working backwards from True North processes and available data to close the gaps from current to desired state,

Create Material Flow Diagrams,

Provide data driven process and engineering related advice to the leadership by written narrative business document

Has the ability to learn and adapt to changing circumstances.

Has Fulfillment Center and/or Sort Center operational skills A day in the life In cooperation with other GES stakeholders, including but not limited to WWRE & Design, ACES/PE, AR, Ops, and others the team aims to optimize building designs, modules, and processes, in ensuring safety, quality, productivity, and AA experience related to sites innovation and design. They will ensure our future buildings, retrofits and expansions will both attend to the operational excellence requirements and have frugal solutions.

Basic qualifications

- Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification in Math, Engineering, Science or Business - Experience with Simulations of automated material handling equipment, packaging technologies, and systems including robotics and high-speed manufacturing – Siemens Plant Simulation X or Flexsim, - Experience with concepts like system architecture, optimization, system dynamics, system analysis, statistical analysis, reliability analysis, and decision making - Experience must involve ownership/management of engineering design projects, along with Experience using business/technical software like MS Excel, MS Project, and CAD software

Preferred qualifications

- Experience with process design based on Lean Principles, along with experience in Capital project management, Our inclusive culture empowers Amazonians to deliver the best results for our customers.
