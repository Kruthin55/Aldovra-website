Marketing internship (brand & communication)

Sarvam AI · Bengaluru

About the role

About Sarvam Sarvam is building the bedrock of Sovereign AI for India, a full-stack sovereign AI platform spanning research, models, infrastructure, and applications, with a singular focus on making AI genuinely work for India. Sarvam works with leading enterprises and public institutions and is backed by Lightspeed, Peak XV, and Khosla Ventures. We partner with India's leading brands, including Tata Capital, SBI Life, CRED, IDFC, and LIC. About the Role We are looking for a sharp, high-ownership Marketing Intern to act as the primary story-builder and content engine across Sarvam’s marketing channels. This is not a passive internship focused on surface-level social updates. You will translate complex frontier AI capabilities like our Vision APIs, Document Intelligence models, and voice platforms into crisp, high-impact stories, viral launch threads, and sharp enterprise collateral. You will sit at the intersection of product, creative execution, and creator operations, helping shape how Sarvam communicates its sovereign AI vision to technical builders, enterprise buyers, and the broader ecosystem. What You'll Do Customer Stories & Launch Comms - Transform technical milestones into narratives: Turn enterprise customer deployments into compelling case studies and written success stories. - Drive product launch comms: Draft multi-channel launch content—from sharp X/LinkedIn feature threads to enterprise mailers for prospects and existing clients. - Simplify the complex: Translate technical AI concepts into clear, punchy prose without losing accuracy or depth. Social Content & Video Operations - Drive video execution: Write scripts for explainer videos and product walkthroughs; coordinate shoots and post-production workflows. - Manage multi-platform publishing: Oversee daily social presence across X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube, ensuring high content velocity and visual quality. Creator & Influencer Operations - Manage creator partnerships: Source, pitch, and recruit relevant tech creators, developer voices, and industry influencers. - Execute campaign logistics: Draft campaign briefs, align timelines, and track deliverables from initial outreach to final publish. What We're Looking For - Editorial precision: Exceptional written English skills with an instinct for tone, structure, and clarity across social, email, and long-form formats. - Technical translation capability: Comfortable diving into technical platforms, developer dashboards, or AI research to write simple, accurate marketing copy. - AI-first workflow: Habitual use of AI tools for rapid research, content structuring, and drafting. - Platform fluency: Deep, native understanding of content formats, algorithms, and tone across X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube. - Execution & ownership: Uncompromising attention to detail, strong organizational discipline, and the ability to drive multiple deadlines without hand-holding. Bonus Points - Hands-on experience with video editing tools or basic script-to-edit workflows. - Familiarity with design tools (Canva, Figma) to quickly mock up assets or social creatives. - Prior experience managing creator outreach, influencer pipelines, or talent operations. - Vernacular fluency in Hindi or a regional Indian language. - Foundational understanding of SEO best practices for written content. Why Sarvam? Sarvam is a fast-moving, high talent-density team building full-stack AI for India, working on problems at the frontier of AI with population-scale impact. You'll work alongside researchers, engineers, builders, and business leaders who move fast and hold each other to a very high bar. High ownership and high impact from day one, AI-first in everything we do, on problems that could change how an entire country works and communicates.
