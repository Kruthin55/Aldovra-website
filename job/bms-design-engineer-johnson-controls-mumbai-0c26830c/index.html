BMS Design Engineer

Johnson Controls · Mumbai

About the role

Job Description Build your best future with the Johnson Controls team As a global leader in smart, healthy and sustainable buildings, our mission is to reimagine the performance of buildings to serve people, places and the planet. Join a winning team that enables you to build your best future! Our teams are uniquely positioned to support a multitude of industries across the globe. You will have the opportunity to develop yourself through meaningful work projects and learning opportunities. We strive to provide our employees with an experience, focused on supporting their physical, financial, and emotional wellbeing. We are a Great Place To Work-Certified organization. Become a member of the Johnson Controls family and thrive in an empowering company culture where your voice and ideas will be heard – your next great opportunity is just a few clicks away! What we offer: Competitive salary Paid vacation/holidays/sick time On the job/cross training opportunities Encouraging and collaborative team environment Dedication to safety through our Zero Harm policy What you will do Design BMS/IBMS systems for buildings and data centers. Prepare control schematics, network architecture, and wiring diagrams. Develop I/O schedules, points lists, and sequence of operations. Review project specifications and technical requirements. Select BMS controllers, sensors, and field devices. Coordinate with HVAC, Electrical, EPMS, and Fire Alarm teams. Prepare BOM, technical submittals, and engineering documents. Support commissioning, testing, and troubleshooting activities. Ensure compliance with industry standards and customer requirements. Experience with BACnet, Modbus, Niagara, Schneider, Siemens, Honeywell, or JCI systems. What we look for BE/ME (Instrumentation / Electrical/ Electronics / Electronics & Communication) Minimum experience 3+ years Strong knowledge of BMS architecture, Supervisory Controllers, DDC controllers, Sensors & Actuators etc. Strong knowledge of Niagara architecture, Platform, & controllers. Experience in software programming and commissioning of Niagara controllers. Basic knowledge of AutoCAD Experience in the field of Industrial Automation (SCADA/HMI screens development) will be added an advantage Experience in remote engineering, installation and commissioning of HVAC & BMS systems. Should have worked for BMS projects like pharmaceuticals, airports, commercial buildings, hospitals etc. Must be a quick learner & brilliant problem solving capabilities Proficient in written and verbal communication Preferred Experience in Building Automation System or BMS Design Engineering.
