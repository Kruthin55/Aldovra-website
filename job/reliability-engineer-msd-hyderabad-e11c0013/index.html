Position Overview: The Reliability Engineer is part of the Enterprise Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) organization responsible for improving the reliability, resilience, observability, and operational effectiveness of business-critical technology services. In this role, you will work across applications, platforms, cloud, infrastructure, network, and shared enterprise services. You will apply software engineering and SRE practices, use enterprise observability and AI-enabled technologies, automate operational activities, and help teams detect, diagnose, recover from, and prevent service disruptions. This role is suited for an engineer who has developed a foundation in software, infrastructure, cloud, operations, or observability engineering and is ready to build deeper expertise in Site Reliability Engineering. Key

Responsibilities

Reliability Engineering Apply SRE principles to improve the availability, resilience, performance, and recoverability of services. Support the implementation and measurement of Service Level Indicators (SLIs), Service Level Objectives (SLOs), and other service-health measures. Analyze service behavior, failure patterns, and dependencies to identify reliability risks and opportunities for improvement. Participate in resilience, failover, recovery, and production-readiness activities. Implement reliability improvements in partnership with application, platform, and infrastructure teams. Observability Use observability platforms to analyze metrics, logs, traces, events, and service topology. Build and improve dashboards, alerts, and service-health views. Correlate telemetry across applications, infrastructure, cloud, network, and shared platforms to support end-to-end troubleshooting. Identify gaps in telemetry, monitoring, and alerting and work with engineering teams to improve observability. Develop experience with modern telemetry and instrumentation approaches such as OpenTelemetry. AI-Enabled Operations Use AI, Generative AI, and AIOps capabilities to assist with operational analysis, anomaly detection, event correlation, troubleshooting, and knowledge retrieval. Apply AI-assisted techniques to accelerate incident investigation and identify potential causes and remediation options. Help develop AI-assisted operational workflows and automation that improve reliability and reduce manual effort. Validate AI-generated insights against telemetry and authoritative operational data before acting on recommendations. Apply appropriate safeguards and human oversight when AI is used in production operations. Incident & Problem Engineering Participate in technical investigation and recovery of production incidents. Use telemetry, dependency information, and engineering analysis to diagnose service failures. Contribute to root-cause analysis, post-incident reviews, and corrective and preventive actions. Identify recurring reliability issues and help implement engineering solutions that prevent recurrence. Validate that remediation actions have resulted in measurable reliability improvements. Automation & Engineering Develop scripts, tools, and automated workflows that improve operational efficiency and reliability. Automate repetitive detection, diagnostic, remediation, and recovery activities where appropriate. Integrate systems and operational platforms using APIs and automation frameworks. Identify operational toil and replace repeated manual activities with sustainable engineering solutions. Contribute reusable automation, runbooks, and engineering patterns that can be applied across services.

Qualifications

Required Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience. Foundational experience in software engineering, cloud, infrastructure, systems engineering, production operations, or a related technical area. Working knowledge of at least one programming or scripting language such as Python, PowerShell, Java, or JavaScript. Understanding of application, infrastructure, cloud, and networking fundamentals. Basic knowledge of monitoring, logging, alerting, and troubleshooting. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Ability to collaborate effectively across engineering and operational teams. Interest in developing deeper expertise in SRE, observability, automation, and AI-enabled operations. Preferred Experience or exposure to one or more of the following: Observability technologies such as Dynatrace, Splunk, Grafana, Prometheus, or equivalent. OpenTelemetry and modern application instrumentation. Cloud platforms such as AWS or Microsoft Azure. Kubernetes and container technologies. Infrastructure as Code and automation technologies such as Terraform or Ansible. ServiceNow or enterprise IT Service Management platforms. CI/CD and modern software delivery practices. SRE concepts including SLIs, SLOs, error budgets, incident management, and post-incident reviews. AI/ML, Generative AI, AIOps, or AI-assisted operational tooling.
