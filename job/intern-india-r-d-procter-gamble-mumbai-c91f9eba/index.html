Intern, India R&D

Procter & Gamble · Mumbai

About the role

Job Location MUMBAI GENERAL OFFICE Job Description OVERVIEW OF THE JOB This opportunity is available ONLY to currently enrolled Master/PhD students. Job Title: Intern, India R&D We are looking for a passionate and talented individual with who demonstrates sharp learning agility and seeks to create in-depth technical mastery in developing superior propositions for the Indian consumer. We aim to develop business leaders with R&D mastery – individuals who constantly innovate and disrupt across product, packaging, and communication in the service of business. In this role, you will work on high impact problems that our businesses face every day and build solutions in collaboration with R&D and cross-functional teams. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE ROLE · Partner with cross-functional teams to understand challenges and define data-centric solutions · Understand market landscape and undertake comprehensive literature reviews to identify opportunities & gaps for our products · Understand business needs and work with on-site teams to develop new solutions to delight our consumers · Build models and develop new methods that can be sustainably utilized by the R&D team to deliver results · Partner with technologists to develop and test out prototype solutions · Capture key insights and technical findings from research projects and share results & recommendations in a clear succinct manner Job

Qualifications

ROLE REQUIREMENTS Currently pursuing M.Sc or PhD in Life Sciences, Chemical Sciences, Design or M.Tech in Bio Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Engineering, Research, or Design Intermediate knowledge of data analytics and computing. Communication Skills : Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English and ability to connect and work with people across countries and cultures. Interpersonal Skills: Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to lead and influence. Collaboration and Leadership : Ability to work effectively in a matrixed, team-oriented environment, demonstrating leadership and collaborative skills. Learning Agility : Ability to work independently, prioritize and perform in a fast-paced environment. Problem-Solving Skills : Ability to troubleshoot issues and implement systemic fixes. As the role would entail you working on P&G products and processes, majority of the work that you will be doing will be confidential in nature. Selected candidates would be required to sign a Confidentiality Disclosure Agreement (CDA) which would also restrict what may/may not be shared with the campus the student is enrolled in – depending on company guidelines at the point. ABOUT US We produce globally recognized brands and we grow the best business leaders in the industry. With a portfolio of trusted brands as diverse as ours, it is paramount our leaders are able to lead with courage the vast array of brands, categories and functions. We serve consumers around the world with one of the strongest portfolios of trusted, quality, leadership brands, including Always®, Ariel®, Gillette®, Head & Shoulders®, Herbal Essences®, Oral-B®, Pampers®, Pantene®, Tampax® and more. Our community includes operations in approximately 70 countries worldwide. Visit http://www.pg.com to know more. We are an equal opportunity employer and value diversity at our company.
