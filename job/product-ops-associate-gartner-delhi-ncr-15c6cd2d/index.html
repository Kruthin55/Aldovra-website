Product Ops Associate

Gartner · Delhi NCR

About the role

About this role: This is a unique opportunity to join the Product Operations team for Gartner Peer Insights (GPI). As a Product Operations Associate, you will support and scale Validation & Moderation (V&M) operations, ensuring the highest quality of global review data while enabling growth across key review‑sourcing programs. You will be empowered to own operational processes, collaborate cross‑functionally, solve problems, and help GPI scale as the market‑leading platform for enterprise technology reviews.

What you’ll do

Manage review content while ensuring accuracy, quality, consistency, and alignment with GPI standards. Engaging with enterprise technology users through structured conversations to capture clear, detailed, and authentic insights. Provide guided conversation around product experiences to people closer to enterprise product buying. Communicate professionally with reviewers and internal stakeholders across global regions. Manage operational workflows from reviewer engagement through content processing and publication. Exercise sound judgment while handling sensitive information, complex situations, and escalations. Maintain operational trackers and monitor key performance, quality, and turnaround-time metrics. Identify opportunities to improve the reviewer experience, content quality, and operational efficiency. Support pilots and new initiatives that strengthen and scale GPI’s content capabilities. Build deep expertise in GPI processes, platforms, policies, and quality standards.

What you’ll need

1-2 years of prior experience in Digital operations/People operations Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the confidence to engage senior professionals. Strong active-listening skills and the ability to ask clear, neutral, and relevant questions. Strong attention to detail and the ability to identify and resolve issues. Strong judgment, empathy, and professionalism when managing stakeholders and sensitive situations. Ability to organize multiple workflows and deliver against quality and turnaround-time expectations. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, supported by effective secondary research. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Outlook, and virtual collaboration tools. Ability to work effectively in a cross-functional, global, and evolving operational environment.
