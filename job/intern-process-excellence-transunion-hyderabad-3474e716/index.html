Intern - Process Excellence

TransUnion · Hyderabad

About the role

TransUnion's Job Applicant Privacy Notice Team Overview The TransUnion Agent & Client Experience (TrACE) team supports Global Operations by improving the experience of Clients and Agents across the support journey. The team is responsible for identifying and eliminating avoidable interactions, enabling effective self-service, and simplifying assisted support through improvements to processes, knowledge, tools, automation, and AI. TrACE serves as the Voice of Support Operations, bringing together Client, Agent, and operational insights to identify root causes, prioritize opportunities, define measurable outcomes, and partner with Product, Service, Technology, Automation, TruOps, CIS, and Delivery teams to drive sustainable improvements.

This job is assigned as On-Site Essential and requires in- person work at an assigned TU office location as a condition of employment. Role Overview And Core

Responsibilities

Data Collection & Cleaning: Gather, clean, and validate raw data from spreadsheets, databases, and internal tools. Process Improvement: Identify gaps or bottlenecks in workflow tracking and help streamline reporting templates. Project Coordination: Help plan, track, and coordinate day-to-day program milestones, action items, and cross-team dependencies. Stakeholder Communication: Take meeting notes, track open risks/issues, and assist in preparing status updates or decks for stakeholders. Ad-Hoc Analysis: Answer business and operational questions by running queries (SQL) or performing basic statistical and trend analyses. Required Knowledge And Experiences Education: Bachelor’s degree Technical Skills: Advanced Excel or Google Sheets (pivot tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, formulas). Data Visualization: Familiarity with tools like Power BI, or basic chart generation.
