KBS Process Associate - Logistics

KONE · Chennai

About the role

Did you know KONE moves over one billion people every day? We employ over 60,000 professionals in over 60 countries worldwide joined together by a shared vision to ‘Deliver the best people flow experience’. As a global leader, we provide elevators, escalators, automatic building doors as well as solutions for maintenance and modernization to add value to buildings throughout their lifecycle. Through more effective People Flow®, we make people’s journeys safe, convenient and reliable, in taller, smarter buildings. Job Title – KBS Process Associate - Logistics Job Location – Ambattur, Chennai Are you a detail-oriented professional who enjoys working with logistics processes, accurate documentation, and business transaction management? Do you thrive in a structured environment where timely execution, data accuracy, stakeholder coordination, and continuous improvement are critical? If you are passionate about supporting logistics operations, maintaining reliable delivery and invoicing records, and learning how AI and automation can improve business processes, we’d love to meet you. Join KONE and play a key role in supporting logistics operations that enable smooth delivery, documentation, and customer service processes. In this role, you will ensure accurate handling of delivery documentation, invoicing transactions, reporting activities, and process compliance, while also contributing to business-led improvements through digital tools, AI awareness, and automation-oriented ways of working. Are you the One ? As a Process Associate – Logistics , you will support logistics operations by performing core transactional and documentation activities, including: Processing Delivery Documentation and ensuring accuracy and completeness Supporting PO Management activities related to logistics operations Creating and maintaining invoicing documentation as per business requirements Handling Help Desk and Portal management requests from internal and external stakeholders Updating logistics data and maintaining accurate records in SAP systems Generating and maintaining routine logistics reports using Microsoft Excel Validating documentation and approvals before processing transactions Coordinating with supply chain, warehouse, and finance teams to resolve operational queries Ensuring compliance with logistics procedures and documentation standards Managing multiple requests while meeting defined service level agreements (SLAs) Maintaining proper documentation and records for audit and compliance purposes Using approved digital tools and AI-enabled capabilities responsibly to support data validation, reporting, documentation review, and productivity improvements Identifying repetitive, manual, or error-prone activities and highlighting opportunities for automation or process improvement What will you be doing ?
