Analyst - Global Trade Content

Thomson Reuters · Hyderabad

About the role

Job Description: We are seeking a detail-oriented professional to support the research, analysis, and maintenance of global trade and tariff content. In this role, you will interpret international trade regulations, ensure the accuracy and quality of regulatory data, support product enhancements, and leverage AI and automation tools to drive operational efficiency and continuous improvement. Key

Responsibilities

Research and interpret global trade regulations, tariff schedules, and related requirements from official sources. Monitor regulatory updates and maintain accurate, up-to-date content within internal systems. Convert complex regulatory information into standardized, software-compatible formats. Perform quality assurance and validation activities to ensure data accuracy and integrity. Investigate and respond to customer, stakeholder, and business inquiries. Support product enhancement initiatives, testing activities, and issue resolution. Create, update, and maintain process documentation and work instructions. Identify opportunities for process improvement and implement solutions using AI and automation tools. Collaborate with cross-functional teams and contribute to knowledge-sharing and training initiatives. Required

Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in International Trade, Business, Economics, Supply Chain Management, or a related field. 1-3 years of relevant professional experience in regulatory research, global trade, customs compliance, content management, business research, supply chain operations, data analysis, or a related field. Strong analytical, research, and problem-solving skills. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, databases, and data analysis tools. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities while maintaining a high level of accuracy and attention to detail. Demonstrated ability to work collaboratively in a team-oriented environment. Preferred

Qualifications

Experience using AI-powered tools to support research, analysis, and process improvement. Knowledge of global trade regulations, customs compliance, or tariff classification processes. Experience with data quality management, content validation, or regulatory research. Familiarity with automation tools and continuous improvement methodologies. Strong customer-service mindset with the ability to effectively address stakeholder inquiries.
