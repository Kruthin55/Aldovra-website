Windows Technical Support Engineer

FIS · Pune

About the role

Application Maintenance and Support Engineer Job Location: Pune Innovation isn’t just what we do; it’s how we work. At FIS, you’ll collaborate with talented people across the globe to create solutions that shape the way the world pays, banks and invests. If you’re ready to grow your career while making a meaningful impact, we’d love to meet you. About the Team The AddVantage Technical Support team provides 24x7 operational and technical support for FIS AddVantage, a real-time, browser-based solution supporting asset management and trust accounting functions. AddVantage environments primarily run on Microsoft Windows platforms and leverage InterSystems Caché databases. These systems are hosted either within FIS data centers or at customer-managed locations. The team is responsible for ensuring high availability, performance, security, and operational excellence across customer environments while providing expert support for business-critical applications and infrastructure. What You Will Be Doing As a Senior Technical Support Engineer supporting AddVantage, you will play a key role in maintaining, enhancing, and supporting mission-critical client environments.

Responsibilities

include: Administering, maintaining, and tuning AddVantage environments running on Microsoft Windows Server 2016 and 2022. Designing, implementing, and supporting automation solutions to improve customer processing and operational efficiency. Troubleshooting and resolving complex production issues, including system outages, performance degradation, and application-related incidents. Planning and executing operating system upgrades, including Windows Server migrations Investigating and resolving issues related to InterSystems Caché databases and associated application components. Mentoring, training, and providing technical leadership to junior team members. Acting as a liaison between the AddVantage technical team and corporate infrastructure teams, including network engineering and security organizations. Reviewing, coordinating, and monitoring infrastructure and network projects impacting AddVantage environments. Developing and maintaining scripts and automated workflows using Tidal Workload Automation and other enterprise automation tools. Monitoring, troubleshooting, and resolving failed batch processes, file transfers, and scheduled tasks. Participating in change management, incident management, and problem management activities to ensure service reliability and continuous improvement. Collaborating with internal support teams, vendors, and customers to deliver timely and effective technical solutions. What You Bring Required

Qualifications

3-6 years of strong experience administering Microsoft Windows Server environments. Experience with secure file transfer technologies, including SFTP, FTPS, and related protocols. Strong customer service and communication skills with the ability to engage both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Proven ability to manage technical projects and initiatives from planning through implementation. Experience supporting enterprise applications in production environments. Knowledge of network infrastructure, networking concepts, and troubleshooting methodologies. Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams, including application support, infrastructure, network engineering, and security teams. Ability to troubleshoot complex technical issues and drive issues to resolution. Experience with IIS management Preferred

Qualifications

Experience with InterSystems Caché or InterSystems IRIS databases. Familiarity with Tidal Workload Automation. Experience supporting IBM Sterling Connect:Direct in a Windows environment. Experience with PowerShell, scripting, and infrastructure automation. Knowledge of enterprise monitoring, scheduling, and operational support processes. Experience with modern observability tools like Dyntrace, Zabbix, and Splunk Experience with modern software delivery and deployment tools such as Octopus On-Call

Responsibilities

This position participates in a rotating on-call support schedule, typically one week per month, including weekend coverage. During on-call periods, team members provide support for critical issues outside normal business hours to ensure the continued availability of customer environments.
