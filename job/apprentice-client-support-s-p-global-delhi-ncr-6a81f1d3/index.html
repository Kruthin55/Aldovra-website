Apprentice, Client Support

S&P Global · Delhi NCR

About the role

About the Role: Grade Level (for internal use): 05 The Team: The Client Operations team is responsible for providing client support on our business intelligence tool by holding investigative conversations & driving product usage and revenue. Being present in the US, Philippines & Pakistan makes it a truly global team. As a member of the Client Services team, you will serve as a product generalist for the Capital IQ and Capital IQ Pro platforms. Working as a team is essential in this role. Additionally, Client Services interact with many internal teams, including Sales, Content, Product, and Technical Support. The Impact: As the face of the organization, your regular day requires you to answer client queries while showcasing your vast knowledge of our tools, data, and the industries we cover. Additionally, you’ll help to accelerate the quality and timeliness of the data, improve operational efficiency to reduce error rates, and improve support survey scores to help maintain retention rates and drive revenue increases. What’s in it for you: Your communication will further improve as you speak to various client partners. You will learn how to critically see problems and identify solutions to give the first-class support to our clients. Sharpen your professional and social skills while interacting with leaders from different industries. Develop your industry knowledge by working with our diverse client base at various financial institutions, government organizations & Universities located across the globe. Be part of a company that is a results-focused meritocracy. We set high standards and value accountability. We seek to identify and reward extraordinary performance with growth opportunities. We pride ourselves in helping our employees attain their career dreams and reach their potential. We value employee development and provide training that will hone both soft and hard skills as you go along with your career with us.

Responsibilities

As a Product Consultant within the Capital Markets Client Services Team Gurgaon, your day-to-day responsibilities will be to: Deliver the best client experience while providing support and finance-related research. Be the client’s advocate in getting the best possible solution to their inquiries. We partner with our clients to ensure that their issues are fully resolved, and they are improving our products’ potential. Be a channel for client feedback. We coordinate with our Content and Product Management team to resolve client inquiries. We also help our content teams develop accuracy by providing real-time client feedback. We echo our clients’ ideas and feedback to our team to make sure that we stay substantial to the market. Shift requirements: Night shifts
