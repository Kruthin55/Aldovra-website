OS Development Engineer

Intel · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Details: Job Description: The Role and Impact: As an OS Development Engineer, you will play a pivotal role in designing and optimizing operating systems to ensure seamless integration with cutting-edge hardware features. Your day-to-day work will involve crafting Device Drivers, hardware abstraction layers, enhancing OS services, and developing user space software subsystems using advanced technologies. This role is critical to driving performance, power management, and innovation in Intel's software ecosystem Business group: You will join Intel's Software division, a dynamic and forward-thinking group focused on driving impactful software solutions across diverse technologies and platforms. The team's work spans foundational software development, enabling Intel's hardware features, and enhancing user experiences globally. This group is integral to delivering innovative solutions that shape Intel's leadership in the technology space. Key

Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain operating system components, including device drivers, hardware abstraction layers and OS services. - Optimize software subsystems for performance, power management, and reliability. - Knowledge on Android runtime and compilers. - Exposure to Android tools. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate software features with Intel's silicon architecture. - Identify and resolve software challenges related to operating system internals and platform features

Qualifications

Minimum Qualifications- - Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field with 4+ years of experience in OS development OR a Master's degree in relevant field with 3+ years of experience. - Proficiency in programming languages such as C, C++, Python, scripting. - Hands-on experience in operating system internals, hardware abstraction layers, or driver development. - Strong knowledge of software architecture, design principles, and optimization techniques. Preferred Qualifications- - Familiarity with technologies like virtualization, containerization, and networking protocols. - Experience with graphics/media user space components. - Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work collaboratively in cross-functional teams. - Passion for driving innovative solutions and optimizing software performance. Join us in shaping the future of technology and making a meaningful impact through your expertise. Apply today to become part of Intel's dynamic and forward-thinking software community.
