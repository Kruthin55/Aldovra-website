Purchase Associate

Maersk · Mumbai

About the role

Purpose : The buyer is responsible for managing purchase requisitions of goods and services for several terminals in APM Terminals. The buyer ensures that supplies are ordered and delivered in a timely manner based on the best total cost of ownership and in compliance with the internal policies and guidelines. The buyer also maintains and updates the vendor database and develops positive relationships with internal and external stakeholders. Principal Accountabilities

Ensure timely, accurate and efficient processing of purchase requisitions/orders in respect to SOPs and internal customers’ requirements as defined in the SLA KPIs. o Process authorized/released purchase requisition lines from IFS10. o Handle emergency purchases and document them. o Ask the requisitioner to provide additional information if the PR is incomplete or incorrect (e.g. missing documentation or incorrect part number or incorrect supplier ID). o Challenge maverick buying cases, understand why and document them. o Get as many supplier quotations as required by threshold level. If unable to do so, ask for exceptional approval. o Process PRs mastered by eCat directly to purchase orders. o Process admin PRs (delivery control code STOP and DELI, and blanket POs) directly to purchase orders. o Put change order reason in post-it notes when creating a CO. Add invoice charges to PO with change order. o Perform delivery follow-up and update Requisitioners in case of delivery date changes (especially for urgent POs). o Support in resolving PO and invoice mismatches.

Maintain and update vendor database to establish reliable supply sources and back-up sources to ensure uninterrupted delivery. Classification: Confidential

Continuously follow up on the proper adherence to established policies and guidelines by the other departments and report effectively all discrepancies and propose ways of improvement.

Maintain and further develop positive relationships between key stakeholders and the Procurement department.

Strive to continuously improve working processes, ensure reliability by maintaining good relations with suppliers and reduce cost and complexity.

Perform other tasks as requested by SCO (Supply Chain Operations) manager.

Respect all safety, security, and health requirements for various work environments. Business Contacts and Authority: Internal

Procurement manager.

Terminal management team.

All relevant stakeholders/requisitioners and functions in the terminal.

Central procurement / group procurement. Nature of contact is internal and while primarily will be one of a shared interest, but there will be instances where interests will be divergent. External

Contractors.

Service providers. Critical Qualifications/Skills/Experience

Bachelor / master's degree in purchasing, business, logistics or management.

Knowledge of purchasing foundations and practices, product and service vendor sources. Classification: Confidential

Strong knowledge of MS office and ERP systems.

Good communication skills in English, Spanish (hearing, speaking, reading and writing).

Proven ability to work under pressure and handle a high volume of requisitions and tight deadlines.

Multi-tasking and prioritizing, planning work activities efficiently to meet deadlines and high-level goals.

Willingly collaborates. Is not afraid to seek advice from others. Puts in extra effort when needed to help others. Recognizes that the ways of getting things done in different departments, organizations are not the same.

Customer service oriented, thinks about the customer when undertaking day-to day work.

Strong negotiation skills to enable the company to procure needed materials, supplies and services of the appropriate levels of quality at the lowest possible cost without compromising long term relationship with stakeholders.

Ability to look objectively at every side of an idea or situation to ensure that all outcomes are thoroughly assessed before deciding on the appropriate course of action.

Knowledge of international and local markets and following the new trends in the fields of spare parts and services for port industry.

The incumbent requires high ethical standards. -Learning and Career Opportunities In this position, the incumbent will have an opportunity to acquire skills and knowledge in the following areas:

Exposure to procurement management practices in a large company.
