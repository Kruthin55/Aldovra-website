AIOps Engineer, NCT

Deutsche Bank · Pune

About the role

Job Description: Job Title: AIOps Engineer, NCT Location: Pune, India Corporate Title: NCT Role Description Deutsche Bank’s Strategy & Innovation Engineering team is part of the TDI Chief Strategy & Innovation Office. The team partners with business and CIO teams to evaluate emerging technologies, build and operate reusable AI capabilities, and enable safe, responsible AI adoption across the bank. As an AIOps Engineer within the India engineering and operations team, you will develop automation and improve the reliability, observability and operational readiness of enterprise AI platforms, shared AI services and agent-based solutions. This hands-on Associate role combines cloud engineering, DevOps/MLOps and production support with AI-assisted incident analysis and controlled remediation. You will collaborate with application, platform, architecture, security and governance teams to turn operational challenges into reusable engineering solutions. What we’ll offer you As part of our flexible scheme, here are just some of the benefits that you’ll enjoy Best in class leave policy Gender neutral parental leaves 100% reimbursement under childcare assistance benefit (gender neutral) Sponsorship for Industry relevant certifications and education Employee Assistance Program for you and your family members Comprehensive Hospitalization Insurance for you and your dependents Accident and Term life Insurance Complementary Health screening for 35 yrs. and above Your key responsibilities Build and maintain reusable AIOps capabilities for telemetry collection, alert correlation, incident context enrichment, root-cause analysis and operational automation. Engineer and support cloud-native AI services on Google Cloud Platform, using GKE, Cloud Run, Vertex AI, storage, messaging and approved enterprise integrations within agreed ownership boundaries. Develop CI/CD pipelines and reusable infrastructure-as-code modules using GitHub Actions, Terraform and container tooling, with testing, security checks and controlled releases. Implement logs, metrics, traces, dashboards, health checks and service-level indicators to improve service visibility and reduce alert noise. Develop AI-assisted diagnostic and runbook workflows, integrating operational knowledge and incident history; evaluate output accuracy and retain human review for consequential decisions. Implement policy-bound remediation through approved pipelines, with least-privilege identities, approval gates, rollback, failure safeguards and traceable action records. Contribute to production incident investigation and engineering fixes; enable L1/L2 support through runbooks, diagnostic guidance, knowledge transfer and clear escalation paths. Improve production readiness through capacity and performance analysis, recovery testing, monitoring, support handovers and release/change documentation. Embed applicable security, data protection and AI controls into engineering workflows; support vulnerability remediation and maintain evidence with designated control owners. Automate onboarding, service-management integration and routine operational tasks; measure improvements in detection and resolution time, manual effort, service reliability and cost. Document reusable patterns and contribute to proofs of concept, technical reviews and continuous improvement with global engineering and support teams. Your skills and experience Hands-on experience in cloud engineering, DevOps, SRE, MLOps or production engineering, with practical ownership of automation and troubleshooting tasks. Practical Google Cloud Platform knowledge, including GKE/Kubernetes, Cloud Run, IAM, VPC networking, Cloud Storage, Cloud Logging and Cloud Monitoring. Experience with Terraform, Git, GitHub Actions or equivalent CI/CD tooling, Docker and secure software delivery practices. Strong Python programming skills, with working knowledge of shell scripting, SQL, REST APIs and Linux troubleshooting. Experience implementing observability, analysing logs and metrics, investigating incidents and creating operational dashboards and runbooks. Working knowledge of AI/ML and Generative AI applications, including model APIs, prompt and inference patterns, retrieval-augmented generation, agent workflows and evaluation. Understanding of least privilege, workload identity, secrets management, network security, vulnerability management and auditable change practices. Understanding of responsible AI, data protection, traceability and human oversight, with the ability to translate control requirements into practical engineering actions. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, clear written and verbal communication, and effective collaboration across distributed technical and control teams. Preferred experience and qualifications: Experience with Vertex AI, OpenTelemetry, ServiceNow integrations, agent frameworks, model/agent evaluation or controlled remediation is an advantage. A bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, engineering or an equivalent discipline is desirable. Relevant Google Cloud, Kubernetes, DevOps/SRE or AI/ML certifications and experience in banking or another regulated industry are advantageous. Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimise workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs. How we’ll support you Training and development to help you excel in your career Coaching and support from experts in your team A culture of continuous learning to aid progression A range of flexible benefits that you can tailor to suit your needs
