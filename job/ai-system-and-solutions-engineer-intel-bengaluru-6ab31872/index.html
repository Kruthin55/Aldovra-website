AI System and Solutions Engineer

Intel · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Details: Job Description: The Role and Impact We are seeking a highly skilled AI Systems and Solutions Engineer to design, develop, validation and debug of Artificial Intelligence (AI) solutions & AI accelerators systems. In this role, you will be responsible for defining and implementing system validation architecture that integrate hardware, software, firmware, and silicon components to meet customer requirements and overcome technical challenges. Your contributions will directly shape the performance, reliability, and capability of cutting-edge AI platforms, enabling transformative innovations in AI technologies. Business Group You will join Intel's Data Center Group (DCG), which focuses on delivering world-class technology and solutions for data-centric applications. With a commitment to innovation, this group drives advancements in data processing, analytics, and infrastructure optimization, supporting Intel's broader vision for powering the future of computing. As part of the AI Validation Cluster team, you will collaborate with experts across disciplines to bring AI solutions from concept to market, ensuring reliability and performance excellence. Key

Responsibilities

- Lead system architecture design, validation and Debug for integrated AI solutions. - Translate business opportunities into technical use cases and product specifications for hardware and software requirements. - Develop and validate AI solutions using advanced methods. - Analyze system-level choices for performance, cost, reliability, security, maintainability, and scalability across integrated AI systems. - Define and execute systems implementation and integration plans for optimal performance and reliability. - Engage in real-world system validation, conduct performance analyses, and optimize prototypes for peak efficiency. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to refine design robustness, drive AI product roadmaps, and influence next-generation technology development. - Prepare technical documentation, conduct benchmark tests, and provide reliable engineering recommendations for AI systems.

Qualifications

Minimum

Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Information Systems, Electrical Engineering, or a related field with 9+ years of experience, or Master's degree with 8+ years of experience, or PhD with 4+ years of experience. - Strong understanding of GPU architecture, including validation exposure to GPU pipeline, firmware, frequency throttling, secure fuse and interrupts. - Expertise in debugging PCIe-specific issues and Linux system/user mode programming. - Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, C, and C++, with experience in shell scripting. - Solid knowledge of PCIe, Memory, Power Management (PM), Firmware, and Bootloader (expertise in at least two domains). Preferred

Qualifications

- Familiarity with machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch and/or TensorFlow and their applications in data science. - Experience deploying and validating AI and machine learning models. - Knowledge of Intel or ARM-based platform architecture and debug tools/frameworks. - Strong analytical problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills. - Demonstrated ability to thrive in a dynamic team environment and manage concurrent tasks effectively. Join us and be part of a team that is shaping the future of AI systems. Your expertise could drive robust solutions that redefine industry standards, contribute to cutting-edge technologies, and create meaningful impact for customers worldwide.
