Campus-Beginner

HCLTech · India

About the role

Job Summary Implementation of compensation practices in accordance with organization norms. Act as the first point of contact for employees for HR-related queries Resolve queries related to Payroll (salary, deductions, payslip),

Benefits

(insurance, leave, PF, gratuity), Performance Bons and Compensation & policies Key

Responsibilities

Compensation letter releases based on pre-defined guidelines issued by Central Planning Team from time-to-time. Strict adherence to all norms and rules laid down on comp derivation including statutory and compliance matters. Apply effective communication strategies for LOB connect. Coordination with all stakeholders to regularize and resolve grey areas. Carry out frequent audits to identify gaps and system challenges. Take initiatives and suggest automations in assigned areas including UAT testing and sharing observation or sign-offs in-order to maximize transactions through system. Respond to compensation related queries coming from SSD channel and through mails from others. Log, track, and manage employee requests in ticketing tools Identify cases requiring escalation, coordinate with HR, Payroll, Finance, etc. Ensure employees receive standardized and correct information Maintain and update: Knowledge base articles, FAQs and SOPs Stay updated on: Policy changes, Payroll updates, Labor law changes Skill

Requirements

Strong quantitative skills Ability to build and maintain relationships with internal stakeholders and cross-functional teams Verbal & written communication and presentation skills Aptitude & passion for both strategy formulation and program execution Organized and methodical, Ability to handle high-volume workload Experience with developing and drafting process and system workflows Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Advance Formulas & Functions Other
