Lab Engineer This role has been designed as ‘’Onsite’ with an expectation that you will primarily work from an HPE office. Job Description: What you will do : A Lab Administrator is responsible for the closure of a high number of trouble tickets submitted by various individuals throughout the company. Lab Administrators have a firm grasp on concepts within the lab and can perform the following tasks with little or no supervision: Installation of network and/or computer equipment Software re-installation of lab devices Adding new devices to our database system Troubleshooting and replacing of broken hardware Building or changing topologies between devices, and documenting the changes Building and deploying Virtual Machines (using ESX vCenter) What you need to bring : Lab Administrators will have hands on experience with unix or Linux configuration and a desire to expand knowledge of Networking and UNIX concepts. Lab Administrators need to be able to configure and troubleshoot JUNOS based routers. Lab Administrators are expected to pursue Juniper Certification to expand their knowledge of the routers they support. Lab Administrators are the first stop along the escalation track for Jr. Lab Administrators. Lab Administrators are expected to mentor and teach Jr. Lab Administrators the ways of the lab. All Lab Administrators are also responsible for keeping a safe, clean, and productive lab environment, which will require manual tasks such as removal of old cable from racks and the auditing and fixing of areas in the lab that have fallen from policy standards.
