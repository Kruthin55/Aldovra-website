CIB Lending Operations Administration Associate-P1

Wells Fargo · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a CIB Lending Operations Administration Associate to support warehouse lending and mortgage financing operations, with a focus on quality oversight, risk management, process governance, and operational excellence. The role will help manage complex operational activities, resolve escalations, strengthen controls, and drive process improvements while partnering with key stakeholders to deliver consistent business outcomes In this role, you will: Ensure the accuracy, completeness, and control integrity of data and documentation across the lending lifecycle; participate in loan administration initiatives, execution of day‑to‑day operational processes, data reconciliations and adherence to established risk and control standards Participate in or provide support for low-complexity initiatives related to framework and control execution within Corporate & Investment Banking Lending Operations as a middle office function Provide transactional support, lending processing fundamentals, policy and procedure comprehension Support low‑complexity loan administration and possibly closing activities, review basic loan documentation and policies, perform credit research and project‑related analysis under supervision Collaborate with team members and follow established processes Review basic or tactical issues, policies, and procedures for which answers can be quickly obtained Support low-risk tasks and deliverables with narrower impact within the functional area Assist with documenting execution frameworks, control activities, and related procedures Perform assigned work under direction from a supervisor, exercising judgment within defined parameters; develop a working understanding of functional processes, policies, procedures, and compliance requirements Provide information to managers, functional colleagues, and stakeholders, including internal customers as applicable Required

Qualifications

6+ months of banking operations, finance, credit, or related experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, or education Desired

Qualifications

Experience in Warehouse Lending, Mortgage Financing, Loan Operations, Quality Assurance, Risk Management, Governance, or Financial Services Operations. Strong understanding of warehouse lending, mortgage operations, quality oversight, and process governance. Experience managing workflow priorities, operational reviews, production expectations, and service-level adherence. Ability to support operational planning, quality strategies, workload management, and governance routines. Experience handling complex escalations and providing subject matter expertise. Exposure to quality reviews, control assessments, remediation, and process improvement initiatives. Strong analytical skills with exposure to operational metrics, risk indicators, quality trends, and corrective/preventive actions. Knowledge of audit readiness, operational risk, regulatory requirements, and control effectiveness. Exposure to process optimization, transformation, automation, and standardization. Experience in coaching, training, knowledge sharing, and quality feedback is an added advantage. Loan IQ experience is preferred but not mandatory. Experience within banking or financial services organizations, particularly in similar lending operations, is preferred. Job Expectations: Willingness to work in US shift timings Should be flexible to work in a hybrid model or from the office daily, based on business requirements.
