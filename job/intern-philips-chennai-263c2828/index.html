About the role

Job Title Intern Job Description

Job description

As a Site Planning Intern at Philips, you will support the Site Planning Team (architects, engineers, and designers) in designing hospital spaces for medical health systems, utilizing AutoCAD and architectural knowledge to prepare layouts for X-rays, MRs, CTs, and IGTs rooms. Your responsibilities

Supporting the development of preliminary project plans according to the specialist requirements.

Supporting the team in the analysis of documentation received

Follow-up and production of architectural floor plans for each project To succeed in this role, you should have the following skills and experience

A student enrolled in a University, ideally studying Architecture, Civil Engineering, etc.

Advance knowledge AutoCAD software

Advance English both verbal and written

Intermediate MS Office (Excel is a must) In return, we offer you

6-months internship based on competitive remuneration

Hybrid working hours. Early morning shift.

Possibility to build your career in an international company that brings innovations that matter

Work in an international environment and the possibility of interacting with Global markets How we work together We believe that we are better together than apart.
