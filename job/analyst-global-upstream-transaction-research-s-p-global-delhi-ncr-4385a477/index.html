Analyst, Global Upstream Transaction Research

S&P Global · Delhi NCR

About the role

About the Role: Grade Level (for internal use): 09 Role Overview: Research Analyst will play a key role in supporting the Low-Carbon M&A research capability, contributing to a broad range of deliverables, including transaction analyses, valuation assessments, insight reports, and analytics dashboards. The role will focus on generating data-driven insights into how global energy companies, financial investors, and strategic players are navigating the energy transition through M&A, joint ventures, partnerships, and other investment structures. The analyst will actively track and analyse low-carbon M&A activity across technologies such as renewable generation, electricity transmission and grid infrastructure, energy storage, CCUS, hydrogen, EVs, and other emerging low-carbon solutions. In addition to maintaining high-quality analytical tools and datasets, the role will involve interpreting market trends, identifying strategic themes, and supporting research publications and client engagements. The analyst will also contribute to the adoption of AI-enabled research workflows to enhance data quality, research efficiency, and the development of differentiated market insights. Key

Responsibilities

Track and analyze low-carbon M&A deals and M&A market trends. Maintain and enhance low-carbon M&A databases, ensuring data accuracy, consistency, and methodological alignment with broader M&A research frameworks. Contribute to the development of insight-led research outputs, including thematic reports, market commentaries, and client-specific analyses focused on low-carbon and power & renewables M&A trends. Provide analytical support for client queries and presentations, including preparation of charts, tables, and written insights. Work closely with global teams to research market developments, emerging investment themes, and evolving corporate strategies; proactively flag key trends, anomalies, and data gaps for review. Support the design and development of an Analytics dashboard to track low-carbon M&A activities and trends. Mentor junior team members as required and contribute to process improvements, documentation, and best practices within the Low-Carbon M&A research workflow.

Requirements

MBA/Master’s degree in energy/finance/general management. Strong understanding of the energy sector, with exposure to power & renewables, low-carbon technologies, or energy transition-related investments preferred. Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with the ability to translate data into clear, structured insights. Ability to leverage AI-driven tools and automation techniques to improve research efficiency, data interpretation, trend identification, and analytical output quality. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with experience contributing to research reports or client-facing content. Experience working with data visualization and dashboard tools (e.g., Power BI, Tableau), including trend analysis and reporting. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, and Word. High attention to detail, strong ownership mindset, and the ability to manage multiple deliverables in a deadline-driven environment. Self-motivated, collaborative, and comfortable working with global teams across time zones. prior experience in energy research, M&A analysis, business analytics, or a related research/consulting role, with increasing analytical responsibility.
