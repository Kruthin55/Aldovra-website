You will investigate and resolve production issues, support customer-facing applications and integrations, and help ensure reliable service delivery for lenders, fintechs, and digital marketplace clients. Working with Engineering, Product, Delivery, and Operations teams across multiple locations, you will contribute to incident resolution, service improvements, and the development of operational knowledge within a growing support function.   What You'll Do (Roles & Responsibilities): Investigate and resolve assigned incidents and service requests, ensuring communication, partner updates, and adherence to agreed service levels. Review client configurations, eligibility rules, scorecard outcomes, and decision logic to identify data, configuration, or application-related issues affecting customer journeys.
