Systems Engineer- Workday - Finance ERP

Job Category Software Engineering Job Details About Salesforce Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together. Here, ambition meets action. Tech meets trust. And innovation isn’t a buzzword — it’s a way of life. The world of work as we know it is changing and we're looking for Trailblazers who are passionate about bettering business and the world through AI, driving innovation, and keeping Salesforce's core values at the heart of it all. Ready to level-up your career at the company leading workforce transformation in the agentic era? You’re in the right place! Agentforce is the future of AI, and you are the future of Salesforce. The Experience Systems Engineer is responsible for administering and managing the Finance systems & applications that support our mission critical business processes. Systems Engineer will be the primary process expert in their service area and will work in collaboration with developers, business stakeholders, and other technology teams in support of Salesforce end to end business processes. Daily activities will include, but are not limited to, enterprise application support, resolving technical and functional system issues, performing trend analysis and identifying enhancements to reduce support requests, and collaborating with development teams to implement new and enhanced functionality. The successful candidate must be agile and able to support multiple priorities. You're Our Person If... 4-9 years of Hands-on expertise in any of the Workday Financials modules (Account Receivables, Payables, Assets, GL, Banking & Settlement). Working knowledge of Workday Security, Business Processes, Reports, Calc Fields, Integrations framework, Studio, BIRT and EIBs. Expertise in Workday Fundamentals and Financial Functional Configurations. Expertise in handling production support cases (L2/L3), incident management and problem management. Certified Workday expert in at-least one of the Financial module. Experience in Accounting Center and Prism Analytics for finance data transformation and reporting. Well-versed with Workday Community and latest features/enhancements of Workday Releases. Analyze and resolve complex configuration, automation, data, security, and integration issues. Perform root-cause analysis and implement preventive solutions rather than relying on workarounds. Experience working in a SOX compliant environment. Demonstrate experience through AI/agentic workflow transformation, including redesigning processes to incorporate AI agents alongside human support engineers. Identify opportunities to automate manual business processes and improve operational efficiency. Even Better If... Experience in global ERP implementation especially implementations related to North America , EMEA, APAC countries Experience working as part of a Scrum team practicing Agile development methodologies. Knowledge of accounting principles and financial cloud applications. Knowledge of Salesforce platform or Mulesoft Cloud hub is a huge plus. Knowledge of Coupa, Concur or other Financials SaaS applications will be an added advantage. Unleash Your Potential When you join Salesforce, you’ll be limitless in all areas of your life. Our benefits and resources support you to find balance and be your best , and our AI agents accelerate your impact so you can do your best . Together, we’ll bring the power of Agentforce to organizations of all sizes and deliver amazing experiences that customers love.
