Executive Assistant

Zinnov · Bengaluru

About the role

About Zinnov

Zinnov is a global management consulting firm that helps organizations make decisions that actually get used — and deliver results that matter. For over two decades, we’ve partnered with leading enterprises, high-growth technology companies, and investors to answer some of the toughest questions they face:

Where should we invest?

How do we scale globally?

What capabilities will win in the next decade?

Our work shapes market entry strategies, global operating models, M&A decisions, and long-term growth bets. We’re known for being data-led, execution-focused, and outcome-driven — not opinion-heavy slideware.

At Zinnov, how you work matters as much as what you deliver. We value independent thinking, crisp communication, and early ownership. With 450+ professionals across 10 global offices, we work across industries including Digital Services, ER&D, Enterprise Software, Semiconductors, Healthcare, BFSI, Automotive, Media & Telecom, and Private Equity.

Zinnov isn’t for everyone. It’s for people who want steep learning curves, honest feedback, and the chance to see their work influence real business decisions — not just presentations.

Why Corporate Functions?

Zinnov’s Corporate Functions teams power how the firm delivers at scale—building the internal engine behind high-growth, high-impact work. You’ll get:

Direct contribution to running and scaling operations that matter—spanning HR & compliance, talent acquisition, marketing, employer branding, facilities/infra, learning & development, and finance & compliance, whichever one you’re here for.

Exposure to operating models where Zinnov provides end-to-end enablement building the internal operating engine, employee experience, and scalable processes.

A highly cross-functional environment—working closely with delivery/PMO and domain teams to drive consistent execution and employee experience at scale.

About the Role

As an Executive Assistant (EA) , you will provide high-trust, high-ownership support to the CFO, CMO, and CHRO , ensuring their time, priorities, and internal/external engagements run smoothly. This role is designed for someone who is highly organized, proactive, and calm under pressure — with strong judgment, discretion, and the ability to manage multiple moving parts in a fast-paced environment.

You will be the coordination backbone for leadership calendars, travel, meetings, communication, and day-to-day execution — ensuring leaders stay focused on the highest-impact priorities.

What You’ll Do

Calendar & Schedule Management

Manage and prioritize complex calendars, meetings, and appointments across time zones.

Proactively resolve scheduling conflicts and protect focus time for key priorities.
