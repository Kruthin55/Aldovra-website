Software Intern

GE HealthCare · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary Job Description Job Summary As a Software Intern at GE Precision Healthcare LLC, you will contribute to the development and enhancement of cutting-edge healthcare software solutions, gaining hands-on experience in a dynamic and innovative environment. You will work alongside experienced engineers to solve real-world challenges in medical technology. Job

Responsibilities

* Assist in the design, development, and testing of software modules for healthcare applications. * Participate in code reviews and contribute to improving code quality and maintainability. * Debug and troubleshoot software issues, identifying root causes and implementing effective solutions. * Document software designs, specifications, and test plans. * Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including product management and quality assurance, to ensure successful project delivery. * Research and evaluate new technologies and tools to improve software development processes. * Contribute to the overall software development lifecycle, from requirements gathering to deployment. Job

Qualifications

* Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field. * Strong understanding of fundamental programming concepts and data structures. * Proficiency in at least one object-oriented programming language (e.g., C++, Java, Python, C#). * Familiarity with software development tools and methodologies (e.g., Git, Agile). * Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills. * Strong written and verbal communication skills. * Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team. Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: No
