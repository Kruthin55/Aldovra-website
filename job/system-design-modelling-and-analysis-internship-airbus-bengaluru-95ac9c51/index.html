System Design, modelling and Analysis : Internship

Airbus · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description: System Design, modelling and Analysis : Internship __________________________________________________________________________ Feeling ready to spread your wings ? What if your adventure started with us ?! Whatever your dream job, we might have the internship for you ! Are you a student looking for an internship that will validate your degree and launch your career ? Imagine yourself at the heart of an internationally recognized company , established on every continent, and rich in diverse backgrounds, expertise, and cultures. Focused on digital and at the forefront of research and innovation , you have the opportunity to be part of this exciting Adventure. Your mission: An internship opportunity titled : Internship Advanced Engineering & System Studies has just opened at Airbus India located in Bengaluru. This is mainly about modelling & simulation of various aircraft systems aimed at design improvement and maintenance of systems (e.g. electrical, fuel, landing gear etc) various inhouse and commercial tools are used to achieve the objectives. Ideally, this is a 6 month internship and it should start in (January, 2027)(date subject to flexibility). This position requires a security clearance or requires to be eligible for clearance by recognized authorities. Your main missions will consist of : Design Support: Learn how to design, development, and optimization of aerospace components and systems for major aircraft programs (e.g., A320, A330, A350 etc) under the guidance of senior staff. Technical Analysis: Learn to support engineering analysis of system and component performance data and help troubleshoot technical challenges by proposing creative, data-driven solutions. Tool Utilization: Learn and apply industry-standard engineering tools and methodologies for design, analysis, and validation. R&D Participation: Learn the process of aircraft system and component technology maturation, using tradeoff studies for initial concept generation, feasibility studies and prototyping. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Learn to work in a collaborative environment of new systems, products and components, gaining exposure to R&D, design, manufacturing, and testing environments. Agile Development: Learn about development of Simulation Development Environment applications using AGILE methodologies. Documentation: Learn how to prepare technical reports and documentation, capturing essential parts of a study and fulfilling the specified requirement. Market & Tech Research: Develop general understanding of emerging technologies and industry trends to stay most efficient and relevant. Your tutor will support you identify your internship objectives aimed at developing your skills This internship could allow you to develop the following specific skills: Data-Driven Problem Solving Aircraft system understanding and Modelling & Simulation Professional Soft Skills Trend Analysis techniques for data Your Boarding Pass: You should be pursuing technical Under Graduate or post graduate in Aerospace, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics or Instrumentation and Control. You consider following as your strength: - Technical skills : - Basic inclination towards aerospace. - Basic behavioural modeling of systems using matlab or simulink - Programming in C, C++, Matlab, java, C #, Python. - Soft Skills : - Strong problem solving and logical thinking ability - Flexibility and adaptability - Ability to work well in a team environment - Language skills : - English : Good understanding This job requires an awareness of any potential compliance risks and a commitment to act with integrity, as the foundation for the Company’s success, reputation and sustainable growth.
