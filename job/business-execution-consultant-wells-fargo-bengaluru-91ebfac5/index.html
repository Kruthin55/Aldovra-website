Business Execution Consultant

Wells Fargo · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role: Corporate & Investment Banking (“CIB”) delivers a comprehensive suite of capital markets, banking, and financial products and services. A trusted partner to our clients, we provide corporate and transactional banking; commercial real estate lending and servicing; investment banking; equity; and fixed income solutions including sales, trading, and research capabilities to corporate, commercial real estate, government, and institutional clients across the globe. Wells Fargo’s Structured Products Group (SPG), part of the Markets division in CIB, provides direct structured lending in addition to the underwriting and distribution of asset-backed securities for clients who access the bond markets through securitizations. The India team works as an extension of the global SPG team across a broad range of asset classes, covering large domestic and international clients, including corporates, non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), financial institutions, insurers, mortgage and real estate finance companies, and investment funds. The Business Execution role supports the SPG Portfolio Management and Underwriting team across controls and governance, data management, credit administration, change management, and transformation initiatives. The role also supports SPG leaders with management reporting, business and headcount updates, and analysis to inform franchise strategy and priorities. The successful candidate will work closely with SPG colleagues in Bangalore and the U.S., with opportunities to learn, develop, and contribute as an integral member of the business. In this role you will: Maintain the control inventory, including up-to-date control descriptions, ratings, and procedure links, and provide subject-matter inputs. Track Shared Risk Platform (SHRP) change requests and coordinate their status and completion with the center of excellence. Coordinate Independent Testing & Validation (IT&V) and other audit engagements, including collecting and organizing testing artifacts, uploading evidence to SharePoint, tracking change requests and progress, and coordinating subject-matter expert walkthroughs. Perform and track control-monitoring activities in line with approved procedures and frequencies, including: Quarterly: Anti-Tying, Deal File Checklist, Record to Apply Jointly, Daily DNT QFC Scrub, and UCC Monitoring. Annually: Medallion Stamp, Notary, and Affiant. Track issues and remediation actions, coordinate monthly updates with COO and Controls partners, and escalate overdue items or emerging concerns. Prepare periodic controls and governance reporting, highlighting status, exceptions, remediation progress, and items requiring management attention. Maintain workflow trackers and perform documented checks to monitor control implementation and identify exceptions for follow-up. Support the development and maintenance of policies and procedures, risk assessments, and control frameworks in collaboration with relevant business and control teams. Support periodic governance reviews, change management, access reviews, and related activities. Liaise with SPG Business Execution, COO, and other teams on business priorities, data management, and U.S.-based COO activities. Leadership Reporting and Strategy Support Assist SPG leaders in preparing management reports, business updates, presentations, and briefing materials for senior leadership and governance forums. Consolidate and analyze headcount and workforce updates, including capacity, and utilization, in coordination with relevant stakeholders. Support leaders in developing and tracking franchise strategy, business plans, and strategic initiatives through analysis, presentation development, and progress reporting. Translate business and operational data into concise insights, highlighting performance trends, key risks, dependencies, and matters requiring leadership decisions. Coordinate reporting inputs across teams, validate data accuracy and consistency, and track milestones and follow-up actions against agreed timelines. Required

Qualifications

2+ years of Business Execution, Implementation, or Strategic Planning experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education. An MBA or equivalent qualification. 2+ years of experience in business execution, project management, or a COO function; experience in controls or risk management is preferred. Experience in a banking or captive environment; knowledge of asset-backed finance or securitization would be an advantage. Technical knowledge of audit, compliance, or governance is preferred. Strong written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills, with the ability to explain complex topics clearly and concisely. Ability to prepare executive-ready presentations, management reports, and business updates, with clear narratives and actionable insights. Ability to consolidate and analyze business, headcount, and capacity data, identify trends, and provide decision-support insights to leaders. Strong stakeholder coordination skills, including gathering inputs, constructively challenging inconsistencies, and following through on deliverables across teams. Strong organizational, project management, and process improvement skills, with attention to detail and adaptability to evolving requirements. A proactive, self-starting approach, with the ability to work independently and take ownership with minimal supervision or follow-up. Ability to prioritize multiple deliverables and meet deadlines in a fast-paced, highly regulated environment. Ability to handle sensitive business, workforce, and strategy information with discretion and sound judgment. Flexibility to work a partial U.S. shift, as business requirements dictate. Desired

Qualifications

Advanced Microsoft Office skills, particularly Excel for data analysis and PowerPoint for management presentations; experience with Power BI or reporting automation would be an advantage.
