Marketing Coordinator-CRDN

Medtronic · Mumbai

About the role

Careers that change lives start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a Mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact. A Day in the Life The Coronary and Renal Denervation (CRDN) portfolio focuses on technologies and therapies that help treat Coronary artery diseases and uncontrolled Hypertension. The Marketing function partners with commercial, clinical, and cross-functional teams to develop and execute marketing programs that support product adoption, customer engagement, market development, and business growth across the CRDN portfolio. This position is based in Mumbai, India and follows a flexible work arrangement. Travel of up to 30% may be required to support customer meetings, physician education programs, conferences, field activities, and other business needs. The Marketing Coordinator supports the planning, execution, and administration of marketing initiatives within the CRDN portfolio. This role coordinates campaigns, events, communications, reporting, and stakeholder activities to support commercial objectives, customer engagement, and portfolio growth.
