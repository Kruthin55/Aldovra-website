About the role

JLL empowers you to shape a brighter way . Our people at JLL are shaping the future of real estate for a better world by combining world class services, advisory and technology for our clients. We are committed to hiring the best, most talented people and empowering them to thrive, grow meaningful careers and to find a place where they belong. Whether you’ve got deep experience in commercial real estate, skilled trades or technology, or you’re looking to apply your relevant experience to a new industry, join our team as we help shape a brighter way forward. What this job involves Oversee revenue accounting processes, including monthly close activities, account reconciliations, and related financial reporting. Participate in all aspects of the revenue month-end close, including preparing journal entries, performing reconciliations, and supporting SEC disclosures and reporting requirements. Ensure compliance with ASC 606 and related guidance, confirming that all revenue is recognized appropriately. Perform preliminary contract analysis and prepare revenue schedules as needed. Maintain documentation to support audit and compliance activities. Research revenue accounting guidance as required and prepare technical accounting memos and contract summaries to support conclusions. Coordinate with the Revenue Manager on judgmental transactions and address any missing information. Maintain comprehensive financial and control records, along with supporting documentation, for audit and Sarbanes-Oxley compliance. Identify and implement process improvements; make recommendations to enhance revenue accounting procedures. Support revenue-related projects, including system design, testing, and implementation of new or updated processes. Sound like you? To apply, you’ll need: Prior experience with revenue recognition under US GAAP (ASC 606), IFRS 15, or Ind AS 115 (preferred). Ability to review, analyze, and interpret contracts. Strong technical accounting and analytical skills. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively under pressure in a dynamic environment. Key skills Excellent organizational, interpersonal, and communication skills (both oral and written). Proven ability to work successfully within a team. What you can expect from us At JLL, we make sure that you become the best version of yourself by helping you realize your full potential in an entrepreneurial and inclusive work environment. We will empower your ambitions through our dedicated Total Rewards Program, competitive pay and benefits package.
