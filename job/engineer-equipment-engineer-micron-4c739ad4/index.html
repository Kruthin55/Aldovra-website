ENGINEER, EQUIPMENT ENGINEER

Micron · Sanand - 303A - AT/SSD/MOD, India

About the role

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. Performs electrical and mechanical troubleshooting to identify and resolve problems in non-functioning electro-mechanical equipment used in the manufacturing process. Dismantles, adjusts, repairs, replaces, and reassembles equipment and components according to layout plans, blueprints, operating/repair manuals, technical drawings, and engineering instructions. Uses appropriate test, measurement, and diagnostic equipment to perform equipment checkouts, fault isolation, and functional verification. Performs preventive maintenance and equipment recovery activities to support equipment reliability, availability, and manufacturing requirements. Rebuilds manufacturing equipment and assemblies as required. Supports equipment setup, calibration, adjustment, and basic parameter verification following approved procedures. Performs equipment modifications, upgrades, and improvement activities as directed by engineers. Supports troubleshooting of recurring equipment issues and provides relevant observations and data to engineers for root-cause analysis and corrective actions. Follows applicable safety requirements, maintenance procedures, equipment specifications, and manufacturing standards while performing maintenance and troubleshooting activities. Maintains accurate maintenance and troubleshooting records and communicates equipment status and unresolved issues to the appropriate engineering or manufacturing team. Supports continuous improvement activities focused on equipment reliability, maintenance effectiveness, downtime reduction, and manufacturing performance. Demonstrates proficiency in AI-enabled ways of working by leveraging AI-assisted tools, data analytics, and digital technologies to improve equipment reliability, troubleshooting effectiveness, maintenance efficiency, and continuous improvement initiatives. About Micron Technology, Inc. We are an industry leader in innovative memory and storage solutions transforming how the world uses information to enrich life for all . With a relentless focus on our customers, technology leadership, and manufacturing and operational excellence, Micron delivers a rich portfolio of high-performance DRAM, NAND, and NOR memory and storage products through our Micron® and Crucial® brands. Every day, the innovations that our people create fuel the data economy, enabling advances in artificial intelligence and 5G applications that unleash opportunities — from the data center to the intelligent edge and across the client and mobile user experience.
