Job description

Overview We are looking for enthusiastic FY 2025-26 BBA/MBA or B. Tech / B.E. graduates (except IT, Computer Science, Biotechnology, Chemical, Biomedical) to join our Infrastructure Services team. Project Coordinator (Fresher) A Project Coordinator supports project managers by coordinating daily activities, tracking progress, maintaining documentation, and facilitating communication. Key

Responsibilities

Assist in project planning, scheduling, and administration. Coordinate meetings, prepare minutes, and track action items. Monitor tasks and update project timelines. Maintain project documentation and status reports. Communicate with team members, stakeholders, and clients. Ensure required tools, access, and resources are available to the team
