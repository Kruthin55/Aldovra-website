IBP/ITP analyst

Capgemini · Chennai

About the role

Choosing Capgemini means choosing a company where you will be empowered to shape your career in the way you’d like, where you’ll be supported and inspired by a collaborative community of colleagues around the world, and where you’ll be able to reimagine what’s possible. Join us and help the world’s leading organizations unlock the value of technology and build a more sustainable, more inclusive world. Your Role In this role you will play a key role in: Support the end-to-end IBP cycle by preparing and publishing data packs for Demand Review, Supply Review, Integrated Reconciliation, and Management Business Review (MBR), ensuring consistency across all planning views. Analyse demand trends, forecast variances, supply constraints, and inventory imbalances to identify risks, opportunities, and performance gaps. Perform root-cause analysis and provide actionable insights to improve forecast accuracy, supply reliability, inventory health, and overall plan quality. Ensure data accuracy, governance, and standardization across planning processes, reports, and business reviews. Develop, maintain, and automate planning dashboards, reports, templates, and analytical models to improve efficiency and reduce manual effort. Collaborate with Demand Planning, Supply Planning, Manufacturing, Procurement, Finance, and Commercial teams to support planning decisions and business reviews. Drive continuous improvement initiatives by simplifying processes, enhancing reporting capabilities, and implementing best practices in planning analytics and automation. Your Profile Hands-on experience with SAP and proficiency in Microsoft Excel. Ability to extract, analyze, and interpret data from Power BI and Tableau dashboards. Strong reporting, data visualization, and analytical capabilities to support business decision-making.
