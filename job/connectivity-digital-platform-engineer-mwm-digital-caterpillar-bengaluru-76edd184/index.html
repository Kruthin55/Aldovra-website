Connectivity & Digital Platform Engineer (MWM Digital)

Caterpillar · Bengaluru

About the role

Career Area: Technology, Digital and Data Job Description: Your Work Shapes the World at Caterpillar Inc. When you join Caterpillar, you're joining a global team who cares not just about the work we do – but also about each other. We are the makers, problem solvers, and future world builders who are creating stronger, more sustainable communities. We don't just talk about progress and innovation here – we make it happen, with our customers, where we work and live. Together, we are building a better world, so we can all enjoy living in it. Key

Responsibilities

Continuously improve the end-to-end digital connectivity architecture, including edge devices, data ingestion pipelines, cloud infrastructure, databases, and customer-facing platforms. Ensure the reliability, scalability, and security of the MWM digital data ecosystem. Define, implement, and maintain operational processes aligned with the MWM digital strategy, including asset onboarding, lifecycle management of connected assets, and third-level (L3) technical support. Collaborate with cross-functional and cross-divisional teams (e.g., Caterpillar Digital and Technology organizations) to align MWM Digital requirements with the global connectivity architecture. Develop and optimize the user experience for internal service teams and customer-facing digital tools. Support the integration of new digital services and analytics capabilities into the existing ecosystem.

Requirements

Degree in Information Technology, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or a comparable qualification. Experience with network technologies, routers, VPNs, industrial communication protocols, and IoT architectures. Experience with VisionLink™, CAT Foresight™, or similar telematics platforms is desirable. Knowledge of gas engine applications and power generation systems is an advantage. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a structured working approach. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work independently and effectively across teams.
