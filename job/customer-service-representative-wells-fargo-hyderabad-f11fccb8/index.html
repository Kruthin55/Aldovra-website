Customer Service Representative

Wells Fargo · Hyderabad

About the role

About this role: At Wells Fargo, a Customer Service Representative delivers advanced support to customer inquiries in a high-volume, inbound environment and resolve complex inquiries. You will support day-to-day customer interactions, process transactions, investigate and resolve customer concerns, and contribute to a positive customer experience. In this role, you will: Manage inbound customer interactions across multiple channels, resolving a broad range of inquiries and service requests Apply product and service knowledge to assess customer needs and deliver accurate, effective solutions Assess and resolve moderately complex customer issues with accuracy and efficiency Adhere to processes, policies, and regulatory requirements while ensuring consistent and compliant service delivery Navigate multiple systems efficiently to research, document, and complete customer transactions in real time Promote adoption of digital capabilities by educating customers on self-service tools and alternative servicing options Collaborate with internal partners to manage escalations, resolve customer concerns, and support service delivery Contribute to continuous improvement efforts that enhance customer experience and operational effectiveness Required
