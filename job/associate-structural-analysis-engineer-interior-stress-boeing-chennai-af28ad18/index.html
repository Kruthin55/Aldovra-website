Position Overview: Boeing India Engineering is looking for Associate Structural Analysis Engineer (Interior Stress) at Boeing India, Chennai. This position will focus on providing support to Boeing commercial airplane customers. Position

Responsibilities

Perform structural analysis using established Analysis tools (hand-calculations methods) and using CAE/FEA tools to validate that components and assemblies meet requirements and specifications

Support test planning, execution and data review for structural verification (component, sub system, and system levels). Prepare test procedures and capture results to substantiate requirements for customers and regulatory agencies

Review test plans and test reports of the seats supplier

Perform and review detailed structural analysis to determine structural integrity (margins of safety), using classical techniques and Boeing tools.

Evaluate structural integrity of aircraft components by way of classical hand calculations and, finite element method

Assist in preparation and maintenance of technical documentation (design reports, test plans/procedures, qualification plans, drawings, and change requests).

Support Gated Reviews to validate the quality and completeness of work packages

Familiar with regulatory framework (FAA and EASA), for warranting safety and regulatory compliance.

Supports structural design and configuration development.

Performs trade studies to optimize structure and meet program requirements. Employers will not sponsor applicants for employment visa status. Basic

Qualifications

(Required Skills/Experience)

Bachelor’s degree in engineering, Engineering Technology (including Manufacturing Technology), Computer Science, Data Science, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, or non-US equivalent qualifications directly related to the work statement

Experience in stress analysis of various aircraft interior commodities.

Experience with structural analysis of metallic and composite structures

Experience in utilizing FEM software (e.g., PATRAN/NASTRAN, Hyperworks)

Experience in utilizing industry CAD systems – CATIA and ENOVIA

Knowledge of airplane operating environments. Robust understanding of engineering fundamentals such as material properties, Free-body diagrams, structural loads and analysis methods

Candidate demonstrates excellent analytical and problem-solving skills

Good verbal and written communication skills to liaise with several Stakeholders

Ability to work across functional teams and organizations Preferred
