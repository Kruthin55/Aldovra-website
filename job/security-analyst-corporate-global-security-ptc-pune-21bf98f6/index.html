Security Analyst - Corporate/Global Security

PTC · Pune

About the role

Our world is transforming, and PTC is leading the way. Our software brings the physical and digital worlds together, enabling companies to improve operations, create better products, and empower people in all aspects of their business. Our people make all the difference in our success. Today, we are a global team of nearly 7,000 and our main objective is to create opportunities for our team members to explore, learn, and grow – all while seeing their ideas come to life and celebrating the differences that make us who we are and the work we do possible. The Analyst, Global Security for India & APAC will assist in to protecting our people, facilities, data, and company brand, by providing best in class Global Security solutions, services, and technology for PTC throughout India and APAC, reporting directly to the regional Manager, Global Security. Position

Responsibilities

Supports regional Manager, Global Security in all global security strategic focus areas to including Physical & Technical Security, Travel Risk Management, and Crisis Response & Resiliency. Provides security office administrative duties as needed, to support PTC employees at the dedicated regional HUB. Performs daily operations in the enterprise video and access control software, to include badging & ID, occupancy reporting and video review. Responsible for security technology health and maintenance processes and procedures. Communicates with PTC managers on office space and restricted space access requests and access rosters. Monitors regional global security mailbox and service ticket system for employee assistance requests, per region. Monitors PTC Crisis Platform for potential threat to PTC employees/business and escalates as needed. Coordinates and communicates with regional property management security teams as needed, to ensure safe and secure environment for PTC employees. Assists in scheduling additional security coverage for PTC events when required. Works closely with PTC Office Managers to support the ERT needs, training, and plans. Assists office managers in region to schedule First Aid, CPR & AED training. Tracks regional global security metrics, as developed by Global Security Leadership Responsible for all other projects assigned by the regional global security manager. Desired Skills Ability to effectively communicate and build influential relationships at all levels of the organization, while building the Global Security brand. Excellent written and verbal communication skills Strong organizational skills Basic

Qualifications

Associate degree or 4+ total years’ experience. 1-3 years’ experience in corporate / global security or similar function. Life at PTC is about more than working with today’s most cutting-edge technologies to transform the physical world. It’s about showing up as you are and working alongside some of today’s most talented industry leaders to transform the world around you. If you share our passion for problem-solving through innovation, you’ll likely become just as passionate about the PTC experience as we are. Are you ready to explore your next career move with us?
