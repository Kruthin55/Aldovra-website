WHAT YOU WILL DO The Associate IS Analyst – Veeva Automation Engineer role at Amgen involves designing and developing complex automation solutions, providing mentorship, and following standard processes. The role requires expertise in multiple programming languages and test automation frameworks, overseeing system integration, and driving process optimization. Responsibilities

Expertise in multiple programming languages relevant to test automation like Java, Python, JavaScript Understanding of software design patterns and architecture Advanced knowledge of different test automation frameworks and patterns. Oversee system integration, testing, and solving. Maintain documentation and ensure compliance with standards. Drive process optimization and continuous improvement. Excellent communication and collaboration skills Proactively identify and implement opportunities to automate tasks and develop reusable frameworks.
