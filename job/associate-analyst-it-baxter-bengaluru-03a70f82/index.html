The Product Security Engineer is a product team’s cybersecurity focal point for secure product development and maintenance of released products. Product Security Engineer is an experienced member of the Baxter Global Product Security team with influence to drive product privacy and cybersecurity features and enhancements. The Product Security Engineer must have deep product knowledge to ensure clinical functionality, expected operating environment, and interoperability to accurately determine a product’s privacy and security risks. Baxter is a leading global medical technology and digital solutions innovator. Our mission is to Save and Sustain lives. Unlock your ambition, turn ideas into world-changing realities, and join an organization where every voice makes a difference, and every difference builds a healthier world. What you’ll be doing Provide privacy and security technical expertise in support of the product team throughout product development, design change, and life-cycle management. Work with the Product Security Leader (PSL) to support the product team with process expertise in Product life-cycle management. Baxter Global Product Security responsibilities: Assess the privacy and cybersecurity state of the product and define product roadmap features/enhancement security with Product Developers and team members. Responsible for security resource is to coordination of product development for cybersecurity features and enhancements in Tool Support for smooth onboarding and executing. Assess product components and SBoM integrated into the product build. Perform defect management for cybersecurity issues with Product Security Architect. Identify operational responsibilities and adherence to cloud standards for cloud-based products. Responsible for Penetration Results and MDS2 documentation. In coordination with the Peers, own and deliver Product Cybersecurity Standard artifacts, which include: Design input activities to identify, evaluate, roadmap, and drive cybersecurity and privacy features and enhancements within product development teams. Create Design Engineering Privacy and Security (DEPS) artifacts for privacy and security risk assessments to engage in domain-specific product threat modeling, attack surface analysis, risk management and reduction. Coordinates with the Peers to support the product team in scheduling and performing vulnerability scans and cybersecurity assessments. Product Security Technical Design Reviews, along with the product Lead System Designer (LSD), responsible for the Product Cybersecurity Standard compliance and other pertinent standards and process. Stay current on healthcare privacy trends and regulatory environment (i.e. FDA, HIPAA, GDPR etc.) to effectively communicate privacy awareness with the product team. Works with the Team and QARA on released product life cycle, including: Participate in post-market product vulnerability monitoring. Participate as a Subject Matter Expertise to determine product vulnerability impact, investigation, and risk assessment. Responsible for product vulnerability mitigation and changes. Responsible for Tool validation and update to ensure accurate versions are used. Address security feedback/questions from the development teams. Create/Maintain responsible records within BGPS Team for secure cybersecurity tools.

What you’ll bring

Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field (e.g. Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Information Security) or in a STEM major (Science, Technology, Engineering, or Mathematics). Sound technical and domain experience in at least two cybersecurity functional technology areas. Technical assessment (system/web application vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, Black, Grey and White-box code analysis, etc.) and security architecture (design of security controls, secure system design, understanding of identity and authentication management, etc.). Experience in a regulated development environment such as healthcare. Demonstrated experience in strategic planning, driving technology, and roadmap planning with a development team. Strong written and oral communication skills. Self-starter, energizing, results oriented, problem solving, and able to multi-task. Applicable cybersecurity and privacy certifications.
