Associate Engineer, Environmental

Flex · Chennai

About the role

Flex is the diversified manufacturing partner of choice that helps market-leading brands design, build and deliver innovative products that improve the world. A career at Flex offers the opportunity to make a difference and invest in your growth in a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment. If you are excited about a role but don't meet every bullet point, we encourage you to apply and join us to create the extraordinary. Job Summary

To support our extraordinary teams who build great products and contribute to our growth, we’re looking to add an Associate Engineer, Environmental in Chennai. What a typical day looks like: Responsible for Collection, analysis & archival of the component information related to Environmental Compliance thro’ Assent Compliance Management. Interaction with Manufacturers across the world to collect the Environmental declarations & provides guidance on Compliance information requested by Requestor. Provide support to Supplier Quality Engineers / Commodity Management / Procurement / Design Engineering team regarding the Environmental Compliance. Handling the regulation changes in CMAP & forecast the risk of the affected parts for the Product Environment Compliance team to alert the site. Provide Training to suppliers on the Environmental Compliance requirements wrt customer needs wherever required. Coordinating with Global Environmental Compliance team for resolution of any New requirements from customer or Compliance related queries from site. Support organization in regulatory compliance / internal / third party audits. The experience we’re looking to add to our team: Knowledge of electrical and electronic components. Excellent written and oral communication skills Mandatory. Working Knowledge of MS Office tools (MS Excel, MS Word). Good team player. Minimum 0 - 2 years of experience in a Manufacturing set up, preferably in EMS industry. Diploma in Electronics and Communication Engineering / EEE / Mechanical.
