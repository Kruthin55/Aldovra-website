Design Engineer

Caterpillar · Chennai

About the role

Career Area: Engineering Job Description: Your Work Shapes the World at Caterpillar Inc. When you join Caterpillar, you're joining a global team who cares not just about the work we do – but also about each other. We are the makers, problem solvers, and future world builders who are creating stronger, more sustainable communities. We don't just talk about progress and innovation here – we make it happen, with our customers, where we work and live. Together, we are building a better world, so we can all enjoy living in it. JOB PURPOSE: Design and development activities from engineering on fuel system components of diesel engine platforms C1.7L – C7L. In this role you will have broad responsibility from concept development to release to production in a manner to ensure products provide superior performance, durability, quality, and serviceability. You will be working new product development as well as current product improvement. Job Duties/Responsibilities may include, but are not limited to: · In this position you will be closely working with Senior engineers / Engineering specialists and execute Design and development activities of Low-Pressure Fuel system engine components. · Partner with customers, product group, and Subject matter experts to define requirements and drive project planning and execution. · Should be able to independently work on engineering actives from concept to production in component / system development projects. Liaise with cross functional teams as required. · Effective use of various engineering tools as part of product development. · Independently execute engineering activities and implement solutions for Continuous Product Improvement (CPI) and New Source Introduction (NSI) projects. · Manage multiple projects and communicate progress against cost, quality, and timeline targets to customers, suppliers, team members, and cross-functional stakeholders. Educational Requirement: BE Mechanical engineering with 8 or more years of relevant experience in engine industry (or) ME / MTech Mechanical engineering (specialization in IC engines / engineering design / thermal engineering) with 6 or more years of relevant experience in engine industry (ideal candidate) Skill Sets/Competencies (Must have): · Product knowledge of Fuel system components like Fuel Filters / Fuel Injection Pump / Fuel Lift pump (Mechanical / Electrical). · Experience of New Product Introduction (NPI) (Concept to Production) and Value analysis / Value engineering techniques. · Able to design components as per reliability / durability requirements and with manufacturing feasibility. · Strong knowledge of virtual analysis tools and GD & T to apply as required. · Knowledge on Data extraction, regression analysis and problem solving. · Excellent Project Management, verbal / written communication, and interpersonal skills. TOP CANDIDATES will have: · Thorough understanding of fuel system necessary · 5+ years of experience designing and developing low-pressure fuel system components in collaboration with global and regional teams. · Experience in working with regional suppliers for Fuel system component development · Good knowledge of cross functional roles in domains such as Manufacturing (Injection Moulding or HPDC), Purchasing, Quality, related to engines. Additional information: This job requires 5 working days from office.
