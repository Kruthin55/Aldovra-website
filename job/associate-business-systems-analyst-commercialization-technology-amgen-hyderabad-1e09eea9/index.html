Associate Business Systems Analyst, Commercialization Technology

Amgen · Hyderabad

About the role

Career Category Information Systems Job Description As a Business Systems Analyst within our global Commercialization Technology team, this role will support the evolution of portfolio governance operations capabilities. You will partner with Product Owners, business stakeholders, and technology teams to understand business needs, translate them into technology requirements, and support the delivery and continuous improvement of innovative technology solutions. This position will provide an opportunity to contribute across the technology delivery lifecycle, including requirements gathering, backlog refinement, testing, documentation, and production support. The successful candidate will work with business and technical stakeholders to understand processes and user needs, help define effective technology solutions, and develop knowledge of enterprise technologies, data, Agile delivery, and emerging capabilities such as AI and analytics.
