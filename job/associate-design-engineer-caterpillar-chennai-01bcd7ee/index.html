Associate Design Engineer

Caterpillar · Chennai

About the role

Career Area: Engineering Job Description: Your Work Shapes the World at Caterpillar Inc. When you join Caterpillar, you're joining a global team who cares not just about the work we do – but also about each other. We are the makers, problem solvers, and future world builders who are creating stronger, more sustainable communities. We don't just talk about progress and innovation here – we make it happen, with our customers, where we work and live. Together, we are building a better world, so we can all enjoy living in it. Role Definition Partners with Gas Turbine Engineering teams and IT organizations to develop, implement, and support digital engineering solutions that enhance product development, engineering productivity, data quality, and lifecycle management. Acts as the primary interface between engineering users and technology teams, translating business needs into scalable engineering tools, automation solutions, and process improvements that support gas turbine design, manufacturing, quality, and product lifecycle activities.

Responsibilities

Serve as the primary liaison between gas turbine engineering teams and IT organizations to identify, prioritize, and implement engineering technology solutions.

Gather, analyze, and document business requirements for CAD, PLM, engineering data management, configuration management, and digital engineering systems.

Lead the development, testing, deployment, and support of engineering tools, applications, workflows, and automation solutions that improve engineering efficiency and reduce manual effort.

Collaborate with engineering, IT, software development, and business process teams to enhance CAD, PLM, and engineering business systems.

Drive adoption of digital engineering technologies, including automation, data analytics, AI-enabled engineering solutions, and workflow optimization.

Support and improve engineering applications used for product design, drawing creation, product structure management, change management, and engineering release processes.

Analyze engineering workflows and identify opportunities to improve productivity, data quality, process compliance, and user experience.

Develop business cases, functional specifications, test plans, validation procedures, and implementation strategies for engineering technology initiatives.

Coordinate system upgrades, configuration changes, new capability deployments, and integration activities while ensuring minimal disruption to engineering operations.

Provide technical support, training, user guidance, and change management for engineering tools and digital processes.

Monitor emerging technologies and industry best practices in CAD, PLM, digital engineering, automation, and AI to recommend solutions that improve product development effectiveness.

Support strategic initiatives that strengthen engineering capability, accelerate product development, improve engineering data quality, and enable digital transformation across gas turbine programs.
