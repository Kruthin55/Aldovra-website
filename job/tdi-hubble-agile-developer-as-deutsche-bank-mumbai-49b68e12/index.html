TDI HUBBLE Agile Developer, AS

Deutsche Bank · Mumbai

About the role

Job Description: Job Title: TDI HUBBLE Agile Developer, AS Location: Mumbai, India Role Description As a TDI HUBBLE Agile Developer, you will take a leading role in building intuitive, high-performance user interfaces and translating business needs into clear, consistent web experiences for reports and analytical views. Working in an agile delivery team, you’ll collaborate closely with product owners and stakeholders to shape UI requirements, produce clean designs and reusable front-end components, and integrate them with scalable services and data APIs. Design and implement responsive UI screens and reusable component libraries (Angular, JavaScript/jQuery, Bootstrap), aligned to usability and accessibility standards Partner with users/product to refine layouts, workflows and interactions, turning requirements into wireframes/prototypes and working UI increments Integrate UI with back-end services and data sources (C#, REST/WCF/Web API, SQL) while ensuring performance, reliability and maintainability Continuously improve UI performance, code quality, and developer tooling; contribute to standards and best practices across the team What we’ll offer you As part of our flexible scheme, here are just some of the benefits that you’ll enjoy Best in class leave policy Gender neutral parental leaves 100% reimbursement under childcare assistance benefit (gender neutral) Sponsorship for Industry relevant certifications and education Employee Assistance Program for you and your family members Comprehensive Hospitalization Insurance for you and your dependents Accident and Term life Insurance Complementary Health screening for 35 yrs. and above Your key responsibilities Build and deliver reusable, component-based modules. Review and challenge solution design and architecture to improve quality, performance, and maintainability. Plan, define, and build rapid development tools that help the Hubble team deliver agreed objectives. Develop robust systems with a long-term view, covering: Retrieve data from multiple sources. Validate data using controls and checks. Process and enrich data. Run complex calculations efficiently. Deliver reports and web views that enable business leaders to slice and dice data. Your skills and experience Graduate/postgraduate degree in a numerate discipline Broad financial market and product knowledge Strong experience with C#, Angular, AG Controls, Rest Services, SQL, jQuery/JavaScript, Bootstrap, WCF/Web API Preferred: experience with additional technologies (e.g., ASP.NET, ASP.NET MVC, Python) Strong quantitative skills with high attention to detail Strong communication skills; fluent in English Numerically literate with a strong eye for accuracy Team-oriented and self-motivated Able to work independently and as part of a team Able to cope well under pressure and tight deadlines How we’ll support you Training and development to help you excel in your career Coaching and support from experts in your team A culture of continuous learning to aid progression A range of flexible benefits that you can tailor to suit your needs
