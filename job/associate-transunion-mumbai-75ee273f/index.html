Responsibilities

Day To Day

Responsibilities

Manages the end-to-end bid process from kick off to submission and post bid reviews. Coordinates and develops content for high quality bid responses, ensuring compliance with client specifications and requirements. Collaborates with internal teams across solutions, legal, finance, operations, HR and global technology to gather information and input for bids. Writes, edits and proofreads compelling proposals, ensuring accuracy and consistency in messaging. Leads bid review with senior management to ensure submissions are aligned with company objectives. Monitors and tracks bid progress, setting and managing timelines to ensure deadlines are met. Conducts market and competitor research to strengthen bids and understand client needs. Maintains the bid library, templates and supporting documents for future use. Identifies opportunities for continuous improvement in the bid process and champion to best practice. Required Knowledge And Experiences Degree educated an advantage, preferably in the Arts, Law or Social Sciences. Strong project management skills with ability to manage multiple bids simultaneously. Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and preferred experience with bid management software and CRM systems. Proven track record in managing successful bids and proposals. Strong interpersonal skills and ability to work collaboratively across departments and levels of seniority. Personal qualities include diligence, an eye for detail, strong organisational skills, deadline driven and motivated to work autonomously.
