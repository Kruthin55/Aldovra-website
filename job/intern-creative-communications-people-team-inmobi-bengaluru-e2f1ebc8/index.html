About the role

About the Role

We are looking for a creative, tech-savvy and ambitious Creative & Communications Intern to join our Talent team for a 3-month internship .

This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys turning ideas into great visuals, videos, stories and experiences . You could be designing a campaign one day, shooting an employee story the next, editing a reel, creating a handbook or experimenting with AI to find a better way to bring an idea to life.

We are looking for someone who doesn't just execute a brief, but can think creatively, figure things out independently and push the quality of the final output.

What You'll Do

Create posters, digital creatives, presentations, emailers, infographics and campaign assets .

Shoot and edit videos, reels, interviews, employee stories and events .

Use a DSLR/professional camera for photography and videography .

Create engaging content across different formats, from static design to short-form video.

Develop digital and physical handbooks, guides and toolkits .

Experiment with AI and emerging creative tools to improve the speed, quality and range of your work.

Help build a repository of reusable creative assets, templates, photographs, videos and content .
