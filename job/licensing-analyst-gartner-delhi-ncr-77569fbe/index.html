What you’ll do

The Licensing Analyst plays a critical analytical and knowledge management role within a high-performing project delivery team supporting software contract optimization engagements. In this role, you will be responsible for managing and continuously enhancing our central vendor intelligence repository, ensuring information is accurate, current, and accessible to consultants and stakeholders across the business. You will gather, validate, analyse, and organise intelligence from a variety of sources, including client engagements, industry research, commercial documentation, and AI-assisted research tools. You will support consultants in client engagements by providing timely relevant vendor intelligence to inform customer engagements.

What you’ll need

3-5 years' experience in an operational, analytical, or research-focused role within the IT industry, with exposure to areas such as software licensing, software asset management, vendor management, procurement, technology operations, commercial operations, or knowledge management. A broad understanding of the enterprise technology landscape, software industry, and how major vendors operate. Basic understanding of software licensing concepts, subscription-based business models, cloud services, and enterprise software procurement practices. Experience maintaining and managing structured databases, repositories, knowledge management platforms, or other information management systems. Strong analytical skills with the ability to assess information from multiple sources, identify trends, and determine relevance, accuracy, and business value. Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage large volumes of information while maintaining high levels of accuracy and attention to detail. Strong written communication and documentation skills, with the ability to create clear, concise, and actionable content for a variety of audiences. Experience using Microsoft 365 applications, particularly Excel, SharePoint, and PowerPoint Confidence using AI and technology tools to improve research, analysis, data management, and operational efficiency. A self-motivated and proactive approach, with the ability to work independently, manage competing priorities, and deliver high-quality outcomes. An interest in technology, software vendors, market developments, and the commercial dynamics of the enterprise software industry.

Naturally curious about technology, software vendors, and industry trends. Highly organized, methodical, and detail oriented. Someone who enjoys researching, analyzing, and connecting information from multiple sources. Comfortable working in a support function that enables consultants and stakeholders to make better-informed decisions. A self-starter who takes ownership of data quality and continuously looks for ways to improve processes. Equally comfortable working independently and collaborating across teams.
