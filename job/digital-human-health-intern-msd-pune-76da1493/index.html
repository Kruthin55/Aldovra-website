Digital Human Health Intern

MSD · Pune

About the role

Job Description Digital Human Health Intern India Hub Delivery – 2027 Internship Program Duration – 6 Months Start – Early January 2027 Locations – Pune Work Mode – Full-time, Work from Office About the Internship: Digital Human Health (DHH) India Internship Program provides students with an opportunity to contribute to real business, analytics, data, AI and technology projects while gaining practical experience in a global biopharmaceutical environment. Interns will work with multidisciplinary teams, apply academic learning to real-world challenges and build an understanding of how digital capabilities can advance commercial decision-making and patient impact. Depending on business needs, academic background and demonstrated skills, interns may be assigned to one or more DHH capability areas. Project scope, tools and deliverables will vary by assignment. Capability Areas: Commercial analytics and insights: Brand strategy and analytics; customer knowledge and measurement; primary market research; market access insights; strategic forecasting; performance measurement; competitive intelligence and commercial assessment. Business analysis, analytics, research and digital-solution support for global and international market priorities. Data science, AI and advanced analytics: Statistics; machine learning; predictive and prescriptive analytics; natural language processing; experimentation; model evaluation; generative AI and agentic AI use cases; responsible AI practices. Data strategy, engineering and management: Data sourcing; data profiling and quality; data preparation; ETL/ELT; scalable data pipelines; data models; metadata; governance; cloud-based data environments and reusable data products. Digital products and commercial technology: Product discovery; requirements gathering; process mapping; solution design; software and application development; APIs; testing; release support; platform configuration and technical documentation. Business intelligence and experience design: Dashboard development; KPI design; data visualization; report automation; user research; user experience and interface design; content and customer-experience solutions. Portfolio and operational enablement: Project coordination; portfolio reporting; workflow improvement; automation; agile ways of working; quality documentation; change enablement and stakeholder communications. Key

Responsibilities

Understand the business problem, project objectives, user needs, success measures and expected deliverables with guidance from the project team. Gather, clean, organize, analyze and interpret data or information using appropriate analytical, research or technology methods. Contribute to models, dashboards, reports, data pipelines, digital products, automation solutions, research outputs or business recommendations, depending on the assigned project. Apply sound problem-solving, coding, statistical, research, design or product-development practices relevant to the assignment. Prepare clear documentation, presentations and demonstrations that communicate methods, findings, limitations, recommendations and business value. Collaborate with business, analytics, data, AI, technology, product and operations colleagues across functions and geographies. Participate in project reviews, learning sessions, monthly check-ins, networking events and the internship showcase or poster session. Maintain high standards of quality, data integrity, confidentiality, cybersecurity, compliance and responsible use of AI. Seek feedback, manage assigned work responsibly and deliver agreed outputs within timelines. Mandatory

Currently enrolled full-time in an accredited bachelor's, master's or PhD program in a relevant discipline, including Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Computer Science, Information Technology, Artificial Intelligence, Engineering, Computational Biology, Life Sciences, Pharmacy, Business or Management, or another related field. Expected graduation on or before June/July 2027. The internship must be a mandatory and formally recognized component of the applicant's academic curriculum. Available for a continuous six-month, full-time internship beginning in January 2027. Able to work from the office for the full internship period. Remote or hybrid arrangements are not available for this role. Open to placement in Pune or Hyderabad based on business requirements. Able to communicate effectively in English, both verbally and in writing. Eligible to undertake the internship in India and able to provide required academic and employment documentation. Required Skills and Competencies: Strong analytical aptitude, structured problem-solving ability and attention to detail. Ability to translate complex data or information into clear insights, recommendations or solution requirements. Working knowledge in at least one relevant skill area such as quantitative analysis, market or business research, programming, data management, software development, product management, process improvement, UX or visualization. Foundational proficiency with common productivity tools, including spreadsheets and presentation software. Ability to learn new methods, tools, technologies and business concepts quickly. Ability to work independently on assigned tasks and collaborate effectively in diverse, cross-functional teams. Professionalism, curiosity, accountability and a strong commitment to quality, ethics, confidentiality and compliance. Interest in applying data, analytics, AI, technology and research to improve healthcare and business outcomes. What Interns Can Expect: Meaningful project work with defined objectives, milestones and deliverables. Guidance from project mentors and opportunities to learn from DHH leaders and capability teams. Exposure to global collaboration, business problem-solving and enterprise ways of working. Opportunities to build professional networks, receive feedback and present accomplishments. Practical experience that supports the transition from academia to industry.
