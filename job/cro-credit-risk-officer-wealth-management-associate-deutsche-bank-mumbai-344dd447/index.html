CRO - Credit Risk Officer-(Wealth Management) - Associate

Deutsche Bank · Mumbai

About the role

Job Description: Your key responsibilities: As a Credit Officer in the Credit Risk Management team covering the Wealth Management business in Asia Pacific, independently assess lending and derivative transactions, including the suitability of the proposed facility type, transaction structure, and the quality of the underlying collateral. Collateral may include marketable securities (equities, bonds, notes), including liquid and less-liquid single stocks; private shares requiring cash flow-based risk assessment and valuation; commercial real estate requiring an understanding of valuation methodologies and market dynamics; residential real estate; hedge fund units; limited partnership interests; aircraft; yachts; and art. Assign credit ratings and prepare independent credit memos based on risk assessment. Make credit decisions within delegated personal authority, where applicable, or submit recommendations to Regional and/or Global Risk Heads. Monitor the portfolio diligently on an ongoing basis, identify emerging pockets of risk and remain informed about global, regional, and local market events and their potential impact on the portfolio. Ensure the portfolio quality remains satisfactory, risk concentrations and limits remain within the defined risk appetite, and exposures comply with applicable risk policies and regulations. Support the preparation of reports and portfolio analysis for auditors, regulators, and senior management. Contribute actively to remediation efforts towards addressing regulatory and audit findings and help drive changes across risk Your skills and experience: At-least 3 years of banking experience, with a focus on structured lending in wealth management or corporate credit risk management. Minimum Bachelor’s or master’s degree in engineering, Finance, Economics, Statistics, Science, or an MBA, with a strong academic record. Strong Excel-based data analysis and financial modelling skills.
