Responsibilities

/ Duties: · Monitor risk Thresholds and Alerts for covered investment centers · Undertake quant projects regarding portfolio construction, portfolio style or other relevant topics and generate clear, articulate and actionable analysis for relevant stakeholders · Develop first-class tools and analytics that highlight drivers of portfolio risk, factor exposures, areas of portfolio concentration etc. · Proactively monitor market risk, liquidity risk, and emerging risks , identify portfolios with significant exposures, alert PMs, and escalate risks to senior risk leaders as needed. · Stay current with daily market developments and news impacting financial markets, performing ad hoc exposure analyses proactively or at the request of senior risk leaders. · Engage the technology group in support of furthering the quality, structure and use of risk data throughout the organization and to facilitate the delivery of state-of-the-art risk analytics and risk models · Leverage performance attribution innovatively within the risk process to provide insights into the investment process, beyond standard reporting. Work Experience / Knowledge: · 2 to 3 years’ financial industry experience is desirable, asset management experience as an analyst is a plus. · Understanding of equity risk factor models, including but not limited to Barra, Axioma, Bloomberg, etc. · Experience in programming (Python, R), data analysis, quantitative model development, and SQL is required. Ability to conduct research directed by senior members of the team and to balance multiple projects concurrently Skills / Other Personal Attributes Required: · Timeliness, Accountability and Ownership · Strong communication skills (verbal and writing) and an ability to frequently interact with investment and product teams. · AI usage proficiency is a plus. · Quantitative background in the equity asset class preferred. · Strong Intellectual curiosity about the financial markets, risk/quantitative research and applications. Formal Education: · Bachelor’s/master’s degree in finance, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, or Engineering from Top Tier tech college License / Registration / Certification: · Completion or progress towards professional accreditations such as CFA, FRM, PRM, CAIA Full Time / Part Time Full time Worker Type Employee Job Exempt (Yes / No) Yes Workplace Model At Invesco, our workplace model supports our culture and meets the needs of our clients while providing flexibility our employees value. As a full-time employee, compliance with the workplace policy means working with your direct manager to create a schedule where you will work in your designated office at least four days a week, with one day working outside an Invesco office.
