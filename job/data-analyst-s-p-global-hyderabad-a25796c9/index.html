Data Analyst

S&P Global · Hyderabad

About the role

About the Role: Grade Level (for internal use): 07 The Team The Fundamentals Center of Excellence (COE) is responsible for maintaining high standards of accuracy, consistency, and quality assurance for financial data across the Fundamentals domain. The COE supports global delivery teams by providing financial subject‑matter support, structured reviews, and quality validations, ensuring financial data meets defined standards before it is consumed by clients and downstream processes. The team works closely with US GAAP–based financial statements and disclosures, applying strong fundamentals of accounting, an audit mindset, and disciplined review techniques to ensure data reliability. The Impact The COE plays a critical role in protecting the quality and credibility of Fundamentals financial datasets. As a Data Analyst – COE (Fundamentals), you will: -Support financial data review and validation activities Apply core accounting knowledge to identify errors or inconsistencies Build a strong foundation in financial statement analysis and audit‑style reviews -Your work will directly contribute to accurate financial data that clients and internal teams can rely on with confidence. What’s In It for You -Build strong hands‑on expertise in financial statements and accounting fundamentals -Learn how financial data is reviewed, validated, and quality‑checked in an enterprise environment -Gain exposure to audit‑style checks, controls, and quality frameworks -Work closely with senior analysts and COE leads to grow financial judgment skills --Develop a strong foundation for long‑term roles in financial analysis, audit, or data quality

Responsibilities

-Perform financial data validation and review under guidance, focusing on US GAAP–based financial statements (Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow, and related disclosures) -Review financial numbers, classifications, and mappings to ensure they align with defined Fundamentals standards and guidelines -Conduct basic cross‑statement checks, variance analysis, and reasonableness reviews -Identify potential data issues, inconsistencies, or gaps and escalate findings with clear documentation -Support quality checks, internal reviews, and audit‑readiness activities -Maintain accurate documentation of reviews, observations, and corrective actions -Follow standard operating procedures (SOPs), review checklists, and quality controls consistently -Collaborate with peers and senior team members to clarify accounting treatments and resolve issues -Participate in training sessions and knowledge‑sharing initiatives to strengthen financial and review skills What We’re Looking For Basic Required

Qualifications

-Solid understanding of financial statements (Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow Statement) -Ability to read and interpret basic financial disclosures and notes -Strong attention to detail and a disciplined, quality‑focused mindset -Willingness to question data, validate numbers, and follow review procedures carefully -Comfortable working with spreadsheets and basic data tools Additional / Good‑to‑Have

Qualifications

Familiarity with US GAAP concepts and common accounting principles Basic understanding of financial controls or review processes Exposure to data validation, reconciliations, or quality checks Willingness to learn query‑based tools or internal research platforms over time What’s In It For You?
