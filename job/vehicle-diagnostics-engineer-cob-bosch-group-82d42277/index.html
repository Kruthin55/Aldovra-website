Vehicle Diagnostics Engineer, COB

Bosch Group · coimbatore, India

About the role

Bosch Global Software Technologies Private Limited is a 100% owned subsidiary of Robert Bosch GmbH, one of the world's leading global supplier of technology and services, offering end-to-end Engineering, IT and Business Solutions. With over 27,000+ associates, it’s the largest software development center of Bosch, outside Germany, indicating that it is the Technology Powerhouse of Bosch in India with a global footprint and presence in the US, Europe and the Asia Pacific region. Role Overview: Perform as an individual developer for the project team in automotive diagnostic content generation for Bosch IAM tool chain. Key

Responsibilities

Develop and author automotive diagnostic content with strong knowledge in vehicle communication networks (CAN/SAE/Flexray/LIN/KWP/MOST/UDS/CANFD/DOIP) Perform vehicle diagnostic content authoring and validation Conduct vehicle testing and diagnostics of various ECU/ADAS applications Create and calibrate application sequences for ADAS controllers with calibration/reset functions Execute reverse engineering processes and implement/validate using vehicle simulators Extract service manuals from OEM portals and perform reverse engineering with OEM tools Manage diagnostic assets including identification, DTC, sensor data, actuators, and special tests Utilise Vector tools and Samtec tools such as CANdela, CANoe, CANdiva, ODX Studio, HSX, HS+, and ODX data Apply excellent problem identification and solving skills with exposure to CAR and 8D methodologies Required Experience: Experience in handling OEM/IAM vehicle diagnostic tools Experience in vehicle diagnostic content authoring and validation Experience in reverse engineering processes and implementation/validation using vehicle simulators Experience in vehicle testing and diagnostics of various ECU systems Experience in ADAS application sequence creation and calibration Vehicle knowledge with ADAS controllers with calibration/reset functions Technical Knowledge: Strong knowledge in vehicle communication networks (CAN/SAE/Flexray/LIN/KWP/MOST/UDS) and related protocols Thorough knowledge of diagnostic assets (identification, DTC, sensor data, actuators, and special tests) Knowledge of ODX data and AUTOSAR (preferable) Exposure to programming languages such as Python, C#, SQL (added advantage) Desired Certifications: On-Board Diagnostics (OBD-II) Certification SAE J2534 Programming & Diagnostics Certification Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) Certifications Certifications related to DOIP   B.E/B. Tech, M.E/M. Tech 3 to 6 Years
