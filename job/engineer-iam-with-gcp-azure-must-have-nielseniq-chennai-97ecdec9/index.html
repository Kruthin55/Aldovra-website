Engineer, IAM with GCP & Azure (must have)

NielsenIQ · Chennai

Engineer, IAM, Chennai/Pune We are looking for an Identity & Access Governance Engineer with strong experience in cloud identity governance, modern authentication and enterprise IGA. The ideal candidate will bring strong expertise in Microsoft Entra ID or comparable cloud identity platforms, with experience across access governance, Conditional Access, SSO/federation, MFA, passwordless authentication, hybrid identity and application integrations. A solid understanding of SailPoint and identity lifecycle governance is required. The role will also contribute to identity modernization, NHI governance, automation and AI-powered IAM capabilities.

Responsibilities

Cloud Identity & Governance Design, implement and continuously improve cloud identity governance and access controls . Manage identity governance across users, groups, roles, applications and privileged identities. Define and maintain RBAC and least-privilege models . Review identity architecture and identify governance, security and operational gaps. Support identity modernization and cloud/platform transformation initiatives . Establish scalable governance standards that can operate across hybrid and multi-cloud environments. Entra ID & Modern Authentication Manage and enhance Microsoft Entra ID identity and access capabilities. Design and govern Conditional Access policies . Support MFA, Passwordless, Passkeys/FIDO2 and modern authentication. Govern enterprise applications, application registrations, service principals and permissions. Support identity risk, privileged access and access hygiene improvements. Support AD and Entra hybrid identity environments. NHI & AI-Agent Identity Governance Drive discovery and governance of Non-Human Identities , including service accounts, service principals, managed identities, workload identities and application identities. Support discovery and inventory of AI agents and autonomous identities . Define governance requirements around ownership, lifecycle and access for AI agents and NHIs. Review roles, permissions and entitlements assigned to AI agents and machine identities. Apply appropriate least-privilege and access-review principles to human, machine and agent identities. Identify unmanaged, orphaned, stale or over-privileged NHIs and help drive remediation. Help establish governance models for the growing use of AI agents and machine-to-machine identities . Automation & AI-Powered IAM Automate IAM/IGA activities using PowerShell, REST APIs, scripting and workflow automation . Identify opportunities to use AI-powered automation for identity governance and operational activities. Automate identity discovery, governance reporting, lifecycle processes and control validation where appropriate. Help build scalable and repeatable identity governance processes rather than relying on manual administration. Must have: Strong hands-on experience in Microsoft Entra ID / cloud identity governance , including Conditional Access, MFA, passwordless/passkeys, RBAC and application identities. Strong Identity Governance & Administration (IGA) knowledge, including JML, provisioning/deprovisioning, access reviews, roles and entitlement governance. Working knowledge of SailPoint and enterprise identity governance processes. Experience delivering identity migration, modernization or transformation programs , including application and SSO migrations. Strong understanding of SSO, SAML, OAuth 2.0, OIDC and modern authentication . Experience with Active Directory and hybrid identity environments. Understanding of NHI and workload identity governance , including service accounts, service principals and managed identities. Experience with IAM automation, APIs and scripting , with an interest in AI-powered identity automation. Good to Have: Experience with AI-agent discovery, inventory, lifecycle and identity/access governance . Deeper SailPoint engineering, integration or application onboarding experience. Experience with large-scale cloud or identity-platform migration programs . Exposure to multi-cloud identity and access governance . Understanding of Okta, Ping Identity and CIAM platforms and concepts. Experience with PAM / privileged identity governance . PowerShell, Python, REST API or workflow automation experience. Exposure to AI/GenAI-powered IAM operations and governance automation . Our
