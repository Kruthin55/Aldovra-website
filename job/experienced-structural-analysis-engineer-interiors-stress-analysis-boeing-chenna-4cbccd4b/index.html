Position Overview: Boeing India Engineering is looking for Experienced Structural Analysis Engineer (Interior Stress) at Boeing India, Chennai . This position will focus on providing support to Boeing commercial airplane customers. Position

Responsibilities

Responsible for performing structural analysis of aircraft components, assemblies, and systems while working as an integrated team Perform detailed structural analysis to determine structural integrity (margins of safety), using classical techniques and Boeing tools. Evaluate structural integrity of aircraft components by way of classical hand calculations and, finite element method Generate documentation to report analysis and test results to support certification/Qualification Support Gated Reviews to validate the quality and completeness of work packages Familiar with regulatory framework (FAA and EASA), for warranting safety and regulatory compliance. Guides structural design and configuration development. Performs trade studies to optimize structure and meet program requirements. Researches and prepares data to support definition of structural design requirements. Employers will not sponsor applicants for employment visa status. Basic

Qualifications

(Required Skills/Experience): Bachelor’s degree in engineering, Engineering Technology (including Manufacturing Technology), Computer Science, Data Science, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, or non-US equivalent qualifications directly related to the work statement Experience in stress analysis of various aircraft interior commodities. Experience with structural analysis of metallic and composite structures Experience with static and durability analysis Experience in utilizing FEM software (e.g., PATRAN/NASTRAN, Hyperworks) Experience in utilizing industry CAD systems – CATIA and ENOVIA Knowledge of airplane operating environments. Robust understanding of engineering fundamentals such as material properties, Free-body diagrams, structural loads and analysis methods Candidate demonstrates excellent analytical and problem-solving skills Good verbal and written communication skills to liaise with several Stakeholders Ability to work across functional teams and organizations Desire to continuously improve our airplanes Preferred

Qualifications

(Desired Skills/Experience): Experience or training with computer programming languages (e.g., Matlab, Python, Visual Basic).
