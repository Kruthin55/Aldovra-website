Asst Process Engineer

Unilever · Haridwar HPC Factory, India

About the role

Job Summary:** We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Assistant Process Engineer to support the optimization, design, and troubleshooting of manufacturing processes within Unilever Plc. This role offers an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced industrial environment and contribute to the efficiency and quality of our diverse product portfolio. Job Responsibilities:** * Assist in the development, implementation, and optimization of manufacturing processes for various Unilever products, focusing on efficiency, cost reduction, and quality improvement. * Conduct process data collection, analysis, and interpretation to identify areas for improvement and support decision-making. * Participate in process trials and experiments, documenting results and proposing recommendations. * Support the troubleshooting of process deviations and equipment malfunctions, working closely with production and maintenance teams. * Contribute to the design and implementation of new process equipment or modifications to existing systems. * Prepare and maintain process documentation, including standard operating procedures (SOPs), process flow diagrams (PFDs), and P&IDs. * Collaborate with cross-functional teams including R&D, Quality, Production, and Safety to ensure seamless process integration and adherence to company standards. * Assist in monitoring and ensuring compliance with safety, environmental, and quality regulations. * Support continuous improvement initiatives and projects under the guidance of senior engineers. Job Qualifications:** * Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering, Process Engineering, or a related scientific/engineering discipline. * 0-2 years of experience in a manufacturing or process-oriented environment (internships or co-op experiences are highly valued). * Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to interpret complex data. * Familiarity with process improvement methodologies (e.g., Lean, Six Sigma) is a plus. * Excellent communication (written and verbal) and interpersonal skills. * Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Word, PowerPoint). * Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team. * A proactive and results-oriented approach with a strong desire to learn and develop. * Knowledge of health, safety, and environmental regulations in a manufacturing setting.
