About the role

Here at Citi, the Apprenticeship Program is intended to help you build a broad skill set and accelerate your career growth by gaining exposure to numerous day-to-day activities. Your time here will look something like this... The Apprenticeship Program begins in June with a short induction, providing apprentices with an overview of Citi, a technology training session, and an introduction to Citi's divisions. As an apprentice you will gain hands on work experience with deliverables and will be expected to add value to their teams during the 12 months. In addition to your daily work, Apprentices can expand their networks here at Citi. We provide you with the knowledge and skills you need to succeed. The Apprenticeship Program is a 12-months opportunity in India. The Program will provide Apprentices with on-the-job-training supporting one of Citi's Business Groups. We'll also be looking for the following: Enthusiasm to learn about the wide range of roles within assigned business/function Ability to work independently and in a team environment with strong communication skills (both written and verbal) at all organizational levels Possesses resiliency to work in a challenging and changing, high volume environment Ability to think globally, creatively, and innovatively Strong analytical and problem-solving skills Advanced MS Word, Access, Excel, and PowerPoint skills We want to hear from you if... You are pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in any discipline with a graduation date between December 2022 and June 2026 Less than 12 months of prior work experience No ESIC/EFP account Ability to relocate domestically Note : Candidates shortlisted will have to register themselves as an apprentice via the government apprentice portal The Central Control Capabilities function is responsible for driving Monitoring & Testing (M&T) of key controls across the enterprise. It comprises of four groups: (1) Control Assessment, (2) Call Listening, (3) Central Testing , and (4) Strategy, Governance & Automation (SG&A). This role is part of the Central Testing team. The Control Assessment and Central Testing functions partner with the Control Owners and the In-business Control Teams to conduct all the M&T activities for the key controls in scope. The Central Testing function is primarily based out of Citi Service Centers in India. It is structured along three verticals: (1) M&T Execution, (2) M&T Automation, and (3) Strategy & Governance. The identified candidate for this role will be part of the M&T Execution team. Job
