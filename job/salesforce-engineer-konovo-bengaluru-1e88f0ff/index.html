Salesforce Engineer

Konovo · Bengaluru

About the role

Konovo is a global healthcare intelligence company on a mission to transform research through technology- enabling faster, better, connected insights.

Konovo provides healthcare organisations with access to over 2 million healthcare professionals and patients—the largest network of its kind globally. With a workforce of over 300 employees across 5 countries: India, Bosnia and Herzegovina, the United Kingdom, Mexico, and the United States, we collaborate to support some of the most prominent names in healthcare. Our customers include over 300 global pharmaceutical companies, medical device manufacturers, research agencies, and consultancy firms.

We are a well established but fast-growing business- powered by innovation, data, and technology. Konovo’s capabilities are delivered through our cloud-based platform, enabling customers to collect data from healthcare professionals and patients and transform it into actionable insights using cutting-edge AI in conjunction with proven market research tools and techniques. Our Whitefield-based India team is our innovation hub and foundation for scaling product and technology globally. It also is our center of excellence for building scalable platforms, AI-enabled capabilities, and next‑generation healthcare technology.

As a Salesforce Engineer at Konovo, you will have the opportunity to design and implement the data layer that drives value for our global stakeholders across Konovo, as you simultaneously surface insights that benefit our customers. J oin us as a Salesforce Engineer and play a key role in shaping cutting-edge solutions, mentoring others, and driving innovative product capabilities at Konovo!

How You’ll Make an Impact

Design, develop, and implement custom Salesforce solutions to optimize our project management platform.

Work closely with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into scalable Salesforce implementations.

Develop and maintain Apex classes, Triggers, Visualforce pages, and Lightning Web components (LWC) .

Integrate Salesforce with Third-party applications, APIs, and internal systems .

Implement and optimize Salesforce workflows, automation, Reports and Dashboards to improve operational efficiency.

Ensure Data integrity, Security, and Compliance within the Salesforce environment.

Collaborate with global c ross-functional teams , including product managers, data scientists, and engineers, to align Salesforce functionality with business goals.
