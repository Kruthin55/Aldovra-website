Storage & Backup Engineer

Sanofi · Hyderabad

About the role

Team: Digital Foundations (within the Digital organization) Digital Foundations is a global team leading Sanofi into the next generation of datacenter and cloud computing, responsible for server, virtualization, storage and backup technologies in cloud and on-premises environments. Our Vision: To expand our capabilities and reach — not just as a technology shift but as a growth enabler, allowing Sanofi to scale operations seamlessly and respond to cyber disasters rapidly. Our strategic investments in backup technologies are designed to foster long-term growth, ensuring every step taken is toward a more robust and dynamic future. Our transformation is built on four pillars : Optimization Take advantage of the latest technologies for maximum efficiency and cost savings. Value Creation Maximize the value of every investment through automation and technology-driven solutions. Obsolescence Remediation Address outdated technology and systems to reduce risk and improve reliability. Process & Automation Streamline processes and automate routine tasks for improved efficiency and cost savings. 💼 About the Job We are looking for a Backup Expert with a strong understanding of cyber recovery to expand and strengthen the Digital Foundations team, based in our Indian hub. Main
