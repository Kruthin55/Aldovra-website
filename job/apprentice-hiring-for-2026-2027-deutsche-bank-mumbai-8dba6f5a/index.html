Role Description Deutsche Bank PB is one of the world’s leading global wealth managers. It serves the holistic needs of 3 million clients and has a unique client proposition, especially for Retail clients, Small medium enterprises, Family entrepreneurs, Ultra High Networth Individuals (UHNWI) and affluent customers. Leveraging its global network and expertise from across Deutsche Bank, it provides capital markets expertise and international solutions tailored to the individual needs of clients. These include wealth planning over generations and international borders, asset management with individual risk management, loans and deposits as well as the development of bespoke solutions for individuals or selected institutions in close collaboration with experts in Investment Banking and Asset Management. The role is part of a desk extension team in PB CFO and supports the CFO / Performance Management team in Germany; having a local reporting line in Mumbai. Your key responsibilities Support the flash and actual processes, including relevant explains, interacting with the regional/central CFO teams and Performance Mgmt teams / business heads where required Ensuring timely completion of monthend process and GGL restatement process Participation in automation initiatives, leveraging tools such as Power Query, Copilot, and other digital solutions to enhance process efficiency and reporting effectiveness. Monthly / Weekly Revenue / Flow / Volume reporting & analysis for senior management Conduct various ad hoc analysis and strategically address issues that are complementary to the regular planning and forecasting processes Other CFO tasks as necessary Your skills and experience Commerce Graduate or MBA Finance with specialization in Financial accounting & reporting Good communication and articulation skills with experience in facing off to business heads Ability to work in a diverse team with cross-cultural exposure Very good desktop and presentation skills Experience in Automation tools like Power Query, Python, Co-pilot etc. preferred Aptitude for analytical tasks and ability to pull out the key messages Very Good Excel working knowledge Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimise workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs. How we’ll support you Training and development to help you excel in your career. Coaching and support from experts in your team. A culture of continuous learning to aid progression.
