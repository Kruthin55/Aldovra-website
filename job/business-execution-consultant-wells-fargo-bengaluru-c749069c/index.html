Business Execution Consultant

Wells Fargo · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Business Execution Consultant. In this role, you will: Participate in a variety of assigned and ongoing business operations to ensure success in meeting business goals and objectives Identify opportunities for process improvement by conducting root cause testing of all compliance and business metrics Determine areas of strength or Business Execution opportunity within defined scope of work Review and research strategies and action plans to establish effective processes while meeting performance metrics and policy expectations Utilize independent judgment to guide moderate risk deliverables Present recommendations to develop, implement, and monitor strategic approaches, effectiveness of support function, and business performance improvement opportunities for managing risks of the business Exercise independent judgment and provide guidance in diverse support functions and operations for a single business group within a line of business Collaborate and consult with leaders and executive management Provide work direction to less experienced Strategy and Execution staff Required

Qualifications

2+ years of Business Execution, Implementation, or Strategic Planning experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired

Qualifications

Strong data analysis and project management skills Knowledge of and ability to use AI Copilot tools Sound knowledge and background of operational processes in a financial services context and good understanding of data and workflow management tools Ability to facilitate workshops, design sessions and gather requirements from SMEs and stakeholders Ability to work in a collaborative environment with an emphasis on teamwork Excellent problem-solving, organizational, and analytical skills, with the ability to evolve strategy based on research, data and industry trends Strong verbal and written communication skills. Able to communicate clearly and concisely with a range of stakeholders across levels spanning Business, Operations, Technology and Product Desire to work in a fast-paced, highly dynamic environment Ability to be flexible and adjust plans quickly to meet changing business needs Ability to prepare reports, governance documents, metrics and presentation materials for various levels of leadership Intermediate / Advanced skills in MS-Office Suite (Specifically Excel / PowerPoint) Individual with an Engineering background plus MBA from a Tier 1 institution is desired Job Expectations: Supporting data intake requests, including data defects, oversight documentation and analysis requests, and coordinating responses and some defect remediation. Supporting the testing and changes to data management tools that capture data quality control results. Assembling monthly reporting for Risk & Control forums to reduce operational risk and improve data quality. Analysis of Data Management Policy & Procedures and supporting development of sustainable BAU processes and procedures.
