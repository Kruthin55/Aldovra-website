We’re looking for highly skilled and experienced content developers with a knack for writing marketing content that drives conversions and is optimized for organic search. If you’re familiar with producing online content, especially for the B2B SaaS world and have an eye for detail, we’d like to meet you. Ultimately, you’ll deliver quality content assets that appeal to our audiences, attract customers, boost awareness, and drive conversions and revenue generation.This job requires someone who loves to communicate through content and can utilize a variety of media and formats to get the job done. Responsibilities

● Baseline knowledge of the SaaS, Salesforce platform, products, and passion for software and technology solutions. ● Ability to understand and articulate technically complex product oﬀerings and needs of the customers. ● Ability to independently create new source content that is clear, technically accurate, delightful, and useful to Salesforce’s customers and prospects. ● Ability to generate and execute innovative “breakout” ideas. ● Skills with a wide variety of technical content authoring tools such as Google Workspace apps, GitHub, Articulate Rise, video editing software, and content formats such as documents and spreadsheets, images, PDFs, MP4s, and more. ● Basic familiarity with HTML/CSS and Web Analytics. ● To work independently to complete multiple projects in a fast-paced and nimble environment globally. ● Ability to collaborate cross-functionally, exerting influence without direct authority. ● Proven ability of critical thinking and show curiosity and ask questions to get to the root of things ● Keep up with digital content trends and courageously try new tools and content formats based on research to innovate. ● Proactively identify and develop ideas to address gaps in our thinking. ● Experience with corporate marketing and branding, and a clear understanding of marketing strategies and concepts. ● Interpret creative direction, follow brand tone and style guides, and keep a beginner’s mind. ● Participate in all necessary internal and external meetings — briefings, status, technical brainstorming sessions, and presentations — with senior leaders ● Excellent English writing and speaking skills with an aptitude to understand the technical details of a product. ● Equivalent degree in Journalism, Liberal arts, Humanities, Literature, Psychology, Sociology etc.
