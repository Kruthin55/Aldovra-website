Job Summary Research, develop, implement and monitor inventory management and distribution logistics systems, concepts and procedures for worldwide parts inventory and operations management. The primary goal is to support the Product Support & Logistics (PSLD) division’s effort in achieving the inventory and customer service objectives. As an Inventory Analyst, you will have responsibility for ensuring the complete and successful testing and conversion planning of all PIC functions in dealers’ ERP conversion projects, and contribute to design, development, and deployment of the new PIC Data Analytics Suite (DAS). What You Will Do

Develop automated processes to aid in data cleansing and maintenance using analytical tools (SQL, SAS, Python, PowerBI).

Develop and test new reporting tools, following standards and best practices.

Support 24x7 PIC systems for dealer order recommendations, willing to work in Europe / US time zone.

Performing work to achieve corporate supply chain performance targets focused on inventory reduction, increased inventory turns and improved parts availability.

Working effectively with diverse, multi-functional teams to apply the supply chain logistics process and leading team efforts to develop a lean parts distribution network. Degree Requirement

Bachelor’s degree in engineering / Math’s / Science / Commerce.

2 to 3 years’ relevant experience in data analytics and visualization tools.
