JOB PURPOSE The Gemini Agent Platform AI Engineer will help accelerate the adoption of Google Cloud, Gemini and agent-based artificial intelligence technologies across Unilever. This is a hands-on engineering role with a strong product engagement dimension. You will partner with product and engineering teams to understand their existing applications and technology landscape, identify opportunities to migrate or modernize solutions onto Google Cloud, and help define and engineer the target solutions. Alongside application migration and modernization, you will design and build artificial intelligence and intelligent agent solutions using Google's technology ecosystem. A key part of the role will be developing reusable engineering components, patterns and implementation approaches that can support multiple enterprise use cases. You will work across the complete development lifecycle, from discovery and technical design through development, testing, deployment, monitoring and continuous improvement, helping teams turn ideas and prototypes into secure, scalable and production-ready solutions. You’ll join a collaborative environment where engineers, architects and product teams work together to solve meaningful problems, experiment with emerging technologies and shape how Google and artificial intelligence capabilities are adopted at enterprise scale. Because here, we don’t just hire for jobs. We invite you to be part of something bigger. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Partner with product and engineering teams to understand existing applications, technical requirements and modernisation needs, and identify appropriate opportunities to adopt Google Cloud technologies. Support the migration and modernisation of applications onto Google Cloud, working with product teams and architects to define target architectures and practical engineering approaches. Design, develop and deploy artificial intelligence applications and intelligent agent solutions using Google Gemini and Google Cloud technologies. Take solutions through the complete engineering lifecycle, from experimentation and technical design through development, testing, deployment, monitoring and continuous improvement. Build reusable components, frameworks and engineering patterns that accelerate future artificial intelligence and Google Cloud implementations. Develop integrations between intelligent agents, enterprise applications, application programming interfaces and data sources. Design solutions that securely connect artificial intelligence models with trusted enterprise information and services. Build and orchestrate workflows involving artificial intelligence models, software tools, business applications and cloud services. Evaluate technical approaches based on engineering feasibility, scalability, reliability, security, operational readiness and cost. Apply strong software engineering practices across source control, automated testing, deployment automation, version management and release processes. Implement appropriate security, identity and access management, monitoring, observability and responsible artificial intelligence practices. Evaluate and improve the quality, reliability and technical performance of artificial intelligence and agent-based solutions. Work closely with architects, product teams and engineering partners to convert business requirements and early prototypes into production-ready solutions. Create clear technical documentation, reusable implementation guidance and engineering standards that enable other teams to adopt proven solutions and patterns. Strong understanding of the software development lifecycle, covering solution design, development, testing, deployment, monitoring and production operations. Experience developing applications using generative artificial intelligence or large language models. Understanding of intelligent agent concepts, including tool use, orchestration, grounding and context management. Experience with cloud-native application architectures, application programming interfaces and integration patterns. Experience with modern software engineering practices, including source control, automated testing, continuous integration and automated deployment. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS Hands-on development experience with Google Gemini and Vertex AI. Experience developing cloud or artificial intelligence solutions using Python. Experience with Google technologies and frameworks for building intelligent agents. Experience with retrieval-augmented generation, embeddings, semantic retrieval and vector search. Experience with artificial intelligence evaluation, monitoring, observability and responsible artificial intelligence controls. Experience creating reusable artificial intelligence components, engineering frameworks or platform capabilities. Experience with application migration and modernisation on Google Cloud. Experience with cloud-native technologies, including containers and serverless architectures. Experience with enterprise application integration and application programming interfaces. Experience working across global, cross-functional product and engineering teams. Experience working with external technology or delivery partners.
