Intern - Legal

GE Aerospace · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary GE Aerospace is seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Legal Intern to support our legal team with various projects and day-to-day operations. This 2-month internship (Dec ‘26 – Jan ’27) offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a dynamic corporate legal environment within the aerospace industry. Job Description Site Overview Established in 2000, the John F. Welch Technology Center (JFWTC) in Bengaluru is our multidisciplinary research and engineering center. Engineers and scientists at JFWTC have contributed to hundreds of aviation patents, pioneering breakthroughs in engine technologies, advanced materials, and additive manufacturing. Roles Overview: Conduct legal research and analysis on a variety of topics, including contract law, companies’ law, intellectual property, real estate laws, regulatory compliances. Assist in drafting and reviewing NDAs, commercial contracts, services agreements, and other legal documentations. Assist the legal team in preparing for litigation and other legal proceedings. Assist with research on corporate laws and company secretarial matters and provide necessary support in drafting secretarial documentations. Assist to maintain legal files and databases, ensuring accuracy and accessibility. Participate in legal department meetings with cross-functional stakeholders and contribute to discussions. Support for preparation of presentations, legal updates and guidance notes. Collaborate with internal stakeholders across various functions to gather information and provide legal support. Assist with defined projects, process improvements and initiatives as assigned by the legal team. Required Qualification: Minimum: CGPA: 7.0/8.0 Shall not have been subject to any disciplinary action or shall have no pending disciplinary proceedings by the Institute within 6 months preceding the date of application. Students with coursework or demonstrated interest in Corporate Law, Aviation Law, Commercial Contracts, Technology Contracts, Employment Laws, Compliance & Ethics Excellent legal research, writing, and analytical skills. Ability to handle confidential information with discretion and sound ethical judgement Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite Ability to work independently and as part of a team in a fast-paced environment. Strong attention to detail and organizational skills. Previous internship experience is a plus. Desired Qualification: Humble: respectful, receptive, agile, eager to learn Transparent: shares critical information, speaks with candor, contributes constructively Focused: quick learner, strategically prioritizes work, committed Leadership ability: strong communicator, decision-maker, collaborative Problem solver: analytical-minded, challenges existing processes, critical thinker At GE Aerospace, we have a relentless dedication to the future of safe and more sustainable flight and believe in our talented people to make it happen. Here, you will have the opportunity to work on really cool things with really smart and collaborative people. Together, we will mobilize a new era of growth in aerospace and defense. Where others stop, we accelerate. Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: No
