Research Analyst

NielsenIQ · Mumbai

About the role

Position Description As a Research Analyst, Line & Price, you will help develop the NielsenIQ Innovation Practice business by executing marketing research studies for our clients.  You will be responsible for compiling, analyzing and interpreting marketing research data, helping CS write client reports, and constructing client presentations with a high degree of independence.  You will support the Line & Price team in driving revenue by delivering excellent project work that will result in repeated business with our clients.  While we seek candidates with proven analytical skills, we provide extensive training to give our Analysts the subject matter expertise to function effectively in our business. This includes training regarding choice-based research methods, designing and analyzing market research, and delivering high impact consultative presentations to our clients.
