Junior Engineer – Engineering Change Management

Flex · India, Coimbatore

About the role

Flex is the diversified manufacturing partner of choice that helps market-leading brands design, build and deliver innovative products that improve the world. A career at Flex offers the opportunity to make a difference and invest in your growth in a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment. If you are excited about a role but don't meet every bullet point, we encourage you to apply and join us to create the extraordinary. Job Summary

To support our extraordinary teams who build great products and contribute to our growth, we are looking to add a Junior Engineer – Engineering Change Management located in Coimbatore . What a typical day looks like: Create part numbers for BOM in Agile and Baan. Create AVL in Agile and Baan systems. Download customer documents such as product specifications and release them in CDC. Work as a Change Analyst during NPI for new changes. Release part lists to production for new models and after implementation of ECOs. Efficiently manage ECOs for manufacturing sites across the globe. Use the Agile (PLM) system for ECO (Engineering Change Order) creation, review, routing, tracking, release, and distribution. Study ECOs and drawings closely and coordinate with site Process and Product Engineering teams. Work closely with Engineering and Materials teams to review product updates and modifications. Respond to business partners (requestors) and customers on queries related to Engineering Change Orders. Ensure adherence to targets set for all process metrics. Demonstrate good people management and leadership skills. The experience we’re looking to add to our team: B.E. (EEE/ECE) graduate with 0–2 years of experience as an NPI/ECO Engineer in a manufacturing setup, preferably in the EMS industry. Hands-on experience in the following domains: NPI (New Product Introduction) ECO (Engineering Change Order Management) BOM (Bill of Materials) Management Hands-on experience in any PLM software linked to the ECO process. Excellent written and oral communication skills in English are mandatory. Knowledge of MS Office and MS Outlook. Good analytical skills. Coaching, mentoring, and conflict resolution skills.
