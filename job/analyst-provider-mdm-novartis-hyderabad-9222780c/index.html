Analyst - Provider MDM

Novartis · Hyderabad

About the role

Job Description Summary The Analyst – Provider MDM will support the strategy, governance, and operational execution of the Provider Master Data Management (MDM) platform. The role works closely with Product Owners, Provider Data SMEs, and cross‑functional partners to ensure accurate, reliable, and high‑quality mastering of Provider, plan, PBM, and commercial healthcare datasets. The Analyst contributes to maintaining standards, supporting stewardship processes, resolving data issues, and driving continuous improvements across data quality and operational workflows. Job Description Analyst - Provider MDM Location – Hyderabad #LI Hybrid Major accountabilities: Support Provider MDM strategy by aligning initiatives with business priorities and stakeholder expectations. Help establish governance, standards, and best practices across HCP, HCO, and Product data domains. Review deliverables, support team processes, and promote accountability and continuous improvement. Partner with stewardship teams to manage curation workflows, exceptions, and data quality processes. Translate Provider business rules into standardized operational logic for consistent execution. Collaborate with SMEs and vendors to resolve data discrepancies and maintain quality standards. Strengthen data quality through profiling, validation, monitoring, alerting, and root cause analysis. Support downstream consumers by ensuring consistent mastered data delivery, matching logic, hierarchy validation, and process optimization. Educational Background: Bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, Information Systems, Engineering, or related field. Essential

Requirements

2-4 years of data management experience with strong expertise in MDM and ELT/ETL development. Hands-on experience with Snowflake, SQL, Python, and orchestration tools such as Airflow or ADF. Skilled in designing ingestion and transformation pipelines for large healthcare datasets. Experience implementing data quality, profiling, validation, logging, and alerting frameworks. Good understanding of Provider, plan, and PBM concepts. Ability to lead data initiatives, mentor team members, and collaborate cross-functionally. Strong analytical, communication, problem-solving, automation, multitasking, and deadline-management skills Skills Desired Alteryx, Analytical Thinking, Brand Awareness, Business Networking, Curious Mindset, Digital Marketing, Marketing Campaigns, Marketing Communications (MarCom), Marketing Planning, Marketing Strategies, Media Campaigns, Process Documentation, Statistical Analysis
