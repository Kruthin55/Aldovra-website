Associate Fraud & Claims Operations Representative-only freshers

Wells Fargo · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role: At Wells Fargo, an Associate Fraud & Claims Operations Representative plays an important front-line role in protecting customers and the company from fraud while delivering a trusted customer experience. Working in a high-volume contact center environment, you will support customers through potential fraud situations by reviewing account activity, investigating concerns, applying appropriate authentication and fraud-prevention practices, and helping resolve issues accurately and efficiently. This role contributes to Wells Fargo’s focus on strong risk management, operational excellence, and customer centricity while giving you the opportunity to build valuable experience in fraud and claims operations. In this role, you will: Review routine fraud alerts, transactions, claims, and referrals by following established procedures to support accurate case outcomes in a high-volume call center environment Gather, verify, and document customer information to initiate and support routine fraud and claims processing Handle multiple routine cases while meeting established quality, accuracy, productivity, and customer service expectations Perform routine operational and customer support activities related to fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and account servicing through voice and non-voice channels Research account activity and transaction details using established procedures to support fraud and claims case review Complete assigned cases accurately and within established timelines, seeking guidance or escalating issues when appropriate Ask relevant questions and review account activity to understand customer concerns and identify potential fraud indicators Communicate clearly with customers, team members, and internal partners to obtain, verify, or provide routine information. Required

Qualifications

6+ months of customer service support experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education PHL only: 6+ months of customer service experience in business process outsourcing (BPO) industry Desired

Qualifications
