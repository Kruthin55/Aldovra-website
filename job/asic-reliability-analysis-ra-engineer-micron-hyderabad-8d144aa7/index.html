ASIC Reliability Analysis (RA) Engineer

Micron · Hyderabad

About the role

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Responsibilities

Responsibilities include, but are not limited to: HTOL Qualification & Execution (Primary Focus) 1. Participate in HTOL qualification plan discussion and review, aligning stress conditions, sample plans, and readout requirements with multi-functional collaborators. 2. Drive burn-in board (BIB) design and manufacturing in collaboration with ASIC Test Engineering and external vendors, from specification definition and design review through fabrication, delivery, and acceptance. 3. Perform BIB and burn-in (BI) program validation to confirm hardware and pattern readiness prior to qualification starts. 4. Set up HTOL ovens and implement HTOL test flows according to defined protocols, monitoring stress conditions throughout the run. 5. Solve hardware, oven, and program issues encountered during HTOL execution, and drive timely resolution to protect qualification schedules. 6. Perform pass/fail verification for tested devices, analyze pre- and post-stress results, and ensure accurate, clear reporting of findings. 7. Support overall reliability infrastructure readiness to enable timely engineering execution and issue resolution. Lab Operations & Cross-Site Collaboration 8. Handle lab logistics, including inventory control, equipment allocation, and scheduling coordination across concurrent projects. 9. Partner with multi-functional teams to support smooth lab operations and efficient resource utilization. Minimum

Qualifications

To be considered for this role, you should have: 1. A Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field. 2. Experience with ASIC burn-in board or load board design and validation, including vendor coordination. 3. Basic working knowledge of ASIC HTOL concepts — including the purpose of accelerated life stress, burn-in hardware, and qualification flow. (Direct hands-on HTOL oven setup/execution experience is not required.) 4. A background in ASIC Test Engineering, Hardware Engineering, or Product Engineering, with demonstrated ability to learn reliability and HTOL methodologies quickly. 5. Strong troubleshooting skills for hardware, equipment, or test-related issues. 6. Ability to analyze test outcomes and provide clear, accurate reporting. 7. Good problem-solving skills and the ability to work effectively across teams. Preferred

Qualifications

The following qualifications are a plus: 1. Broad ASIC knowledge extending beyond reliability — such as ASIC test program development, product engineering, silicon bring-up, hardware design, or yield analysis. 2. Experience with socket selection and thermal/signal integrity validation for burn-in applications. 3. Experience supporting lab technicians in configuring, setting up, and maintaining testers, handlers, and probers. 4. Experience in lab logistics coordination, inventory control, and equipment scheduling. 5. Understanding of reliability test execution flows and hardware readiness requirements for ASIC products. 6. Strong sense of ownership, execution discipline, and attention to detail. AI in Job
