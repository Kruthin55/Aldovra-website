Software Engineer

GE HealthCare · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary This position involves understanding the design of components and developing them within an application or product. The role requires strong hands-on experience in development, preferably using Java, Spring Boot, and AWS cloud services. The candidate will collaborate with team members to understand and integrate these components within and across subsystems. Attention to detail is crucial to ensure non-functional requirements are met during implementation and testing, achieving the best possible performance, quality, and responsiveness of the applications as per product requirements. GE HealthCare is a leading global medical technology and digital solutions innovator. Our purpose is to create a world where healthcare has no limits. Unlock your ambition, turn ideas into world-changing realities, and join an organization where every voice makes a difference, and every difference builds a healthier world. Job Description In this role you will be responsible for Cloud Design & Implementation: Low level design, develop, and deploy cloud-based solutions using AWS services such as EC2, S3, Lambda, DynamoDB, SQS, EventBridge. Implement IoT solutions utilizing AWS IoT Core, Greengrass, and other relevant AWS IoT services. Develop, deploy and test microservices using ECS/EKS-based containerized applications using Docker and ECS/EKS and Lambda. Develop and maintain backend services and microservices using Java and Spring Boot and test using Python BDD. Maintain code quality through best practices, unit testing and test automation. Participate in software design reviews, conduct code reviews and peer testing for other members in the team Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Basic hands-on/fundamentals of infrastructure using IaC tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or similar. Automate deployment processes and ensure consistent environments across multiple stages and regions using CI/CD best practices with source control tools like GIT/GITLAB. System Optimization & Troubleshooting: Monitor and optimize cloud-based systems for performance, scalability, and security. Troubleshoot and resolve complex cloud infrastructure and Java application issues. Collaboration & Documentation: Work closely with internal and external teams to ensure cloud solutions meet business requirements. Create and maintain detailed documentation including low level designs, deployment scripts, troubleshooting guides and operational procedures. Security & Compliance: Basic knowledge of security practices in healthcare (nice to have) Ensure compliance with industry standards and regulatory requirements. Continuous Improvement: Stay updated with the latest advancements in AWS, IoT, ECS/EKS, IaC, and Java/Python technologies. Proactively suggest improvements and enhancements to existing systems and processes. Mentorship & Guidance: Demonstrates the ability to excel both as an individual contributor and as a collaborative team player. Foster a collaborative and inclusive team environment, encouraging continuous learning and professional growth. Conduct code reviews, provide constructive feedback, and promote best practices. Education Qualification Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or “STEM” Majors (Science, Technology, Engineering and Math) with minimum 3+ years of experience in cloud engineering with a focus on Java, Python, AWS, Kubernetes/ECS, CI/CD. Experience : Hands-on experience with AWS services (EKS, S3, Lambda, DynamoDB, SQS, CloudWatch) and good to have IoT technologies (AWS IoT Core). Working knowledge of in ECS/Kubernetes, Docker, SQL and NO-SQL databases. Good to have knowledge on UI/UX with Javascript, Angular, React technologies Business Acumen: Comfortable working within a team structure and collaborating with colleagues to overcome challenges and achieve goals. Capable of understanding/communicating the importance of key business/customer objectives. Able to provide clear and concise information for team use, with some experience in presentations and storytelling. Leadership: Able to identify challenges and collaborate with team members to find solutions and leverage resources. Demonstrated ability to work with blended teams, including 3rd party partners and customer personnel. Demonstrated Change Management /Acceleration capabilities Strong interpersonal skills, including creativity and curiosity with ability to effectively communicate and influence across all organizational levels Proven analytical and problem resolution skills Effective communication and collaboration skills, with experience working closely with cross-functional teams. Inclusion and Diversity: GE Healthcare is an Equal Opportunity Employer where inclusion matters. Our salary and benefits are everything you’d expect from an organization with global strength and scale, and you’ll be surrounded by career opportunities in a culture that fosters care, collaboration, and support. #Everyroleisvital #LI-MP2 Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: Yes
