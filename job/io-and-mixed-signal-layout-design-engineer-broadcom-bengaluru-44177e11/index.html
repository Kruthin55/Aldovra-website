IO and Mixed Signal Layout Design Engineer

Broadcom · Bengaluru

About the role

Please Note: 1. If you are a first time user, please create your candidate login account before you apply for a job. (Click Sign In > Create Account) 2. If you already have a Candidate Account, please Sign-In before you apply. Job Description: Job Description: Design full custom layouts of I/O libraries and Analog Mixed Signal IP's in planar and FinFET technologies. Drive critical floor-planning decisions to ensure optimal placement and routing that meets design requirements and performance metric. Perform comprehensive layout verification to ensure high quality and compliance Complete ownership from end to end deliverables with quality within the schedule. Adapt quickly to new methodologies, remain open to new ideas, and communicate effectively with the teams across all sites. Skills and

Qualifications

Required: B.Tech in Electronics/Electrical engineering with 3+ years of experience Hands-on experience in I/O Library and Analog Mixed-signal IP development and verification Solid understanding of Planar and FinFET layouts and process technology In-Depth knowledge of ESD and Latch-up principles for I/O and Analog layouts Proficiency in using Cadence Virtuoso Layout suite and Siemens CALIBRE verification tools. Expertise in Layout verifications and debugging: DRC/LVS/ANT/ERC/DFM, LPE, PERC, EM and IR etc Familiar with the Physical Design flow: LEF generation and PnR Knowledge of AI, automation in layout, Script Programming and SKILL Programming is a plus. Excellent written and verbal communication skills to collaborate with circuit designers and teams across all sites Self-motivated, capable of working independently, and a strong collaborative team player.
