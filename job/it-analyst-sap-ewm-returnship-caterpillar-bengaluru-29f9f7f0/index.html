Job Summary The role will focus on SAP EWM maintenance, support, and process optimization, ensuring seamless integration with SAP ECC, SPP, CRM, and other enterprise applications to support business objectives. What You Will Do Required Business/Personnel Skills: · Provide Solution Performance (analysis/tuning) and work across business/technology teams to ensure alignment between business solution definition & systems architecture for the organization · Analyze user requests and software requirements for feasibility of design and potential impact while cognizant of time and cost constraints · Strong ability to identify, interpret and evaluate user and system requirements. He/she should be able to interact with business in a timely and proactive manner to understand their problem and suggest solutions. · Strong ability to troubleshoot issues and make system changes as needed to resolve issues · Able to respond to demanding target dates · He/she is expected to prepare documents for new/complex issues / functions and share with team. What You Have Required Technical Skills & Experience: · 4 to 6 years of SAP EWM implementation or EWM maintenance/support experience required · Candidate must provide demonstrated functional knowledge in SAP EWM with expertise in Inbound, Outbound and Internal processes · Candidate must provide demonstrated knowledge of expertise in SAP archiving in either the EWM or ECC components using the SARA transaction · Candidate should have knowledge of EWM, ECC, SPP & CRM integration and various other SAP interfaces Desired Skills: · Candidate should have knowledge in SAP ABAP development (coding, testing and debugging) Additional Information · Able to work outside of first shift, including evenings, nights, weekends, holidays, extended hours, and on-call rotations. We are a 24/7 support team in Bangalore. · Strong verbal and written English communication skills, problem solving skills, customer service and interpersonal skills. · Strong ability to work independently and manage one’s team, as well as, being a collaborative team member Education Qualification: · Bachelor's degree in computer science; or computer engineering; or equivalent IT related degree required Caterpillar is not currently hiring individuals for this position who now or in the future require sponsorship for employment visa status; however, as a global company, Caterpillar offers many job opportunities outside of India which can be found through our employment website at www.caterpillar.com/careers .
