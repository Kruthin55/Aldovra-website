IT Technical Analyst

Johnson Controls · Bengaluru

About the role

What you will do Johnson Controls invites you to apply for an internship located in Bengaluru, India . This internship is designed to provide you with a positive work/training experience and challenging objectives. Specific identified internship responsibilities are outlined below. How you will do it: Gathering and analyzing data for the solution design Interpreting gathered data into RTM. Help in completing all the documentation for the projects Submitting reports to the relevant department heads and management Finding patterns and trends in the analyzed data Helping the management and other teams draw business goals and needs Establishing new data gathering and analysis techniques within the organization Formulating procedural artifacts like Test scripts, Training docs, Cut over steps and deployment plans. Closely work Project manager and other stake holders. Demonstrate superior project management skills to develop detailed task-level plans towards timely completion of project milestones. Coordinate user training and acceptance criteria with the business leads (project managers, leads and / or change agents). Work directly with the IT and PMO department to plan and manage application upgrades, user-testing, issue resolution and subsequent release to production, while closely aligned with Corporate Change Management processes. Liaise between the Business Units Teams and the IT organization; Foster and maintain good relationships with key internal customers. Automation skills using UI path, Power apps and Power automate. Great to have a candidate who developed an application or automation Define duration: This will be a full-time summer 2027 internship What we look for Required

Qualifications

Currently enrolled as a full-time student at an accredited India college or university Pursuing a degree in Engineering Hours/week requirement - 40
