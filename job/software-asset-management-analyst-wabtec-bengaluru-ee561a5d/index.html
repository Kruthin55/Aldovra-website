Software Asset Management Analyst

Wabtec · Bengaluru

About the role

Who will you be working with? You will work within Wabtec's Corporate Solutions organization as part of the Software Asset Management (SAM) function. You will partner closely with business requestors, application owners, Strategic Sourcing, Procurement, Finance, Accounts Payable, IT service management, cybersecurity, audit and compliance teams, and regional IT stakeholders. You will also collaborate with software publishers, resellers, service providers, and the Flexera platform team to support the complete software asset lifecycle. This analyst role combines day-to-day operational ownership with process improvement. You will serve as a key point of contact for software requests, licensing, procurement, renewals, catalog administration, vendor portal activities, reporting, and issue resolution while helping mature SAM governance, controls, data quality, and automation. How will you make a difference? ·    Own the end-to-end Software Request process managed in ServiceNow, from intake and validation through approvals, fulfillment, status of communication, and closure. ·    Administer software licensing and entitlement activities in Flexera, partnering with the Flexera platform team to improve data quality, inventory coverage, integrations, reporting, and platform effectiveness. ·    Coordinate software purchasing activities in Coupa, including requisition creation, purchase order processing, receipt confirmation, and invoice issue resolution in partnership with Procurement, Finance, Accounts Payable, vendors, and requestors. ·    Lead operational execution of the SAM renewal process by maintaining the renewal calendar, confirming demand and ownership, validating license positions, coordinating quotes and approvals, and reducing the risk of service interruption or unplanned spend. ·    Maintain an accurate, usable software catalog and improve standardization by identifying duplicate, obsolete, unsupported, underused, or nonstrategic software products. ·    Reduce software sprawl and improve the organization's area of control through rationalization, reclamation, reuse, standardization, lifecycle governance, and stronger ownership of software assets. ·    Produce metrics and actionable insights that improve cost transparency, license compliance, request performance, renewal readiness, data quality, and process maturity.
