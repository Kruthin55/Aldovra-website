Citi - Markets, Summer Analyst, India – APAC, 2027

Citi · Mumbai

About the role

You're the brains behind our work. You’re ready to bring your knowledge from the classroom to the trading floor, and Citi wants to help you get there. Whether it’s honing your skills or building your network, we know that success can’t come without growth. Our programs equip you with the knowledge and training you need to play a valuable role on your team and establish a long-term career here. At Citi, we value internal mobility, and career growth is not a question of if, but when. Citi Markets is looking for Summer Analyst to join the Markets team in India. Citi Markets provides world-class products and financing solutions for corporations, governments, as well as institutional and retail investors through our dominant underwriting, sales and trading, and distribution capabilities. Our broad product knowledge and on-the-ground local markets expertise - coupled with complementary strengths in technology and distribution - allows us to offer clients a wide range of comprehensive financial solutions. We address their needs with products in all discipline currencies; sectors; and geographies; The breadth and depth of our underwriting, structuring, securitisation, sales and trading and distribution capabilities span asset classes and currencies, sectors and industries. Our expertise covers a vast array of products including Equities, Rates, Credit, and Foreign Exchange (FX). We want to hear from you if.. You are a penultimate year student with a Master's or Undergraduate degree, in any discipline, from a top academic institution working towards a competitive GPA and graduating between Dec 2027 - June 2028. Your time here will look something like this.. The Markets Summer Analyst Program is for 10 weeks, consisting of 2 rotations in various trading and sales desks within Citi Markets. This program also includes orientation, classroom training, self-learning opportunities, lecture series, networking events, etc. It is carefully designed to help summer interns assimilate into the Citi Markets organization, to strengthen their markets knowledge & technical skills, and to enrich their overall learning experience. Interns are required to work closely with their Program as well as Business Managers during period of Internship. Who we think will be a great fit As industries all over the globe continue to restructure and grow, we are hiring interns who have openness to diversity, a global perspective on the future of banking, and are eager to make an impact on the corporate level. We will also be looking for the following You have exceptional analytical and quantitative skills, enabling you to tackle complex challenges. You have demonstrated strong leadership, teamwork, and communication skills, making you an effective collaborator and leader. You have a good understanding of finance and an interest in Markets. You are proficient in programming languages (VBA, Python) and Microsoft office, especially Excel. You have excellent interpersonal skills, coupled with a strong attention to detail and exceptional planning and organisational skills. You are intellectually curious and proactively seek out new and creative ideas. You are a self-starter who thrives both independently and as part of a collaborative team, with a strong commitment to personal growth and career development, including mobility and flexibility. You have an unwavering commitment to integrity and ethical decision-making. During your time with us, you will be developing skillsets such as Teamwork - A global team with multiple support functions requires the candidate to have strong networking and interpersonal skills, enabling them to work effectively as part of a team. Resilience - Being adaptive to change and solutions focused. Analytical thinking - Driving decision making and future actions through data analysis. Communication - Opportunity to develop skills presenting to, and regularly communicating with clients, regional and global senior management. We encourage early applications as we recruit on a rolling basis.
