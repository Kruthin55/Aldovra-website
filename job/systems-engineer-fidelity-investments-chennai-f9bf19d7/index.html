Systems Engineer

Fidelity Investments · Chennai

About the role

Job Description: Job Title – Systems Engineer Systems Engineer will ensure the system stability of both on-premise and cloud applications. The Purpose of This Role Work along with the Dev team to maintain critical business applications. Support migration of applications to cloud. Contribute and follow engineering best practices Manage Dev/Ops for non-prod environments The Value You Deliver Will be part of Production support on-call and related core processes. Will be driving Incident Management, Problem Management, Change Management, Release Management, BCP/DR, Batch Monitoring, Application and Infrastructure support functions. Will get complete ownership and accountability to maintain Production stability Will have to collaborate with peers and the leadership team on process improvement ideas, policy and procedure enhancements and opportunities to improve the customer service experience. Would get opportunities to innovate and provide solutions to Automation and Continuous Integration and Deployment processes Would get opportunities to learn and support application components on Cloud Should be able to able to understand the existing systems, code and propose fixes for issues. Constantly find newer and better ways to achieve operational excellence. The Skills that are Key to this role Technical / Behavioural Strong knowledge on UNIX Scripts Strong knowledge on Control-M Good analytical and problem solving skills Ability to handle incident bridge calls and crisis situations to mitigate incident impact. Experience in incident management and problem management. SQL & PL/SQL - Expertise in writing and debugging/Trouble shooting skills on SQL and PL-SQL code, Stored Procedures, Functions & Triggers Good understanding of Observability Strong knowledge on release and change management Ability to understand the business criticality of various applications as they relate to sophisticated business processes. Familiarity with Agile Methodology Good communication skills Team Player Quick Learner The Skills that are Good To Have for this role Basic Understanding / knowledge on cloud technologies like AWS Presentation skills Knowledge of Capital Markets/Alternative Investments DevOps experience (Udeploy/Jenkins, good to have) Debugging skills for java applications. How Your Work Impacts the Organization FIT (Fidelity Institutional Technology) - provides a wide range of Institutional Services technology solutions for our FI business partners. Our team provides technology services in the area of development, maintenance, quality assurance and production support of mission critical brokerage applications. As with most of the businesses these days, technology plays a significant role in business stability and growth. The ever-evolving technology landscape and the choices that it offers, have a direct correlation with the success of the business. The Expertise We’re Looking For 2+ years of total experience. Engineering Graduate/CS graduate/MCA equivalent Location : Chennai Shift timings: 11:00 am - 8:00pm Certifications: Category: Information Technology
