About Lowe’s Lowe’s is a FORTUNE® 100 home improvement company serving approximately 16 million customer transactions a week in the United States. With total fiscal year 2024 sales of more than $83 billion, Lowe’s operates over 1,700 home improvement stores and employs approximately 300,000 associates. Based in Mooresville, N.C., Lowe’s supports the communities it serves through programs focused on creating safe, affordable housing, improving community spaces, helping to develop the next generation of skilled trade experts and providing disaster relief to communities in need. For more information, visit Lowes.com. Lowe’s India , the Global Capability Center of Lowe’s Companies Inc., is a hub for driving our technology, business, analytics, and shared services strategy. Based in Bengaluru with over 4,500 associates, it powers innovations across omnichannel retail, AI/ML, enterprise architecture, supply chain, and customer experience. From supporting and launching homegrown solutions to fostering innovation through its Catalyze platform, Lowe’s India plays a pivotal role in transforming home improvement retail while upholding strong commitment to social impact and sustainability. For more information, visit Lowes India About The Team The Content Strategy & Governance team at Lowe’s India is dedicated to enhancing the digital customer experience by developing and implementing comprehensive content strategies for Product Detail Pages (PDPs) and User-Generated Content (UGC). This team ensures that all content aligns with business objectives, maintains consistency across platforms, and meets quality and compliance standards. By collaborating with cross-functional teams—including product management, UX/UI design, and analytics. The team optimizes content performance, integrates user feedback, and drives innovation in content creation and management. Their efforts contribute to increased customer engagement, trust, and conversion rates, supporting the organization's overall growth and competitiveness in the retail market. Job Summary As an Analyst, you will play a key role in conducting various analytical activities to support decision-making within the organization. Your responsibilities will involve performing competitive analysis, gap analysis, and ensuring adherence to standard governance practices. Additionally, you will contribute to internal reporting, providing accurate and timely key performance indicators (KPIs). You may also be involved in tasks such as UAT, list ownership, video analysis, and more. Roles &

Responsibilities

Core

Responsibilities

Analyze the competitive landscape to identify market trends, key competitors, and potential growth opportunities for the company. Identify and assess gaps in product offerings, pricing strategies, and market positioning, offering recommendations for improvements. Assist in maintaining standard governance practices to ensure organizational consistency and compliance. Contribute to the preparation and delivery of internal reports, presenting critical findings and insights to relevant stakeholders. Share ownership responsibilities for lists such as Digital Commerce Review (DCR) and Product Line Review (PLR), ensuring they reflect current market trends and products. Collaborate in User Acceptance Testing (UAT) processes to assess new software, tools, or systems for alignment with company requirements and standards. Evaluate and review videos, extracting relevant insights and trends related to the company's products or services. Assist in managing intake forms for data collection and analysis, ensuring they are kept up-to-date and well-structured. Contribute to User-Generated Content (UGC) moderation based on FTC guidelines, ensuring that user-generated content adheres to legal and ethical standards. In this role, you will be a valuable member of the analytical team, playing a significant role in data-driven decision-making and supporting the organization's competitiveness and regulatory compliance. You should have excellent written and verbal communication, time management and stakeholder management skills.
