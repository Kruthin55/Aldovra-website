Data Engineer

Chevron · Bengaluru

About the role

Total Number of Openings 1 The Chevron Engineering and Innovation Excellence Center (ENGINE) in Bengaluru India brings together the resources and expertise of the Chevron global network with talent in India to enhance agility and technological innovation to optimize solutions for the world’s current and future energy challenges. As one of the leading energy providers worldwide, Chevron is involved in the production of crude oil and natural gas, manufacturing of transportation fuels, lubricants, petrochemicals, and additives, and the development of enabling technologies. Chevron's vision is to be the global energy company most admired for its people, partnerships, and performance. With a clear purpose to develop affordable, reliable, ever-cleaner energy that enables human progress, we believe human ingenuity has the power to solve any challenge and overcome any obstacle. Meeting the world’s growing energy needs requires the pursuit of innovations and advancements that deliver a better future for all. About the position: As a Data Engineer, you will support the design, development, testing, and deployment of data solutions that enable Digital Twin initiatives and enterprise analytics. You will work with senior data engineers, product teams, and business stakeholders to develop data pipelines, integrations, and data quality solutions across engineering systems and cloud platforms. This role provides an opportunity to build technical expertise in Facilities Engineering data, Digital Twin solutions, and Agile software delivery within the Oil & Gas industry. Technical Skills: Data Engineering Working knowledge of the Azure data platform, including Azure Data Factory, Databricks, and cloud-based data solutions. Proficiency in Python programming for data processing and automation. Understanding of ETL/ELT processes, data pipelines, and data modeling fundamentals. Familiarity with data governance, data quality, and data profiling practices. Experience developing reports, dashboards, or analytics solutions. Understanding of DevOps practices, source control, and CI/CD pipelines. Experience working with SQL databases, REST APIs, data lakes, and other structured and unstructured data sources. Knowledge of data integration and transformation techniques for engineering and operational data. Facilities Engineering Data Services & Workflows Support functional testing and unit testing of data pipelines, models, and data products. Assist with implementation of data quality rules and resolution of data defects. Familiarity with tag aliasing, entity resolution, and entity relationship management. Basic understanding of tag-to-document associations and information management taxonomies (e.g., CFIHOS). Support the creation and maintenance of data quality reports. Perform data enrichment and augmentation activities to improve engineering datasets. Assist in tag extraction and scraping activities from engineering documents. Build and support API connectors for engineering and operational data sources. Product Development Assist with the design and maintenance of conceptual, logical, and physical data models. Develop and support data pipelines, data validation rules, and testing activities. Participate in Agile development processes including sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives. Support requirements gathering, design, development, testing, and deployment of data solutions. Apply data analytics techniques to support business decision-making. Seek guidance from senior data engineers and actively participate in knowledge sharing and skill development activities. Soft Skills: Teamwork & Collaboration Work effectively with business owners, analysts, product owners, data engineers, and other project team members. Contribute to Agile delivery teams and support successful execution of sprint commitments. Demonstrate a willingness to learn new tools, technologies, and business processes. Communication Effectively communicate technical information to team members and stakeholders. Participate in requirements gathering sessions and discussions with business users. Document technical solutions, data requirements, test results, and process improvements. Problem Solving & Organization Demonstrate strong analytical and troubleshooting skills. Investigate data quality issues and support root cause analysis activities. Manage assigned tasks effectively and deliver high-quality work within established timelines. Support process improvement initiatives and identify opportunities for automation and efficiency. Chevron ENGINE supports global operations, supporting business requirements across the world. Accordingly, the work hours for employees will be aligned to support business requirements. The standard work week will be Monday to Friday. Working hours are 8:00am to 5:00pm or 1.30pm to 10.30pm.
