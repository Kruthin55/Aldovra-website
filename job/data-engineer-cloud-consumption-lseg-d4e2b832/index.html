Data Engineer - Cloud Consumption

LSEG · BLR-Divyasree Technopolis, India

About the role

Overview – Data Engineer, Cloud Consumption LSEG and Microsoft are engaged in an exciting strategic partnership for the development of next-generation data, analytics and cloud infrastructure solutions. Our customers’ needs are evolving and so are financial markets. This partnership will transform the way customers discover, analyse and trade securities around the world. It will also advance our cloud strategy and build the improved resilience, efficiency and agility that our customers need. As part of the LSEG Microsoft Partnership (LMP) the Data Engineer, Azure Cloud Consumption is responsible for overseeing data analysis activities across all aspects of cloud consumption, leveraging analytic and technical skills to innovate, build, and maintain well-managed data solutions and capabilities to govern the reporting, evaluation and optimisation of cloud finances. Key

Responsibilities

Responsible for the maintenance, ongoing evaluation of and continual improvement of the cloud consumption model, providing feedback on data inputs and clarity on any assumptions to maintain robust oversight of consumption evaluation, modelling and reporting Ensures related data stores and model inputs adhere to defined best practices for curation and maintenance of accurate data points across the lifecycle of each application migration. Evaluate and forecast cloud consumption and develop and maintain reports for various forums detailing progress, risks and opportunities Model cloud consumption including scenario and sensitivity analysis – to inform decision making, insights, and identify gaps and trends. Optimise cloud consumption models across product development and technology application migration. Track and validate actual delivery performance to ensure assumptions are in sync with the product build and migration timelines. Drive greater transparency across delivery teams to inform reporting and improve root cause analysis – and make recommendations for improvements. undefined Skills and

Qualifications

10+ years of relevant industry experience. Technical Knowledge: Understanding of fundamentals of operating infrastructure in the Cloud, preferably Azure. Modelling Skills: Experience of creating sophisticated, long term cost models – from a delivery, product, customer, technology and financial perspective. Analytical Skills: Ability to interpret usage data and analytics to make informed recommendations and track actions associated to the recommendations. Technical Skills: Excel and Power BI are essential to succeed in this role. Experience with Python and Databricks would be beneficial. Excellent Communication Skills: both verbal and written. Problem-Solving Skills: Curiosity about identifying issues and providing effective solutions.
