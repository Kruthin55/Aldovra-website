About the Role

CloudSEK is looking for an Offensive Security Intern – Customer Threat Advisor to help customers convert cyber intelligence into clear action.

This is a customer-facing security role for someone with a strong understanding of cyber threats who can communicate technical findings clearly to CISOs, security teams, and business stakeholders.

The role sits at the intersection of customer success, threat intelligence, AI security, infrastructure vulnerability management, and security consulting.

What You Will Do

Own Customer Threat Outcomes

You will help ensure that customers clearly understand the threats affecting them and the value CloudSEK is delivering.

You will

Review threat intelligence findings for assigned accounts.

Identify high-priority incidents that need customer attention.

Understand and explain the business impact of threats.

Assist in sharing critical incident updates with CISOs and security teams.

Follow up on important incidents until there is action or closure.

Role Summary

We are seeking a technically strong and motivated Security Engineering Intern to join our team, focusing on offensive security, exploitation, and automation .

The ideal candidate will possess a strong understanding of Red Teaming methodologies , hands-on knowledge of web application and API penetration testing , and proficiency in scripting, security automation, and vulnerability exploitation techniques .

You will work on validating real-world security risks and providing technical insights and advisory to clients.
