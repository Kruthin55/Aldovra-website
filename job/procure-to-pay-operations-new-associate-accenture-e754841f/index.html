We embrace the power of change to create value and shared success for our clients, people, shareholders, partners and communities.Visit us at www.accenture.com What would you do?

You will be aligned with our Intelligent Finance Operations vertically and will support Procure to Pay (P2P) operations for the client.

You will be part of the team at Accenture s Jaipur Delivery Center (JPDC3), performing P2P transactions under the guidance of your team lead.

Procure to Pay covers PO and non-PO invoice processing, vendor master data management, payment runs, T&E, vendor reconciliations, AP query, helpdesk management and period-end close activities.

You will index and process invoices, update data in client systems and respond to basic queries as per defined procedures.

You will meet daily productivity and quality targets and follow the defined controls and SOPs. What are we looking for?

Primary skill; Procure to Pay, Accounts Payable (invoice processing), freshers are welcome

Basic understanding of accounting concepts (debit, credit, invoices, payments)

Working knowledge of MS Excel and MS Office

Attention to detail and willingness to learn

Good written and verbal communication skills

Ability to work well in a team

Based in or willing to relocate to Jaipur and work from the JPDC3 office

Flexible working in the time zone of the supported country Roles and

Responsibilities

In this role you are required to solve routine problems, largely through precedent and referral to general guidelines.

Your primary interaction is within your own team and your direct supervisor.

In this role you will be given detailed instructions on all tasks.

The decisions that you make impact your own work and are closely supervised.

You will be an individual contributor as a part of a team with a predetermined, narrow scope of work.
