AI Model Optimization Engineer

Intel · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Details: Job Description: The Role and Impact Intel is seeking an AI Model Performance Optimization Engineer to help deliver high-performance AI solutions that enable the next generation of client computing experiences. In this role, you will enable, validate, benchmark, optimize, and debug AI models to ensure they run efficiently and accurately across Intel hardware platforms. Working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning frameworks, software engineering, and hardware acceleration, you will collaborate with engineers across Intel to improve AI model performance, scalability, and adoption by customers and partners worldwide. This is an exciting opportunity for engineers passionate about Generative AI, machine learning systems, model optimization, and performance engineering who want to influence technologies used by millions of people globally. What You Will Do

Key responsibilities

will include but not limited to: Enable, validate, benchmark, and optimize AI models on Intel platforms to address customer and market segment needs. Analyze model performance and identify optimization opportunities. Debug issues across AI frameworks, software stacks, and hardware platforms. Develop and execute tests to ensure model functionality, accuracy, and performance. Collaborate with software, hardware, compiler, and architecture teams to solve technical challenges. Utilize AI-assisted development tools to improve engineering productivity. Contribute code, bug fixes, documentation, and test coverage to internal and open-source projects. Work effectively with globally distributed engineering teams. Mentor junior engineers and share technical expertise across the team. Behavioral traits that we are looking for: Curiosity and Learning Agility: Demonstrates a passion for learning emerging AI technologies, frameworks, and development methodologies. Problem Solving: Approaches complex technical challenges with structured analysis and creative thinking. Ownership: Takes initiative, drives issues to resolution, and delivers high-quality results with accountability. Collaboration: Works effectively across software, hardware, architecture, and product teams to achieve shared objectives. Adaptability: Thrives in fast-paced environments where priorities and technologies evolve rapidly. Communication: Communicates complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences. Global Teamwork: Works effectively across geographically distributed teams and diverse perspectives.
