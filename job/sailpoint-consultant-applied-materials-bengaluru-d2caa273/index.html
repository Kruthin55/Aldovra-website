Responsibilities

Has strong knowledge and experience with Identity Governance and Administration (IGA) concepts, principles, and technologies. Strong experience with hands-on knowledge on SailPoint ISC and IdentityIQ development and deployment Participate in requirements gathering sessions. Experience in onboarding Connectors Ex. JDBC, Delimited File, AD, Workday, WebService, SCIM etc. Experience in SailPoint IdentityIQ certifications such as Application Owner certification and Entitlement Owner certification etc. Extensive understanding of SailPoint IIQ LCM workflows and experience in customizing workflows. Have good understanding of SCIM REST API, Java, Json, XML, Webservices Have experience with implementing custom rules, forms, workflows, reports and configuring various type of access certifications in IIQ. Good hands on writing the ldap queries and sql query Create Automation or Manual Test Plans for application testing. Experience with SQL Databases Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills required. Support internal and external assessments and audits. Report information security administration processes and practices violations. Monitors contingent workers work quality and provides guidelines in the delivery of project and support services. Adheres to compliance processes and procedures Required (candidates must have this experience): Bachelors Science in Computer Science or equivalent experience Candidate with 5-7 years IAM product suite implementation experience. 3+ years with SailPoint IdentityIQ (IIQ) 6.x & 7.x,8.x including Life Cycle Manager (LCM) and Compliance Manager (CM), all SailPoint IIQ modules along with installation and upgrade experience. Expertise in User Provisioning, Access Certifications, Access Request, Workflow, Delegated Administration, and Password Self-Service in SailPoint IdentityIQ. Experience in Java/J2EE development, XML, XHTML, JSF, CSS, HTML, JavaScript, AngularJS, PowerShell, Java, BeanShell, Web Service and SQL Databases with development experience. Knowledge of IdentityNow will be added advantage.
