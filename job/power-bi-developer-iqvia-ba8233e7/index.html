Power BI Developer

IQVIA · Kochi, India

About the role

Job Overview Lead the design and delivery of end-to-end BI and analytics solutions using Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and Tableau. Provide hands-on technical leadership across data integration, semantic modeling, dashboard development, visualization, and performance optimization. Design solutions leveraging Microsoft Fabric capabilities, including Lakehouse, Data Warehouse, Data Factory, Dataflows Gen2, notebooks, pipelines, and OneLake. Develop and govern scalable Power BI semantic models, DAX measures, dashboards, and reports. Provide technical leadership for Tableau dashboards, data models, performance optimization, and visualization standards. Translate business requirements into robust technical designs and implementation approaches. Establish BI architecture, coding standards, reusable components, governance, and development best practices. Perform technical/design reviews and troubleshoot complex data, performance, and integration issues. Mentor BI developers and provide technical direction across multiple projects and workstreams. Collaborate with stakeholders, data engineers, architects, and project teams to ensure successful and timely delivery. Support effort estimation, solution design, technical documentation, release planning, and deployment. Promote adoption of modern analytics capabilities and identify opportunities to migrate or modernize legacy BI solutions. IQVIA is a leading global provider of clinical research services, commercial insights and healthcare intelligence to the life sciences and healthcare industries. We create intelligent connections to accelerate the development and commercialization of innovative medical treatments to help improve patient outcomes and population health worldwide.
