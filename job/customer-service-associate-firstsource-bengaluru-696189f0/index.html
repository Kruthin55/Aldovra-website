Customer Service Associate

Firstsource · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description – Banking Chat Executive Location: Mumbai (WFO) Account: Nationwide Customer Service Executive – Asynchronous Chat BGV clearance Job Purpose Handle customer queries through asynchronous chat. Provide accurate, timely, and professional responses. Deliver excellent customer service and meet business targets. Key

Responsibilities

Respond to customer queries through chat. Manage multiple chats efficiently. Follow banking policies and procedures. Ensure proper customer authentication. Take ownership of customer issues. Provide clear and complete information. Identify and support vulnerable and complaint customers. Escalate complex queries when required. Maintain quality and compliance standards. Achieve CSAT, productivity, and response time targets. Skills Required Good written and verbal English. Strong customer service skills. Good typing and computer skills. Ability to manage multiple chats. Good problem-solving skills. Willingness to work in rotational shifts.(24/7) Qualification & Experience Graduate in any discipline.
