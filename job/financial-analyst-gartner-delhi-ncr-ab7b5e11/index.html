Financial Analyst

Gartner · Delhi NCR

About the role

Gartner is looking for a well-rounded Financial Analyst who can provide critical business partner support to our Global Services & Delivery business units. The candidate will be responsible for supporting Executive Services and Global Services & Delivery leadership with data insights, budgets and forecasts, and other ad hoc analyses. What you will do: Financial Reporting & Variance Analysis : Ensure accurate and timely reporting of monthly and quarterly financial performance, key business metrics, and variance analysis to support executive decision-making. Ad Hoc Data Modeling & Analytics: Provide ad hoc data modeling and analytics to the business to help guide strategic and operational initiatives and drive performance improvements. Synthesize and translate complex datasets into meaningful executive-level business insights. Leverage statistical modeling techniques to identify underlying trends, analyze and solve business challenges, and make actionable recommendations to business unit leaders. Process Improvement & Operations: Identify opportunities to automate data extraction, streamline internal financial workflows, and implement robust data operation processes to increase team efficiency. Reporting Infrastructure & Automation : Design, build, and maintain interactive Power BI dashboards and financial reports to streamline monthly reporting cycles and reduce manual workloads. Business Partnership & Communication: Partner cross-functionally with leaders across Finance, Service Delivery, HR, and Recruiting to support workforce planning, expense management, and strategic initiatives. Translate complex data into clear, actionable business insights using effective visual storytelling (charts, tables, presentations). What you will need: Education & Experience: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Statistics, or a related field with 3+ years of relevant industry experience with a large organization (or a master’s degree with 2+ years of experience). Financial Modeling & Standards: Advanced financial modeling skills in Microsoft Excel, coupled with a solid understanding of financial principles. Data Synthesis: Proven ability to extrapolate large volumes of data, distilling complex metrics into executive-level insights. Global Collaboration: Strong verbal and written communication skills with experience working effectively across matrixed, cross-functional, and global virtual teams. Business Intelligence & Technical Tools: Hands-on experience developing dashboards in self-service analytics tools (Power BI preferred). Working knowledge of SQL is a strong plus.
