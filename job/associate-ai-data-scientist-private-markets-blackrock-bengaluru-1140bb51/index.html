Associate, AI Data Scientist- Private Markets

BlackRock · Bengaluru

About the role

About this role Build efficient, trusted agentic AI for private-markets intelligence Research, build and scale advanced agentic systems and fit-for-purpose language models that turn complex information into dependable data, research and decision intelligence. Your team You will join a multidisciplinary AI and data science team focused on applying advanced AI to high-value challenges in private markets. The team brings together data science, engineering, product and domain expertise, and works closely with users to develop practical capabilities that improve how information is acquired, researched and managed. You will work in an environment where applied research is expected to become dependable production capability. We value intellectual curiosity, rigorous experimentation, strong engineering and responsible AI, with quality, security and appropriate human oversight designed in from the start. Your role & impact As an Associate, AI Data Scientist, you will be a hands-on technical contributor responsible for researching, building and scaling enterprise-grade agentic AI capabilities. Working directly with users and senior technical leaders, you will translate complex private-markets workflows into well-evaluated systems that move from experimentation to governed production and sustained adoption. Your core mandate is to advance multi-agent LLM systems through effective agent design patterns, rigorous evaluation and disciplined model engineering. You will develop workflows that plan, retrieve, reason, use tools, verify and escalate appropriately, while selecting the simplest architecture and model that can meet the required quality, latency, cost and control objectives. You will also help establish the lifecycle for open-source small language models and LLMs, from model discovery and benchmarking through training, adaptation, fine-tuning, deployment, monitoring and retirement. Your impact will be visible in stronger data quality, faster research, scalable automation and reusable AI capabilities that help BlackRock Preqin remain at the forefront of private-markets data and technology. apply fit-for-purpose routing and batching where they improve efficiency. · Develop model-adaptation strategies using prompt and context optimisation, supervised fine-tuning, parameter-efficient fine-tuning or other suitable techniques, supported by governed dataset creation, experiment tracking, reproducibility and controlled promotion criteria. · Operate the lifecycle for trained and fine-tuned open-source models, including data and model versioning, lineage, experiment and artifact management, evaluation gates, registry and release controls, deployment, performance and drift monitoring, rollback, retraining triggers and retirement. · Build production-quality Python components that integrate models with enterprise data, retrieval services, APIs, workflow orchestration and human-review experiences. · Improve grounding and trust through attribution, provenance, uncertainty handling, validation checks, access controls and clear separation of source evidence from model-generated conclusions. · Own delivered reliability across testing, observability, latency, cost and maintainability, and diagnose model, data, prompt and orchestration failures in production. · Document findings, reusable patterns and trade-offs; contribute to design reviews; and communicate results clearly to research, product, engineering, risk and senior stakeholders. · Partner with engineering, product, risk, privacy, information security, legal and compliance colleagues to support responsible, secure and accountable use of AI.
