Apprentice, Channels Client On-boarding Specialist

S&P Global · Hyderabad

About the role

About the Role: Grade Level (for internal use): 05 ​The Team: The Data Management Solutions (DMS) On-Boarding team understands the complexities involved with our datafeed/API products and is responsible for reviewing and facilitating entitlement requests involving our DMS platforms. This entails managing the fulfilment cases created after Sales Opportunities/Quotes are submitted for DMS platforms; reviewing the product components in these Trials/New Sales/Renewals; understanding package relationships, technical dependencies, and licensing requirements; and working with our Fulfilment team to ensure that the client has the correct entitlements. The team is in constant communication with Datafeed Application Specialists (DAS), Relationship Managers (RM), Sales and Product Managers (PM) and will be expected to understand our DMS product and processes. Our team culture is built on proactiveness, business excellence, both independence and interdependence, continuous learning, and improvement, with everyone working towards a common goal to support the Commercial Organization and the company's business overall. The Impact: This team acts as a gatekeeper and ensures that clients receive the correct product and packages for all DMS products. It includes the process from the moment that the fulfilment request is created until the Fulfilment Team adds what is requested by Commercial teams and ultimately by the client. You will be encouraged to raise improvements to the process and update the procedures. By enthusiastically identifying issues with the process, the Associate will help provide excellent support to our internal customers. What’s in it for you: It will provide you with an opportunity to learn and improve your knowledge about our DMS products, processes, and business. You will liaise with different stakeholders and your input will make a difference regarding the overall customer experience. You will have an integral part of the product development cycle and roll out.

Responsibilities

You will have exposure to the whole range of DMS product and will be expected to understand end-to-end the operational side of the business, along with each of the products provided. The process involves reviewing Trials/New Sales/Renewals and its product components, approvals, and third-party licenses by confirming the correct product components are added so the client receives the product and data as expected. The job involves keeping the information correctly documented in Salesforce and being able to manage/improve/update the different tools used by the DMS team for the completion of the on boarding of clients. This position requires attention to detail as it involves validation of approvals and third- party licenses by confirming the correct product components are added so the client receives the product and data as expected. You will need to demonstrable evidence of planning and organization skills and can work quickly and accurately in a pressured environment. Ability to remain positive and calm and project a “can do” attitude. The candidate will be in continuous communication with Application Specialists, Relationship Managers, Sales reps and Product Managers Ensure strong lines of communication and working relationships are maintained at all times across the team, stakeholders, process owners, and business partners – developing trust and accountability, and recognizing that a strong working relationship is in the best interest of our customers.
