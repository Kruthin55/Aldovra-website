Tax Analyst

LSEG · Bengaluru

About the role

The main responsibilities of this role will be assisting in Indirect tax compliances. The selected candidate for this role is required to ensure all information is provided timely, coordinate with tax consultants and service owners, and ensure timely tax payments and all other tax filing compliances. Key

Responsibilities

The selected candidate will have ownership for: Assisting/providing information for indirect tax filings of overseas countries. Coordinating with tax consultants and service owners on tax compliance. Preparing Tax accounts reconciliations/Blackline Reconciliation. Assisting with the preparation and posting of tax journals. Supporting for various reports and request from the internal business team. Able to communicate with Tax authorities on the Various issues and resolve in timely manner Candidate Profile The candidate should possess the following: A high degree of organization and ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment. Possessing a Finance/Accounting/commerce/ degree or professional qualification (CA/CIMA/ACCA) at the intermediary level. Minimum of 2 experience in oversea tax compliance work or overseas support services will be an added advantage. Good accounting knowledge with strong analytical and problem-solving skills including approach of thinking end to end. Hands on experience in Oracle.
