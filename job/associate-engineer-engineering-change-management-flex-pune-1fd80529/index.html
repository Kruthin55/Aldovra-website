Associate Engineer, Engineering Change Management

Flex · Pune

About the role

Flex is the diversified manufacturing partner of choice that helps market-leading brands design, build and deliver innovative products that improve the world. A career at Flex offers the opportunity to make a difference and invest in your growth in a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment. If you are excited about a role but don't meet every bullet point, we encourage you to apply and join us to create the extraordinary. Job Summary

To support our extraordinary teams who build great products and contribute to our growth, we’re looking to add a Associate Engineer, Engineering Change Management located in Pune. What a typical day looks like: Keeps track of received data and source documents. Prepares and sorts source documents, and identifies and interprets data to be entered. Types process and status reports, and assigns and monitors document numbers. Receives all forms of product documentation (electronic and hard copy). Performs data validation tasks by checking the information vs. alternate data sources. Contacts those preparing source documents to resolve questions, inconsistencies or missing data. Performs data corrections according to direction. Enters alphabetic, numeric or symbolic data from source documents into computer following format displayed on screen, and enters necessary codes. Collects and distributes documentation required for processing Engineering Change Orders (ECO's). Collects and reports process performance metrics. Tracks work completed by customer program for reporting purposes. The experience we’re looking to add to our team: Diploma E&TC or Electrical with 0 to 2 years of experience in EMS industries/Process engineering. Should be willing to work in any shift.
