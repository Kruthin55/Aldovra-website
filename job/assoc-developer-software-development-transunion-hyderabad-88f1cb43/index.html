Responsibilities

The Associate Developer is an entry-level software engineering role responsible for supporting the design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance of software applications and platform services. The role works closely with experienced developers, architects, and engineering teams while building hands-on technical expertise. Required Knowledge And Experiences Key

Responsibilities

Develop and enhance application components based on defined technical requirements and design specifications. Write clean, maintainable, and testable code following established development standards. Participate in code reviews and incorporate feedback from senior developers and technical leads. Perform unit testing, troubleshooting, debugging, and resolution of application issues. Support application deployments and validate functionality across development and test environments. Work with APIs, databases, and application integrations as required by the assigned product or platform. Support CI/CD and engineering automation activities. Assist with operational issues by reviewing application logs and troubleshooting defects. Maintain appropriate technical documentation for developed components and changes. Work collaboratively with development, QA, DevOps, architecture, and product teams. Build knowledge of the organization's applications, platforms, engineering standards, and development practices.
