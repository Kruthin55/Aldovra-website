Job Requisition: Engine Installation Engineer The Building Construction Products Division Engine Systems Team is looking for an Engine Installation Engineer to be responsible for the Engine Installation System designs on multiple machine platforms for the Backhoe Loaders (BHL), Compact Track Loader (CTL) and Skid Steer Loader (SSL) products. This role will be responsible for defining Installation system requirements at the start of any new product development cycle, utilize analytical tools to determine optimum system design, and collaborating with our purchasing team to evaluate various supplier’s Installation components. Addition to upfront system design, the successful candidate will be responsible for all Engine Installation components validation activities including test planning, data analysis and effectiveness of Engine system to meet requirements. What will you do: · Establish Engine Installation requirements with inputs from other machine systems including engine, interior/exterior machine sound, hydraulic systems, and HVAC systems · Responsible for Engine system development, including Fuel System, Couplings, Engine Mounts, Air Intake System and EATS installation. · Extensive collaboration with component suppliers and internal purchasing resources to find solutions optimized for performance and cost · Responsible for all Engine Installation validation activities including analysis, test planning and post processing test data · Resolve any issue found during development or on customer machines with robust and fully validated solutions What You Have (Basic Qualifications): · Degree requirement : Bachelor’s degree in engineering or Masters’s degree in engineering. · Analytical Thinking: Proven ability to use tools and techniques to analyze data and determine the root cause of complex technical problems and create alternative solutions. · Computer-Aided Design & Engineering: Knowledge of tools and techniques of specialized graphics programs; ability to produce, analyze, simulate, and test product design drafts. · Product Design and Development: Knowledge of construction products, ability to design, build, and develop a new product from concept through to production. · Project Management: Proven ability to perform complex assignments, prioritize workload, and manage multiple projects simultaneously. Excellent organizational, communication, and presentation skills are required. Willing and able to collaborate with international teams in various time zones. Top candidates will also bring: · Previous experience in Engine Installation design from concept to production. · Strong knowledge of Backhoe Loaders, Skid Steer Loaders or Compact Track Loaders. Additional Information: · Primary Position Location: Tiruvallur, Tamil Nadu · This position does not offer relocation assistance. · Visa sponsorship is not available for this posting. · 10% of domestic and international travel is expected. This position requires working onsite five days a week. Relocation is available for this position. Visa Sponsorship is not available for this position.
