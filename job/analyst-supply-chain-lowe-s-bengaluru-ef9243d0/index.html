Responsibilities

Assist in the report creation and analysis to assess how both short and long-term volume forecasts may impact Inbound, Outbound, Storage and product flow capacities for one node Support the integration of volume flows into the weekly/monthly/yearly capacity and network forecasting processes Assist the Inventory Planning team with combining and comparing proposed product flow plans to the established flow plans and their relation to capacity Perform root cause analysis of current sales and product velocity shifts to determine how they may impact upcoming storage forecasts and capacities Analyze, predict, and monitor how the Inventory and Replenishment Planning groups activities will affect future inventory and product flow Work with the Flow Forecasting analysts to ensure clear and concise communications through email and direct communications for both strategic and tactical consumption to the appropriate stakeholder groups Conduct Ad-Hoc analysis using existing data sources and reports to provide any additional actionable insights as they may be needed Years of Experience Overall 2+ years of Experience 2+ years of experience in Supply chain Management 1+ years experience of S&OP/S&OE Education Qualification & Certifications (optional) Required Minimum
