AI-Enabled Tools Development and Automation Engineer

Intel · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Details: Job Description: We are looking for a talented Engineer who is proficient in developing AI-enabled Tools and Workflow Automation Solutions by combining Generative AI, Agentic AI, software automation, and systems engineering. As we evolve our engineering solutions, we want to strengthen the team with an engineer who can help drive our transition toward AI-enabled and increasingly agent-driven engineering, while retaining strong software, Linux, systems, and automation fundamentals. The candidate should be able to understand traditional engineering workflows and identify opportunities to make them more intelligent, self-diagnosing, knowledge-driven, and autonomous, while appropriately combining AI reasoning with deterministic software development. Business group: At Intel Corporation, our teams focus on advancing technology to enrich the lives of every person on Earth. This position is part of a group dedicated to creating and refining tools that enable world-class engineering and cutting-edge innovation. By developing high-quality software solutions, the team plays a crucial role in supporting Intel's broader mission of delivering industry-leading silicon and driving technological progress. Key

Responsibilities

includes, but not limited to: - Utilize modern programming languages, secure coding practices, software development methodologies, and AI to create smart, innovative and reliable tools and automation solutions. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand requirements and implement solutions that advance tool functionality and efficiency. - Participate in continuous improvement initiatives to optimize workflows and methodologies, ensuring scalability and adaptability of tools. - Identify opportunities to progressively transform existing engineering workflows into AI-driven and autonomous workflows. - Build AI agents for engineering automation, diagnostics, troubleshooting, and knowledge retrieval. - Develop RAG solutions using engineering documentation, logs, historical issues, and structured/unstructured data. - Build reliable workflows combining AI reasoning with deterministic execution, validation, and guardrails. - Evaluate AI solutions for accuracy, relevance, reliability, and failure modes.

Qualifications

BTech or MTech or Equivalent degree in the field of Computer Science or related fields with 4+ years of relevant experience. Skills- Software and Systems Engineering Strong Python programming and software-engineering fundamentals Good working knowledge of Linux operating systems REST APIs, Client/Server architecture, Microservices, and Docker Git repo usage and practices Strong debugging, log analysis, and root-cause-analysis skills Good understanding of networking Ability to develop modular, reusable, and scalable automation frameworks- AI / Agentic Engineering Hands-on experience developing Generative AI/LLM-based applications Agentic AI and AI-agent workflows Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Prompt and context engineering Embeddings, semantic search, and vector databases Tool/function calling and AI-to-system integration MCP or similar agent, tool integration concepts AI evaluation, grounding, hallucination mitigation, and guardrails - Additional Experience (exposure to the following would be beneficial, but should not be treated as mandatory hiring criteria) System provisioning and configuration Platform Engineering BIOS, UEFI, firmware Lab infrastructure and asset management Job Type: Experienced Hire Shift: Shift 1 (India) Primary Location: India, Bangalore Additional Locations: Posting Statement: All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, religious creed, sex, national origin, ancestry, age, physical or mental disability, medical condition, genetic information, military and veteran status, marital status, pregnancy, gender, gender expression, gender identity, sexual orientation, or any other characteristic protected by local law, regulation, or ordinance.
