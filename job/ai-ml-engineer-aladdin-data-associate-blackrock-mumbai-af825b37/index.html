AI/ML Engineer, Aladdin Data, Associate

BlackRock · Mumbai

About the role

About this role Your team BlackRock Data Office (BDO) combines industry-leading data standards with advanced intelligence capabilities to transform how we operate and define what is possible with technology at enterprise scale. We partner with client-facing teams to address their data and artificial intelligence (AI) needs while maintaining a long-term focus on transformation. Our cross-functional team brings together engineers, data scientists, data analysts, and domain specialists. The team works on strategic and transformative initiatives across generative AI, predictive systems, and data-intensive operations, partnering with stakeholders globally to enable business transformation at scale. Your role and impact As an AI/ML Engineer, you will help design and build production-grade AI and machine learning (ML) solutions. You will combine hands-on software engineering with first-principles problem solving, translating business needs into technical solutions and evaluating emerging AI/ML capabilities for practical application. You will work closely with domain experts, data analysts, engineers, and business stakeholders, contributing high-quality code and helping take solutions from early-stage ideas through reliable production deployment. Your responsibilities In every role at BlackRock, you'll be expected to apply sound judgement and critical thinking to solve complex problems, adapt as the business evolves, and combine the curiosity to explore new approaches and technologies with the rigor to challenge the results. The scope of this role also includes the following responsibilities: Translate project specifications and business requirements into robust technical designs and architectures, using first-principles problem solving to evaluate solutions and build consensus. Develop and demonstrate emerging ideas to stakeholders, clearly communicating complex technical concepts to build alignment around proposed solutions. Partner with domain experts and data analysts to translate business processes into technical solutions and evaluate emerging AI and machine learning capabilities for relevant use cases. Make frequent, high-quality code contributions across existing and greenfield projects. Deploy reliable services across development and production environments while adhering to enterprise software development lifecycle standards. Contribute to scalable, production-grade AI and machine learning solutions that support business transformation across BlackRock Data Office initiatives. Your experience Undergraduate degree in a relevant technical or scientific field; a postgraduate degree in a relevant field is preferred. 5+ years of software engineering experience designing, building, and deploying production-grade systems, with demonstrated systems-level problem-solving capabilities. 2+ years of Generative AI or machine learning engineering experience, including production-grade AI/ML systems; machine learning or statistical modeling experience is valued. Expert-level proficiency in Python. Expert-level proficiency in SQL and data systems. Understanding of natural language processing, with professional experience using pre-GPT transformer technologies such as BART, is valued. Strong communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences; expert-level Java proficiency is also valued. Our benefits To help you stay energized, engaged and inspired, we offer a wide range of benefits including a strong retirement plan, tuition reimbursement, comprehensive healthcare, support for working parents and Flexible Time Off (FTO) so you can relax, recharge and be there for the people you care about. Our hybrid work model BlackRock’s hybrid work model is designed to enable a culture of collaboration and apprenticeship that enriches the experience of our employees, while supporting flexibility for all. Employees are currently required to work at least 4 days in the office per week, with the flexibility to work from home 1 day a week. Some business groups may require more time in the office due to their roles and responsibilities. We remain focused on increasing the impactful moments that arise when we work together in person – aligned with our commitment to performance and innovation. As a new joiner, you can count on this hybrid model to accelerate your learning and onboarding experience here at BlackRock. Guidance on AI use for candidates At BlackRock, AI has long been part of how we work – enhancing decision-making, improving operations, and helping us deliver better outcomes for clients. We encourage candidates to use AI thoughtfully to learn, prepare, and work more effectively; but during our interview process, we want to focus on getting to know you through your own experiences, thinking, and judgment. To support you, we’ve provided guidance on when and how to use AI during our hiring process so you can approach each step with confidence and showcase your best self. About BlackRock At BlackRock, we are all connected by one mission: to help more and more people experience financial well-being. Our clients, and the people they serve, are saving for retirement, paying for their children’s educations, buying homes and starting businesses. Their investments also help to strengthen the global economy: support businesses small and large; finance infrastructure projects that connect and power cities; and facilitate innovations that drive progress. This mission would not be possible without our smartest investment – the one we make in our employees. It’s why we’re dedicated to creating an environment where our colleagues feel welcomed, valued and supported with networks, benefits and development opportunities to help them thrive. To learn more about BlackRock, please visit Careers.BlackRock.com . We also encourage you to get to know us on LinkedIn , Instagram , YouTube , X , and TikTok . BlackRock is proud to be an Equal Opportunity Employer. We evaluate qualified applicants without regard to age, disability, family status, gender identity, race, religion, sex, sexual orientation and other protected attributes at law.
