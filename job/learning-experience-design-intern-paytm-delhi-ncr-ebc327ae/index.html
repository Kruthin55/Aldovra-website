Learning Experience Design Intern

Paytm · Delhi NCR

About the role

Role Overview 1. Video Production & Multimedia Content Creation Assist in creating training videos, explainer videos, and basic animated content. Support video editing activities, including trimming, adding transitions, syncing audio, and incorporating visual elements. Assist in preparing storyboards and visual concepts for training videos. Create short videos, teasers, and awareness content for internal communication and learning initiatives. 2. Graphic Design & Visual Communication Create posters, banners, infographics, learning calendars, and other creative communication materials. Design basic illustrations, icons, and visual assets for training presentations and digital learning modules. Convert information into simple and visually appealing designs. Follow existing brand guidelines and maintain consistency in layouts, fonts, and colours. 3. AI-Powered Content Creation Explore and experiment with AI tools for generating images, videos, voiceovers, and other creative content. Assist in creating AI-generated visuals, characters, animations, and learning scenarios. Use AI tools to support content creation and improve creative workflows. Stay curious about emerging AI tools and explore their practical applications in digital learning and content development. Required Skills & Tools Basic understanding of graphic & Motion design and an interest in creating visual content. Familiarity with Canva or similar design tools. Basic knowledge of video editing tools such as Premiere Pro, or similar applications. Basic knowledge of Microsoft PowerPoint. Interest in exploring AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Canva AI, and other generative AI platforms. Good written and verbal communication skills. Creative mindset, attention to detail, and willingness to learn new tools and technologies. Good to Have (Not Mandatory) Exposure to Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, or After Effects. Basic understanding of animation and video production. Familiarity with Articulate Storyline 360 or Rise 360. Awareness of e-learning platforms and Learning Management Systems (LMS). A portfolio of college projects, personal designs, videos, or other creative work.
