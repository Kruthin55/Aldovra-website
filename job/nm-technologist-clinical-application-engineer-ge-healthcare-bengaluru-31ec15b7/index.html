NM Technologist & Clinical Application Engineer

GE HealthCare · Bengaluru

About the role

Job Description Summary The Nuclear Medicine Technologist / Clinical Applications Engineer role brings hands-on clinical Nuclear Medicine and Molecular Imaging expertise to the Engineering organization. The role represents the clinical user throughout the design, development, verification, and validation of PET/CT and SPECT/CT imaging systems. The role ensures that Engineering decisions on workflow, image quality, quantitative accuracy, and radiation safety are grounded in clinical practice, reducing field issues and supporting safe, high-quality diagnostic imaging. GE Healthcare is a leading global medical technology and digital solutions innovator. Our mission is to improve lives in the moments that matter. Unlock your ambition, turn ideas into world-changing realities, and join an organization where every voice makes a difference, and every difference builds a healthier world. Job Description Roles and

Responsibilities

· Perform and support end-to-end PET/CT and SPECT/CT clinical and non-clinical workflows, from protocol selection to acquisition, reconstruction, and image review. · Support CT-related aspects of PET/CT workflows, including CT protocols, attenuation-correction CT, and contrast workflows. · Handle liquid radioisotopes/radiopharmaceuticals, including F-18 FDG, for authorized PET system testing and engineering experiments. · Perform and support PET/SPECT Daily QA, calibration, quality-control activities, and system-readiness testing. · Prepare, position, and scan PET/SPECT phantoms. · Perform and support NEMA and defined system-performance testing, including sensitivity, uniformity, and spatial-resolution testing. · Measure radioactive activity using dose calibrators. · Perform radiation and contamination surveys, follow ALARA and personnel-monitoring requirements, and comply with applicable radiation-safety, regulatory (including AERB), and site-authorization requirements for liquid-isotope activities in the Bangalore MI bays. · Translate clinical workflows, user needs, and field feedback into system design inputs, system requirements, use cases, test cases, and acceptance criteria. · Participate in design reviews to identify workflow-optimization and product-improvement opportunities. · Perform and support Engineering Verification and Clinical Validation through radioactive-source-based testing, including image-quality, quantitative imaging, and system-performance evaluations, as well as clinical image evaluations and usability studies, to confirm that user needs and intended use are met. · Reproduce, document, and troubleshoot clinical application, workflow issues and support engineering defect investigation and verification. · Evaluate advanced clinical applications, including advanced reconstruction, quantitative and dynamic imaging, motion correction, and cardiac, neurology, and oncology workflows. · Evaluate advanced post-processing, visualization, and treatment-response applications, including fusion, segmentation, and lesion quantification. · Verify DICOM image flow, PACS and workstation connectivity, and image transfer, and troubleshoot basic interoperability issues in clinical imaging environments. · Develop clinical evidence, best-practice content, and workflow insights to support product definition and clinical claims. · Support New Product Introduction (NPI) programs, including prototype evaluation, formative testing, system integration, software releases, and upgrades for PET/SPECT products. · Develop clinical training content and deliver on-system training, practical demonstrations, and competency assessments. · Collaborate with Systems, Software, Hardware, V&V, Image Quality, Medical Physics, Service, Quality, Regulatory, EHS, and Radiation Safety teams. · Maintain engineering test, QA/QC, clinical application, and radiation-safety documentation. . Non-hybrid, full-time (5-days) position on site. Educational

Qualifications

· Bachelor's degree or postgraduate degree in Nuclear Medicine Technology (NMT). Required Skills · 5+ years of professional experience in Nuclear Medicine / Molecular Imaging, with hands-on PET/CT and/or SPECT/CT experience in a clinical or engineering environment. · Mandatory hands-on experience in handling sealed and liquid radioisotopes/radiopharmaceuticals, including PET/SPECT radiotracers. · Strong knowledge of Molecular Imaging principles and PET/SPECT imaging workflows. · Working knowledge of CT fundamentals, attenuation correction, contrast workflows, and PET/CT protocol optimization. · Strong knowledge of Nuclear Medicine instrumentation and radiation physics. · Hands-on experience in PET/SPECT system-level testing. · Strong focus on safety, quality, data integrity, and traceability. · Strong analytical, troubleshooting, collaboration, and communication skills, with the ability to bridge clinical users and Engineering teams. Desired Characteristics · Prior experience in Clinical Applications, medical-device Engineering, Systems Engineering, or Verification & Validation, including familiarity with medical-device design control processes. · Knowledge of advanced PET/SPECT post-processing and treatment-response applications. · Experience developing clinical evidence, best-practice content, or workflow insights to support product definition and clinical claims. · Working knowledge of DICOM image flow, PACS and workstation connectivity, image transfer, and basic interoperability troubleshooting within clinical imaging environments. · Awareness of emerging Molecular Imaging workflows, evolving clinical practices, and relevant technology trends.
