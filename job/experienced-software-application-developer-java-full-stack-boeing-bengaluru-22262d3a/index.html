Position Overview Boeing Digital Services Technology India is currently looking for an Experienced Software Application Developer – Java Full Stack to join their team in Bangalore, IN to support end-to-end software delivery within our digital aircraft maintenance product suite. This role is grounded in strong software development fundamentals, with an emphasis on leveraging modern AI-assisted tools to build cloud-native systems at the cutting edge of aerospace technology. The core activities include designing, developing, and testing of the Full Stack Modules and components of the maintenance performance toolbox product. You serve as a subject matter expert for software domains, system-specific issues, processes, and regulations. Tracks and evaluates software team to ensure product and process conformance to project plans and industry standards. You will work closely with Developers, Engineering, Greenfield Leadership, Data Science, and Architects to deliver product features and capabilities that keep AI and ML at the center of our solutions. You will be modernizing systems that have been running the business for years, while building new ones designed to run it for years to come. Position

Responsibilities

Collaborate with stakeholders, product owners, and program teams to understand business needs and technical constraints. Support deployments, troubleshoot issues in real time, and ensure successful adoption in production environments. Act as a technical liaison between stakeholders, field teams, and internal product teams to improve product fit. Estimate work accurately, participate in backlog refinement, and deliver committed sprint and PI objectives. Raise impediments and risks promptly and work with relevant stakeholders to resolve them. Mentor junior engineers and contribute to technical decision-making, solution quality, and engineering best practices. Contribute to reusable patterns, deployment accelerators, and continuous improvement. Basic
