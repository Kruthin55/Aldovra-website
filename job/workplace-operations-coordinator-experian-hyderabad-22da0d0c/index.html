Workplace Operations Coordinator

Experian · Hyderabad

About the role

Experian is a global data and technology company, powering opportunities for people and businesses around the world. We operate across a range of markets, from financial services to healthcare, automotive, agribusiness, insurance, and many more. Experian invests in people and new advanced technologies to unlock the power of data. We have an amazing team of 25,200 people in 32 countries.

What you'll do

As a Workplace Operations Coordinator, you will support the day-to-day facilities and workplace operations for our Hyderabad office, ensuring a safe, efficient, and positive employee experience. You will: Coordinate daily workplace and facilities operations, maintaining high standards across the site. Monitor service delivery and work closely with vendors, contractors, and internal stakeholders to resolve operational issues. Manage planned and reactive maintenance activities, ensuring timely completion and accurate record keeping. Oversee workplace helpdesk requests, driving prompt resolution and service excellence. Conduct regular workplace audits and inspections, implementing corrective actions where required. Support vendor onboarding, performance management, and operational reviews. Assist with facilities budget management, invoice verification, purchase orders, accruals, and forecasting activities. Use operational data and employee feedback to identify and implement workplace improvements.

Qualifications

Membership of the Institute of Workplace and Facilities Management, or recognised equivalent Degree or recognised equivalent What we expect from you Experience in workplace, facilities, or operations management within a fast-paced environment. Strong vendor, contractor, and stakeholder management skills. Understanding of workplace health, safety, compliance, and operational best practices. Experience supporting budgets, invoices, purchase orders, and financial administration. Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities effectively. Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive approach to continuous improvement. Effective verbal and written communication skills with the ability to build relationships across all levels of the organization. High attention to detail and a commitment to delivering exceptional workplace experiences. Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, PowerPoint, and Word. A collaborative, adaptable, and customer-focused mindset. Our uniqueness is that we celebrate yours.
