Associate, AI Engineer

BlackRock · Mumbai

About the role

About this role Business Overview: The Strategic Initiatives team within Outsource Platform Services develops technology solutions and reengineers processes that enable scalability and innovation across the middle office outsourcing business. The team acts as a strategic enabler across the platform ecosystem, advancing AI capabilities, improving processes, and developing solutions that support BlackRock and more than 20 client organizations. The team works at the intersection of business, data, and technology, partnering with users, designers, developers, and product managers to turn ideas and business challenges into practical, scalable solutions. As an Associate on the Strategic Initiatives team, you will help design and deliver scalable technology and AI-enabled solutions that improve how the organization operates and serves clients. You will combine hands-on software engineering with Generative AI and agentic AI capabilities to solve complex business problems and translate emerging technologies into practical applications. You will work in an Agile, collaborative environment and contribute across the development lifecycle, from understanding business needs and prototyping solutions through engineering, testing, integration, and delivery. The role offers the opportunity to work with Python, Java, AI frameworks, cloud technologies, APIs, distributed data technologies, and modern software engineering practices. Roles & Responsibility Design, develop, and deliver scalable technical and AI-enabled solutions, translating business needs and creative concepts into production-ready prototypes. Develop solutions supporting BlackRock and client organizations, partnering with users to understand business needs and design practical solutions within the Aladdin ecosystem. Build clean, testable, production-quality software using Python, Java, AI technologies, and relevant open-source tools, with an emphasis on incremental innovation. Deliver effectively within an Agile, fast-paced, geographically distributed development environment. Solve complex business and technology problems, applying data, process optimization, and emerging technologies to improve scalability and operational outcomes. Collaborate with users, designers, developers, product managers, colleagues, and clients to deliver solutions through a culture of teamwork, transparency, and support. Contribute to a culture of innovation and continuous improvement, including appropriate participation in open-source and engineering communities. AI Engineering Skills Hands-on experience developing applications using LLMs and Generative AI , including prompt engineering, structured outputs, tool/function calling, and LLM API integration. Experience building agentic AI workflows , including state management, orchestration, tool integration, and multi-step reasoning workflows. Understanding of RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) concepts, including embeddings, vector search, document retrieval, and grounding LLM responses. Familiarity with AI evaluation and observability , including techniques for assessing response quality, accuracy, hallucination, latency, and reliability. Exposure to MCP (Model Context Protocol) or similar approaches for connecting AI agents with enterprise tools and data sources would be advantageous. Experience integrating AI services into applications using REST APIs , preferably with frameworks such as FastAPI .

Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical discipline, with 3+ years of hands-on Python development and strong foundations in object-oriented programming, data structures, application programming interfaces (APIs), and software engineering. 2+ years of experience building Generative AI and large language model (LLM) applications, including prompt engineering, structured outputs, tool/function calling, and LLM API integration. Experience developing agentic AI workflows using frameworks such as LangGraph, LangChain, or Google ADK, including state management, orchestration, tool integration, and multi-step workflows. Understanding of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), embeddings, vector search, and grounding, with exposure to AI evaluation, observability, enterprise tool integration approaches such as Model Context Protocol (MCP), and REST API frameworks such as FastAPI. Strong software engineering fundamentals, including object-oriented design, design patterns, source control, continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD), cloud and container technologies, and unit and integration testing. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate business challenges into practical AI-enabled solutions, independently troubleshoot issues, and take ownership of deliverables. Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work across geographically distributed teams and demonstrate curiosity, adaptability, attention to detail, critical thinking, and continuous learning. Our benefits To help you stay energized, engaged and inspired, we offer a wide range of benefits including a strong retirement plan, tuition reimbursement, comprehensive healthcare, support for working parents and Flexible Time Off (FTO) so you can relax, recharge and be there for the people you care about. Our hybrid work model BlackRock’s hybrid work model is designed to enable a culture of collaboration and apprenticeship that enriches the experience of our employees, while supporting flexibility for all. Employees are currently required to work at least 4 days in the office per week, with the flexibility to work from home 1 day a week. Some business groups may require more time in the office due to their roles and responsibilities. We remain focused on increasing the impactful moments that arise when we work together in person – aligned with our commitment to performance and innovation. As a new joiner, you can count on this hybrid model to accelerate your learning and onboarding experience here at BlackRock. Guidance on AI use for candidates At BlackRock, AI has long been part of how we work – enhancing decision-making, improving operations, and helping us deliver better outcomes for clients. We encourage candidates to use AI thoughtfully to learn, prepare, and work more effectively; but during our interview process, we want to focus on getting to know you through your own experiences, thinking, and judgment. To support you, we’ve provided guidance on when and how to use AI during our hiring process so you can approach each step with confidence and showcase your best self. About BlackRock At BlackRock, we are all connected by one mission: to help more and more people experience financial well-being. Our clients, and the people they serve, are saving for retirement, paying for their children’s educations, buying homes and starting businesses. Their investments also help to strengthen the global economy: support businesses small and large; finance infrastructure projects that connect and power cities; and facilitate innovations that drive progress. This mission would not be possible without our smartest investment – the one we make in our employees. It’s why we’re dedicated to creating an environment where our colleagues feel welcomed, valued and supported with networks, benefits and development opportunities to help them thrive.
