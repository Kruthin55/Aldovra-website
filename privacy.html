Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Aldovra may collect and use information when you use the website and platform. The exact data processed depends on the features you use and the services enabled in the current release.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account and profile details such as name, email, phone number, education, location and career preferences.
  • Resumes and information you choose to provide for resume analysis or career guidance.
  • Assessment responses, scores, attempt records and related integrity evidence where the assessment feature explicitly uses it.
  • Support messages, feedback, and transaction references if paid services are enabled.
  • Technical and usage information needed for security, troubleshooting and platform operation.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to create and maintain your profile, provide resume insights, deliver assessments, record results, match opportunities, respond to support requests, protect the platform, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.

3. AI and automated processing

Some features may use automated or AI-assisted processing to analyse resume text, suggest skills or provide career guidance. These outputs can be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed. Do not upload information you are not authorised to share.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers that support hosting, authentication, databases, AI features, security or payments when those services are enabled. We do not claim that every possible feature or provider is active in every release. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the platform.

5. Resumes, assessment evidence and recruiter visibility

We aim to use uploaded resumes and assessment information to provide the requested platform features. Where a feature makes a profile or result visible to recruiters, the platform should explain that visibility in the relevant flow. Avoid uploading sensitive personal data that is not needed for your career profile. Contact us if you have questions about visibility or deletion.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. During a monitored assessment we store small camera photos and screenshots taken at random moments (and when activity such as a tab switch is detected), low-quality audio, and the assessment events. These files are deleted automatically 7 days after an attempt that scores below 75%, 90 days after a passing attempt, and 7 days after an unfinished session. We keep only your two most recent resume files. Your environment video stays on your own YouTube account; we store only its link. Other information is retained for as long as reasonably needed for platform operation, records, dispute handling, security or legal requirements, then deleted or de-identified.

7. Your requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to identity verification and applicable legal or operational retention requirements. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children

The platform is designed for students and job seekers. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the situation.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the platform changes. For privacy questions or requests, email support@aldovra.com.
