Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before starting.

Is my first assessment attempt free?
Yes. Your first Aldovra assessment attempt is free.
How much does a reattempt cost?
Additional assessment attempts are planned at ₹100 per attempt.
What happens after I request an assessment?
Your assessment request is reviewed through Aldovra's assessment workflow. Once approved, you receive the assessment details and instructions.
Will I take the assessment inside the dashboard?
The student dashboard is used to track your assessment status and results. Assessment details and the assessment link are provided through the Aldovra assessment workflow.
What happens after my assessment?
Your score and verification information can be added to your Aldovra skill profile after the assessment process is completed.
