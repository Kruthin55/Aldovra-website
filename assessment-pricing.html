Assessment Pricing

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Your first assessment is free

How pricing works

Creating an Aldovra account is free, and your first skill assessment is free. After that, you pay only for an extra attempt or an extra skill. Every price is shown on the dashboard before you pay, and you pay through our payment partner PayU (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets).

Price list

A score of 75% or more passes the skill, so no reattempt is needed. Prices include all charges; there is no subscription and nothing renews automatically.

Reattempt rules

  • Wait at least 24 hours between attempts of the same skill.
  • You can take at most 4 attempts in any 30 days.
  • Every attempt has fresh questions; you never see the same question twice.

What a payment includes

Each payment buys one assessment attempt for the skill shown at checkout. It does not buy a passing score, a certificate, verified status, a job, an interview or a recruiter response. A certificate is issued only after you score 75% or more, submit your environment video and our team approves it.

Payment confirmation

A payment counts as successful only after we confirm it with PayU. The attempt then unlocks on your dashboard. If money left your account but the attempt did not unlock, email support@aldovra.com with your transaction ID; see our Refund and Cancellation Policy.
