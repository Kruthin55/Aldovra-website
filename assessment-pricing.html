Assessment Pricing

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Current status: no paid checkout

Assessment access

Aldovra's assessment and skill-verification features may be available according to the current platform flow. During payment-provider setup, paid reattempt collection is not enabled. Do not make a payment to an unofficial account or assume a draft price is active.

Planned reattempt pricing

Earlier product planning considered ₹50 or ₹100 reattempt charges depending on the assessment rules and result. These are not active charges while online checkout is disabled. If introduced, the exact price, eligibility and included service will be displayed in the platform before the student confirms payment.

What a paid attempt would include

If paid reattempts are activated, payment would be for the specified assessment reattempt only, subject to the checkout description. It would not buy a passing score, verified status, job placement, interview or recruiter response. Students would receive the attempt or access explicitly described at checkout after the transaction is verified.

Payment confirmation

When checkout is introduced, a payment should be considered successful only after the platform verifies the transaction with the payment provider. If you are charged but do not receive the eligible attempt, contact helloaldovra.com@gmail.com with your transaction reference.
